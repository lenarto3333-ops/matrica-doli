## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a born leader with the strength of spirit of a lion — a family pattern in which wisdom and noble authority border on pride and a hunger to dominate, until the person learns to lead others rather than rule over them.

## Positive

Unmistakable leadership qualities and the ability to inspire and lead, deep wisdom born of life experience, unshakable courage in physical and moral trials, an unwavering sense of justice and measured decision-making, genuine nobility and high moral principles, and a readiness to protect the weaker without expecting reward.

## Negative

Excessive pride and a tendency to see others as lesser, a hunger for power and a tendency to exploit others' weaknesses, narrow-mindedness and a flat refusal to accept others' opinions, a stubborn unwillingness to admit one's own mistakes, self-adoration and a painful reaction to criticism or to others' successes, and an authoritarian leadership style that smothers initiative and keeps those around in fear.

## Summary

The lion's strength serves the good when it leads others forward rather than holding them in submission.
