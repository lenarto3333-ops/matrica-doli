## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about hidden feelings and unfulfilled dreams — an ancestral pattern where a person either sincerely accepts their life as it is, or flees reality into fantasy and forbidden liaisons.

## Positive

A sincere love of life and the ability to find many reasons for joy in it, reliability at home and at work, backed by a stable income and colleagues' respect, a sober, realistic view of things free of illusion, faithfulness and constancy in relationships, valuing the comfort of home over outside adventures, and an absorbing hobby alongside work that produces tangible results.

## Negative

Constant dissatisfaction with family, work, and social standing, coupled with a feeling of being underappreciated, unrealized dreams of prestige and success with no concrete steps taken toward them, a shaky self-image swinging between inflated self-regard and total self-doubt, an endless search for oneself through frequent job and hobby changes without real commitment to anything, and secret affairs on the side, where personal happiness is justified without regard for the consequences to one's family.

## Summary

True intimacy is born not in secret dreams, but in honesty with oneself and with those one loves.
