## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about boundless creative power — an ancestral pattern where a brilliant vision of the world borders on detachment from reality, until the person learns to carry an idea all the way through to completion.

## Positive

Boundless creativity and the ability to see potential in the most ordinary things, a fresh perspective that breaks out of formulaic thinking and connects the unconnectable, deep passion and a genuine devotion to one's craft in the noblest sense, sharp intuition that arrives in flashes of inspiration, and the courage to bring the new and unexpected into being.

## Negative

Isolation caused by a language others can't understand and by emotional detachment, perfectionism bordering on self-punishment and unfinished projects in pursuit of an unattainable ideal, resistance to routine that leads to missed deadlines and broken promises, self-centeredness and dismissal of others' opinions and criticism, and a blurred line between imagination and reality that gets in the way of concrete action.

## Summary

Talent becomes strength only when a flight of imagination lands as something real and finished.
