## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with missed opportunities — a family pattern in which the ability to turn past mistakes into valuable experience and an endless postponing of life out of fear of a new mistake live side by side, until the person learns to act here and now.

## Positive

The ability to turn missed chances into a source of valuable experience, heightened attentiveness in decision-making after painful lessons, the resolve to act when a new opportunity appears, a particular sense of the value of the present moment and a reluctance to put life off, and a sober realism in assessing situations without illusory hopes.

## Negative

Constant regret that breeds general dissatisfaction with life, a habit of postponing important decisions again and again despite lessons already learned, a paralyzing fear of failure that gets in the way of action, getting stuck in the past at the expense of the resources of the present, and passively waiting for a miracle instead of taking one's own steps.

## Summary

Past losses stop weighing a person down the moment they become a lesson for a decisive step forward, rather than a reason to wait for the perfect moment.
