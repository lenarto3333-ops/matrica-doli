## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a talent for bringing people together around a shared goal — a family pattern in which natural charisma and a gift for inspiring a team can either unite whole communities or curdle into a selfishness that destroys others' trust.

## Positive

Natural charisma and the ability to inspire and support the people around them, an art for finding common ground with all kinds of people, an insight that sees the big picture and builds workable strategies, a flexible mind when it comes to embracing change, integrity and responsibility that earn trust, and a rare talent for assembling a team and inspiring it to act.

## Negative

A self-centered focus that breeds discord within the group, impatience and shallowness that leave matters unfinished, a lack of empathy that gets in the way of building sincere relationships, a rigid inflexibility that stalls progress toward shared goals, and a gap between words and actions that undermines others' trust.

## Summary

The mission of unity is fulfilled when personal charisma serves the shared cause rather than one's own ambition.
