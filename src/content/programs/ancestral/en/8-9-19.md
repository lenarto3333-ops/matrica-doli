## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the struggle between inexhaustible optimism and deep despair — an ancestral pattern in which faith in a better future borders on a tendency to give up, until the person learns to search for a way out instead of drowning in hopelessness.

## Positive

An inexhaustible optimism and love of life even in difficult circumstances, a charisma and sense of humor that make one the center of attention, a balanced self-esteem free of false modesty or arrogance, an ability to inspire and reassure others in stressful moments, and strategic thinking with a knack for seeing the whole picture.

## Negative

Sinking into despair and inaction instead of seeking solutions, a conscious avoidance of support even when it's badly needed, devaluing one's own talents and achievements, resisting growth and turning down new opportunities, and a lack of clear goals along with a habit of simply drifting along.

## Summary

Hopelessness retreats the moment a person allows themselves to accept help and take the first step instead of waiting for a miracle.
