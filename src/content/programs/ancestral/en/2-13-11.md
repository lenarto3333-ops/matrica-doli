## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the natural authority of a mentor — an ancestral pattern where genuine inspiration for others borders on intoxication with power, until the person learns to lead without crushing those they lead.

## Positive

An inexhaustible source of inspiration and motivation for others even in difficult situations, sincere selfless support and a knack for finding the right words, wise mentorship that doesn't impose one's own opinion, a sense of fairness and the ability to weigh all sides objectively, an appreciation for discipline backed by one's own example of being organized, and authority that rests on respect rather than fear.

## Negative

Intoxication with power and the illusion of one's own infallibility, merciless criticism that finds fault even where none exists, a mania for control and distrust of delegating tasks to others, an inability to forgive mistakes and a tendency to turn a small slip into grounds for prolonged reproach, overweening pride and a refusal to admit one's own flaws, and an inner conflict between the wish to be loved and the wish to dominate.

## Summary

A true mentor leads others forward through example and respect, not through fear and control.
