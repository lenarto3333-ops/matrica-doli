## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the depth of attachment in relationships — an ancestral pattern in which loyalty and a capacity for emotional closeness live alongside dissolving into a partner and a fear of being alone, until the person learns to keep hold of themselves within a union with another.

## Positive

A loyalty and reliability that hold up even through hardship, the ability to form deep bonds through openness, empathy, and sincerely sharing oneself, practical and emotional support for loved ones balanced with one's own needs, a calm that gives others a sense of safety, and a stability and predictability that inspire trust.

## Negative

A fear of standing out that leads to a complete loss of one's own individuality, emotional manipulation through guilt, tears, and blame, a fear of loneliness that keeps one clinging to toxic relationships, chronic self-doubt and devaluing one's own achievements, passive aggression and avoidance of responsibility, and blurred personal boundaries.

## Summary

Closeness to one's true self, not to others' expectations of a relationship, is what makes attachment healthy.
