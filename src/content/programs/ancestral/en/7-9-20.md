## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship to resources and inheritance — an ancestral pattern in which the ability to grow and wisely manage one's assets lives alongside a fear of losing what has been earned and a painful stinginess, until the person learns to trust and share without fearing they will be left with nothing.

## Positive

A talent for wisely managing resources and growing both material and spiritual wealth, financial stability through well-weighed decisions, ongoing growth and a genuine curiosity about the new, generous mentorship and a readiness to share experience, and a caring nature that brings warmth and comfort into the family.

## Negative

A painful drive to accumulate combined with excessive secrecy, stinginess that denies oneself even the essentials, a wall of silence and unwillingness to share information, stagnant resources and untapped potential, passivity and dependence on others' decisions, and conflicts over inheritance that tear family relationships apart.

## Summary

True wealth grows when it is shared, not hidden behind a wall of silence.
