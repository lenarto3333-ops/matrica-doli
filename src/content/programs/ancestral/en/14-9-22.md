## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a relentless thirst for knowledge and understanding the world as a coherent system — a family pattern in which erudition and a gift for teaching live alongside the danger of turning any conversation into a lecture, until the person learns to share knowledge instead of imposing it.

## Positive

A phenomenal memory and the capacity to accumulate knowledge from the widest variety of fields, a genuine drive to get to the bottom of things instead of settling for surface answers, a natural gift for teaching, explaining the complex simply, a systemic view of the world as a web of interconnections, and the ability to find unconventional solutions at the intersection of different fields of knowledge.

## Negative

A habit of turning any conversation into a lecture or a lengthy explanation, an almost total inability to admit one's own mistakes and taking any remark as a personal insult, a painful nitpicking over small details, arrogance and a hidden contempt for less-informed people, and gradual self-isolation caused by a difficult temperament.

## Summary

Knowledge becomes a gift only when it's shared with respect for the listener, rather than used to prove one's own superiority.
