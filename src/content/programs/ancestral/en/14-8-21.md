## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about holding opposites within oneself — a family pattern in which the ability to see a situation from different sides and reconcile the irreconcilable lives alongside swinging between extremes, until the person learns to find inner balance instead of picking a single side.

## Positive

The ability to view a situation from several angles at once and understand the relativity of any truth, a natural gift for peacemaking that finds common ground between warring sides, flexible thinking and ease in switching between different systems of belief, a diplomatic talent for finding words acceptable to everyone, and a holistic perception of the world as a single interconnected system.

## Negative

Constantly swinging between opposite poles and exhausting emotional whiplash, a paralysis of decision-making when seeing every side makes a final choice impossible, an inner war between reason and feeling that drains all one's strength, double standards applied to oneself versus others, and chronic indecisiveness that turns life into a string of postponed choices.

## Summary

Seeing every side becomes wisdom rather than a trap when it's paired with the courage to eventually make one's own choice.
