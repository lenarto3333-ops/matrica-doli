## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about inner wholeness and balance — a family pattern in which strength of spirit and peace of mind live alongside inner emptiness and anxiety, until the person learns to fill themselves from within rather than seek support outside.

## Positive

A whole, integrated character and inner strength that help maintain balance under any circumstances, resilience in the face of hardship, harmony within the family and reliability at work, a calm disposition that draws people in, and strong health with an active way of life.

## Negative

A feeling of inner emptiness and being lost, nervousness and irritability that damage family and work relationships, excessive dependence on others' opinions and a constant need for approval, chronic fatigue and health problems, and an impatience that pushes one to flee from responsibility.

## Summary

Calm and strength return the moment a person learns to rely on their own wholeness rather than on others' recognition.
