## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the test of power and leadership — a family pattern in which wise leadership for the common good and tyranny for the sake of one's own ego live side by side, until the person learns to use power as service rather than dominance.

## Positive

A natural ability to lead through respect and trust rather than coercion, the capacity to take responsibility while recognizing how decisions affect others, magnetic charisma grounded in sincerity and inner strength, strategic thinking that foresees long-term consequences, and a willingness to share power so people can grow and develop their talents.

## Negative

An obsession with power for its own sake and a drive to control everyone and everything, tyranny and the suppression of others' will, a dependence on power like an addiction, manipulation, intrigue, and psychological pressure, and excessive pride that prevents admitting one's own mistakes.

## Summary

Power heals rather than destroys when it serves people instead of one's own ego.
