## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about family hierarchy and the distribution of roles within a family — a family pattern in which respect for traditional order lives alongside its distortion, until the person learns to build a healthy structure of relationships instead of inherited chaos.

## Positive

A commitment to traditional values in which every family member knows their place and role, genuine love and a great deal of time devoted to raising children, special respect for the older generation, passed on in turn to one's own children, an inclination toward working in structured organizations with a clear system of management, and thoroughness and foresight in caring for the family's well-being and comfort.

## Negative

A distorted view of family hierarchy inherited from a broken home, unbalanced roles in which the father takes no part in raising the children, along with the resulting gaps in their upbringing, reversed roles in which the wife takes on the entire burden of providing and suppresses her husband's initiative, no clear division of household duties and chaos in daily life, serious difficulties around having and raising children, and strained relationships with relatives along with unresolved property disputes.

## Summary

A healthy hierarchy is not about subordination but about a conscious, mutually agreed balance of roles within the family.
