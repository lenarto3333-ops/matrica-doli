## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a path through trials and temptations — a family pattern in which willpower and self-destructive impulses live side by side, until the person learns to make a conscious choice.

## Positive

A deep drive for self-knowledge, resilient willpower, the ability to overcome temptations and hardships, wisdom and endurance gained through trials, a subtle emotional depth, inspired creativity, and the capacity for conscious choice.

## Negative

Uncontrolled impulsiveness, a tendency toward addiction, a loss of self-control under stress, unstable moral bearings, self-destructive tendencies, and neglecting priorities for the sake of momentary desires.

## Summary

This is a tendency that can be recognized and worked through — not a sentence.
