## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a mismatch between effort and result — a family pattern in which energy and a sincere willingness to help live alongside a sense of futile labor, until the person learns to look not for more effort but for a different direction for it.

## Positive

High energy and initiative, determination and productivity, a readiness to take an active part in what's happening, empathy and a capacity for compassion, and generosity paired with a genuine desire to help others.

## Negative

Considerable effort with no matching return in any undertaking, financial hardship and family tension, including difficulty conceiving a child, a sense of the pointlessness of one's efforts, uncertain prospects for results, and disappointment and emotional discomfort from the mismatch between effort and reward.

## Summary

Results come when energy is aimed at a precise target, rather than scattered across everything at once.
