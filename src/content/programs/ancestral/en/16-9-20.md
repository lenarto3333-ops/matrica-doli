## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the courage to rethink what's established and build something new — a family pattern in which strength of spirit and firm principles live alongside the risk of turning one's own convictions into dogma, until the person learns to combine confidence with respect for others' opinions.

## Positive

A readiness to experiment and try unconventional solutions where others cling to the familiar, the ability to actively shape one's own future rather than wait passively, a remarkable strength of spirit in the face of obstacles, clear principles that are never betrayed for short-term gain, taking responsibility for one's own actions and their consequences, and a high degree of organization and a planned approach to one's affairs.

## Negative

Convictions carved in stone that resist any new ideas, hostility toward differing views and a certainty that one's own position is the only correct one, an inability to listen and a habit of interrupting before hearing the other person out, treating any compromise as a personal defeat, a habit of being an unsolicited critic that damages relationships, and an exhausting perfectionism with unattainable demands placed on oneself and others.

## Summary

Reforming strength serves the good when new rules are born through dialogue, not imposed in place of old dogmas.
