## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a taste for life and the ability to savor every moment — a family pattern in which genuine optimism and a sensuous delight in the world live alongside the risk of placing momentary pleasure above duties and people, until the ability to combine joy with responsibility comes along.

## Positive

A contagious optimism and love of life, the ability to find joy in small things and inspire others with it, spontaneity and a readiness to act without excessive hesitation, complete immersion in the present moment, and a heightened sensitivity to tastes, scents, and beauty all around.

## Negative

Dangerous impulsiveness and actions later regretted, shirking duties for the sake of momentary desires, a tendency toward addictions — from gambling to shopping — chasing ever stronger sensations, inconsistency and a loss of interest in tasks and relationships once the initial excitement fades, and a selfish hedonism that comes at others' expense.

## Summary

A taste for life fully reveals itself when pleasure walks alongside discipline instead of replacing it.
