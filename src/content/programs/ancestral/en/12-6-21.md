## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with life's trials — a family pattern in which inner resilience in the face of hardship and a habit of getting stuck in the role of a victim of circumstance live side by side, until the person learns to take responsibility for their own life instead of looking for someone to blame.

## Positive

Inner strength that allows one to endure prolonged hardship, the ability to adapt to change while preserving one's own integrity, a sober, undistorted view of any situation, a healthy attitude toward loss as a natural part of life, and compassion born of one's own pain that helps support others.

## Negative

Constant complaining and collecting grievances as a way of relating to others, learned helplessness and a belief that circumstances cannot be changed, a habit of shifting responsibility onto outside culprits, getting stuck in prolonged grief after loss, and passivity disguised as humble acceptance of fate.

## Summary

Resilience overcomes victimhood the moment a person acknowledges their own power to influence their circumstances.
