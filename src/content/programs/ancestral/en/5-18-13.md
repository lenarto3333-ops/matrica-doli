## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the willingness to sacrifice oneself for others — a family pattern in which sincere devotion can become a genuine gift or turn into stubborn self-sacrifice that harms the self and intolerance of others' weakness.

## Positive

Selfless devotion and a readiness to help even at the cost of one's own effort, resilience and optimism that find happiness despite hardship, warmth of heart, empathy, and compassion for others, an art for negotiation built on charisma and well-spoken words, and an unshakable faith that keeps morale up even in hopeless situations.

## Negative

Stubborn inflexibility that works against one's own interests, an intolerance of children's tears and tantrums that erupts into shouting, a confusion of cause and effect that leads to mistaken conclusions, quarrelsomeness and irritability over the smallest thing, and getting stuck in painful memories of past sacrifices and mistakes.

## Summary

Self-sacrifice becomes strength only when the line between caring for others and caring for oneself stays clear.
