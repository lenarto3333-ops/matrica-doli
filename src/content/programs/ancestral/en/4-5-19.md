## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the accuracy of self-assessment — an ancestral pattern where a person either soberly knows the limits of their own capabilities, or constantly over- or underestimates their strength, undermining trust in themselves and others.

## Positive

An objective self-assessment free of exaggeration or understatement of one's own abilities, reliability and honesty in word and deed, a readiness to help others with advice and action, a life maturity and wisdom that allow confident action in difficult situations, and determination and persistence in reaching one's goals despite obstacles.

## Negative

A distorted sense of one's own capabilities — sometimes inflated, sometimes deflated — that gets in the way of planning one's life, a childlike immaturity and dependence on others' opinions instead of making one's own decisions, unreliability and a tendency to break promises for personal gain, an unwillingness to share knowledge or emotional support with loved ones, and a lack of life direction, drifting passively without real effort toward growth.

## Summary

Strength is fully revealed only when a person sees themselves honestly — without exaggeration and without self-deprecation.
