## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the ability to hold other people's secrets and trust — an ancestral pattern where keen observation and an inability to hold one's tongue live side by side, until the person learns to treat what they hear responsibly.

## Positive

Keen observation and the ability to read nonverbal cues and subtext, reliability and the capacity to keep both others' and one's own secrets, impartiality and fairness in judging people and situations, empathy and a readiness to offer wise counsel, and perceptiveness that guards against manipulation and deceit.

## Negative

Dangerous carelessness with other people's secrets, an inability to recognize the consequences of one's own words for others, a tendency toward petty dishonesty for momentary gain, undermining trust and relationships by disclosing others' secrets, and recurring situations where lost trust costs a friendship or a reputation.

## Summary

The gift of understanding people becomes true strength only when backed by the ability to stay silent — then others' trust works for you, not against you.
