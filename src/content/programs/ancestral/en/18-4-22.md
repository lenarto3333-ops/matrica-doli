## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a natural need for exploration and change — a family pattern in which freedom from convention and a talent for improvisation live alongside the risk of never seeing anything through to the end, until the person learns to turn motion into direction.

## Positive

An inexhaustible curiosity and a lifelong pull to explore the new, a remarkable adaptability that turns any change into an opportunity rather than an obstacle, a masterful improvisation, where life resembles a jazz composition of variations rather than a rigid theme, freedom from convention and the accumulation of rich life experience, and the ability to draw interesting people and unusual opportunities through sheer openness.

## Negative

A paralyzing indecisiveness and avoidance of serious commitments, energy that leaks away into endless deliberation instead of action, a habit of abandoning important undertakings halfway and piling up unfinished projects, a passive drifting through life with no clear direction, and an illusion of movement that actually conceals standing still.

## Summary

Wandering stops being an escape from purpose when at least one path is seen through to the end before setting off on the next.
