## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the power of words and the fear of them — an ancestral pattern in which a gift for vivid, persuasive speech lives alongside a fear of voicing one's own opinion and standing up for oneself, until the person learns to trust their own voice as much as others trust them with their secrets.

## Positive

A masterful command of language and an ability to captivate listeners with vivid imagery, clarity and logic in thinking, remarkable courage and resilience in the face of hardship, an ability to listen attentively without rushing to conclusions, composure under pressure and rational decisions, and reliability in keeping others' secrets.

## Negative

A tendency to avoid communication and withdraw into oneself, self-doubt and confusion in one's own words, an inability to defend one's opinion in an argument and a quick surrender to another's position, suppressing grievances inwardly instead of resolving conflicts openly, and a fear of change and clinging to the familiar that stalls growth.

## Summary

One's voice becomes true strength when a person allows themselves to speak as freely as they know how to listen to others.
