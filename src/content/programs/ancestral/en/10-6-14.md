## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about sudden turns of fate that either toughen a person or terrify them — a family pattern in which easy adaptation to change and a paralyzing fear of uncertainty live side by side, until the person learns to meet the new with inner resilience.

## Positive

An ability to adapt quickly to changing circumstances, openness to new experiences and a broadening of horizons, a constant drive for self-improvement, seeing change as an opportunity rather than a threat, a taste for the unpredictable and for adventure, and an active pursuit of fresh impressions instead of stagnation.

## Negative

Inner instability and frequent shifts in mood or decisions, anxiety that becomes a constant companion, clinging to a familiar comfort zone against the need to grow, a loss of self-control and impulsive reactions under stress, confusion in setting goals and priorities, and a paralyzing fear of uncertainty that gets in the way of action.

## Summary

A turn of fate stops being frightening once change is met as a chance to grow rather than a threat of losing one's footing.
