## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the power of convictions, which can either lead others forward or enslave them — a family pattern in which firmness of principle and a despotic imposition of one's truth live side by side, until the person learns to respect others' freedom to think differently.

## Positive

Firmness of conviction paired with a broad outlook, the ability to view things objectively without being swayed by emotion, deep erudition across a wide range of fields, constant self-development and an understanding of one's own capabilities, complete devotion to a cause that inspires others, and openness to the new and a readiness to experiment.

## Negative

Emotional coldness and a tendency to turn into a true despot, an unconscious replaying of an authoritarian relationship model, a categorical imposing of one's own opinion and a rejection of other viewpoints, suppressing loved ones' self-expression and controlling their autonomy, subordinating all of life to work without balance, and religious or ideological intolerance toward those who think differently.

## Summary

Convictions become a strength rather than a burden on others when they are matched by respect for everyone's right to think for themselves.
