## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about searching for one's true place in life — a family pattern in which adaptability to new circumstances coexists with a fear of change that pushes the person into occupying someone else's job, relationship, or role instead of their own.

## Positive

Flexibility and quick adaptation to new conditions, a well-developed intuition that works like an inner compass for decisions, sociability and the ability to negotiate with anyone, a knack for quickly discovering one's true calling in a profession, and a strong family built on mutual respect and trust.

## Negative

A fear of change and discomfort with anything new, confusion and mistakes in unusual, high-stress situations, distrust of new people and reluctance to make contact, work that drains rather than develops because it isn't a true calling, and a marriage to "the wrong person," held together by habit rather than love.

## Summary

One's true place is found once a person dares to choose by the heart's calling instead of staying where it's merely familiar.
