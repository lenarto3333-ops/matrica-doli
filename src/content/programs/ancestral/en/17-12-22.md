## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the courage to radically rethink one's picture of the world — a family pattern in which inner freedom from imposed beliefs lives alongside the risk of total disorientation and intolerance toward others, until the person learns to change their views without losing their footing.

## Positive

A willingness to regularly revisit one's own beliefs rather than let them calcify, freedom from imposed thought patterns and family and social stereotypes, a genuine curiosity about the unknown paired with critical thinking, a conscious courage to challenge established dogmas, and the ability to see deeply into the essence of things and spot hidden patterns.

## Negative

A complete disorientation, wandering among contradictory ideas with no stable footing, denying inconvenient facts as a defense mechanism and constructing an artificial picture of the world, a radical all-or-nothing thinking that sees reality only in black and white, breaking off past relationships and looking down on "unenlightened" people, and aggression toward anyone who doubts the correctness of one's new views.

## Summary

A shift in worldview brings true freedom when new beliefs are built on facts, rather than simply replacing one extreme with another.
