## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about faith in a better future — a family pattern in which sincere optimism and inspired dreaming live alongside naivety and detachment from reality, until the person learns to combine ideals with a sober view of life.

## Positive

Inexhaustible optimism and a knack for seeing the good in any situation, a thirst for growth and constant self-improvement, high moral standards and loyalty to honesty and fairness, the ability to dream and to inspire others with a vision of a better future, and a genuine passion for one's causes that infects others with enthusiasm.

## Negative

A naive gullibility that leads to disappointment and being deceived by others, detachment from reality and disregard for objective facts, a paradoxical tendency toward dejection and pessimism despite one's idealism, perfectionism and intolerance of imperfection in oneself and others, and constantly inflated expectations of life that end in disappointment.

## Summary

Idealism becomes a real strength when the dream rests on a sober assessment of reality instead of replacing it.
