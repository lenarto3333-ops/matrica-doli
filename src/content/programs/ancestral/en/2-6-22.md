## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a passionate nature that craves true freedom of feeling — an ancestral pattern where sincere infatuation and detachment from reality live side by side, until the person learns to build relationships with real people rather than idealized images.

## Positive

Passion and full commitment in work, hobbies, and love, a taste for life and the ability to delight in small things, inner freedom paired with respect for others' autonomy, unconventional creative thinking, loyalty and reliable support for loved ones, refined aesthetic taste, and the ability to create harmony around oneself.

## Negative

Escaping into fantasy instead of dealing with practical matters, impulsiveness and rash actions with serious consequences for relationships, idealizing a partner and refusing to see their real traits, emotional dependency and a constant need for outside approval, and the risk of self-destructive habits as a way to escape inner pain.

## Summary

Passion stops being a trap once infatuation is aimed at a real person rather than one's own dream of them.
