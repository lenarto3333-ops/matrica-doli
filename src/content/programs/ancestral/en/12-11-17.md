## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about creative imagination and detachment from reality — a family pattern in which a vivid creative gift and an escape from practical life live side by side, until the person learns to back their fantasies with real action.

## Positive

Vivid creativity and the ability to generate new ideas where others see only emptiness, a lively imagination and a gift for envisioning scenarios in advance, an inspiring influence on others and support for their growth, unconventional thinking that finds unexpected solutions, and the courage to experiment and learn from one's own mistakes.

## Negative

Detachment from everyday reality and neglect of practical matters, words that don't match actions, which erodes others' trust, sharp mood swings that destabilize relationships and projects, difficulty being understood because of figurative language that practical people find confusing, chaos in one's affairs, and an unwillingness to accept deadlines and planning.

## Summary

A rich imagination becomes a true gift when it is backed by the habit of seeing one's ideas through to completion.
