## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about devoted service to a dream and to ideals — a family pattern in which inspired dedication to a cause and painful, self-destructive self-sacrifice live side by side, until the person learns to care for themselves as much as for their goal.

## Positive

The ability to tell what matters most from what doesn't and to manage time and resources wisely, a willingness to sacrifice personal comfort for a shared cause, the capacity to inspire others and see beauty in the everyday, an incurable idealism and a pursuit of excellence without compromising one's conscience, and full-hearted devotion to a chosen cause.

## Negative

Self-sacrifice bordering on self-destruction, at the cost of health and relationships, blind fanaticism that rejects criticism and turns those who disagree into enemies, unrealistic plans that lead to constant disappointment, disregard for one's own physical and emotional needs, and a selfish expectation that others should sacrifice for one's dream too.

## Summary

A dream becomes a source of strength rather than a trap when a person pursues it without forgetting to care for their own well-being.
