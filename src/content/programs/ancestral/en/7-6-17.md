## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a multiplicity of feelings and choices in relationships — an ancestral pattern in which a many-sided nature and an easy adaptability live alongside a tangled personal life and difficulty making a final choice, until the person learns to build one deep relationship instead of several parallel ones.

## Positive

A many-sided personality with wide-ranging interests, flexible thinking and an ease at switching between tasks, a creative approach to solving problems, emotional stability and calm in stressful situations, and quick adaptation to new circumstances that makes for a valuable employee and a reliable partner.

## Negative

A tangled personal life with feelings for several people at once, the risk of infidelity even within a strong marriage, complications from divorce or children from previous relationships, indecision when choosing between people, pursuits, or life directions, and a hidden emotional instability beneath a mask of outward calm.

## Summary

True harmony arrives when the energy of scattered choice turns into a conscious decision to be with one person.
