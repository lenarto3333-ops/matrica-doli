## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program of leadership and power — an ancestral pattern where outstanding organizational abilities can serve a shared cause, or can turn into a thirst for control over others, until the person grasps the difference between leading and ruling.

## Positive

Outstanding organizational abilities and the ability to see the whole picture, a natural leader who inspires and motivates those around them, a charisma that draws people and trust, strategic thinking, and the ability to spot trends before others do.

## Negative

An insatiable thirst for power and control, authoritarianism and an unwillingness to tolerate any objection, a readiness to use any trick to hold on to power, a distorted view of reality and a dismissal of facts that contradict one's own convictions, skillful manipulation of people, and a paranoid suspicion of conspiracies.

## Summary

A ruler's true power lies not in subjugating others, but in the ability to lead honestly, keeping that strength in service of the common good.
