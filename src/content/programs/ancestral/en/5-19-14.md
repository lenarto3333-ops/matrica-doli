## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with material wealth — a family pattern in which generosity and financial wisdom can either bring true freedom of choice or curdle into insatiable greed and paranoia over losing money.

## Positive

Confident financial stability achieved through work and practical skill, boundless generosity in sharing material goods, time, and knowledge, comfort and security for the family through quality housing, education, and healthcare, genuine freedom of choice in every area of life, and a steady investment in one's own growth and in supporting others' educational projects and ventures.

## Negative

Insatiable greed that spreads into every area of life, an inflated self-image and a conviction of one's own infallibility, social isolation and a loss of touch with reality, a life entirely governed by material values and judging people by their money, and paranoia and distrust of everyone, even those closest, driven by the fear of losing one's fortune.

## Summary

Wealth serves the person, not the other way around, only when money remains a means of freedom rather than a measure of self-worth.
