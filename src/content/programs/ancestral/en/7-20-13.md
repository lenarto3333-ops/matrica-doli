## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the capacity to constantly renew oneself, living through inner deaths and rebirths, — an ancestral pattern in which a pull toward transformation borders on a fear of the unknown, until the person learns to move through change consciously rather than in a panic.

## Positive

Constant renewal and a readiness to let go of the old without fearing change, a steady drive toward personal growth and new skills, an ability to see opportunities even in difficult circumstances, a capacity to release past trauma in a healthy way, a developed self-reflection and deep self-knowledge, and a spiritual steadiness that provides support through periods of crisis.

## Negative

Painful, nearly destructive inner transformations, a paralyzing fear of the unknown and of new stages of life, a fracturing of one's own identity after every change, a panicked fear of the future and an inability to plan ahead, confusion and a loss of bearings during transitional periods, and severed social ties precisely when support is needed most.

## Summary

Rebirth stops being torment once a person learns to move through change consciously, leaning on loved ones rather than fleeing from them.
