## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the balance between lightness and depth of feeling — an ancestral pattern in which sparkling humor and a gift for bringing people together live alongside a fear of showing genuine feelings or drawing attention to oneself, until the person learns to be natural both in jest and in earnest.

## Positive

A subtle, tactful humor that eases tension without crossing lines, an ease at switching between a playful and a serious tone, a talent for bringing different people together in a warm atmosphere, sharp observation and accurate conclusions drawn from small details, and inspired, well-structured speech that rallies people to act together.

## Negative

An emotional reserve that others read as coldness and distance, a fear of public speaking and leadership roles, shyness that gets in the way of building relationships and taking part in groups, a lack of humor or, conversely, excessive touchiness about others' jokes, and a put-on seriousness that hides vulnerability and pushes people away.

## Summary

True lightness arrives when humor and sincerity of feeling exist not in place of each other but together.
