## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with learning and authority — a family pattern in which the ability to learn directly from life lives alongside a rejection of any teachers or advice, until the person learns to accept others' experience without losing their own independence.

## Positive

Self-directed learning through personal experience and a deep understanding of things, the ability to turn one's own mistakes into steps of growth, developed critical thinking that spots the weak points in any theory, strong analytical abilities and the skill of piecing together a coherent picture from scattered facts, and independence of judgment and the freedom to think outside established patterns.

## Negative

A complete rejection of authority and a refusal to draw on others' wisdom, impenetrable pride and a conviction of one's own exceptionality, repeating others' mistakes by refusing to learn from their experience, conflicts with mentors even over well-meaning advice, and slowed growth from refusing the help of experienced people.

## Summary

Independence of thought becomes a strength when paired with a willingness to accept another's wisdom now and then.
