## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program of spiritual seeking and self-knowledge — an ancestral pattern where inner light and wisdom coexist with a closed-off focus on oneself, until the person learns to share their inner peace with the world instead of hiding from it.

## Positive

An inner light that illuminates the way for oneself and others, an unquenchable thirst for self-knowledge through meditation and reflection, remarkable calm even amid the whirl of events, an energy like a fresh mountain stream that creates a harmonious atmosphere around itself, and subtle intuition bordering on clairvoyance combined with sober analysis.

## Negative

Withdrawal behind an impenetrable wall and indifference to others' problems, unpredictable emotional outbursts over trifles, chronic dissatisfaction and a habit of finding fault with everything and everyone, a tendency toward verbal aggression and a knack for finding others' sore spots, and fixation on trivial details at the expense of seeing what truly matters.

## Summary

Wisdom only gains meaning when the inner light is turned toward others as well, not sealed away inside oneself.
