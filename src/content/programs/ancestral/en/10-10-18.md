## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about trust in a higher order of things — a family pattern in which deep spirituality and the ability to accept life with humility live alongside a fear of the unknown and a stubborn denial of the spiritual, until the person learns to let go of control where control is impossible.

## Positive

Deep spirituality and religiosity that grant inner harmony, an ability to trust fate and accept what happens with humility, a realistic view of things, selfless work and an iron will, and self-sufficiency, independence, and continual growth in skills and outlook.

## Negative

A sharply negative attitude toward religion and the esoteric that sparks conflict, a relentless fear of missing something that keeps one in constant stress, discomfort with the unknown and a skepticism that gets in the way of making the right decisions, success that seems to slip away, and a conscious rejection of life's unpredictable sides that leaves a person rigid and detached.

## Summary

Trust in life comes not through controlling every detail, but through a willingness to accept what cannot be foreseen.
