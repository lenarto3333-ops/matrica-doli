## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about deep expertise and respect for knowledge — a family pattern in which fundamental erudition and reliability live side by side with the risk of hardening into one's own convictions, until the person learns to pair experience with openness to the new.

## Positive

Deep professional knowledge and broad erudition, a thoughtful approach with careful attention to detail before decisions, honesty and integrity in following the rules, a reliability that colleagues can count on in any situation, stability and clarity of thought even in turbulent times, and a charismatic leadership that inspires others.

## Negative

An inertia of thought and resistance to new ideas and methods, a stubborn clinging to one's own convictions against the facts, formulaic judgments that limit openness to unconventional approaches, an excessive conservatism of views that gets in the way of learning useful new skills, and difficulty communicating with people who hold different convictions.

## Summary

True expertise reveals itself when accumulated knowledge goes hand in hand with a readiness to keep learning and to reconsider one's own views.
