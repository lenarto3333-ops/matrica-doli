## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the ability to see potential where others see only the ordinary — an ancestral pattern where an inspiring dream borders on a dangerous obsession with the unattainable, until the person learns to tell a real goal apart from a mirage.

## Positive

Unconventional thinking and a vision of possibilities beyond ordinary perception, a healthy realism and the ability to let go of unattainable goals without regret or self-recrimination, creatively channeling unfulfilled fantasies into art, music, or literature, hard-won experience with disappointment that allows one to sincerely warn others against self-deception, and resilience of character and optimism forged by overcoming setbacks.

## Negative

An obsession with plainly impossible goals that consumes emotional, temporal, and material resources for years, an escape into a world of illusion where reality becomes too painful and the line between imagination and fact grows blurred, chronic despondency from repeated disappointments and a bitterness that keeps one from enjoying even modest achievements, time and opportunities for real growth lost while chasing phantoms, and financial trouble from an unfounded belief that the dream will come true quickly.

## Summary

A dream remains a strength as long as it leads you forward, not when it consumes an entire life in pursuit of the unattainable.
