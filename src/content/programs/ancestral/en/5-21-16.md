## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with things and money — a family pattern in which freedom from material dependence coexists with the risk of either chaotically hoarding what's unneeded or destructively impulsive spending, until the person learns to handle what they have consciously.

## Positive

Freedom from materialism and joy in simple things, financial wisdom and careful handling of money without excessive debt, the ability to value a person rather than their status or wealth, a steady drive toward growth and learning, and care for one's health through routine, movement, and proper nutrition.

## Negative

A tendency to turn one's home into storage for useless clutter, impulsive purchases alternating with stinginess, chasing luxury and status items at the expense of real needs, a chronic dissatisfaction with what one has and endless grumbling, and a dependence on debt paired with living beyond one's means.

## Summary

Possessions stop running one's life once a person learns to choose consciously rather than out of anxiety or a passing impulse.
