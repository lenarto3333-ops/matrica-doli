## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about an unconscious pull to harm oneself precisely when things are going well — a family pattern best recognized first, so it can be turned into a resource rather than lived through blindly.

## Positive

A powerful zest for life, inner joy and sincerity, decisiveness and a sense of responsibility — a person like this fills those around them with vital energy. A strong inner power of transformation, the ability to be reborn after a crisis, and a sharp intuition for what has already outlived its usefulness make it possible to overcome hardship and reach one's goals while keeping relationships strong.

## Negative

A tendency, inherited from the family line, to unconsciously harm oneself — sabotaging one's own success, relationships, or health right when things get "too good." An unconscious pull toward self-destruction drives rash actions and a readiness to sacrifice family and career for questionable impulses; risky experiments push loved ones away and undermine trust, while constant risk-taking takes a toll on finances, emotions, and career.

## Summary

Recognized in time, this pattern turns destructive force into the very same zest for life that originally pushed in the other direction.
