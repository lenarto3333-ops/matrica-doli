## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the ability to transform and rethink one's own life — a family pattern in which the ability to see new opportunities in change lives alongside a fear of fate's major upheavals, until the person learns to trust the process of change.

## Positive

Psychological flexibility and the ability to adjust to circumstances without an inner breakdown, the ability to see new opportunities where others see obstacles, a thirst for knowledge and a constant drive to expand one's horizons, ambition in working on oneself, and successful decisions thanks to quick adaptation to new conditions.

## Negative

Resistance to major change and rejection of even beneficial transformations, emotional instability and anxiety in the face of the new, a fear of change that causes favorable opportunities to be missed, severed social ties in moments of crisis, and loneliness and a loss of meaning from an unwillingness to move forward.

## Summary

The gift of transformation fully reveals itself when fate's changes are seen not as a threat, but as a path to a new self.
