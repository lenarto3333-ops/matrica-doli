## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the trial of prosperity — an ancestral pattern where material wellbeing becomes either a foundation for generosity and caring for others, or a trap of mindless consumption.

## Positive

Foresight and skillful management of money that combines thrift with bold investment, generosity and a readiness to share resources, time, and attention, the ability to create a comfortable and cozy space for oneself and others, hospitality and an open home for friends and family, an inner stability that inspires those around them in hard times, and a sincere ability to appreciate what one already has.

## Negative

Impulsive spending without regard for real needs or consequences, debts and loans taken on for momentary pleasures, living only for entertainment while work feels like a burden and obligations to others get ignored, an inner emptiness beneath outward glamour and a lack of depth in self-development, dependence on indulgences — overeating, alcohol, gambling, judging people by their wealth, and chasing status symbols even on borrowed money.

## Summary

Prosperity reveals a person's true worth only when they know not just how to receive, but how to share and appreciate what they already have.
