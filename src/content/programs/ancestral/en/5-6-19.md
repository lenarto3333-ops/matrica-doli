## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the fire of passion that blazes in the heart — a family pattern in which the gift of inspiring others and loving fervently lives alongside addiction and destructive jealousy, until the person learns to master their own flame rather than be ruled by it.

## Positive

A passionate absorption in whatever captures one's interest, pouring full energy into it, a rare gift for inspiring others with one's ideas and enthusiasm, boundless devotion in relationships — romantic, family, or professional — vivid emotional depth and the ability to delight in small things, and the power to ignite positive change in others' lives simply through one's presence.

## Negative

An obsessive fixation on the object of passion with a loss of touch with reality, self-worth and emotional state entirely dependent on the object of love, destructive jealousy over nothing, complete with interrogations, accusations, and manipulation, emotional exhaustion that spills into mood swings and prolonged apathy, and a loss of rational thinking in moments of intense feeling, leading to impulsive decisions with heavy consequences.

## Summary

The fire of love becomes a gift rather than a curse when passion is directed toward growth rather than dependence on another person.
