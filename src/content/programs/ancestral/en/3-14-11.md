## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a restless relationship with money — an ancestral pattern where a person is torn between craving wealth and fearing its loss, until they learn to determine their own, genuine attitude toward material things.

## Positive

A wise moderation, where every purchase is filtered through real need, investing in self-development, knowledge, and spiritual practice as true wealth, freedom from attachment to possessions, generosity and a readiness to share knowledge and resources, and the ability to create a modest yet cozy and tasteful space around oneself.

## Negative

Endless wavering between a hunger to earn and a sudden renunciation of money, a hidden envy of the well-off disguised as philosophical criticism of wealth, self-deception through excuses like "money is dirty," anxiety about the future despite having enough resources, guilt over any purchase, and harsh judgment of both the rich, as soulless, and the poor, as lazy.

## Summary

Money stops being a source of anxiety once a person honestly acknowledges their true attitude toward it — then moderation becomes freedom rather than an escape from choice.
