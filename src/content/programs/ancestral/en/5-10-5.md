## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about an unrelenting thirst for knowledge and the drive to become a recognized authority in one's field — a family pattern in which deep devotion to a chosen path and inner indecision compete for the upper hand, until the person learns to turn knowledge into action.

## Positive

An insatiable thirst for knowledge and continuous professional growth, recognized authority and the ability to inspire others through expertise and vision, a devoted, consistent commitment to a chosen path despite obstacles, honesty and fairness as the basis of every decision, and a magnetism for success that grows out of focus and the ability to seize opportunities.

## Negative

Passivity and indifference in place of active movement toward a goal, chronic pessimism and a sense that effort is futile, an inner contradiction that turns into indecision and postponed decisions, a fear of change and the opportunities lost because of it, and shifting moods and intentions that leave what's been started forever unfinished.

## Summary

Knowledge becomes power only once it is carried through into action — determination turns accumulated wisdom into real achievement.
