## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a heightened sense of justice — an ancestral pattern in which a rare ability to see the essence of things and weigh right and wrong objectively lives alongside the risk of turning into an eternal judge who sees nothing around but violations.

## Positive

A gift for seeing the heart of a conflict and finding common ground even between irreconcilable sides, an ability to rise above personal likes and dislikes for the sake of objective judgment, a perceptiveness that picks up hidden motives and reads between the lines, a fine sense of harmony that instantly catches any falseness or imbalance, and a natural inclination toward professions tied to law and the defense of justice.

## Negative

A constant outrage at the world's injustice that turns every day into tilting at windmills, a conviction that only one's own truth is real, coupled with contempt for others' opinions, a chronic conflict with any authority perceived as a source of injustice, an exhausting perfectionism that never lets any result be good enough, and a habit of passing judgment on others while remaining blind to one's own flaws.

## Summary

A heightened sense of justice becomes a gift rather than a burden once it learns to notice imperfection in oneself, not only in the world around.
