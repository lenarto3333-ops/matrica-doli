## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about personal freedom and the boundaries of responsibility — a family pattern in which the ability to defend one's own interests conflicts with the habit of taking on others' guilt, until the person learns to tell care apart from self-sacrifice.

## Positive

Independent thinking free of imposed stereotypes, the ability to build harmonious, balanced relationships, the capacity to firmly defend one's own boundaries, walking away from toxic dynamics instead of enduring them, and a constructive approach to problems paired with strategic, long-term thinking.

## Negative

A tendency toward self-sacrifice and a conviction that one is irreplaceable to others, a persistent habit of putting others' problems above one's own needs, an unhealthy dependence in relationships, a habit of taking responsibility for others' mistakes, and a fear of taking control of one's own life.

## Summary

Caring for others stops being exhausting once a person first learns to look after their own boundaries and needs.
