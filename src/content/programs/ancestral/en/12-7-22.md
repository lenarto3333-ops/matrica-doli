## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about resourcefulness in the face of difficulty — a family pattern in which a creative, unconventional approach to obstacles and a tendency to bend the rules and honesty for personal gain live side by side, until the person learns to reach their goals without betraying their own principles.

## Positive

Exceptional resourcefulness and unconventional solutions to everyday problems, flexible thinking and quick adaptation to changing circumstances, the ability to improvise and stay clear-headed under pressure, a skill for breaking complex tasks into simple steps, a fresh perspective on familiar things, and persistence without unnecessary stubbornness in overcoming obstacles.

## Negative

Treating rules as optional suggestions, which undermines others' trust, avoiding responsibility by shifting blame onto others, exploiting other people's vulnerabilities for personal gain, a willingness to distort facts and act dishonestly for a quick win, and situational morality that makes the person unpredictable.

## Summary

Resourcefulness becomes a true gift only when the roundabout path reaches its goal honestly, not at the expense of others' trust.
