## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the influence of one's environment on one's fate — an ancestral pattern in which fortunate coincidences and the support of like-minded people live alongside the risk of falling under the harmful influence of dubious people, until the person learns to trust their own judgment.

## Positive

A favorable circle of people who support and inspire, lucky turns of circumstance that help one reach a goal, independence of judgment and a resistance to blindly following others' influence, unshakeable self-confidence, and a lively curiosity paired with a constant drive for new knowledge and skills.

## Negative

High vulnerability to others' influence and an ease of falling in with dubious company, difficulty making decisions independently and excessive conformity, self-worth that depends too heavily on others' opinions, an inability to break off unhealthy relationships, and a tendency toward various dependencies as an escape from reality.

## Summary

One's environment shapes one's fate only until the person learns to consciously choose whom to walk through life with.
