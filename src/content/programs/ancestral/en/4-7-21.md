## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the ability to see far ahead and take on truly large-scale undertakings — an ancestral pattern where farsightedness and detachment from reality live side by side, until the person learns to turn big ideas into concrete steps.

## Positive

Farsightedness and the ability to work out the consequences of decisions in advance, an analytical mind that spots hidden patterns in complex systems, enthusiasm and energy channeled into important work, treating obstacles as valuable experience, and a broad view that holds both the details and the big picture at once.

## Negative

Building unworkable plans detached from reality, ignoring everyday matters and one's own exhaustion pushed to the limit, standards for oneself and others set too high, dreams that remain nothing but a project with no action behind them, and a self-destructive readiness to sacrifice one's health for the sake of a goal.

## Summary

A large-scale vision works when big dreams are backed by small daily steps.
