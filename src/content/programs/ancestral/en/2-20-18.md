## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a deep connection to the feminine nature, to cycles, and to intuition — an ancestral pattern where a gift for healing and a subtle sense of the hidden compete with captivity to one's own emotions, until the person learns to wield this gift consciously.

## Positive

Highly developed intuition and a subtle understanding of feminine nature, a gift for healing and supporting others, a deep awareness of the maternal role, the ability to see the hidden motives behind people and situations, and a natural wisdom that comes from attunement to life's cycles.

## Negative

Slavish dependence on one's own emotions and moods, a tendency to manipulate feelings instead of speaking honestly, living in illusion and self-deception, an excessive attachment to cycles and phases that gets in the way of decisive action, and irrational decisions driven by the mood of the moment.

## Summary

Intuition and the gift of healing become true gifts only when a person learns to be the mistress of her emotions, not their hostage.
