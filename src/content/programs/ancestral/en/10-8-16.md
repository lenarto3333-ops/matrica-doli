## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the art of reading people and conducting negotiations — a family pattern in which sharp insight and a tendency toward intrigue and manipulation live side by side, until the person learns to use their gift honestly rather than for personal gain.

## Positive

A gift for foresight and reading between the lines, a focus on what matters most and the ability to filter out the secondary, flexibility and quick adaptation to change, a sober, realistic view free of illusions, and the art of negotiation backed by charisma and communication skill.

## Negative

A tendency toward intrigue and manipulation for personal ends, lying and hypocrisy as a habitual tool, an absence of moral boundaries and a readiness to betray even loved ones, an egoism that dictates every action, a chase after money, status, and power at the expense of the spiritual, and a general distrust from those around them along with growing loneliness.

## Summary

A sharp mind and a talent for negotiation serve the good only when directed at an honest deal, not at hidden gain at someone else's expense.
