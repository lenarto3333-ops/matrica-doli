## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about faith in higher powers and moral choice — an ancestral pattern where sincere spiritual light lives alongside the temptation to deny everything sacred, until the person chooses what their inner core will rest on.

## Positive

Deep faith in a higher power and an optimistic outlook on the world and other people, a sincere drive toward spiritual growth, strong empathy and altruism where moral values outweigh material gain, the ability to resolve conflicts peacefully and communicate with respect, and emotional openness and stability that inspires others by example.

## Negative

An aggressive denial of the spiritual and skepticism about human goodness, a pragmatic materialism where gain matters more than feelings or morals, emotional instability and difficulty showing warmth toward loved ones, aggressive manipulation and provocation of those around them, and a destructive influence through harmful habits and inappropriate behavior in public.

## Summary

This program is a choice between a temple and ruins within oneself — and it is the conscious choice of a moral foundation that turns faith into true strength rather than a weapon against others.
