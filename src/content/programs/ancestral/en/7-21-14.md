## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about pain passed down through generations via violence and self-punishment — an ancestral pattern in which deep sensitivity and a capacity for empathy border on cruelty toward oneself and others, until the person learns to accept imperfection without punishing it.

## Positive

A deep capacity for empathy and sincere care for loved ones, honesty and a tendency toward self-reflection, an ability to keep a situation under control, a subtle perception of the world and acceptance of one's own emotions, reliability in friendship, and tenderness and sensitivity to others' pain.

## Negative

A tendency toward violence against oneself and others, as if in punishment for one's own imperfection, a burden of guilt and a habit of punishing oneself for mistakes, defensive barriers that hide vulnerability, a fear of love and rejection, an exhausting perfectionism and self-criticism, and pessimism along with anxiety about health.

## Summary

A vulnerable soul yearning for acceptance hides behind the harshness — and it is accepting oneself, not punishing oneself, that heals this wound.
