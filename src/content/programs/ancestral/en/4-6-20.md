## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a power of love capable of healing and uniting people — an ancestral pattern where sincere compassion and a painful emotional dependency live side by side, until the person learns to love without dissolving into the other.

## Positive

Deep compassion and the ability to feel another's pain, a gift for healing not just the body but the soul, the ability to unite people across any differences, a readiness to give one's time and resources for others, and a sincere drive toward continuous spiritual growth.

## Negative

A painful dependency on the object of one's love and an inability to stand on one's own, losing one's own sense of self and shifting values depending on who's around, manipulating others' feelings for personal gain, emotional tyranny and unpredictable mood swings, and disregard for other people's personal boundaries.

## Summary

Love becomes a saving force when it keeps its own identity intact beside another, rather than dissolving it into them.
