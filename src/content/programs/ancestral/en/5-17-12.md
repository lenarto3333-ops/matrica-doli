## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about visibility and vulnerability to others' judgment — a family pattern in which the courage to speak before an audience coexists with a fear of disgrace that makes a person dread any mistake made in front of others.

## Positive

The courage to speak before a large audience and start conversations with strangers, unshakable focus and a readiness to work hard toward a goal, an impeccable image and clear, persuasive speech that inspires trust instantly, openness to criticism and the ability to learn from mistakes, and diplomacy paired with a talent for building relationships through tact and shared interests.

## Negative

A constant fear of becoming an object of ridicule that blocks decisive action, a reluctance to admit obvious mistakes and take responsibility for them, a narrowed range of choices due to self-doubt and turning down promising opportunities, serious conflicts caused by an unwillingness to compromise, and inner barriers of fear that hold back development in every area of life.

## Summary

Public confidence grows once the right to make a mistake is accepted as naturally as the right to succeed.
