## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about power as either a tool or an end in itself — a family pattern in which leadership rooted in service to others and an obsession with control live side by side, until the person learns to use influence for the common good rather than for power itself.

## Positive

Natural charisma and the ability to lead, a sense of responsibility for others' fate and readiness to serve a shared cause, the skill of building and growing large-scale projects, willingness to take risks for an important goal, and strategic thinking with a vision reaching years ahead.

## Negative

An obsessive drive for power at the expense of moral principles, manipulating people and being willing to sacrifice relationships for it, an insatiable thirst for control, suppressing others' will and ignoring their opinions, and treating power as an end rather than a means.

## Summary

Influence serves the greater good only as long as it remains a means to a shared goal, not an end in itself.
