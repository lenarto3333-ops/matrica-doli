## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the capacity to love with one's whole being, which, left without limits, turns into dependence on another person — an ancestral pattern in which sincere devotion borders on losing oneself, until the person learns to love while remaining a whole, intact self.

## Positive

An ability to love sincerely and devotedly, reliability and loyalty in relationships, a passion and energy that inspires others, a romantic view of the world and an eye for beauty in the ordinary, a readiness to help selflessly, guided by fairness and honesty, and a full engagement and sense of responsibility toward what matters.

## Negative

A painful dependence on a partner's attention along with controlling calls and messages, a panicked fear of loneliness that keeps one clinging to toxic relationships, a careless choice of partners with no regard for compatibility, idealizing a partner while ignoring warning signs, self-denial for others' sake to the point of losing one's own boundaries, and a complete dissolving into another person, abandoning one's own interests and social circle.

## Summary

Love heals rather than enslaves when a person preserves their own identity and boundaries even within the deepest feeling.
