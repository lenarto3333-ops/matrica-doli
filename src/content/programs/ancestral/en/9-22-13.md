## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the bond between generations, which either nourishes the soul or weighs it down — a family pattern in which deep respect for one's lineage and the unreleased souls of ancestors live side by side, until the person restores the severed family ties.

## Positive

An inexhaustible reserve of vital energy that makes it possible to overcome difficult tasks, a strong patriarchal family built on mutual respect and the authority of elders, warm relationships with close and distant relatives, a careful preservation of family traditions and the memory of ancestors, and a gift for anything connected to the history and memory of one's lineage.

## Negative

An unexplainable, heavy melancholy and a depressed state with no visible cause, frequent quarrels and property disputes within the family, severed ties with relatives who cannot be found, years of neglect toward one's family home and roots, and difficulty conceiving and carrying a child.

## Summary

Peace arrives once a person restores the broken threads to their lineage and returns to forgotten roots.
