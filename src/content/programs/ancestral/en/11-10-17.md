## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the ability to turn experience into wisdom — a family pattern in which learning from any situation and a paralyzing regret over missed opportunities live side by side, until the person learns to let go of the past in favor of acting in the present.

## Positive

Inexhaustible energy and enthusiasm in moving toward one's goals, an ability to draw a lesson from any experience — successful or not, the capacity to fully inhabit the present moment, flexible priorities and a readiness to change course for something more important, and sharp intuition paired with a drive for constant self-improvement.

## Negative

Deep regret over past decisions that poisons the present, harsh self-criticism to the point of undermining self-confidence, chronic pessimism that sees a threat in every opportunity, paralyzing indecision and an inability to act, and a fear of the new that pushes one toward stagnation.

## Summary

Experience, even painful experience, becomes a foundation the moment a person stops mourning the past and allows themselves to move forward.
