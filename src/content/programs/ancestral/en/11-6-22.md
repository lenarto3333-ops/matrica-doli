## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about natural charisma and leadership — a family pattern in which the ability to lead others and a sincere love for children lives alongside the temptation to manipulate and pressure people for one's own ends, until the person learns to lead rather than subjugate.

## Positive

Born leadership and remarkable charisma that make it possible to lead all kinds of people, a genuine love for children and a knack for connecting with them, original thinking and analytical ability, strong self-belief and determination, an energy that inspires those around them, and mastery in analyzing complex situations.

## Negative

A pronounced tendency toward manipulation and even blackmail for one's own purposes, destructive consequences for the people nearby and strained relationships with children, reckless risk-taking without weighing the consequences, indifference to others' feelings and needs, excessive attachment to material things, and a readiness to resort to "dirty tricks" to get results.

## Summary

The gift for leadership unfolds fully when it leads people through trust, not through pressure and manipulation.
