## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a rare talent and uniqueness that a person turns either into service to the world or into a cult of their own exceptionality — an ancestral pattern in which a creative gift lives alongside the temptation of superiority.

## Positive

An exceptional creative gift that turns ordinary things into works of art, a sincere wish to benefit others through one's creativity, a sharp mind and wit that help in difficult situations, an impeccable style and aesthetic sensibility, and well-earned recognition and respect from others.

## Negative

A bout of "star syndrome" and excessive pride after early success, an inability to accept criticism, deep depressive slumps during creative dry spells, an obsession with status symbols and financial overspending to maintain an image of success, professional failure in negotiations and business decisions, and a midlife crisis after forty marked by regret and dissatisfaction.

## Summary

Exceptionality reveals itself fully only when talent serves others rather than one's own ego.
