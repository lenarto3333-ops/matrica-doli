## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a hunger to live life to the fullest — an ancestral pattern in which a generous soul and a gift for vividly savoring life live alongside the risk of squandering resources and fleeing responsibility into momentary pleasure, until the person learns to combine a taste for life with care for the future.

## Positive

Boundless life energy and generosity, confidence in one's own exceptional nature and ambition in financial matters, refined taste and a knack for genuine enjoyment of life, the capacity to feel deeply and empathize with others, and the ability to let go of the past without grudges and move forward lightly.

## Negative

Living for the day with no thought of consequences, excessive spending on entertainment and an abdication of responsibility, unstable relationships and the risk of addictions — from alcohol to gambling, a conscious refusal of one's own opportunities and low self-esteem, substituting shallow connections for deep relationships, and unresolved childhood trauma that distorts one's view of the world.

## Summary

A taste for life becomes a true gift when generosity is directed not only at momentary pleasure but also at one's own future.
