## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship to true value — an ancestral pattern in which pragmatic determination and worldly wisdom live alongside perfectionism and detachment from everyday reality, until the person learns to value the material and the spiritual at once.

## Positive

Determination and realism that allow one to focus on what matters without getting lost in details, worldly wisdom and a rational approach to everyday problems, harmony between spiritual and material values, an inner confidence free from painful comparison with others, and self-motivation independent of others' approval.

## Negative

A perfectionism that leads to hours spent fixating on minor details, a straight-A-student syndrome marked by painful sensitivity to criticism and constant comparison with others, detachment from everyday practical reality and a reluctance to handle practical matters, financial instability from a refusal to earn, and chronic frustration from a lack of recognition.

## Summary

The true value of things and achievements is revealed only when perfectionism gives way to inner calm.
