## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the boundary between modesty and concealing the truth about oneself — a family pattern in which sincerity and the urge to hide behind a veil of secrecy struggle against each other, until the person learns to share their life openly while keeping their dignity intact.

## Positive

A healthy pride in one's own achievements without boasting, genuine warmth in relationships with children and family, maturity of character and self-confidence, the ability to assess oneself soberly while keeping a balance between pride and modesty, and calm composure in the face of criticism and others' ill will.

## Negative

Excessive guardedness and unwillingness to share personal or family successes, a tendency to embellish reality or flatly lie to make an impression, inconsistency of views that breeds misunderstanding with others, difficulty making even simple everyday decisions, and a habit of shifting responsibility onto others.

## Summary

The truth about oneself stops being a burden when it is told with dignity rather than hidden out of fear.
