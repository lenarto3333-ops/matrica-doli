## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about accepting one's own body and beauty — an ancestral pattern where the ability to see beauty in imperfection competes with merciless self-criticism, until the person learns to value themselves beyond other people's standards.

## Positive

Self-acceptance and the ability to see beauty in imperfection, valuing inner qualities above appearance, well-developed empathy and compassion for others, independence from beauty standards imposed by society, and a holistic understanding of attractiveness that includes energy and kindness.

## Negative

Merciless self-criticism and dissatisfaction with one's own appearance, a tendency toward social withdrawal, constant comparison of oneself to others, a distorted perception of one's own image, compulsively checking oneself in the mirror and endlessly "fixing" one's appearance, and sabotaging one's own happiness and relationships out of a sense of unworthiness.

## Summary

The body stops being an enemy the moment a person learns to see it not as a display case for others' judgment, but as a home worthy of care and acceptance.
