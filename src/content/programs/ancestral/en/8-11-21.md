## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the gift of creating something new and being ahead of one's time — an ancestral pattern in which visionary thinking borders on detachment from reality, until the person learns to turn ideas into concrete steps.

## Positive

An ability to conceive, refine, and bring breakthrough ideas to life, a developed intuition that anticipates trends and opportunities, a steady drive toward growth and self-improvement, a rich imagination and unconventional solutions beyond the usual frameworks, and a contagious optimism that inspires others toward ambitious goals.

## Negative

Escaping into fantasies detached from reality, an open rejection of mundane, everyday matters, social alienation from thinking too far outside the norm, contempt for social norms and traditions as needless restrictions, and an excessive perfectionism that leads to procrastination and conflict.

## Summary

Ideas change the world only when the visionary learns to carry their plans through to a practical result.
