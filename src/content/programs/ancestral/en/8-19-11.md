## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the right to shape one's own fate — an ancestral pattern in which inner self-sufficiency and determination to reach one's goal live alongside the temptation to bend everyone and everything around to one's will, until the person learns to respect others' freedom as much as their own.

## Positive

A confidence that fate is shaped by one's own hands, not by circumstance, a rare self-sufficiency and independence from others' judgments, determination and a methodical, step-by-step pursuit of goals, a resolve to act even amid uncertainty, a sense of responsibility for one's own decisions without shifting blame onto others, and a clear inner moral compass that does not bend to outside pressure.

## Negative

A self-centeredness that constantly pulls attention toward oneself and drives loved ones away, a stubbornness that turns any compromise into a personal insult, a hunger for control and a drive to meddle in others' decisions, a deafness to opinions that differ from one's own, a life of extremes with no balance between work and rest, and a tendency to judge people and relationships purely through the lens of advantage.

## Summary

True power over one's own fate begins where the need to govern others' fates ends.
