## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about true and false authority — an ancestral pattern in which sincere respect for people and the ability to earn trust through deeds live alongside the temptation to build authority on empty words and other people's merits, until the person learns to value real achievement over the illusion of greatness.

## Positive

Deep respect for people regardless of their status, authority earned among colleagues and loved ones through reliability in relationships, modesty and realism — results speak for themselves without boasting, wise parenting built on mutual respect rather than pressure, and sincere gratitude for simple things and joy in small pleasures.

## Negative

Contempt for others' experience and a conviction in one's own infallibility, loss of others' respect due to a lack of real achievement, a tendency to embellish successes, claim credit for others' merits, and build unrealistic plans, an authoritarian manner with children that pushes them away, and an inner emptiness even amid outward success.

## Summary

True authority is built not on words but on respect for others and honesty with oneself.
