## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a magnetic charisma that captivates everyone around it — an ancestral pattern where the gift of feeling and inspiring others deeply can either heal or turn into a tool of power over other people's feelings.

## Positive

Natural charisma and an energy that draws people in, a subtle sense of what others need — passion or tenderness, a challenge or support, deep empathy and the ability to read hidden desires, emotional freedom and sincerity in expressing feelings, and the ability to inspire others to grow and awaken their hidden talents.

## Negative

Manipulatively playing on others' vulnerabilities for power, money, or amusement, an inner emptiness and diminishing satisfaction from each new conquest, an emotional "vampirism" that feeds on others' passion, jealousy, and despair, a pathological inability to stay faithful and a constant search for new conquests, and deep psychological wounds left in partners after a relationship with such a person.

## Summary

Magnetism becomes a gift rather than a weapon only when it is aimed at genuine connection, not at confirming one's own power over others.
