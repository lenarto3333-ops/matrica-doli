## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program of living at the extremes — an ancestral pattern where everything turns out either remarkably well or in one unbroken run of misfortune, with no calm, stable middle path.

## Positive

Financial stability and a successful career, an unfailingly positive outlook on life and the ability to find joy in small things, decisiveness and energy in new undertakings that inspire others, a refined aesthetic sense and love of beauty, and an active lifestyle full of sport and travel.

## Negative

An endless run of bad luck, where trouble with money, work, and everyday life follows one setback after another, chronic pessimism and dark thoughts even in good times, indifference to beauty, art, and music, rash actions and risky ventures taken without regard for the consequences, and a feeling that failure is an unshakeable companion in everything.

## Summary

The middle ground between "best" and "worst" becomes accessible once a person learns to value stability just as much as dramatic highs or lows.
