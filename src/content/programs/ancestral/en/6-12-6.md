## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about giving of oneself for the sake of loved ones — an ancestral pattern in which a sincere readiness for self-sacrifice and creative drive live alongside chaos and flight from responsibility, until the person learns to give love without destroying themselves in the process.

## Positive

A readiness to sacrifice a great deal for an important goal and unshakeable resolve, creative abilities and unconventional solutions to complex problems, a warmth that creates an atmosphere of comfort, a life-affirming optimism and an eye for beauty in small things, and compassion paired with a readiness to help those in need.

## Negative

Chaotic behavior and an inability to prioritize among many tasks at once, a habit of blaming circumstances instead of taking responsibility, impulsive spending driven by emotion, neglected health from a lack of routine and rest, a pessimism that blocks the view of new opportunities, and an inner emptiness despite outward success.

## Summary

Self-sacrifice becomes a gift of love rather than a loss of self when caring for others does not crowd out caring for one's own life.
