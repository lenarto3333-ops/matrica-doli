## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a pull toward risk and thrill — a family pattern in which courage and a hunger for a vivid life live alongside a dangerous draw toward unjustified risk, until the person learns to channel their boldness constructively.

## Positive

Courage and resolve in meeting challenges where others back down, a hunger for new experience and an unwillingness to settle for routine, unconventional thinking that finds fresh solutions in familiar things, easy adaptation to change and the ability to see new opportunities in it, and an energy that inspires and lifts up those around them.

## Negative

An addiction to adrenaline that demands ever more dangerous situations for satisfaction, impulsive decisions made without weighing the consequences, irresponsibility and a habit of blaming circumstances instead of oneself, dangerous carelessness and disregard for basic precautions, and a distorted perception of risk that overestimates one's own abilities and underestimates real threats.

## Summary

Courage becomes a gift when the hunger for adventure is matched by good judgment and care for oneself.
