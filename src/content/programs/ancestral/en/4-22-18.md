## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a crisis of faith — in oneself, in people, or in a higher power — a family pattern in which a sincere inner foundation and total cynicism live side by side, and it is up to the person whether that faith grows stronger or gets trampled.

## Positive

Inner strength built on sincere faith in one's own abilities and the conviction that fate is in one's own hands, openness to new ideas, a selfless readiness to help others, natural leadership qualities and the ability to inspire those around them, and the ability to value what one already has and find happiness in small everyday joys.

## Negative

Total cynicism and the loss of faith in anything noble, an existential crisis in which neither work, relationships, nor growth feel meaningful, extreme self-centeredness that accounts only for one's own wants, impatience, quick anger, and an inability to compromise, frequent run-ins with the law, and seeing the world as a hostile, meaningless place.

## Summary

Faith that has been trampled once can be rebuilt — and then it becomes the sturdiest foundation in life.
