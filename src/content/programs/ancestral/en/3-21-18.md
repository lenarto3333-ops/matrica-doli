## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the path from self-deception to a sober view of life — an ancestral pattern where a person either stands firmly on the ground, accepting reality as it is, or hides in a world of illusion to avoid discomfort.

## Positive

A realistic view of the world that accepts both the good and the difficult without distortion, concrete action instead of empty dreaming, grounded in a sober assessment of the situation, gradual, stable success achieved through realistic goals and an honest assessment of one's own resources, flexibility and a readiness to change course when circumstances demand it, and ongoing self-development along with a stable self-image that doesn't depend on others' opinions.

## Negative

A tendency to believe what one wishes were true rather than what is real, which over time grows into an elaborate system of self-deception, a habit of shifting responsibility onto circumstance, fate, or other people and adopting a victim stance, ignoring inconvenient facts that contradict one's own picture of the world, painful disappointments and shaken self-belief every time reality shatters the illusions, and dreams of a better life with no concrete steps taken, draining the energy needed for real achievement.

## Summary

Reality stops being frightening once it is accepted as a foundation for action, not a threat to hide from in daydreams.
