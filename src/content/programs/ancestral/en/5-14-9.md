## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about an outward calm that conceals a deeply rooted grievance — a family pattern in which inner balance and a thirst for revenge live side by side, until the person learns to let old wounds go instead of nursing them.

## Positive

Inner balance and the ability to stay rational under difficult circumstances, a talent for finding compromise without betraying one's own principles, sincerity and integrity in expressing one's views, persistence and focus in pursuing a goal, and emotional maturity paired with the ability to support others.

## Negative

Old grievances that ripen into a burning thirst for retribution, hidden toxicity masked as calm, sudden outbursts of anger over trivial matters followed by shame, suffocating control over loved ones disguised as care, and a futile struggle to change other people and prove oneself right.

## Summary

Inner peace becomes real only once grievances are released rather than suppressed — forgiveness frees more power than any retribution ever could.
