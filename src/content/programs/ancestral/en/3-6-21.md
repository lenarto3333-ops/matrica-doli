## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about unconditional love for people — an ancestral pattern where a sincere capacity to accept everyone without exception coexists with a lack of one's own boundaries, until the person learns to love others without forgetting themselves.

## Positive

A sincere capacity to love people without conditions or judgment, deep empathy and the ability to sense others' emotional states, genuine generosity and a readiness to help without expecting gratitude, the ability to forgive and let go of grudges, and a multifaceted personality that easily finds common ground in different circles.

## Negative

Excessive trustfulness and a lack of critical thinking, vulnerability to manipulation by others, emotional fragility and painful sensitivity to criticism, a tendency toward self-sacrifice at one's own expense, an absence of personal boundaries and an inability to say no, and chronic neglect of one's own needs.

## Summary

Love without limits becomes a strength only when it makes room for oneself too — boundaries don't diminish a generous heart, they protect it.
