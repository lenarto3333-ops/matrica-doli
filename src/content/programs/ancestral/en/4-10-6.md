## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the burden of decisions that determine others' fates — a family pattern in which sincere service to the collective and the temptation of authoritarian power live side by side, until the person learns to lead without oppressing.

## Positive

A sense of responsibility and reluctance to offload one's tasks onto others, natural organizational ability and a talent for building order out of chaos, a readiness to protect and support without humiliating, a drive for fairness even at personal cost, and genuine leadership grounded in natural authority rather than fear.

## Negative

Authoritarianism and imposing one's own opinion as the only correct one, stubbornly clinging to familiar methods that no longer work, total control and distrust of others' decisions even in trivial matters, suspicion and hunting for hidden motives where none exist, and dogmatism paired with an unwillingness to reconsider one's own beliefs.

## Summary

Leadership becomes service rather than a burden when power rests on trust instead of control.
