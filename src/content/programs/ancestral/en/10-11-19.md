## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about burning bright and the risk of burning out fast — a family pattern in which life wisdom and inexhaustible enthusiasm live alongside inner burnout and scattered focus, until the person learns to protect their own flame rather than burn themselves to nothing.

## Positive

Life wisdom and a clear, steady outlook on the world, a reliable foundation — stable work and a strong family, patience and the ability to focus methodically on a task, wide-ranging interests that make for a many-sided personality, and inexhaustible optimism paired with contagious enthusiasm.

## Negative

Constant inner tension that literally forces one to push through life, changeable passions that flare up brightly and burn out fast, sharp emotional swings from elation to emptiness, unfinished business and projects, and superficial knowledge across many fields without real mastery in any.

## Summary

Energy works for a person when it has a steady channel, rather than scattering itself in flare-ups that fade as quickly as they appeared.
