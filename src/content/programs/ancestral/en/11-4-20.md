## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about strength of spirit and the ability to walk one's own path — a family pattern in which unbending will and leadership live alongside stubbornness and a hunger for control, until the person learns to admit their mistakes while remaining strong.

## Positive

A rare determination and a gift for setting ambitious goals and pursuing them relentlessly, an iron will and control over one's emotions and actions, an unbreakable character capable of overcoming life's trials, freedom of choice and a readiness to stand by one's own opinion, an inspiring example of leadership within a team, and viewing difficulties as a challenge to one's own abilities.

## Negative

Stubbornness that crosses reasonable limits and prevents admitting mistakes even when clearly in the wrong, black-and-white thinking with no room for nuance, a hunger to control people and situations, workaholism at the expense of personal life and health, emotional guardedness that gets in the way of building relationships, and a tendency toward confrontation and provoking conflict.

## Summary

Willpower becomes a support rather than a barrier when it can bend where flexibility is needed, instead of only pushing.
