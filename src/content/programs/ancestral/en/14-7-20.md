## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about the drive for safety and stability — a family pattern in which the ability to build a secure harbor for oneself and loved ones lives alongside a fear of taking risks for new opportunities, until the person learns to combine peace with an openness to moving forward.

## Positive

The ability to create a safe, harmonious space where everyone feels protected, a home that serves as a refuge and the skill of balancing work and rest, reliability and a word that is always kept, wisdom gained through overcoming hardship that makes one a valued advisor, and care for loved ones and the ability to give them a sense of safety.

## Negative

A fear of risk that leads to turning down promising opportunities in favor of stagnation, gradual social isolation as one's circle narrows, resistance to change and clinging to outdated patterns of behavior even to one's own detriment, a halt in personal growth from fear of the unknown, and fleeing problems instead of solving them, which lets unresolved issues pile up.

## Summary

A quiet harbor becomes a true refuge when its walls don't prevent occasionally sailing out into open water.
