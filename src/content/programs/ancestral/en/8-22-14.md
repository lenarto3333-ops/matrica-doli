## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a complicated relationship with money — a family pattern in which generosity and sensible thrift live alongside a fear of depriving oneself of necessities, until the person learns to trust themselves and allow themselves to live fully rather than only accumulate.

## Positive

Generosity and readiness for a grand gesture without fear of loss, an understanding of sound budgeting, the ability to save without feeling deprived, emotional restraint and adaptability to circumstances, genuine compassion beneath a reserved exterior, and energetic determination even while outwardly calm.

## Negative

Excessive stinginess that denies necessities to oneself and loved ones, impulsive large expenditures that compensate for periods of harsh frugality, emotional instability with sharp swings from warmth to coldness, distorted priorities — skimping on essentials for the sake of status items, and a habit of constantly postponing real life for later.

## Summary

Money stops being a source of anxiety once thrift serves life instead of replacing it.
