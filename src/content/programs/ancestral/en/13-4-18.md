## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about one's relationship with power and responsibility — a family pattern in which the ability to work as an equal team member lives alongside a fear of taking on a leadership role, until the person learns to accept responsibility without apprehension.

## Positive

A genuine team spirit and the ability to work toward a shared goal without the urge to dominate, trust in others and masterful delegation of duties, a noble modesty free of excessive ambition, flexibility in decision-making and willingness to follow someone else's lead, and conserving energy for creativity and growth instead of a struggle for power.

## Negative

A paralyzing fear of responsibility that freezes the will before important decisions, systematically avoiding leadership positions and promotions, shifting responsibility onto others and looking for excuses, unconsciously sabotaging one's own career growth, and a habit of staying in the shadows and unnoticed even when every chance to shine is available.

## Summary

Strength lies not in avoiding power, but in accepting as much responsibility as one is truly ready for.
