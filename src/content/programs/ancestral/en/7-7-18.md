## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about movement and change in life — an ancestral pattern in which a pull toward new experience and an easy adaptability live alongside a fear of stepping toward change, until the person learns to trust themselves and act instead of waiting.

## Positive

A passion for travel and new impressions, career growth through decisiveness and initiative, an ease at changing professions and applying diverse talents, an ability to adapt quickly to unfamiliar situations and make new connections, and steadiness and reliability — emotional control and faithfulness to one's word.

## Negative

A fear of moving and traveling that limits personal growth, a reluctance to change jobs even for better prospects, passivity in relationships and waiting for a partner to simply appear, a fear of entrepreneurship driven by doubt and anxiety about risk, and indecision and timidity that lead to missed opportunities.

## Summary

Growth begins with one small step toward what was once avoided.
