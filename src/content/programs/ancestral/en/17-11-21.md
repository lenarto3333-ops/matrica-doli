## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about stability and the ability to be a guiding point for others — a family pattern in which reliability and selfless help live alongside the risk of the lighthouse's own deep loneliness, until the person learns to receive support as generously as they give it.

## Positive

An incredible stability and reliability that others can count on in any situation, a consistency of character and loyalty to one's own principles, the ability to be a clear point of reference for others in moments of crisis, an optimism that shines through even in the gloomiest circumstances, and selfless help given without expecting reward or recognition.

## Negative

A deep loneliness that comes with the role of observer and guide, a dependence on outside emotional or material support to sustain one's own effectiveness, a sense of self-worth entirely tied to usefulness and the ability to help others, being confined to the role of the one who only points the way without actively getting involved, and the risk of one's wisdom growing outdated in a changing world.

## Summary

A lighthouse shines brightest when it also allows itself, at times, to be the ship that needs help.
