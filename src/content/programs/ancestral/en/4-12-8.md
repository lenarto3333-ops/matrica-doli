## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about someone who once held power and influence but lost their standing — a family pattern in which the ability to let go of the past and a chronic grievance over lost greatness live side by side, until the person learns to turn the experience of falling into strength.

## Positive

A practical understanding of how power and systemic weaknesses work, the ability to trust a team and grow its potential through delegation, freedom from attachment to material status, the capacity to rise again and start over after a fall, and a gift for mentoring and passing on experience to those facing similar trials.

## Negative

Chronic resentment and treating loss as an injustice, sabotaging others' decisions and constant defiance, behind-the-scenes intrigue and manipulation instead of open interaction, compulsively reminding others of past achievements, and an obsession with revenge that poisons every area of life.

## Summary

True greatness lies not in a restored status but in the strength that comes from accepting loss.
