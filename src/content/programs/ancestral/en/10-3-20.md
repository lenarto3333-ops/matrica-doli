## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about trust between men and women within the family line — a family pattern in which sincere closeness and deep suspicion toward women live side by side, until the person learns to build relationships on trust rather than fear of betrayal.

## Positive

Trusting and respectful relationships with women, genuine love for children and a desire for a large family, deep respect for the mother and the maternal role, a responsible approach to family relationships, and emotional stability paired with strong physical health.

## Negative

Deep suspicion and distrust of women stemming from past betrayals, fear of financial manipulation by a partner, anxiety surrounding pregnancy and childbirth, strained relationships with a mother-in-law or other female relatives, and serious emotional disorders that require professional help.

## Summary

Trust is restored once the lineage's past betrayal is no longer projected onto present relationships.
