## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about a person's place within large collectives and systems — a family pattern in which a talent for teamwork lives alongside a persistent sense of not belonging, until the person learns to find their place without losing their own footing.

## Positive

A rare talent for working effectively within a large collective and a sincere desire to contribute to a shared cause, diplomatic flexibility and the ability to find compromises without abandoning one's principles, a sharp analysis of large volumes of information and a gift for spotting hidden patterns, the ability to see untapped potential in people, and a genuine willingness to quietly help the vulnerable without seeking recognition.

## Negative

A persistent sense of rejection by various structures and a chronic feeling of being an outsider in any environment, an inability to compromise and an excessive bluntness that drives away potential allies, a pull toward solitary work or even a solitary life, a feeling of being unnecessary within a team despite real professionalism, and family disapproval of chosen partners that creates constant tension in relationships.

## Summary

A true place within the system is found once a person stops proving their usefulness and starts acting from a sincere wish to be of service.
