## Essence

This is an inherited ancestral program about strength and power passed down as a legacy — an ancestral pattern in which decisiveness and a gift for leadership live alongside the temptation to dominate others, until the person learns to serve and protect instead of ruling.

## Positive

Decisiveness in difficult decisions, strategic thinking that anticipates situations ahead of time, deep professionalism and competence, a natural leadership gift for inspiring and guiding others, iron self-discipline, and a readiness to stand up in defense of the weak.

## Negative

An overweening pride that pushes away even the most patient loved ones, indifference to others' pain, a willingness to cross ethical lines for the sake of a goal, an unhealthy obsession with power that crowds out relationships, a refined manipulativeness, and a deliberate self-isolation that cuts off emotional ties to reality.

## Summary

The warrior mage's true strength is revealed not in subjugating others but in serving and protecting those who cannot stand up for themselves.
