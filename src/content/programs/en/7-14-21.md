## Essence

This program is about the combination of sensuality and restraint — a delicacy around sex that can become either charm or a barrier.

## Positive

A natural delicacy allows for a subtle attunement to a partner, and the power of suggestion and the unspoken turns seduction into an elegant game that deepens trust.

## Negative

An inherited inability to voice one's own desires, along with shame and guilt over expressions of sexuality, which blocks initiative and breeds dissatisfaction.

## Summary

Finding a voice for one's own desires turns modesty from a barrier into a refined trait of character.
