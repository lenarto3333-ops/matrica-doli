## Essence

This program is about finding balance between physical attraction and deep feelings — the line between passion and love is often blurred.

## Positive

The ability to combine the fire of passion with tenderness and emotional openness creates relationships of remarkable intensity and sincerity.

## Negative

An inherited confusion between sex and love — dependence on passion, devaluing feelings that lack fiery sex, and emotional whiplash.

## Summary

By learning to distinguish attraction from attachment, this person discovers both passion and true, deep love.
