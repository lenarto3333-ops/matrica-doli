## Essence

This program is about a striking variability in the intimate sphere — temperament shifts dramatically depending on circumstances or partner.

## Positive

The ability to adapt to a partner's needs, to keep renewing intimacy, and to avoid routine makes relationships lively and unpredictable in a good way.

## Negative

An inherited instability that leaves a partner never knowing what to expect — sharp swings from passion to coldness, and inner contradictions about one's own desires.

## Summary

Variability becomes a strength when it rests on self-knowledge rather than chaotic mood swings.
