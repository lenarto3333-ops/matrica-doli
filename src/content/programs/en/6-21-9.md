## Essence

This program is about a constant search for new sensations in sex — one that can either revitalize a relationship or destroy it.

## Positive

The ability to keep passion and novelty alive in a long relationship, turning intimacy into an ongoing adventure that keeps the partnership from becoming routine.

## Negative

An inherited, recurring pattern of betrayal — chronic dissatisfaction with the current relationship pushes this person toward affairs on the side, destroying trust.

## Summary

That same pull toward novelty, directed inward at the relationship itself, becomes a source of its constant renewal rather than betrayal.
