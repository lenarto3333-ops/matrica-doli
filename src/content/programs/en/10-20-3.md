## Essence

This program is about a rich intimate life with many partners and a pull toward new experience.

## Positive

Openness to the new, ease in making connections, and the ability to live in the moment allow this person to gather rich experience and shed their complexes.

## Negative

An inherited inability to sustain long relationships, and a dependence on an endless search for new impressions that never fills the inner emptiness.

## Summary

The freedom to explore becomes a true gift once it is paired with the ability to stop and build a deep connection.
