## Essence

This program is about perceiving sex as a way to heal emotional wounds and restore inner balance.

## Positive

A gift for releasing tension and soothing emotional pain through touch, creating a space of unconditional trust and deep emotional connection with a partner.

## Negative

An inherited dependency on intimacy as an escape from inner problems — a constant search for "rescue" in a new partner, fear of loneliness, and the dissolving of one's own boundaries.

## Summary

Intimacy heals for real only when it complements one's own inner work rather than replacing it.
