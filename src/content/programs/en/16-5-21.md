## Essence

This program is about experiencing sex as a sacred union of two souls, where fidelity is the highest value.

## Positive

The ability to build extraordinarily strong relationships with a single partner, deepening trust, understanding, and pleasure over the years.

## Negative

An inherited tendency toward jealous possessiveness and emotional dependence — or, conversely, a paralyzing fear of starting a relationship at all, born of a fear of betrayal.

## Summary

Fidelity becomes a gift rather than a prison when it leaves room for trust instead of the fear of loss.
