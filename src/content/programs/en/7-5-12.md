## Essence

This is a karmic program about powerful sexual energy and magnetism — a person who naturally draws attention and is able to give genuine pleasure.

## Positive

Charisma, confidence, mastery of seduction, and honesty of intention make it possible to build vivid relationships in which a partner feels desired and fulfilled.

## Negative

An inherited tendency toward sexual obsession — a constant chase for new sensations, emotional burnout, manipulation, and an inability to form deep attachment.

## Summary

This tendency is not a sentence but an energy that can be channeled into honest, deep partnership.
