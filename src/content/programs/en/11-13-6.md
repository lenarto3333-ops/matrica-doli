## Essence

This program is about the pull toward deep intimacy alongside an equally strong fear of being hurt — past experience has built powerful defensive walls.

## Positive

The ability to turn difficult experience into wisdom, gradually learning to trust and build honest, deep relationships in which intimacy becomes an act of mutual healing.

## Negative

An inherited fear of physical and emotional contact — impenetrable walls, and self-sabotage of relationships once closeness becomes too real.

## Summary

Trust is built step by step, and every small move toward another person is already a victory over the old fear.
