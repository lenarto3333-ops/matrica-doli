## Essence

This program is about the ability to dissolve into a partner's desires and find real pleasure in giving pleasure to another.

## Positive

A subtle sense of a partner's needs, unconditional trust, and openness to experimentation make this person an attentive, generous lover capable of creating an atmosphere of complete acceptance.

## Negative

An inherited inability to say no and defend one's own boundaries — the person forgets their own desires, attracts selfish partners, and gradually loses the capacity to feel pleasure themselves.

## Summary

Caring for a partner remains a gift only when there is also room, alongside it, for one's own desires.
