## Essence

This program is about primal, physical passion that recognizes no conventions.

## Positive

Powerful sexual energy, complete uninhibitedness, and a subtle sense for a partner's desires make intimacy vivid, spontaneous, and passionate.

## Negative

An inherited tendency to lose control over one's instincts — excessive aggression, disregard for a partner's boundaries, and an inability to form emotional closeness.

## Summary

Tamed by care, this wild energy becomes a source of strength rather than a threat to the relationship.
