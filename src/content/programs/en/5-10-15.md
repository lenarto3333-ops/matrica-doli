## Essence

This program is about an inner contradiction between modesty and passion — the person carries both poles within at once.

## Positive

The ability to play with contrasts — tenderness and boldness, restraint and hunger — makes intimacy intriguing and rich, and accepting both sides of one's nature grants inner freedom.

## Negative

An inherited conflict between moral convictions and suppressed desires breeds guilt, a double life, or the complete suppression of sexuality.

## Summary

Accepting both sides of one's nature without judgment turns the inner war into a source of depth and freedom.
