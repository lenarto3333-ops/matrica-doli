## Essence

This program traces a path from youthful idealization of sex to a mature understanding of relationships.

## Positive

The ability to learn from disappointment, to accept a partner as a whole person — strengths and flaws alike — and to build relationships where romance is balanced with realism.

## Negative

An inherited tendency to repeatedly idealize new partners only to face painful disappointment, accumulating grievances and emotional immaturity in intimacy.

## Summary

Each disappointment, consciously worked through, brings a person closer to relationships built on genuine acceptance rather than illusion.
