## Essence

This program is about a sharp surge in sexual desire during moments of life upheaval.

## Positive

The ability to turn stress into powerful desire, using intimacy as a resource for overcoming hardship and strengthening the relationship in difficult times.

## Negative

An inherited dependence on acute situations to keep libido alive — losing desire during calm periods, and unconsciously stirring up conflict just to reignite passion.

## Summary

By learning to find passion in calm as well, this person breaks free from dependence on crisis.
