## Essence

This program is about a repeating cycle of "conquest — cooling off," where the thrill of the chase matters more than intimacy itself.

## Positive

A natural gift for seduction and the ability to sustain intrigue and passion make relationships vivid and emotionally rich.

## Negative

An inherited loss of interest the moment the goal is reached, an addiction to the adrenaline of the chase, and an inability to form deep attachment.

## Summary

Directed into a long-term relationship, the hunter's thrill can become a source of ever-renewing passion rather than an escape from it.
