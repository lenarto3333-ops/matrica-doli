## Essence

This program is about the deep imprint of past experience that accompanies a person into every new intimate relationship.

## Positive

The ability to turn what was lived through into wisdom — a clear understanding of one's own needs and boundaries, and the skill of avoiding old mistakes to build mature, conscious intimacy.

## Negative

An inherited habit of comparing a new partner to the past, a fear of reliving old pain, and the projection of old grievances onto the present relationship, which blocks genuine trust.

## Summary

By letting go of the past, this person turns painful experience into the foundation of truly warm intimacy.
