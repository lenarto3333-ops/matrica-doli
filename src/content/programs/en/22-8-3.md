## Essence

This program is about experiencing sex as an endless field for experimentation and new discoveries.

## Positive

A creative approach to intimacy, openness to the new, and a rich imagination turn this person's intimate life into a kaleidoscope of vivid impressions that sustain passion for years.

## Negative

An inherited dependence on constant novelty — pressure on a partner, superficiality in relationships, and an inability to enjoy simple, plain intimacy.

## Summary

Variety enriches a relationship when it does not crowd out emotional warmth, but complements it.
