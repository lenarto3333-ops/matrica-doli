## Essence

This program is about waiting for the one who will awaken a hidden passion — after a long period of being closed off, this person is capable of opening up like a flower.

## Positive

A true awakening of sensuality with the right partner, deep gratitude and devotion, and a fresh perception of every touch.

## Negative

An inherited tendency toward passive, endless waiting for "the one," emotional inaccessibility, and suppressed desires disguised as romantic patience.

## Summary

Awakening comes sooner when met with a small step forward, rather than simply waiting for a miracle.
