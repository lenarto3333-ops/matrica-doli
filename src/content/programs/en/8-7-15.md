## Essence

This program is about perceiving sex as a spiritual sacrament — a way of uniting souls, not merely a physical act.

## Positive

The ability to turn intimacy into a deep emotional and spiritual merging with a partner, creating a special atmosphere of trust where physical pleasure is inseparable from warmth of the soul.

## Negative

Over-complicating intimacy into an elaborate ritual with countless conditions, devaluing "plain" sex that lacks "spiritual meaning" — which breeds tension, shame, and suppressed desire.

## Summary

A balance between sacredness and naturalness preserves the depth of the bond without losing lightness and pleasure.
