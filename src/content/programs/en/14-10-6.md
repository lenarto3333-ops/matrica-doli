## Essence

This program is about a drive for perfection in the art of love — intimacy as a refined craft.

## Positive

A virtuoso command of technique, charisma, and an intuitive understanding of a partner make intimacy an unforgettable experience, full of attention to detail.

## Negative

An inherited obsession with the "tally" of conquests and technical flawlessness at the expense of emotional closeness, leaving relationships shallow.

## Summary

Skill directed at genuine connection rather than self-affirmation turns this program into a true gift for a partner.
