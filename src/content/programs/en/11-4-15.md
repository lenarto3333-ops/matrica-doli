## Essence

This program is about the drive for control in intimate relationships — a person unwilling to be led, who sets the rules of the game themselves.

## Positive

Confidence and leadership create a space of trust where a partner can let go of inhibitions and discover new facets of pleasure, and passion is paired with responsibility for shared enjoyment.

## Negative

An inherited tendency to suppress a partner's will — dominance hardens into rigidity, jealousy, and psychological pressure that erodes trust.

## Summary

Strength directed toward care rather than subjugation turns this energy into a source of genuine passion, not power for its own sake.
