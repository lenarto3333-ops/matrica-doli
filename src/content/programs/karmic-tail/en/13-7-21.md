## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about brutal acts that led to the tragic deaths of innocent people — perhaps the role of an executioner, a prison guard, or a ruthless soldier — and now the soul seeks atonement through serving life instead of destroying it.

## Positive

A genuine calling to save human lives in professions such as surgeon, midwife, or suicide-prevention counselor, the ability to create things beneficial to health or write books that help many people, a readiness for large-scale charity work and continuous spiritual growth, a talent for helping others grow, and an understanding that the present role is only temporary.

## Negative

A pull toward ordinary stability despite being ill-suited to routine work, a sense that life doesn't come easily, the risk of destructive consequences and premature losses if the path of atonement is abandoned, and excessive parental over-protectiveness that keeps a child from finding their own way.

## Summary

By choosing service and the saving of others' lives over escaping into the ordinary, a person gradually frees themselves from a heavy karmic debt.
