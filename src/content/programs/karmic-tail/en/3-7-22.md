## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a lack of freedom — imprisonment, dependence on others or on circumstances, the "gilded cage" of excessive care, or immobility following a serious injury. In the present incarnation, the soul once again encounters constraints — family traditions, debts, discipline, or health conditions — in order to learn inner freedom where outer freedom is unavailable.

## Positive

The ability to preserve inner freedom and spiritual steadiness even in constrained circumstances, the ability to accept physical or life limitations without bitterness, independence of thought and freedom from others' pressure, a readiness to support those who are genuinely in trouble, and openness to new things — travel, projects, changes — that keep one from fixating on a single situation.

## Negative

A tendency to avoid close relationships out of fear of losing freedom, recurring constraints — financial debts, legal cases, strict control at work, caring for someone, or illness — a sense of being cornered despite outwardly decent conditions, distrust of people and a determination to act alone, and the risk of genuine unfreedom brought on by one's own carelessness.

## Summary

Freedom begins from within — by accepting what cannot be changed and opening your heart to people, you break this cycle of constraint.
