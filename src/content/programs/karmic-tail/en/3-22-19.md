## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a tragic loss connected to children — perhaps a mother who lost a child and could not forgive herself, or a child who felt that its parents failed to protect it — and this mark still affects one's relationship with motherhood and children today.

## Positive

Generosity, optimism, and kindness toward loved ones and oneself, respect for a woman's right to make her own decisions about motherhood, a willingness not to postpone having children, and the ability to find fulfillment in child-related professions — teacher, psychologist, or children's party planner.

## Negative

Deep resentment toward one's mother or a constant need to earn her approval, difficult decisions about pregnancy that often reflect unresolved maternal traumas, an anxious sense that life could end abruptly, which breeds a rush to get everything done, and excessive over-protectiveness of one's own children or an excessive preoccupation with childhood themes.

## Summary

By forgiving the past and allowing themselves and their loved ones the freedom to choose, a person frees the maternal line of their family from old pain.
