## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about the eternal conflict between genuine love and bodily passion — the soul once chose physical attraction over spiritual closeness, through a marriage of convenience, infidelity, or the pressure of tradition, and instead of harmony received only fleeting pleasure and emotional pain. In the present incarnation, the same dilemma returns once again.

## Positive

Heightened sexual potential and vivid sensuality, the ability to deeply get to know a partner before trusting a relationship, the potential to unite spiritual closeness and physical attraction into a harmonious bond, the ability to learn from past mistakes and turn them into wisdom, and a readiness to build relationships on trust, openness, and fidelity.

## Negative

Difficulty distinguishing fleeting passion from true love, a tendency toward numerous partners without deep emotional attachment, an attraction to unconventional behavior and various addictions, and a sense of being lost and lonely due to an inability to find a truly close person.

## Summary

Harmony comes when physical attraction and spiritual closeness go hand in hand — and if needed, there is no shame in seeking professional support.
