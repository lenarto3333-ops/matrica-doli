## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a soul that could not cope with its trials due to a lack of inner strength and optimism — without family support or the ability to process its feelings, it concluded that death was the only way out.

## Positive

The ability to value one's own health and take care of oneself, the ability to see multiple ways of solving any problem instead of feeling trapped, gratitude for life's simple moments, and a drive to fill everyday life with vivid experiences instead of monotony.

## Negative

A weakened instinct for self-preservation, an attraction to risky behavior and dangerous situations, difficulty overcoming hardship and a tendency to avoid problems rather than solve them, indifference to the value of life, and an inability to build anything lasting.

## Summary

By learning to value every moment and to seek a way out where once there seemed to be none, a person regains their taste for life.
