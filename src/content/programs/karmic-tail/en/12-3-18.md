## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about unbearable physical pain that drained the soul — the person blamed others for their suffering and eventually began to identify themselves solely with their torment, losing touch with their own essence. In this life it shows up as a distorted relationship with the body and pain, along with anxiety over the health of loved ones.

## Positive

The ability to accept the body with its limitations as a foundation for helping others, the capacity to view pain as the body's useful signal rather than a threat, a readiness for volunteer care of vulnerable people, a balance between self-care and service to others, and deep compassion for the physical and emotional suffering of others.

## Negative

A distorted relationship with one's own body — ranging from excessive over-care to complete neglect of nutrition and rest, a panicked avoidance of any pain, physical or emotional, extending even to a fear of blood, injections, or minor injuries, intense anxiety over the health of children or elderly parents, and a tendency to shift responsibility for one's own suffering onto others.

## Summary

Accepting the body and pain as part of life rather than an enemy is the path that transforms an old trauma into a source of care for oneself and others.
