## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about the inability to trust oneself — the person avoided important decisions and shifted responsibility onto others, losing their own individuality and consciously renouncing leadership and the unfolding of their potential.

## Positive

The ability to build self-trust and recognize one's own potential, the capacity to trust other people and the world at large, willingness to allow oneself to be happy despite past disappointments, the maturity to take responsibility for one's decisions and their consequences, and the ability to see change as an opportunity for growth.

## Negative

Fears that prevent self-expression and decision-making, indecisiveness and avoidance of responsibility through a constant search for others' approval, freezing up under the pressure of difficult situations, and dependence on the support of a reliable partner, without which obstacles seem insurmountable.

## Summary

By learning to trust their own judgment and take responsibility for themselves, a person reclaims the power they once gave away to others.
