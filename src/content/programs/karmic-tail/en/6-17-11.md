## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a talent set aside for the sake of stability rather than expression — the fear of losing prosperity, family pressure, or a single failure that undermined self-belief. In the present incarnation, similar abilities again lie undeveloped, because material security seems more important than the risk of following one's calling.

## Positive

The presence of real innate abilities and potential, the capacity for deep self-reflection and self-knowledge, prudence in weighing risk against stability, the ability to learn from past mistakes, and a readiness to experiment and try new things once fear finally releases its grip.

## Negative

An inner conflict between ambition and fear of failure, prioritizing material security over the expression of one's own gift, being distracted by temptations — a new position, relationships — that lead away from one's true purpose, the risk of projecting unfulfilled dreams onto one's children instead of pursuing one's own development, and an upbringing that once failed to properly support the talent.

## Summary

Stepping out of the comfort zone and giving oneself permission for self-fulfillment is never too late — and this is where true happiness lies.
