## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about self-sacrifice — the soul was a living bridge between worlds, experienced difficult romantic and family conflicts, but acted with deep devotion and a sense of duty. In the present incarnation, despite favorable conditions and the support of loved ones, the person finds it hard to make use of the opportunities available to them.

## Positive

The capacity for deep self-devotion and compassion, a keen sense of duty that drives sincere, sometimes heroic acts, inner spiritual conviction and willpower, the ability to draw support and care from those around them, and the potential to turn loved ones' feelings of guilt into a genuine, mutual bond.

## Negative

An inability to openly speak about one's own feelings and needs, subconsciously using illness as a way to draw the family's attention and care, loved ones' habit of indulging this weakness and relieving the person of responsibility for their own life, and a fear of appearing weak that prevents them from asking for real support.

## Summary

By learning to speak directly about your needs and take responsibility for your own life, you turn the care of those around you into a genuine support rather than a crutch.
