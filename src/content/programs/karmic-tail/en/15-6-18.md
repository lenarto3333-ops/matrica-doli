## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about magical knowledge turned toward evil — the soul was born with special abilities but used them in dark rituals, causing suffering to others, and lived without principle, indulging its own desires. In this life it shows up as pronounced self-centeredness and disregard for other people's boundaries.

## Positive

The ability to respect other people's personal boundaries, well-developed skills of healthy communication, a drive toward balance in all areas of life, the ability to help without imposing, and a deep interest in studying karmic laws and one's own path.

## Negative

Pronounced self-centeredness and narcissistic traits, acting exclusively in one's own interests even at the cost of others' suffering, an attraction to dark esoteric practices, manipulative relationships built on personal gain, and a fixation on appearance along with a craving for luxury.

## Summary

Power once abused serves for good once a person learns to respect others' boundaries and direct their gifts toward support rather than control.
