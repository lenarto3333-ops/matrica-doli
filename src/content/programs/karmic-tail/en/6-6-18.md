## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a deep but unhappy love — the soul knew the full force of feeling, yet it either went unreturned, or the lovers were separated by circumstances, social condemnation, or a loveless forced marriage. In this life it shows up as emotional coldness stemming from childhood and difficulty expressing feelings sincerely.

## Positive

The ability to acknowledge and accept one's own feelings and relationship problems, developed self-acceptance and the ability to love oneself, a readiness to seek professional support in working through trauma, a drive toward personal growth through learning and new knowledge, and openness to new connections along with faith in positive change.

## Negative

A deficit of parental warmth in childhood that shapes the later life script, conflicts with relatives filled with mutual resentment, serious difficulty in sincerely expressing love toward a partner, and attempts to fill an inner void by searching for a "rescuing" partner.

## Summary

What breaks this cycle is not the search for a perfect partner, but working through childhood trauma and unconditional self-acceptance — that is when love becomes mutual.
