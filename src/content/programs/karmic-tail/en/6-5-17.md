## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about extraordinary beauty and charisma that the soul used for its own benefit, becoming arrogant and indifferent to others' feelings. In the present incarnation, similar trials repeat themselves through attractiveness or talent that tempt one to feel superior to those around them.

## Positive

Natural attractiveness and strong charisma, the ability to easily draw people and attention, the potential to achieve what one wants without excessive effort, the possibility of directing this force toward harmonious cooperation rather than rivalry, and the ability to sense the true value of humility and sincerity.

## Negative

A tendency toward arrogance and contempt for ordinary people, manipulating loved ones and holding inflated expectations without real merit, undermining trust through constant pressure on the family, the risk of a sudden loss of status that forces a confrontation with real hardship, and prejudice regarding others' appearance, beliefs, or social standing.

## Summary

True strength lies in humility and respect for others, and genuine help to those around you brings more than any outward advantage.
