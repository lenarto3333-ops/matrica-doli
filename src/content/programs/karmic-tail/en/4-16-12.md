## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about the mishandling of power — the soul either abused its own control over others, or, conversely, was weak and avoided responsibility, or suffered under someone else's tyranny. In this life it echoes as a distorted relationship with the father and a lack of understanding of how to properly wield power and care within the family.

## Positive

The ability to recognize the limits of one's own power and responsibility, a readiness to accept and forgive one's father and one's own past, a deep understanding of the male and female roles within the family, unconditional love for children without attempts to control them, and the ability to resolve conflicts constructively instead of pressuring or fleeing.

## Negative

A distorted or absent connection with the father, a tendency toward either excessive control over loved ones or irresponsible passivity, a repetition of the family's pattern of tyranny or one's own victim status, a distorted understanding of roles in relationships, and difficulty building a warm, equal family.

## Summary

Power becomes a blessing only when it is accompanied by responsibility and love — by learning this, a person breaks the cycle of tyranny or weakness and builds a family on trust rather than control.
