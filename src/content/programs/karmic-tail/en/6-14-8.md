## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about immense power and influence — the soul was a leader whom crowds followed, whose word determined the fate of cities or nations. In the present incarnation that scale of power is gone, and the person often fails to recognize their own potential, hiding insecurity behind a mask of outward confidence.

## Positive

Natural charisma and the ability to lead others, an innate sense of one's own significance and mission, the ability to make decisions that affect others, the potential to become an authoritative leader who is trusted without coercion, and strength of character that shows even in moments of doubt.

## Negative

Underrating one's own achievements despite real, visible success, a fragile confidence that shatters at the slightest criticism, a tendency to take on other people's emotions and problems, the risk of manipulating people instead of exerting honest influence, and an inner sense of being a victim of circumstance despite significant potential.

## Summary

It is worth recognizing one's own uniqueness and worth without comparison to others — then genuine confidence will replace the mask, and true influence will follow on its own.
