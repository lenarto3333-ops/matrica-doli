## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about the abuse of power — the soul was an overseer, an enslaver, or a domestic tyrant who found pleasure in humiliating others and restricting their freedom. In the present incarnation the person gets a chance to reconsider this power, often choosing professions in law enforcement, financial oversight, or management.

## Positive

A natural ability to manage and control processes, a strong character and the ability to keep a situation under control, the potential to assert oneself through an honest rather than forceful stance, the ability to strengthen family bonds through openness and gratitude, and sincere love for loved ones that endures even alongside a difficult temperament.

## Negative

A tendency to assert oneself at others' expense, a habit of building a network of people indebted to you, the risk of experiencing the flip side of this program — becoming the victim of injustice instead of the overseer — and despotism within family relationships that provokes conflict despite sincere feelings.

## Summary

Honest relationships free of manipulation and selfless help to those in need release a person from the old karmic debt of power.
