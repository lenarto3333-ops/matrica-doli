## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about physical strength used for evil — the soul possessed considerable power but turned it toward the harsh subjugation of others, whether as a commander who treated subordinates severely, or as a person who used aggression for personal gain while masking deep inner pain. In this life it shows up as impulsive aggression directed at those who are weaker.

## Positive

The ability to acknowledge the problem and consciously control emotional impulses, the skill of tracking personal triggers through self-observation, a drive toward emotional balance through reflection and meditative practices, a healthy lifestyle and physical activity as an outlet for tension, well-developed communication skills, and a willingness to seek professional help.

## Negative

Physical aggression directed mainly at the weak and defenseless, the consequences of a turbulent youth — health problems and emotional disorders, controlling behavior and domestic violence in relationships, and spiritual stagnation caused by difficult life circumstances.

## Summary

Strength tamed by awareness and self-control stops destroying and starts protecting — this is the direction in which the work on this tail lies.
