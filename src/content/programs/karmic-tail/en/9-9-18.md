## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about the deliberate use of hidden knowledge to harm others — the person studied esoteric practices and applied them for revenge, jealousy, or personal gain, fully aware of the cost of this choice, which left behind a heavy trace of loneliness and distrust.

## Positive

The ability to trust one's own intuition and sense hidden processes with subtlety, depth of knowledge and a natural drive to seek out like-minded people, self-confidence when there is an opportunity to openly share experience and expertise, and an attraction to pursuits that bring genuine satisfaction and allow authentic self-expression.

## Negative

Deep distrust of people, rooted in childhood lessons about caution, a habit of relying only on one's own strength and avoiding requests for help, fear of the unknown and of change, unstable self-esteem that swings between superiority and humiliation, and a tendency to withdraw into oneself and suspect others of insincerity.

## Summary

When knowledge and intuition once again serve people rather than work against them, the old burden lifts — and trust in the world gradually returns.
