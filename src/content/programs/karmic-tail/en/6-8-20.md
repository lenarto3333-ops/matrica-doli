## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a break with one's family line — betraying the family's cause, choosing love over duty to the family, or a serious wrongdoing for which relatives disowned the person. In the present incarnation this fracture echoes as resentment toward loved ones, despite a longing to earn their recognition.

## Positive

The ability to remain loyal to family and honor the legacy of one's ancestors, the potential to find one's own true calling despite the pressure of family expectations, wisdom and diligence, provided they do not turn into excessive self-sacrifice, and the ability to love and accept children unconditionally regardless of circumstances.

## Negative

Resentment toward relatives despite a longing for their recognition and approval, professional obstacles — such as having one's achievements credited to others — difficulty achieving personal goals, and the risk of children being born with serious health problems as an echo of the old break with the family line.

## Summary

By restoring a genuine connection with their family line and accepting their own children and themselves unconditionally, a person closes this old fracture for good.
