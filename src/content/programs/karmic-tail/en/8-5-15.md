## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about betrayal and the destruction of family bonds — the soul either did not value marital fidelity and broke its promises easily, or was enslaved by addiction or uncontrollable anger that turned the home into a battlefield. In this life it echoes as strict moral tests centered on honesty and faithfulness.

## Positive

Honesty and the ability to keep one's word, a readiness to renegotiate agreements when circumstances change rather than deceive, the ability to forgive others' betrayals while holding onto one's own principles, compassion born from pain once experienced, and realistic, illusion-free expectations of relationships.

## Negative

A tendency to experience the very pain once inflicted on others, exposing situations that follow any dishonesty or betrayal, recurring family traumas — violence, addiction, or infidelity — experienced as a victim or witness, and the temptation to seek easy ways out through deception.

## Summary

Faithfulness to oneself and to one's word is the central lesson of this tail: by upholding it, a person stops the cycle of betrayal and pain within their own family.
