## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about rebellion against family — the soul refused to acknowledge any rules, considered them "shackles on a free person," quarreled with relatives over its way of life, and severed all ties with them completely. In this life it shows up as estrangement from family and a pull toward destructive forms of freedom.

## Positive

The ability to rebuild family relationships after a rupture, a readiness to adopt a constructive rather than destructive outlook on the world, respect for family traditions, the ability to expand one's circle of contacts beyond negative influences, and the courage to seek professional support in overcoming addictions.

## Negative

A dysfunctional family environment or estrangement from parents, criticism and rejection from relatives, disconnection from one's own roots, self-destructive patterns such as addictions or gambling, and an attraction to fringe groups as a substitute for the family that was lost.

## Summary

True freedom is built not on severing ties with family, but on the ability to return to it on new, healthy terms.
