## Essence

This is a karmic tail from a past life about a fearless "warrior of faith" who was sincerely convinced of their own rightness and imposed their worldview on others, sometimes through aggressive means, believing this to be their duty to the world.

## Positive

Sincere conviction and strength of faith in what one does, a readiness to build a flexible dialogue with others once one becomes aware of one's own stubbornness, the ability to channel idealistic energy into projects that benefit society, and a genuine desire to help people, even if the form that help takes needs adjusting.

## Negative

An ego-centric worldview that often disregards others' thoughts, needs, and wishes, resistance to any alternative viewpoints, a narrowed circle of contact limited to like-minded people, rejection of constructive criticism, and a tendency to impose one's point of view on others, even when this comes not from malice but from a sincere desire to help.

## Summary

Recognizing every person's right to their own path turns the strength of conviction from a burden into a genuine foundation for good deeds.
