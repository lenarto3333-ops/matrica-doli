# 3. The Empress — Financial Blocks

## In Brief

A typical financial block for this energy is self-sacrifice: investing resources in caring for others while forgetting to name a fair price for one's own work. Another common block is anxiously accumulating “for a rainy day” far beyond any real need. This brings no peace and only strengthens attachment to material possessions as the sole source of security.

## Natural Strength

Behind this block lies a valuable quality—deep generosity and a natural gift for creating value that people can physically experience. The problem is not generosity itself, but the way it sometimes becomes a refusal of one's own financial reward. When valued fairly, the ability to create tangible, high-quality results is a powerful financial tool of this energy.

## Positive Expression

In the positive, this energy transforms natural generosity into fairly valued work: the person creates comfort, care, or a quality product while confidently naming a price that reflects the effort invested, without embarrassment or self-deprecation.

## Negative Expression

In the negative, the block appears as chronically working “out of love” while feeling uncomfortable asking for decent compensation, or accumulating resources out of anxiety rather than as part of a genuine financial strategy. Another expression is giving loved ones excessive financial support at the expense of one's own stability.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

A common trigger is a situation in which invested care or labor goes unnoticed or unpaid, reinforcing the belief that asking for money is somehow “unseemly.” Another is a past experience of sudden financial instability that fuels anxious accumulation.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is helpful to review one's pricing regularly, measuring it against the true value of the care and labor invested rather than false modesty. A second step is to practice setting aside part of one's income specifically for personal enjoyment, not only for other people's needs or a “rainy day.”

## Main Task

The main task is to transform natural generosity into a fairly valued resource rather than a cause of self-sacrifice or anxious accumulation.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I name a fair price for my work, or do I diminish it out of false modesty?
- How much is my accumulation of resources guided by a genuine strategy rather than anxiety?
- Are there situations in my life where generosity harms my own financial stability?

## Practical Plan

1. Review the price of your service or work and compare it with the true value of the care you invest.
2. Set aside part of your next income exclusively for your own enjoyment.
3. Identify one situation in which excessive financial generosity harms you and consciously set a boundary there.
