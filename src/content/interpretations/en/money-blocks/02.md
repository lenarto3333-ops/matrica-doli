# 2. The High Priestess — Financial Blocks

## In Brief

A typical financial block for this energy is concealing one's true financial situation even from loved ones, making it harder to receive timely support or advice. Another common block is passivity in stating one's own financial worth: the person avoids asking for fair compensation or voicing their needs, hoping someone will notice without being told.

## Natural Strength

Behind this block lies a valuable quality—a deep intuition for financial risks and opportunities. The problem is not caution itself, but the way it sometimes grows into complete secrecy. Used judiciously, this ability to sense the “right” financial move is a valuable tool of this energy.

## Positive Expression

In the positive, this energy transforms intuition into an ability to make balanced financial decisions and share them with trustworthy people. A person in the positive can speak openly about financial needs and difficulties, receiving support in time rather than only after the situation has become critical.

## Negative Expression

In the negative, the block appears as chronically keeping financial difficulties quiet “until things get really bad,” which makes timely help more difficult. Another expression is stagnation caused by excessive caution, when an intuitive sense of risk is treated as a final reason not to act even when checking the facts would be worthwhile.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

A common trigger is an experience in which openly discussing finances led to judgment or disappointment. Another is growing up in an environment where money was not discussed openly, even within the family.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is helpful to identify at least one person with whom financial matters can be discussed openly and then gradually practice that openness. A second step is to test intuitive feelings about financial risk against the facts instead of automatically treating them as a final reason not to act.

## Main Task

The main task is to turn financial intuition into a tool for balanced choices rather than a reason for complete secrecy or stagnation.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I share my true financial situation with at least one person I trust?
- How well can I ask for fair compensation or state my financial needs directly?
- Do I check my intuitive sense of financial risk against the facts before turning down an opportunity?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one person with whom you can begin discussing financial matters openly.
2. Once this month, directly ask for fair compensation or the financial support you need.
3. Check one intuitive feeling about financial risk against the facts instead of automatically declining.
