# 15. The Devil — Ancestral Tasks, Patterns, and Resources

## Brief Essence

The ancestral script of this energy is symbolically connected to the experience of power, wealth and dependence in the family history: the family may have had people with significant material wealth, influence or power over other family members, and along with this - a history of addictions (alcohol, gambling, unhealthy attachment to a person or thing), forced control of one relative over others, or destructive jealousy that split family relationships. These stories were rarely spoken openly, and that is why they are passed on not as a learned lesson, but as a hidden desire for power and wealth mixed with an equally hidden fear of dependence.

The ancestral task of this energy is to learn to take from the family the hidden gift of magnetism, influence and material success, without inheriting with it either the ancestral tendency to dependence and control over others, or the opposite habit of suppressing one's own desires for fear of repeating the fate of the ancestors.

## Natural Strength

The ancestral resource of this energy is a symbolic legacy of people of influence and wealth: the family could have entrepreneurs, leaders, charismatic figures who knew how to attract resources, lead people and build material well-being even in difficult times. This ability, even if rarely spoken of or condemned, has left descendants with a profound gift for seeing possibilities where others see only obstacles.

The second resource is the ancestral understanding of human nature: if there were people in the family who knew how to read the motives and desires of others - negotiators, healers, those to whom they came for advice on delicate matters - this insight becomes a legacy that the descendant can use honestly, instead of repeating the manipulative patterns, if they too were in the family history.

The third force is the ancestral experience of overcoming addiction: if someone in the family has managed to break free from the grip of a bad habit, destructive relationship or someone else's control, this experience of liberation, even if it has been kept silent, remains a powerful resource of resilience for descendants who face similar trials.

## Expression in the Positive

When the ancestral karma is processed, the descendant is able to consciously take the ancestral gift of influence and magnetism and use it honestly: to build wealth and authority with his own work and understanding of people, without repeating the ancestral tendency to control relatives or to accumulate wealth at the expense of someone else's weakness.

In the positive, one becomes the one who breaks the ancestral cycle of addiction—to substances, gambling, destructive relationships, or obsessions with power and control—showing children and younger relatives that power and wealth can coexist with freedom, not enslavement.

Ancestral resources of insight and leadership are turned into a conscious gift of mentoring: a person helps relatives see their own value and potential, shares the experience of how ancestors, despite the temptations and addictions of their time, still found the strength to preserve dignity and connection with the family.

Another positive expression is the ability to talk about family histories of power, addiction, and control without silencing or shame: acknowledging that there were people in the family who succumbed to temptation or enslaved others themselves, honoring the complexity of that experience, and at the same time consciously choosing a different, freer path for themselves.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

A woman's ancestral resource manifests itself as the ability to consciously use one's own attractiveness and influence in the family and outside it, based on the example of women of the family who knew how to build prosperity or lead people, without losing their own dignity and subjugating their loved ones.

She is able to openly talk with her daughters and younger relatives about ancestral stories of addictions, power or jealousy, turning these stories not into a source of shame, but into a warning and at the same time an inspiration - an example of how one can emerge from such a legacy free and strong.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, the ancestral resource in the positive is manifested as the ability to carry ancestral power and authority honestly, relying on the example of ancestors who knew how to build influence and wealth, without enslaving loved ones for this. He takes on their power, consciously rejecting their tendency to control or depend on them, if any.

He takes on the role of the person in the family who helps younger relatives to recognize early signs of dependence or an unhealthy desire for power over others, drawing on inherited, if often silent, ancestral wisdom about the true price of freedom.

## Expression in the Negative

When ancestral karma is not worked through, a descendant may unconsciously repeat the ancestral script of addiction: falling under the power of a bad habit, substance, gambling, or destructive relationship in the same way as an ancestor, without even knowing for sure about this part of the ancestral history.

Another manifestation of the negative is a hereditary tendency to control over loved ones: if someone in the family imperiously subordinated other family members to him - financially, emotionally or literally - the descendant may unconsciously repeat this pattern, either by becoming such a controlling relative himself, or by attracting people who behave in the same way into his life.

In the negative, a family ban on the manifestation of one's own desires is also possible: if the ancestors had to hide ambitions, wealth or power for fear of condemnation or envy, the subconscious instruction "it's better not to show what you really want" could be fixed in the family, and the descendant may feel vague guilt for his own success or attractiveness.

Another sign of the negative is family jealousy and rivalry for resources or attention, which has been silenced to such an extent that subsequent generations feel indescribable tension around the topic of money, inheritance, or family attention without understanding where this ancient rivalry comes from.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is situations related to money, inheritance or family influence, which unconsciously activate the family memory of ancient conflicts over power and resources in the history of the family. The second trigger is contact with ancestral themes that have traditionally been silenced: ancestral dependencies, stories of control by one relative over others, hidden rivalries for attention or inheritance. The third trigger is the moment when one's own success or attractiveness causes an unconscious feeling of guilt or fear of repeating the fate of an ancestor who was ultimately defeated by power or temptation.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

The first step is to learn as much as possible about the family's history of power, wealth, and dependence: who had influence or wealth, who was prone to bad habits, and how it affected the family. The second step is to consciously separate one's attitudes about success, attractiveness, and control from ancestral scripts: to recognize which fears about one's own power are not really personal, but inherited.

The third step is to allow oneself to openly seek success and recognition where one's lineage once knew only shame or silence, while learning to keep integrity and respect for others as a central guide. The fourth step is, if possible and willing, to carefully talk with relatives about the silenced topics of ancestral dependencies or control, creating space for greater understanding between generations.

The fifth step is to consciously create new family examples of a healthy attitude to power and wealth: moments when the success of one family member becomes a reason for the joy of others, and not for envy or rivalry.

## Main Task

The main ancestral task of this energy is to interrupt the cycle of ancestral dependencies and hidden rivalry for power or resources and to pass on to future generations not the doom of repeating other people's enslavement, but a healthy ability to attract wealth and influence while remaining free and honest. It means respectfully accepting the complexity of the experience of ancestors, whose temptations and addictions were often dictated by the circumstances of their time, and at the same time consciously choosing for themselves a different, freer trajectory of power without enslavement.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What do I know about the history of power, wealth or dependence in my family?
- Are there themes of control, jealousy, or resource rivalry in my family that are usually kept quiet?
- How much of my guilt for my own success or attractiveness belongs to me personally, and not inherited from my family?

## Practical Plan

1. Ask an older relative about a history of influence, wealth, or dependence in the family that has not previously been openly discussed.
2. Write down one subconscious guideline for power, success, or desire that you may have inherited and question whether it is still relevant to you personally.
3. Identify one family situation in which a pattern of control or dependence repeats itself, and make a plan to gradually get out of it.
4. To create or support one family tradition that celebrates the success of each family member without envy and rivalry — a joint holiday, a ritual of recognition of achievements.
5. If there is an opportunity and readiness, carefully talk with a relative about the topic of long-standing addiction or control, which was previously kept silent in the family.
6. Write a short letter (it is not necessary to send it) to one of the ancestors with thanks for the transmitted power and magnetism and recognition of the enslavements that you want to heal in this generation.
