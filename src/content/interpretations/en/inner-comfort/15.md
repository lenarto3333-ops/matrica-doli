# 15. The Devil — Point of Inner Comfort

## Understanding the Point of Inner Comfort

The point of mental comfort shows a state in which it is easier for a person to recover, make decisions and feel that he lives in harmony with himself. It's not a requirement to stay in one mood all the time, and it's not an assessment of character. Rather, it is a hint about the conditions that support internal balance and about signals that the usual way of life has begun to exhaust.

For the fifteenth energy, comfort is connected with the right to feel one's own attractiveness and not be ashamed of it: to be noticed, desired, influential — and at the same time remain the master of one's own resources and desires, and not their hostage. It is important for a person to have a space where he can see the essence of things and people deeper than the polite facade - to understand what others really want, what lies behind the words, where the advantage or weakness is hidden - and use this knowledge honestly, not as a weapon. This is not a need to control everything around you, but a deeper trust in your own magnetism: in the fact that you can attract money, attention, opportunities and people by your very nature, without fuss and apologies for the power of your own desire. When this right is recognized, there is a rare ability to see through and at the same time remain free; when magnetism is shamed or desire harshly condemned, an inner disorder appears, which often hides behind a mask of indifference or excessive self-control.

## What Fills You with Energy (15)

The most fulfilling feeling is that one's own magnetism is recognized and not tried to be extinguished. When appearance, charisma, energy make an impression on others and this is recognized as a natural force, and not as something suspicious or "too much", the tension subsides by itself.

The resource makes it possible to enjoy - taste, touch, beauty, comfort, material wealth - without the constant feeling of guilt for pleasure. The fifteenth energy comes to life when it is allowed to want openly and get what it wants, without making excuses for its appetite for life.

Fills the ability to see through people: to recognize true motives, hidden weaknesses, unspoken desires - and use this understanding to help, negotiate, influence honestly, not to manipulate or keep someone on the hook. When this insight is valued as a gift and not as a threat, a sense of one's own necessity appears.

A resource is an accumulation — money, influence, connections, things that have real weight and value. It is important for the fifteenth energy to see the result of its own attractiveness and grasp in something tangible, and not just in verbal recognition.

It is important to have the right to want badly and not to hide it: to recognize for yourself and for others that desire - for money, intimacy, status, pleasure - is not a shame, but is part of a living, full-blooded nature. When there are people around who are not afraid of someone else's passion, there is a space for honesty.

Honesty in power relations is also fulfilling: the ability to use charm and influence for real contact, not for covert game — and at the same time the right to acknowledge one's own shadow side without pretending it doesn't exist.

## Resourceful State of the Energy

In a resource, a person naturally attracts people, opportunities and resources to themselves - not because they require attention, but because they feel inner strength and clarity of desire. She is able to see what others really want and uses this understanding to build sincere contact, to negotiate honestly or to help, rather than to subtly control other people's decisions.

A rare ability to enjoy the material and sensual side of life appears without excess and without shame: delicious food, pleasant things, money, bodily pleasure are perceived as a natural part of a full life, and not as a weakness that should be worried about. The resourceful fifteenth energy knows how to want and get, while remaining the master of the situation, and not its prisoner.

In such a state, true self-control appears: a person knows what exactly controls him - habits, urges, attachments - and consciously decides how much to give in to them. She does not need to control others to feel safe, because she is confident in her own attractiveness and is not afraid to lose it.

## Signs of Accumulated Tension

The first sign is often a craving that doesn't subside no matter how much it is satisfied—a constant feeling of "I still don't have enough" directed toward food, shopping, attention, work, or relationships. This is not a "weak will", but a sign that some deeper need remains unsatisfied and is replaced by something that can be obtained quickly.

The second sign is jealousy and the need to control a person or a situation as if it were property: constant checking, suspicion, fear of losing what is considered "own". This often masks a deeper fear—that, unchecked, one's own attractiveness will not be enough to hold someone's attention.

Another signal is the use of charm as a tool of manipulation: a person notices that he is not building a relationship for the sake of sincere contact, but to gain benefit, to control other people's decisions or feelings, and that this begins to happen almost automatically, even without a clear intention.

Accumulated tension also manifests as a feeling of “captivity”—when a habit, substance, relationship, or behavior pattern controls a person more than they control them, and attempts to stop bring anxiety or guilt instead of relief. If it lasts a long time, is accompanied by a feeling of loss of control over one's own life or an addiction that harms health or relationships, it is worth contacting an appropriate specialist.

## To Restore Your Strength

1. Identify one habit or desire that has recently controlled you more than you control it, and honestly name it to yourself without self-judgment.
2. Allow yourself one small, conscious pleasure today—guilt-free and without excess.
3. Ask yourself: do I really want this, or am I trying to fill a void that is about something else entirely?
4. Notice if you are trying to control a person or situation out of fear of losing them instead of trusting your own attraction.
5. Notice if you're using charm or influence to get what you want instead of talking honestly about your own desires.
6. Allow yourself to acknowledge your own strength and attractiveness out loud, at least to yourself, without belittling.
7. Practice the pause between desire and action—a short delay that allows you to choose consciously rather than automatically.
8. Talk to someone who knows how to listen without judgment when you feel like a habit or person is "holding" you tighter than you'd like.
9. Remind yourself that acknowledging your own shadow—greed, jealousy, need to control—is not the same as surrendering to it.
10. If you feel that some kind of addiction or obsessive pattern is ruling your life for a long time and it is exhausting, consider talking to a psychologist who will help you regain inner self-control consciously.

## Summary: A Gradual Return to Balance

The balance of the fifteenth energy is not restored when a person tries to suppress all his desires and is ashamed of his own magnetism, and not when he allows drives and habits to rule his every step indiscriminately. It returns when attraction once again becomes a conscious force, not a tool of control or a mask for emptiness, and when pleasure and wealth come as a natural result of honest, deep engagement with the world, not as a way to hold onto someone or possess yourself.

You don't have to choose between the power of desire and freedom from it. Mature comfort unites them: the right to want and enjoy remains valuable, and the ability to see when desire begins to control you frees you from invisible captivity. Return to balance in small steps—one honestly named desire, one pause before automatic action, and one trust that true magnetism needs no control to stay strong.
