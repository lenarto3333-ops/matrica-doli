# 13. Death — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the thirteenth energy pushes the financial flow to a new level through knowing how to end the old at the right time and start fresh from zero; at its worst, this is exactly where a fear of change hides, keeping them stuck in a "swamp" and blocking movement toward new money.

## Positive

+ **Project-based work.** Work with a clear beginning and end, rather than endless routine.
+ **Freelancing and contracts.** Income tied to specific tasks rather than a permanent position.
+ **A sense of direction in crisis.** The ability to stay grounded when everything around them is falling apart.
+ **Turning crisis into gain.** Knows how to turn a difficult situation into a new earning opportunity.
+ **Applying creativity.** Unconventional solutions where others follow the beaten path.
+ **A fresh start from zero.** Willing to start over once the previous path has run its course.
+ **Reviving projects.** A talent for bringing back to life what others have already written off.
+ **A love of big money.** Sees large sums as opportunities rather than a threat.
+ **Decisiveness and courage.** Ready to act fast when the right moment arrives.
+ **Joy in change.** New prospects and novelty spark inspiration rather than anxiety.

## Negative

- **Unfinished business.** Abandons projects halfway, never carrying them through to a result.
- **Work as a swamp.** A place they should have left long ago, but can't find the strength to move.
- **Fear of decisive action.** Freezes before a step that could change everything.
- **Rejecting innovation.** Clings to old methods even once they've stopped working.
- **Fixation on their own opinion.** Unwilling to hear other options and approaches.
- **Fear of change.** Anxiety before any new stage, even a beneficial one.
- **Uninteresting but "profitable" work.** Stays where the pay is, even though they've long wanted to leave.
- **"Money is evil."** A belief that pushes away large sums out of fear.
- **Fear of starting their own business.** A dream that stays just a dream for years.
- **Fear of taking risks.** Avoids any step that doesn't come with a hundred percent guarantee.
- **A lack of warmth toward people.** Handles matters decisively and firmly, but without genuine warmth for those around them — and without it, people won't trust their money to someone, even someone who genuinely holds power over the situation.

## Recommendations

- Don't chase quick income — transformation needs time to take root.
- Keep learning new things, and invest money in learning, not only in saving.
- Plan your time and finances in advance, so that change doesn't turn into chaos.
