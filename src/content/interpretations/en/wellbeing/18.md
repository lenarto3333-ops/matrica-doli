# 18. The Moon — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through trust in intuition and the gentle, almost magical ease with which plans come together. At its worst, that same sensitivity turns into a fear of taking action, and unclear processes turn into debt, deception, and dependence on someone else's will.

## Positive

+ **Productive night work.** The ability to work effectively and generate ideas at night, when others are resting.
+ **The power of spoken plans.** Plans come together faster and more easily once they're said out loud.
+ **Ease of execution.** Projects and plans come together with almost no resistance, as if falling into place on their own.
+ **Magic in creative work.** Unusual, almost magical solutions appear precisely in creative work.
+ **Flexibility of perspective.** Able to look at a situation from different angles rather than just one.
+ **Breadth of vision.** Able to take in a situation as a whole, not just one piece of it.
+ **Openness to money.** An inner readiness to receive money without inner resistance or guilt.
+ **Fears as a resource.** Knows how to turn their own anxieties into fuel for action rather than a paralyzing block.
+ **Enjoyment in thinking about money.** A light, pleasant relationship with the subject of finances, without tension.
+ **Trust in intuition.** The ability to make decisions based on an inner sense, not only on facts.

## Negative

- **Fear of taking the first step.** A paralyzing fear of starting to bring a project to life.
- **Harm from gossip.** Slander and gossip end up destroying a shared venture.
- **Fraud and deception.** A heightened risk of being deceived in financial matters.
- **Using other people's ideas.** Appropriating or using others' knowledge and work instead of developing their own.
- **Loans and debt.** Financial obligations that push plans off indefinitely.
- **Postponed plans.** Constantly putting off important steps for "later."
- **Pressure from management.** Restrictions from superiors or colleagues that get in the way of acting independently.
- **Fear of big money.** An inner fear of having and holding onto large sums.
- **Envy of the wealthy.** Comparing themselves to people who earn more instead of focusing on their own path.
- **Fear of starting their own business.** Unwillingness or fear of starting a business despite having the potential for it.
- **Negative thinking.** Anxious images and looping fears wear down productivity and breed a fear of doing something wrong.

## Recommendations

- Live in harmony with your own natural cycles of activity and rest, without constantly forcing yourself.
- Consciously manage your thoughts and see things as they really are, rather than through a haze of worry.
- Make a vision board, spend more time around people, and keep a gratitude journal to sustain an easy flow of money.
