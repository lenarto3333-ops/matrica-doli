# 6. The Lovers — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, this energy opens up an easy flow of money through work they love and genuine partnerships. At its worst, disappointment in people and undervaluing their own talent cut that flow off.

## Positive

+ **Partnership.** Easily builds a venture with someone else or a team, where each person's strengths complement the other's.
+ **Matchmaking.** Naturally connects people, ideas, and resources — and earns from doing it.
+ **Building connections.** Creates a network of contacts that eventually turns into a source of opportunities.
+ **Personal charm.** Charisma and an appealing way of presenting things make any offer more persuasive.
+ **Love for their work.** Chooses work by calling rather than calculation alone, which strengthens the outcome.
+ **An easy flow of money.** Income arrives naturally when the work brings genuine enjoyment.
+ **Belief in their own venture.** A conviction that the chosen line of work really does bring good income.
+ **Trust in people.** Openness to partnership without constantly expecting a catch.
+ **Self-love.** Values their own time and work, so won't settle for less than they deserve.
+ **A sense of self-worth.** Inwardly allows themselves good income, without underselling their own value.

## Negative

- **Unable to share responsibilities.** Takes on too much, or the opposite — dumps the whole venture on a partner.
- **Relationships for gain.** Builds personal connections purely as a career tool, which undermines sincerity and trust.
- **Avoiding decisions.** Passes responsibility for an important financial choice onto someone else.
- **Showing off.** Focuses on looking significant rather than actually working toward the result.
- **Taking on too much.** Promises more than can realistically be delivered, and doesn't manage to follow through.
- **Not loving their own work.** Does work that brings no enjoyment, which blocks the natural flow of income.
- **Undervaluing their own hobby or talent.** The belief that a beloved pursuit is "not serious" and will never turn a profit.
- **Disappointment in people.** After a bad experience, closes off from partnership and trust.
- **Fear of betrayal.** Worry that a partner or client will let them down gets in the way of building long-term relationships with money and people.
- **Emotionally driven decisions.** Makes financial decisions under the sway of momentary feelings rather than a clear head.
- **Living in dreams instead of acting.** Knows how to earn and is capable of it, but stalls waiting for inspiration, living in fantasies of future success instead of taking concrete steps.

## Recommendations

- Do the work you love — it's the shortest path to opening an easy flow of money.
- Delegate routine tasks instead of trying to do everything alone.
- Make a choice and don't beat yourself up over its consequences — decisiveness strengthens trust in your own financial judgment.
