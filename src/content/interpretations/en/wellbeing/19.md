# 19. The Sun — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through openness and scale — recognition, authority, and influence over people draw income in on their own. At its worst, that same light dims under the weight of greed and control, and money stops flowing freely.

## Positive

+ **Fame and prosperity.** Public recognition goes hand in hand with financial wellbeing.
+ **A drive for excellence.** High standards of quality in everything they take on.
+ **A broad scale.** Aims not for a narrow niche but for big, large-scale ventures.
+ **Respect and authority.** Their opinion is taken into account and listened to.
+ **A high standing.** A visible, influential place in society or their professional circle.
+ **Influence over the masses.** The ability to sway the opinions and decisions of large numbers of people.
+ **Recognition and awards.** Regularly receives awards and public approval for their work.
+ **Generosity and philanthropy.** Willing to share resources with others without losing anything for it.
+ **Money from multiple sources.** Income comes in through more than one channel at once.
+ **Money that multiplies.** A steady sense that their finances tend to grow rather than shrink.
+ **Money at work.** Understands that capital should be working and generating extra income, not sitting idle.

## Negative

- **Indecisiveness.** Struggles to make decisions even on important financial matters.
- **Unable to set goals.** A hazy sense of what they actually want to achieve.
- **Unable to define tasks.** Struggles to turn a goal into concrete, doable steps.
- **Hiding their abilities.** A habit of concealing their real talents and potential from others.
- **A scarcity mindset.** Focused only on basic material needs, without a wider view.
- **Work with no higher purpose.** Earning money purely for its own sake, with no meaning or inspiration behind it.
- **Greed.** Excessive hoarding that blocks the natural movement of money.
- **A stalled flow of money.** Fear of spending or sharing that cuts off new money coming in.
- **"I control everything."** Excessive control over finances and people that crowds out trust.
- **Pressuring others.** Trying to get their way through pressure instead of open interaction.
- **Fear of a large-scale project.** Drawn to easy, small gains instead of setting a big goal and taking on a project of real scale.

## Recommendations

- Give to charity and share resources — this opens the flow of money rather than closing it off.
- Delegate part of your workload to others, and don't be afraid to spend money on growth and quality of life.
- Widen your circle and openly showcase your achievements instead of hiding them.
