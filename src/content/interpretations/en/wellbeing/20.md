# 20. Judgement — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through work they've been drawn to since childhood — a calling, acceptance of family lineage, and gratitude for what's already there. At its worst, that same energy keeps them stuck in work "beneath their level," in broken promises, and in an unspoken conflict with their parents.

## Positive

+ **Work by calling.** Work that resonates with their inner nature rather than being chosen out of calculation.
+ **Searching for their life's work.** A persistent, and eventually successful, search for that one direction that's truly theirs.
+ **A pull since childhood.** Fulfillment in the field they were drawn to from an early age.
+ **The ability to create on a large scale.** Capable of creating projects that go beyond the everyday.
+ **Serving a greater cause.** Work that benefits their family line, community, or country.
+ **Honoring their homeland.** Work that brings honor to their native land and the people around them.
+ **High dedication.** Willing to commit fully to a project rather than halfway.
+ **Accepting the tasks of their lineage.** Accepts what's been inherited from their family and knows how to make use of it.
+ **Gratitude for what they have.** The ability to be glad for what's already there, not only focus on what's missing.
+ **Peace with their family.** Accepts and appreciates their family as it is.

## Negative

- **Work beneath their level.** A job that doesn't match the person's real potential and abilities.
- **Working against their own family.** Activity that runs counter to the interests of their family or homeland.
- **Broken promises.** Systematically failing to keep agreements at work.
- **Office gossip.** Spreading rumors and talking behind people's backs instead of having a direct conversation.
- **Judging colleagues.** A tendency to judge the people around them instead of seeking to understand.
- **Judging their parents.** Inwardly judging their own parents or family for their decisions.
- **A low, depressed state.** A tendency toward depressive states that drain the energy needed for work.
- **Unable to enjoy money.** No sense of enjoyment even once the money is actually there.
- **"Money only comes through hard labor."** An inherited family belief that limits easier paths to income.
- **A break with their family.** A lost or unspoken connection to family history and support.

## Recommendations

- Host family gatherings and support relatives financially — this strengthens the family's resources.
- Work together with family members where possible, and consciously accept your family as it is.
- Work through inherited parental beliefs like "money only comes through hard labor," to open up easier paths to income.
