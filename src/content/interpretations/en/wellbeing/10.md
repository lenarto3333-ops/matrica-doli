# 10. Wheel of Fortune — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the tenth energy opens a financial flow easily and effortlessly, through trust in fortunate circumstances; at its worst, this is exactly where waiting for a miracle without taking action settles in, along with the belief that money only comes through hard labor.

## Positive

+ **Luck in business.** Money arrives more easily than expected, as if fate is lending a hand.
+ **Work found by calling.** The right work finds them, even when they weren't specifically looking for it.
+ **Unexpected windfalls.** Wins and bonuses show up where others are counting only on the plan.
+ **Seasonal work.** Income tied to periods of activity rather than a rigid schedule.
+ **Being in the right place.** A knack for showing up exactly where and when it matters.
+ **Fortunate coincidences.** Unexpected encounters and events that open up new opportunities.
+ **Ease in earning.** The ability to enjoy their work rather than endure it.
+ **Gratitude.** Genuine appreciation for any amount that comes in, large or small.
+ **Ease of movement through life.** Moving without strain, relaxed, without a constant fight over every step.
+ **A sense of abundance.** A steady feeling that there's always enough money for what's needed.

## Negative

- **A rigid schedule.** Being locked into a strict timetable smothers this energy's natural ease.
- **Laziness about work.** Puts things off when inspiration or "the right moment" is missing.
- **Failed projects.** Decisions made on a whim, without real grounding in the facts.
- **Rejecting chance.** Distrust of unexpected opportunities, fear of straying from the plan.
- **Settling for one place.** Gets stuck in a job that's long since run its course, out of fear of change.
- **Looking for easy paths without effort.** Waits for a miracle instead of taking real steps toward good fortune.
- **Chronic dissatisfaction.** A constant sense that something is missing, no matter how much there is.
- **A lack of gratitude.** Takes money and opportunities for granted, without appreciation.
- **"Money only comes through hard labor."** A belief that rules out easy, enjoyable earning.
- **Grievances against the world.** Makes demands of life and people instead of actively taking part in their own good fortune.

## Recommendations

- Trust the flow of life in money matters instead of trying to control every step.
- Be grateful for any amount that comes in — gratitude opens the channel for more.
- Choose company that strengthens your luck, rather than people who pressure you with demands and rigid schedules.
