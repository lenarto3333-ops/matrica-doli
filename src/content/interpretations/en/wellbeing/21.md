# 21. The World — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through scale — reaching an international audience, working across different cultures, and taking on large projects. At its worst, that same space narrows down to tight limits, limiting beliefs, and a fear of stepping outside the familiar.

## Positive

+ **Peacemaking.** The ability to bring different people together and smooth over conflict instead of escalating it.
+ **Working with foreign companies.** Successful collaboration with international partners and markets.
+ **Working online.** Ease in building a business specifically in the online space.
+ **Working with a large audience.** Comfortable communicating with and working for a mass audience.
+ **Knowledge of foreign languages.** Language skills that open up additional financial opportunities.
+ **A broad, well-rounded perspective.** Able to see a situation from several cultural and conceptual angles at once.
+ **Quick to learn.** Picks up new knowledge and skills quickly at any age.
+ **International recognition.** Success and respect that reach beyond a single country.
+ **Global projects.** Involved in large-scale work that affects many people.
+ **Self-belief.** An inner confidence that doesn't depend on outside limits or borders.
+ **Growing income.** A steady sense that earnings increase with every new step.

## Negative

- **Low qualification.** A lack of the skills needed to move to a new level of income.
- **A lack of education.** Noticeable gaps in knowledge that slow down growth.
- **Limiting beliefs.** Inner assumptions that narrow their sense of what's possible.
- **Rigid thinking.** Keeps doing things the usual way even once it's stopped working.
- **A narrow target audience.** Artificially narrows the range of people they could work with.
- **Limiting their own potential.** Consciously or unconsciously downplaying their own potential.
- **"I limit myself."** Constant self-limitation in places where there's actually room to grow.
- **"You can't earn that much."** A belief in an income ceiling that can't be broken through.
- **"You can't earn honestly."** A belief that big money is only possible through dishonest means.
- **Prone to loans and debt.** A habit of closing financial gaps with borrowing instead of systematically growing income.
- **Closed off in communication.** Unwilling to share ideas and inspire others, which narrows the range of opportunities that could open up a new scale.

## Recommendations

- Avoid loans and debt, and instead set big, ambitious financial goals for yourself.
- Travel to different countries and consciously broaden your cultural and professional horizons.
- Stay actively engaged online — this is exactly where this energy expresses itself most fully.
