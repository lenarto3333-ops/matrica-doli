# 7. The Chariot — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, this energy turns momentum, drive, and delegation into confident, growing income. At its worst, a lack of direction and total control over everything brake the very momentum that should be generating money.

## Positive

+ **Skillful delegation.** Knows how to delegate so the team moves fast and without hitches.
+ **High standing and influence.** Naturally rises to positions where important decisions get made.
+ **Unshakeable authority.** A reputation trusted even in a competitive environment.
+ **A competitive spirit.** The thrill of chasing a result drives them forward and lifts income.
+ **Charisma.** Draws in people and opportunities through sheer momentum.
+ **Readiness to move.** Doesn't cling to one place if an opportunity calls for relocation or mobility.
+ **Capacity for calculated risk.** Takes big bets when they see real potential.
+ **Trust and delegation.** Confidently hands off part of the responsibility to others instead of trying to control everything personally.
+ **Teamwork.** Easily achieves results together with others, not alone.
+ **A conviction of abundance.** Confidence that there's enough money in the world for everyone removes the inner competition for "the last piece."
+ **An easy flow of money.** A genuine sense that money can come without superhuman effort, as long as there's movement toward a goal.

## Negative

- **No clear goal.** Moves energetically but without direction, and effort gets scattered.
- **Refusing to work as a team.** Tries to do everything alone, which slows their own pace.
- **Passivity and dishonesty at work.** Loses the motivation to keep moving and starts cutting corners instead of delivering a real result.
- **Attachment to one place.** Gets stuck somewhere that's long since run out of room to grow.
- **No strategy.** Acts reactively, without a plan, and loses part of their potential income.
- **Spreading across too many roles.** Takes on everything at once, and quality — along with reputation — drops.
- **Deliberately dragging out deadlines.** Slows down deliveries, undermining clients' or partners' trust.
- **The belief "nothing will get decided without me."** Never lets themselves rest or trust the team.
- **Total control over everything.** Wears themselves out trying to oversee every single process.
- **No sense of life purpose.** Without a clear direction, the energy of movement turns into chaotic bustle.
- **The belief that "easy money doesn't exist."** Subconsciously makes the path to income harder than it needs to be.

## Recommendations

- Set clear, specific goals — this energy of movement needs a clear direction in order to turn into income.
- Inspire people and gather a team around a shared goal, rather than trying to control everything alone.
- Find the right approach for each person instead of pushing and pressuring — this speeds up results more than force does.
