# 12. The Hanged Man — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the twelfth energy strengthens other income sources through service and selfless work that benefits others; at its worst, this is exactly where a victim mindset lives, along with an inability to take fair pay for their own work.

## Positive

+ **Patience and persistence.** The ability to see work through to the end without giving up halfway.
+ **Work as self-fulfillment.** Work that provides a sense of meaning, not just a paycheck.
+ **Good through good for others.** Income that arrives naturally when they bring benefit to people.
+ **Selflessness.** Material success isn't the top priority — and that's exactly why it comes.
+ **Innovation and creativity.** An unconventional outlook that opens up new sources of income.
+ **Valuing other people's work.** Genuine respect for what other people do.
+ **Willing to pay others for their work.** Pays for other people's work without haggling or devaluing it.
+ **Master of their own life.** A sense that they are the one making decisions about their money and time.
+ **Clear personal boundaries.** Clear rules about how much they work and under what conditions.
+ **Able to say "no."** Can decline without feeling guilty.

## Negative

- **Judgment and criticism.** A habit of judging others (and themselves) instead of looking for a solution.
- **Burdensome obligations.** A pile of promises that drain more than they give.
- **Working for low pay.** Agrees to amounts that don't match the effort put in.
- **Blaming circumstances.** Explains away failures with outside factors instead of looking at their own steps.
- **People-pleasing.** Tries to be liked instead of honestly standing up for their position.
- **Sacrificing themselves for their profession.** Works themselves to the bone for someone else's recognition.
- **Devaluing other people's work.** Unwilling to pay others fairly.
- **Not loving themselves.** An inner belief that they don't deserve good treatment or good income.
- **Unable to say no.** Agrees to everything asked of them, even at their own expense.
- **Unable to accept payment for their work.** Feels shame or guilt about being paid.
- **A victim mindset.** A sense that life and money happen to them, rather than with their participation.

## Recommendations

- Keep a balance between giving and receiving — service without mutual exchange wears you out.
- Allow yourself to spend money on yourself, not just on others.
- Set personal boundaries and take fair pay for your work — that's not greed, it's fairness.
