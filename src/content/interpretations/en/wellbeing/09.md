# 9. The Hermit — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the ninth energy lifts other money channels through depth of knowledge and a willingness to share wisdom with the world; at its worst, this is exactly where the main block hides — the belief that a large income isn't needed, and that their own ideas are of no interest to anyone.

## Positive

+ **Depth of knowledge.** A thorough, not superficial, approach to studying the subject that brings income.
+ **Intellectual work.** Money comes through mental effort rather than physical labor.
+ **Remote work.** The ability to work productively alone, without an office or constant oversight.
+ **Private practice.** Knows how to run a business that doesn't need publicity or visibility.
+ **Students and an audience.** A gift for gathering people around them who need exactly the knowledge they have.
+ **Responsibility.** Willing to stand behind the quality of what they say and do.
+ **Sharing knowledge through writing.** Income through writing, teaching, consulting, mentoring.
+ **Trust in the world.** Open to people and circumstances instead of constant wariness.
+ **Self-confidence.** A firm grounding in their own opinion, without needing to constantly prove they're right.
+ **Support for their ideas.** A conviction that the world is ready to accept the value they have to offer.
+ **Accepting others.** The ability to recognize the value of other people's experience, not just their own.

## Negative

- **Delays in work.** Projects and tasks that keep getting pushed to "later."
- **Wasted time.** Hours go into deliberation instead of real action.
- **The appearance of being busy.** Creates the look of work without any tangible result.
- **Discomfort in a group.** Teamwork drains more than it gives.
- **Chronic lateness.** A lack of punctuality that undermines partners' and clients' trust.
- **Fear of looking foolish.** Stays silent when speaking up would have been worthwhile.
- **"I don't need much."** A mindset that denies their very right to abundance.
- **Asceticism.** A deliberate rejection of the material as a supposedly "higher path."
- **"I'm smart, everyone else is foolish."** An arrogance that isolates them from people and opportunities.
- **Withdrawal and self-doubt.** Afraid to step out of their "burrow" and face the world.
- **"Nobody needs my ideas."** A belief that devalues their own worth before they've even tried to show it.

## Recommendations

- Study the spiritual laws of money, not only practical earning schemes.
- Share your knowledge and wisdom with those who truly need it, instead of keeping it to yourself.
- Grow within a circle of like-minded people — trusting others opens access to opportunities that are hard to see alone.
