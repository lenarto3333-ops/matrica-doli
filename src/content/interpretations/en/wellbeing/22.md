# 22. The Fool — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through freedom and joy — when work brings enjoyment and room for creativity, income flows easily. At its worst, that same lightness turns into naivety about money and an exhausting routine with no reward.

## Positive

+ **Working with children.** A natural ease and inspiration in work connected to children.
+ **A creative approach.** Solves problems in fresh, lively ways instead of relying on templates.
+ **Joy and inspiration from their work.** Work that generates energy on its own, rather than draining it.
+ **Freedom of action.** Able to act freely and easily in any circumstances.
+ **Resourcefulness and imagination.** A rich imagination and quickness in finding unconventional solutions.
+ **Activity and movement.** Constant energy and readiness to take on something new.
+ **A light attitude toward money.** Able to earn and spend without excess tension or worry.
+ **Money for growth.** Sees money as a resource for growth, not an end in itself.
+ **Responsibility for their own standard of living.** Understands that quality of life depends on their own decisions.
+ **An easy flow of income.** A sense that money comes naturally, without excessive effort.

## Negative

- **Naivety in financial matters.** A trusting nature that leaves them open to rash decisions and being deceived.
- **Monotonous work.** Routine activity with no variety or novelty.
- **No creativity in their work.** Unable to bring imagination and unconventional thinking to their everyday tasks.
- **A rigid schedule.** Strict, inflexible constraints that smother this energy's natural need for freedom.
- **Work with no reward.** Effort put in that doesn't convert into fair financial return.
- **Undervalued work.** A sense that what they put in goes unnoticed and unappreciated.
- **"It's hard for me to earn money."** An inner belief that money is difficult and hard to reach.
- **Working at something they don't love.** Earning through work that brings no enjoyment at all.
- **An irresponsible attitude toward finances.** Careless handling of money without considering the consequences.
- **No flexible schedule.** Locked into a rigid routine that goes against this energy's very nature.

## Recommendations

- Choose a flexible schedule and work with enjoyment wherever possible.
- Take a light attitude toward money — allow yourself to spend on things that bring pleasure, without turning it into guilt.
- Don't be afraid of adventure and risk, and build at least one source of passive income.
