# 16. The Tower — the energy of financial wellbeing

This energy lifts money to a new level through the destruction of the old — a crisis that had to be rebuilt from often becomes the most powerful financial starting point. At its worst, that same force turns into a string of setbacks and dependence on other people's opinions and status.

## Positive

+ **Through destruction to the top.** The ability to turn crisis and collapse into a springboard to a new level of income.
+ **Unbreakable resolve.** An inner resilience that won't let them give up when everything has to be rebuilt from scratch.
+ **Starting from zero.** Knows how to build a business literally from nothing, with no starting capital or support.
+ **Systematic growth.** Methodical business development where every step leads to the next peak.
+ **Willing to start over.** No fear of tearing down the old once it's stopped working, in order to build differently.
+ **Money for the growth of the soul.** Sees money as a resource for spiritual growth, not an end in itself.
+ **Freedom from the material.** No rigid attachment to things or status — which frees them from the fear of losing them.
+ **A sense of self-worth.** An inner right to prosper that doesn't depend on outside circumstances.
+ **Constant growth.** A conscious forward movement in spiritual and personal terms, which pulls financial growth along with it.
+ **Hard-won experience.** Every crisis survived becomes a proven skill at surviving and rising again.

## Negative

- **Professional setbacks.** A string of failures at work or in business that undermine financial stability.
- **Bankruptcy.** The risk of losing everything saved through a sudden business or project collapse.
- **Frequent job changes.** Constant layoffs or forced moves from one place to another.
- **Betrayal by a partner.** Losing money or a business through the dishonesty of someone they were in business with.
- **Work under compulsion.** Working out of "have to" rather than choice — almost involuntary labor.
- **Vulnerability to pyramid schemes.** Heightened susceptibility to fraudulent schemes and promises of easy money.
- **"Everyone owes me."** A belief that others are obligated to compensate them for hardship endured.
- **Pressuring people.** Trying to get money or gain through pressure and manipulation instead of agreement.
- **"Without money I'm nobody."** Self-worth entirely dependent on financial status.
- **Attachment to the material.** Excessive dependence on things and status, which turns any loss into a catastrophe.
- **Clinging to a perfect outcome.** Fixated on one specific final result instead of valuing the journey itself and the growth it brings.

## Recommendations

- Use spiritual practices and a change of scenery (travel, outdoor sports) as a way to reset your financial mindset after a crisis.
- Don't be afraid to start a new project or change fields if the old path has run its course.
- Stay resilient through hard times — it's resilience, not avoiding difficulty, that opens the way to the next financial level.
