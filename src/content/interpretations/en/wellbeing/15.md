# 15. The Devil — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the fifteenth energy strengthens every other money channel through magnetism, connections, and the ability to earn quickly; at its worst, this is exactly where the main block hides — the temptation to manipulate, and the belief that without money they're nobody.

## Positive

+ **Fast career growth.** Rapid leaps where others take years.
+ **Easy money.** The ability to earn without excessive effort, using a natural gift.
+ **A read on people.** A precise sense of others' motives and weaknesses.
+ **Useful connections.** A network that opens doors to new opportunities.
+ **Influence and authority.** A natural ability to sway other people's decisions.
+ **Crafting an attractive image.** Knows how to present themselves and their business so people believe in it.
+ **Money for spiritual growth.** Sees wealth as a tool for growth, not an end in itself.
+ **Freedom from attachment to the material.** Enjoys wealth without being dependent on it.
+ **A right to prosper.** An inner acceptance that they deserve material comfort.
+ **Caring for people.** Compassion and support as part of their relationship with money and power.

## Negative

- **Betrayal by partners.** Trust that gets used against them in shared ventures.
- **Criminal schemes and deception.** The temptation to earn through dishonest, risky means.
- **Manipulating people for gain.** Relationships built on benefit rather than sincerity.
- **Shady lines of work.** Earning in fields that go against their own values.
- **Manipulation and blackmail.** Pressuring others instead of reaching an honest agreement.
- **Aggression toward people.** Harshness and pressure as a way to get what they want.
- **Grievances and dissatisfaction with people.** A constant sense that others are doing something wrong.
- **"Everyone owes me."** A belief that people are obligated to compensate them for something.
- **"Without money I'm nobody."** Self-worth entirely tied to their bank balance.
- **"Only wealth will make me happy."** Happiness postponed until some unreachable sum.
- **Wanting to be liked by everyone.** An unconscious urge to atone for others' faults and please everyone drains their inspiration, and no one pays for being "nice."

## Recommendations

- Learn to part with money easily — funds spent on a good cause return as new opportunities.
- Don't manipulate people for gain — honest influence lasts longer than coercion.
- Treat yourself gently and let go of situations that can no longer be changed — this frees up energy for new earnings.
