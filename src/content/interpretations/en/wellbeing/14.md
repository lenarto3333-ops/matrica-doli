# 14. Temperance — the energy of financial wellbeing

At its best, the fourteenth energy stabilizes and strengthens other money channels through measured, creative work done at the soul's calling; at its worst, this is exactly where greed, instability, and the belief that creativity doesn't pay settle in.

## Positive

+ **Steady activity.** Even, consistent work without sharp spikes or crashes.
+ **Stable income.** Money arrives regularly rather than in waves.
+ **A middling position and salary.** A comfortable level without an exhausting chase for record numbers.
+ **A measured, unhurried pace.** A work rhythm that doesn't wear out the body or mind.
+ **Reliably carrying out their duties.** Dependability others can count on.
+ **Modest needs.** Contentment with what they have, without a constant chase for more.
+ **Trust in their inner voice.** Decisions grounded in their own soul rather than others' expectations.
+ **Earning through creativity.** Income from what brings genuine enjoyment.
+ **Living by the heart's calling.** Choosing work by resonance, not only by payoff.
+ **Faith in their own talents.** Confidence that their abilities are genuinely worth fair pay.

## Negative

- **An unstable schedule.** Chaotic work with no clear rhythm.
- **Greed.** A drive to accumulate more than is genuinely needed.
- **Carelessness with responsibilities.** A sloppy attitude toward what they've taken on.
- **Laziness at work.** Energy that drifts away instead of going into the task.
- **Chasing a quick grab.** Short-term gain instead of steady growth.
- **Anger and resentment toward management.** Built-up frustration that poisons relationships at work.
- **Giving up steady income for side gigs.** Scatters effort instead of relying on a stable income.
- **Long-term saving for things that don't matter.** Money set aside for things that bring no joy.
- **Unwilling to listen to their own soul.** Decisions made against their inner sense of things.
- **"You can't really earn from creativity."** A belief that blocks income from what genuinely inspires them.
- **A prestigious but uninteresting job.** Status is there, but enjoyment and energy are not.
- **Living beyond their means.** Wanting to live at a level their actual income doesn't yet support — money goes toward appearances while debt piles up.

## Recommendations

- Pursue creativity at least as a hobby — it opens a channel for extra income.
- Listen to your own intuition in financial decisions, not only to logic.
- Develop your creative potential and show it to the world — visibility strengthens income.
