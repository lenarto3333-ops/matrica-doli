# 3. The Empress — Daily Energy in the Negative

## In Brief

Today may bring exhaustion from constantly caring for others: there is no energy left for yourself, yet the wish to rest is accompanied by guilt. Anxiety may also arise around resources—money, time, or energy—even when there is no objective reason for it.

## Natural Strength

Even on a difficult day, the third energy retains its capacity to create comfort and care. Direct it not toward doing one more thing for others, but toward your own body: warm food, calm, and simple physical comfort.

## Negative Expression

You may feel irritated that no one appreciates your care, be tempted to take on too many of other people's tasks, or, conversely, hoard excessively “just in case” because of a vague anxiety. The body is especially sensitive to overwork today.

## What to Avoid Today

Avoid making new promises to care for or help others when your own resources are already depleted, as well as large impulsive purchases made “just in case.” Also avoid small acts of self-sacrifice that quietly accumulate into exhaustion.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

Give yourself one simple physical act of care—delicious food, a warm bath, or peaceful rest. Before taking on someone else's task, ask whether you genuinely have the capacity. Brief contact with nature may restore more energy than another attempt to provide for everyone.

## Main Message of the Day

Care for others will be more sincere today if you begin by caring for yourself. Allowing yourself to rest is not selfishness, but a way to preserve generosity for the long term.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Have I saved at least a little care for myself today?
- What am I doing from anxiety about resources rather than genuine need?

## Practical Plan

1. Give yourself one simple act of care that feels pleasant to your body.
2. Decline one task belonging to someone else for which you currently lack the capacity.
3. Spend a few minutes in contact with nature or in quiet, without tasks.
