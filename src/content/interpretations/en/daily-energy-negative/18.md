# 18. The Moon — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today, subtle intuition can lead to the wrong place - instead of a hint, it turns into disturbing illusions, when imagined scenarios seem more real than facts. The day passes under the sign of the shaky line between true inner knowledge and the fear that pretends to be it.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the energy of the eighteenth carries a rare gift - a deep sensitivity to the atmosphere, moods and what is hidden behind words, and the ability to create the desired with imagination. This gift does not disappear, even if today it is temporarily lost under anxiety or suspicion.

## Expression in the Negative

It is possible to be suspicious of people and situations without real grounds, to scroll through gloomy scenarios in your head that never happened, or to suddenly plunge into sadness for no apparent reason. Or the opposite - suppressing one's own emotions, trying to rationally explain everything instead of simply feeling what is really happening inside.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid important decisions made under the influence of a disturbing fantasy or an unverified suspicion - today the imagination tends to paint the worst. You should also avoid being completely immersed in your own thoughts alone, without any contact with reality or people you trust.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

If you find yourself having disturbing thoughts or suspicions, try asking yourself if it's a real feeling or a figment of your imagination, and check it with the facts. It's helpful to allow yourself to feel the emotion that arises, rather than immediately analyzing or chasing it away—acceptance is often more soothing than explanation.

## Main Message of the Day

Today asks you to trust the still small voice within, while distinguishing it from the voice of anxiety masquerading as foreboding. Real intuition doesn't scare or paralyze—it just quietly knows.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is this a real feeling or a picture painted by anxiety — and what will checking it against the facts show?
- Am I suppressing my own emotions today, trying to rationally explain everything instead of simply feeling it?

## Practical Plan

1. If an anxious suspicion or gloomy scenario comes to mind, check it against the real facts before acting.
2. Allow yourself to feel the emotion that's rising instead of immediately analyzing or suppressing it.
3. Spend a few minutes alone in silence to tell the quiet inner voice apart from the voice of anxiety.
