# 15. The Devil — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today, the day may feel as if something stronger than reason is pulling you along—desire, habit, or someone else's attraction is overpowering a sober choice. There is a temptation to give free rein to what you know well should be restrained, or to find yourself under the influence of someone else's magical pressure, almost without noticing it.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the energy of the fifteenth retains a sharp gift to see the essence - what a person really wants, what he hides, what he is guided by. Today, this insight should be directed not at manipulation, but at honest recognition of one's own and other people's true motives under superficial words.

## Expression in the Negative

A craving for something excessive — food, shopping, the screen, the attention of a certain person — is possible, which is difficult to stop with an effort of will. Or a jealous, possessive feeling toward someone close to you that makes you want to control or test rather than just be there for you.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid decisions made under the influence of a strong impulse, which will most likely cause regret tomorrow. It is also worth avoiding the temptation to use charm or pressure to get what you want by bypassing an honest conversation - these days it is especially easy to turn into manipulation.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

Before giving in to a strong desire, take a short pause and honestly ask yourself what you really want behind this impulse. If you notice jealousy or a desire to control someone, say it directly to yourself - the very admission loosens the grip of the feeling.

## Main Message of the Day

Desire in itself is not hostile - it becomes dangerous only when it controls invisibly. The real power today is not to suppress all impulses, but to remain conscious within one's own desire, and not to be led by it.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What am I longing for today so much that, looking closely, I understand that it will not bring long-term satisfaction?
- Where am I today trying to get something through pressure or charm instead of honest conversation?

## Practical Plan

1. Notice one strong impulse during the day and pause for at least a minute before acting.
2. If jealousy or a desire to control someone arises, write down the insecurity behind it.
3. At the end of the day, name one honest desire and one automatic, almost obsessive desire — and mark the difference between them.
