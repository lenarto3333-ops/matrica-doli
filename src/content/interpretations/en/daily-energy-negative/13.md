# 13. Death — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Short summary

Today, the day can bring the feeling that something familiar is coming to an end, and something new is not yet visible - and this causes anxiety, not relief. There is a temptation to cling to something that has already exhausted itself, or, on the contrary, to abruptly and rudely end the situation, just so as not to experience the uncertainty of the transition.

## Natural force

Even on a negative day, the thirteenth energy retains the ability to see exactly what is no longer working and needs to be completed. It should be used not for panicked destruction or stubbornly holding on to the old, but to honestly admit: something has ended, and resistance to it only continues the pain.

## Manifestation in the minus

A sharp reaction to the slightest hint of change is possible - irritation, resistance, the desire to keep the situation as it is, even if it already costs strength. Or the opposite manifestation: an impulsive desire to cut everything off at once, to destroy instead of gradually letting go.

## What to avoid today

It is better to avoid sudden, final decisions made in a state of irritation or fear of loss - today it is easy to confuse a true conclusion with an emotional breakdown. It is also worth avoiding stubbornly holding on to something that has clearly outlived its usefulness, just because the unknown scares more than the familiar.

## How to go through the day more gently

Allow yourself to admit: if something feels finished, it is not a disaster, but a natural stage that prepares the place for something new. Before acting on irritation, pause and ask yourself if this is a decision to end or escape from the discomfort.

## The main message of the day

Resistance to completion takes more energy than completion itself. Allow yourself to let go of what has already been exhausted - gently, not sharply.

## Questions for self-reflection

- What exactly today am I holding on to because of fear, and not because of a real need?
- Where am I confusing an honest conclusion with the desire to destroy everything with one sharp movement?

## Practical plan

1. Identify one situation that feels draining and honestly address it to yourself without rushing into drastic action.
2. Before making any final decision today, give yourself a break of at least a few hours.
3. Make one small farewell gesture—symbolic or practical—to ease the transition.
