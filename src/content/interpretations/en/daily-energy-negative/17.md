# 17. The Star — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today, the inner light can lead in the wrong direction: either inflate the ego to the need for constant admiration, or, on the contrary, fade to the feeling that dreams and talent are worthless. The day passes under the sign of a shaky balance between star sickness and deep self-doubt.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the seventeenth energy carries a rare gift — the ability to see perspective where others see only darkness, and to inspire others by example. Today, this gift does not disappear anywhere, even if it is temporarily hidden under anxiety or an excessive need for recognition.

## Expression in the Negative

Irritation due to lack of praise, arrogance towards those who seem "less talented", or constant comparison of one's day with other people's successes is possible. Or the opposite pole - a sudden feeling that all dreams are meaningless, and talent is not needed by anyone, and the desire to hide from any attention or appreciation.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid comparing yourself with others, because today it either inflates the ego or drowns in a sense of one's own insignificance - and neither of these poles provides real support. It is also worth avoiding decisions made either in pursuit of the admiration of others, or under the influence of sudden despair in one's own strength.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

If you find yourself needing constant validation of your own worth or thinking "I'm worthless", try to remember one specific small thing that was done honestly and well without an audience. It is helpful to remind ourselves that true light needs neither applause nor denial to remain true.

## Main Message of the Day

Today calls for a return to the quiet, steady light within—the one that shines regardless of attention or lack thereof. True power is not in showiness or denial, but in calm self-belief that does not need proof.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Am I seeking outside confirmation of my own worth today instead of trusting the quiet inner light?
- Am I comparing myself to others in a way that either inflates my ego or drowns me in a sense of worthlessness?

## Practical Plan

1. If you notice a need for constant praise, or the opposite thought "I'm worth nothing," recall one small thing you did honestly, without an audience.
2. Consciously hold back from comparing yourself to others' successes at least once today.
3. At the end of the day, name one thing where you stayed true to yourself regardless of others' reactions.
