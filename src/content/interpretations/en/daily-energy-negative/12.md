# 12. The Hanged Man — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today may feel like a day of being stuck: decisions hang in the balance, tasks remain on hold, and you may be tempted to wait for the “perfect moment” instead of taking even one step. Inner fatigue may arise, along with the sense that time is passing while you watch your own life from the sidelines.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the twelfth energy retains its ability to view a situation from an unusual angle and notice what others miss in their haste. Use this gift not to justify inaction, but to find an unconventional way forward when the direct route seems blocked today.

## Expression in the Negative

You may feel trapped in a state of waiting, postponing a decision again and again because “the time is not right yet.” Lethargy can set in, making even a simple task feel difficult to begin, along with a tendency to analyze the same situation repeatedly instead of finally acting.

## What to Avoid Today

Avoid important decisions made from fatigue or inner resistance; today it is easy to confuse a wise pause with ordinary procrastination. Also avoid blaming yourself for moving slowly or comparing yourself with people who happen to be moving faster today.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

Allow yourself to slow down without guilt, but choose one small step that must be completed by the end of the day, however modest, so that you do not become entirely stuck. Try viewing a difficult issue from another angle: what advice would you give yourself if you were observing the situation from outside it?

## Main Message of the Day

Today’s pause is not an escape from action, but a way to see what haste conceals. Let yourself notice it, then take one small step.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I genuinely need another pause, or am I simply postponing an uncomfortable decision?
- What might I see if I looked at this situation from an unexpected angle?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one decision you have postponed “until a better moment,” and set a clear deadline for making it.
2. Spend 10 minutes in silence, considering a difficult situation as though it concerned someone else rather than you.
3. Take one small step in a stalled matter, even if it seems insignificant.
