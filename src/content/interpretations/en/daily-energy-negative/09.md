# 9. The Hermit — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today, it is easy to shut people out even when what you truly want is support. Instead of an honest pause, a protective distance appears: silence instead of conversation, “I’m fine” instead of asking for help. The risk of the day is mistaking withdrawal for rest.

## Natural Strength

Even on a difficult day, the ninth energy retains its keen analytical mind. Direct it not toward endlessly revisiting your own or other people’s mistakes, but toward calmly observing what is making you want to escape contact right now.

## Expression in the Negative

You may feel that everyone around you is too loud, superficial, or demanding, and want simply to disappear from view. At the same time, loneliness grows inside—sustained by that very distance. Thoughts tend to circle without leading to action: analysis for its own sake rather than for the sake of a decision.

## What to Avoid Today

Avoid drastic decisions such as “I’m not talking to anyone ever again,” as well as completely ignoring messages from loved ones—this is more escape than self-care. Do not let your inner voice turn solitude into proof of your superiority over the “superficial” people around you.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

Allow yourself time alone, but give it a clear limit rather than using it as a way to disappear indefinitely. If you catch yourself thinking, “No one will understand me anyway,” test that belief with at least one brief, honest message to someone close. Be direct with yourself about the difference between “I need some time alone” and “I am hiding from pain.”

## Main Message of the Day

Distance chosen out of self-love heals. Distance chosen out of fear deepens loneliness. Today, honestly ask yourself which one is guiding you now.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I genuinely want to be alone, or am I hiding from someone or something?
- Whom am I keeping at a distance today even though I actually need their support?

## Practical Plan

1. Give yourself time alone with a clear limit instead of staying away “until you feel like coming back.”
2. Write down one thought that keeps circling rather than continuing to replay it in your mind.
3. Send one brief, honest message to someone you are keeping at a distance today.
4. Before bed, check whether your pause has turned into completely ignoring those close to you.
