# 16. The Tower — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today, the day can bring a sudden upheaval - news, disruption of plans or a sharp dispute that destroys what seemed stable. Something that was held on to with all its strength may not last today, and the first feeling is shock, not relief.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the energy of the sixteenth carries the ability to see the unadorned truth—what has long been crackling beneath the surface of calm. Today, this clarity should be used not for panic, but for honest recognition: something really is no longer the same as before.

## Expression in the Negative

An explosion of irritation or anger is possible, which erupts more sharply than one would like, or news that suddenly changes the plans for the day. The body may react with tension, anxiety or a feeling that the ground is slipping away from under the feet, even if nothing dramatic has happened on the outside.

## What to Avoid Today

It's best to avoid decisions made in the heat of shock or anger—it's easy to say or do things today that you'll regret tomorrow. You should also avoid trying to hold on to the old at all costs if it is already showing signs that it will not last.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

If a disruption or unpleasant surprise occurs, give yourself a few minutes to just breathe before reacting or deciding anything. It is useful to remind ourselves that sudden change is not always a disaster, but sometimes the only way that something frozen will finally move.

## Main Message of the Day

What is collapsing today has most likely been unstable for a long time - the earthquake only made it visible. The real power of the day is not to hold everything in place at all costs, but to survive the shock without losing yourself.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What exactly have I been holding onto with my last strength for a long time now, and is it really worth continuing to hold on?
- How much am I allowing myself to simply live through the shock, instead of immediately looking for someone to blame or rushing to fix the situation?

## Practical Plan

1. If you notice a burst of irritation or shock, take a few minutes to breathe before reacting or deciding anything.
2. Write down one thing that "cracked" or broke today, and honestly ask yourself whether it was really as stable as it seemed before.
3. At the end of the day, note one way you managed to get through the shock without losing yourself.
