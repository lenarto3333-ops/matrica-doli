# 22. The Fool — Daily Energy in the Negative

## Brief Essence

Today there is a risk of either making a rash decision — jumping into a risky decision or a frivolous promise without thinking about the consequences — or, on the contrary, being afraid of any change and clinging to the usual routine as if it were the only protection. Both extremes are about the loss of trust in the natural flow of life.

## Natural Strength

Even on a negative day, the twenty-second energy carries a rare gift — the ease with which you can start something from a clean slate, and a sincere trust that life will tell you the next step. This gift does not disappear anywhere, even if today it is suppressed either by anxiety or excessive impulsivity.

## Expression in the Negative

Adventurous behavior is possible - a rash decision, a promise that will not be fulfilled, or a frivolous attitude to a serious matter for which you will have to be responsible. Or vice versa — sudden stiffness, fear of taking even a step aside from the usual, rejection of an opportunity just because it is unfamiliar.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid important decisions made "on emotions" and without any pause for reflection - today it is easy to promise more than you want to fulfill later. It is also worth avoiding the decision to abandon everything new just out of fear — today's anxiety before the unknown is easily mistaken for prudence, although it is not.

## How to Move Through the Day More Gently

If you find yourself wanting to do something sudden and risky, try to give yourself a short pause and honestly ask who or what will be affected by the consequences. If, on the contrary, you find yourself afraid of the new, it is useful to take one small step towards it - not the whole leap at once, but just a test touch to the unfamiliar.

## Main Message of the Day

Today asks us to remember that true freedom is not in carelessness and not in seclusion, but in trust in life, which does not forget about responsibility for one's own steps.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is this decision coming from a genuine impulse, or from a wish to escape responsibility?
- Where does fear of the new pass itself off as prudence today, even though it isn't?

## Practical Plan

1. Before acting on a strong impulse, give yourself a brief pause and honestly weigh the consequences for others.
2. If you catch yourself fearing something new, take one small, test step toward it rather than the whole leap at once.
3. At the end of the day, name one promise or decision you're willing to stand behind.
