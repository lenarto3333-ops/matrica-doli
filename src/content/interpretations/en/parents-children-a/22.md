You came into this world with openness to new experiences and an ease in starting fresh where adults see only risk. Your task is to teach your parents spontaneity and trust in life, to show them that the absence of rigid frameworks isn't irresponsibility — it's the ability to stay alive and flexible.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your spontaneity was tightly restricted, or conversely no one paid attention to you and left you completely to your own devices, you may not have learned to connect freedom with the consequences of your actions. In adult life, this can show up as a tendency to act without thinking things through and to avoid responsibility for the outcomes of your own decisions.
