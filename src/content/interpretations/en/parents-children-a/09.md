You came into this world with a deep, thoughtful nature — you naturally need time alone to sort yourself out and find your own answers. Your task is to teach your parents to respect your need for quiet and not mistake it for detachment, to show them the value of self-reflection that can't be rushed.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your wish to be alone was seen as withdrawal or a cause for worry, and your reflection was met with impatience, you may have learned to retreat even further into yourself. In adult life, this can show up as difficulty asking for support and a tendency to solve everything alone, even when people around you are ready to help.
