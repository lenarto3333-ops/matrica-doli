You came into this world with a sincere heart and a heightened sense of truth in relationships — you pick up on falseness and pressure faster than adults are willing to admit. Your task is to teach your parents to value genuine choice and real closeness over formal "how things should be," and to show them that true harmony is built on honesty, not convenient compromises.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your choices were dismissed in your family, or others decided for you "what's best," you may have grown up feeling you couldn't trust your own desires. In adult life, this often turns into difficulty making decisions on your own and an anxious fear of making the "wrong" choice, even in small things.
