You came into this world with a drive toward wholeness — you naturally sense when scattered pieces come together into a complete whole, and you know how to see things through to the end. Your task is to teach your parents to value completion and acknowledged results, to show them that finishing something matters more than rushing to start the next thing.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your efforts and results went unnoticed or were dismissed in your family, with everyone immediately asking "what's next," you may have learned that completion has to be earned through endless improvement. In adult life, this turns into difficulty calling something finished until it's "perfectly ready," even when the result is already quite good.
