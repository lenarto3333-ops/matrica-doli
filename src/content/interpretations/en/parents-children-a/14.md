You came into this world with a natural sense of harmony — even amid chaos, you know how to find balance and gently reconcile things that seem incompatible. Your task is to teach your parents to value gradualness and diplomacy, to show them that not every issue needs to be resolved sharply and immediately by picking a side.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your family had a lot of head-on conflict, or conversely expected immediate, decisive action from you, you may have learned to avoid any extremes at the cost of delay. In adult life, this can show up as difficulty making choices and a tendency to postpone decisions just to avoid creating tension.
