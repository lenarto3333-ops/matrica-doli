You came into this world with a quiet but real strength — not in force and pressure, but in patience and gentle influence that works more reliably than any raised voice. Your task is to teach your parents to value composure and calm persistence, to show them that gentleness and strength aren't opposites but one and the same.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your gentleness was mistaken for weakness and you were pushed to be "tougher," or conversely expected to be constantly compliant, you may have learned to suppress your emotions instead of expressing them at the right time. In adult life, this can build into long, silent patience that suddenly breaks in a sharp outburst no one saw coming.
