You came into this world with an innate sense of order and responsibility — even as a child, you knew how to turn chaos into clear structure. Your task is to teach your parents the value of stability and consistency, to show them that firm boundaries and predictability are not boring — they're a form of care.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your family lacked stable rules, or conversely, order was held together by fear and rigidity, you may have taken on the role of the "adult who can't make mistakes" too early. In adult life, this turns into excessive control — over yourself and your loved ones — and difficulty relaxing until everything is arranged "correctly."
