You came into this world with a gift for explaining complex things in simple words and a natural pull toward knowledge and tradition. Your task is to teach your parents to value experience and continuity, to show them that wisdom isn't passed on through strictness but through genuine interest in what came before us and what's worth preserving.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if knowledge and rules in your family were delivered as dogma, without explanation or warmth, you may have learned that love has to be earned through correct behavior. In adult life, this can show up as a tendency to lecture instead of simply being present, and difficulty accepting other people's experience when it differs from the "right" one.
