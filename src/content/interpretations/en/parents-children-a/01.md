You came into this world with a strong will and a natural gift for acting first — starting things rather than waiting for permission. Your task is to teach your parents to trust your initiative and give you room to try, make mistakes, and lead the way, instead of racing ahead and making decisions for you.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your initiative was suppressed and your independence was seen as disobedience, you may have grown up feeling that your will was an inconvenience to others. In adult life, this turns into a habit of taking everything on yourself and carrying it alone — because asking for help or sharing control feels risky.
