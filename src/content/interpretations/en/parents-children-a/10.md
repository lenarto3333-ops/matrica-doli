You came into this world with an ease around change and a natural sense for the right moment — where others cling to the familiar, you move naturally with the flow of life. Your task is to teach your parents to trust change, to show them that not everything needs to be controlled and that a new turn of fate can be a gift rather than a threat.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if any change in your family was met with anxiety, and your flexibility was labeled as flakiness or inconsistency, you may have learned that trusting life is dangerous. In adult life, this turns into attempts to tightly control what is, by nature, meant to change, and anxiety in the face of life's natural turns.
