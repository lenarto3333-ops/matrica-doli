You came into this world with remarkable resilience in crises — where others feel lost, you know how to quickly regroup and find new footing after any upheaval. Your task is to teach your parents not to fear sudden change, to show them that real stability is born precisely after hardship, not in its absence.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your childhood was full of instability, sudden crises, or the sense that the ground could give way at any moment, you may have learned to live in constant readiness for the worst. In adult life, this turns into a subconscious expectation that anything stable is about to fall apart, even when there's no real reason for it.
