You came into this world with the ability to look at familiar things from an unfamiliar angle and find solutions where others see a dead end. Your task is to teach your parents to value pausing and time spent "doing nothing," to show them that an outside perspective and unhurried timing are sometimes more important than immediate action.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your slower pace or need for pauses was mistaken for laziness, and you were rushed to "figure things out" faster, you may have learned to doubt your own rhythm. In adult life, this can show up as getting stuck waiting for the "right moment" and struggling to simply begin without waiting for perfect conditions.
