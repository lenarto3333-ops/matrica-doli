You came into this world with natural sincerity and the ability to fill the whole space around you with joy — people find it easier to breathe near you. Your task is to teach your parents openness and simplicity in relationships, to show them that genuine joy and warm connection matter more than complicated formalities.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your openness and joy were dampened by excessive seriousness, or you were asked to "act like an adult," you may have learned to hide your real feelings behind a smile. In adult life, this can show up as a habit of avoiding difficult, uncomfortable topics to keep things light, and a fear of spoiling the mood with an honest conversation.
