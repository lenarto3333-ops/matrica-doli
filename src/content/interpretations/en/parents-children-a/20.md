You came into this world with the ability to look honestly at the road you've traveled and draw real conclusions from it, noticing even at a young age what works in your family and what doesn't. Your task is to teach your parents to live in line with their own values, to show them that an honest look at the past frees you rather than condemns you.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if decisions in your family were made without reflection, and mistakes were either hushed up or held against you forever, you may have learned to judge yourself more harshly than you deserve. In adult life, this turns into a habit of re-examining past decisions with excessive self-criticism, instead of simply taking the lesson and moving on.
