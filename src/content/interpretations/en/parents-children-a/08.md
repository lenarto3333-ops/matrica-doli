You came into this world with a heightened sense of fairness — you pick up on falseness, bias, and broken promises before others even notice a problem. Your task is to teach your parents to take responsibility for their words and to treat everyone in the family equally, without hidden favorites or double standards.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if you felt you were treated unfairly, or that the rules at home shifted depending on the adults' mood, you may have grown up with a constant need to prove you were right. In adult life, this turns into excessive criticism of yourself and others, and a habit of dividing people into those who are "right" and those who are "wrong."
