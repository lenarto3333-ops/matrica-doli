You came into this world with a rare ability to let go of what has run its course and start over without excessive fear — where adults cling to the familiar, you naturally sense when it's time to change. Your task is to teach your parents not to fear endings, to show them that a closed chapter makes room for something new rather than meaning loss.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if any change in your family was treated as a catastrophe, and partings and endings were hushed up or turned into drama, you may have learned that letting go is dangerous. In adult life, this turns into a fear of finishing chapters and a habit of holding on to relationships, jobs, or habits that stopped working long ago.
