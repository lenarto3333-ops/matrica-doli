You came into this world with an inner drive — the ability to mobilize and move toward a goal while others are still deliberating. Your task is to teach your parents to value forward motion, to show them that change and tangible progress aren't a threat to stability but a natural part of life worth supporting rather than holding back.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your drive was held back or seen as stubbornness, you may have learned to get your way through force and argument. In adult life, this can show up as a tendency toward conflict and a need to control the course of events and the people around you, instead of moving together with them.
