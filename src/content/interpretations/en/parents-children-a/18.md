You came into this world with heightened sensitivity to atmosphere and mood — you pick up on what's hidden beneath words long before it's said out loud. Your task is to teach your parents to accept emotions, their own and others', without fear, to show them that sensitivity isn't weakness but a subtle instrument for understanding what's really going on.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if emotions were hushed up in your family, and a tense atmosphere was never named directly, you may have grown up sensing tension that no one ever acknowledged out loud. In adult life, this turns into a tendency toward anxiety and illusion — imagining things that aren't really there, a result of the lack of clarity you had growing up.
