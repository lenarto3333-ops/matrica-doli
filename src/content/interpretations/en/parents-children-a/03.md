You came into this world with a rich imagination and a natural gift for filling the space around you with warmth and beauty. Your task is to teach your parents to value creativity and care not as something secondary, but as a real strength that makes a home feel alive rather than just a list of duties.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your care was taken for granted and your creative impulses were dismissed as "not serious," you may have gotten used to giving more than you receive in return. In adult life, this often turns into a habit of dissolving into caring for others while forgetting to ask yourself what you actually want.
