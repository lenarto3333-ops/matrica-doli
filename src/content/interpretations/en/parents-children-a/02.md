You came into this world with a subtle intuition and an ability to listen to yourself where others look for answers only in logic and words. Your task is to teach your parents to trust the invisible — your hunches, your silences, your need to sit in quiet — instead of demanding constant explanations and "reasonable" causes.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your sensitivity was treated as strangeness or secrecy, and your silence was mistaken for distance, you may have learned to hide your real feelings behind silence. In adult life, this can show up as difficulty speaking about your needs directly and a habit of keeping important things to yourself until it becomes too heavy to bear.
