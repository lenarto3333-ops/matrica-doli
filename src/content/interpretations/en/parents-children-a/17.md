You came into this world with a gift for inspiring others — your natural faith in a better future and your ability to see possibility where others see only current struggles. Your task is to teach your parents not to lose hope and to protect their own dreams, to show them that believing in good things isn't naivety — it's real strength.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your dreams were dismissed as "unrealistic" or you were rushed to "come back down to earth," you may have learned to hide your inspiration and shape your wishes around others' expectations. In adult life, this can show up as a risk of drifting too far into dreaming, losing touch with the real steps needed to make them happen.
