You came into this world with a sober sense of the material world and the limits of what's possible — you understand early on what's real and what's just nice-sounding words. Your task is to teach your parents freedom from imposed rules and other people's expectations, to show them that real support doesn't come from depending on others' approval, but from your own choices.

**Possible childhood difficulties:** if your family had many hidden rules, manipulation, or codependent relationships, and your need for boundaries wasn't respected, you may have learned to accommodate others just to avoid conflict. In adult life, this can show up as a tendency to fall into dependent relationships or habits that are hard to break free of on your own.
