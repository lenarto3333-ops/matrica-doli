# 18. The Moon — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the eighteenth arcana is a time of heightened intuition, rich imagination and deep immersion in the inner world, when the subconscious is actively trying to suggest something. This is a delicate time when the line between reality and imagination becomes thin, and it is important to learn to distinguish true knowledge from disturbing fantasy.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ability to sense hidden processes, moods and trends long before they become obvious facts. This is a good time for creativity, working with the subconscious, deep inner changes and healing emotions that have been neglected for a long time.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the period brings accurate intuitive hints, creative inspiration and the ability to bring the desired closer to reality with imagination. A feeling of deeper contact with oneself appears - a person better understands his own true feelings and what he really wants.

## Expression in the Negative

On the negative side, the period can bring waves of anxiety, suspicion, or even depression, when imaginary scenarios seem more real than facts. It is also possible to escape into fantasy instead of action, when a person lives an imaginary life, and real affairs remain motionless.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period worsens if a person suppresses or rationalizes his own emotions for a long time, not giving himself the right to simply feel them. The second trigger is a lack of support for facts, where any uncertainty is automatically filled with the most alarming assumptions.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to regularly check your gut feeling with the real facts so that your intuition remains a guide and not a source of anxiety. It is also important to allow yourself to experience the emotions that arise during this period, instead of suppressing or overanalyzing them.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to learn to distinguish between true inner knowledge and disturbing illusion, using heightened sensitivity as a tool, not a source of fear.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Which disturbing thoughts of this period are confirmed by facts, and which ones exist only in my imagination?
- What do I really feel if I allow myself not to explain or suppress these feelings?

## Practical Plan

1. Keep short notes about the intuitive premonitions of this period and compare them with what is actually happening.
2. Take a few minutes each day to just feel the emotion that comes up without trying to immediately explain it.
3. Once every few weeks, check whether your decisions of this period are based on reality or on disturbing fantasies.
