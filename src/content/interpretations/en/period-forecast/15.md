# 15. The Devil — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the fifteenth arcana is a time of a heightened sense of one's own desires and one's own attractiveness: something that a person has long held back or denied in himself, now begs outward with redoubled force. This can be a period of temptations, passionate passions and at the same time an opportunity to finally see what or who really controls the choice.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is a keen sense of the essence: a person sees the hidden motives of others, recognizes falsehood and feels exactly what really brings pleasure or benefit, and what only creates an illusion. This is a good time for matters related to persuasion, finances, attracting attention or resources.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this period brings a noticeable increase in influence and material well-being: natural charisma attracts profitable opportunities, and a deep understanding of human desires helps to build relationships and agreements for mutual benefit. Courage appears to honestly recognize one's own desires and act in accordance with them.

## Expression in the Negative

At its worst, a period can turn into an addiction—to a person, a habit, a substance, or one's own desire for control—that imperceptibly subjugates decisions and time. There may also be jealousy, excessive possessiveness in a relationship, or the feeling that someone is exploiting someone else's trust or weakness for their own gain.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is aggravated if a person has long denied his own true desires, and now they break through any boundaries at once, without control. The second trigger is an environment or relationship built on manipulation or hidden pressure, which is easy to fall into right now due to heightened sensitivity to someone else's charisma.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to honestly tell yourself what you really want during this period, instead of acting under the influence of impulse or other people's pressure. It's also important to regularly check relationships and habits for signs of addiction or control — both your own and someone else's — until they are firmly established.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to learn to consciously use one's own magnetism and deep vision of the essence, not allowing any desire or person to completely subordinate one's choice.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What or who has the most power over me right now, and do I realize why?
- Where do I feel that I am acting under the pressure of desire, and not from my own free choice?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one desire or habit that has recently gained excessive power over your time or decisions.
2. Check key relationships once a week for signs of hidden pressure, jealousy, or control.
3. Allow yourself to honestly recognize one true desire of this period and take a conscious, not an impulsive, step towards it.
