# 20. Judgement — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the twentieth arcana is a time of honest conclusions and true rebirth, when the past finally receives a clear assessment, and a new one is born on its basis. This is a favorable time to honestly look at the path traveled and take a step forward from it, and not live in a constant return to what has already been lived.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ability to soberly assess the past, hear the inner call for renewal, and rely on the support of family and accumulated experience. This is a good time to finish old business, have honest conversations with family, and make decisions based on your own values, not fear or habit.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the period brings a sense of completion — the old chapter of life is closed, and instead there is clarity about the next step. Confidence in one's own intuition grows, and relationships with family or the past get a chance for reconciliation and renewal.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the period can turn into an endless review of the past — a constant return to decisions already made, self-blame or exacerbation of old family conflicts that suddenly become louder. It is also possible to feel as if the past passes judgment that cannot be appealed or closed.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is aggravated if a person avoids an honest look at the past and accumulates unspoken claims against himself or his family until they break through on their own. The second trigger is unfinished family or property issues that have been postponed for years and now require a final solution.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It's helpful to deliberately set aside time for honest summaries—without drama, but also without minimizing what happened—and draw one clear conclusion from them. It is also important to talk openly with the family where reconciliation is long overdue, allowing yourself and others the right to an imperfect past.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to accept the past as it was, draw an honest conclusion from it and allow yourself a real revival, not a retrial of what has already been lived.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What past decision or situation do I relive in my mind over and over instead of drawing a conclusion and letting go?
- Is there an unfinished topic in the family that right now calls for an honest conversation and reconciliation?

## Practical Plan

1. Write one summary of this period - honestly, without belittling and without self-accusation.
2. If there is a long-overdue family or property issue, take one concrete step toward resolving it.
3. Once every few weeks, check if you are moving forward or if you are going back in your mind to an already lived decision.
