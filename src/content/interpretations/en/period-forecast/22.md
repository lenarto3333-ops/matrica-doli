# 22. The Fool — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the twenty-second arcana is a time of freedom and new experience, when there is an opportunity to start something from a clean sheet and let go of old frameworks. This is a favorable time for spontaneous decisions, travel and any steps that previously seemed too risky.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ease with which you can start a new business or stage, and a sincere trust that life will tell you the next step. This is a good time for new experiences, acquaintances and going beyond the boundaries of the usual routine - geographical, professional or personal.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the period brings a feeling of freedom and lightness: decisions are made without undue weight, new opportunities appear by themselves, and trust in life allows you to boldly start anew. Sincerity in relations with oneself and others increases - it is easier to speak directly and without masks.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the period can turn into a series of rash decisions and escape from responsibility for their consequences, when lightness turns into frivolity. The opposite is also possible - fear of any change, due to which a person refuses chances and clings to a limited, familiar picture of life.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is exacerbated if a person grabs every new opportunity without thinking, accumulating unfinished business and unfulfilled promises. The second trigger is sudden uncertainty that causes such intense fear that a person prefers not to change anything at all, even when it hurts.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to consciously distinguish a real opportunity from an impulsive impulse - to pause before making big decisions and honestly assess the consequences. It is also important to practice taking small steps towards the new if the fear of change keeps you in place for too long.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to allow yourself the freedom of new experiences, maintaining responsibility for your own decisions and not allowing the fear of the unknown to narrow your life to the usual circle.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What decision of this period did I make too quickly, without thinking about the consequences?
- What opportunity do I refuse only because of fear of the unknown?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one new decision or opportunity this period and honestly assess whether it was thoughtful or impulsive.
2. Choose one thing that you have given up out of fear, and take a small test step towards it.
3. Once every few weeks, check whether the balance between freedom and responsibility in your life is not shifted to one side.
