# 19. The Sun — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the nineteenth arcana is a time of growth, recognition and natural joy, when things started with an open heart receive support and move on a large scale. This is an auspicious time to act boldly and publicly, and not hide your plans in the shadows out of caution.

## Natural Strength

The power of this period is the ability to attract opportunities, people and recognition with sincerity and warmth, without the need to fight or prove something by force. This is a good time for launching large projects, public steps, strengthening relationships and any business that requires scale and openness.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the period brings visible results, well-deserved recognition and a feeling that life is finally "blooming" after a more difficult time. There is more energy to share it with others, to inspire and lead without fear of being noticed.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the period can turn into a pursuit of constant confirmation of success, when any pause or failure is perceived as a personal disaster. It is also possible to have pride that alienates loved ones, or exhaustion from the role of someone who should be a source of light for everyone around.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is aggravated if a person ignores the real limits of his own resource, trying to maintain a pace and scale that are not available without rest. The second trigger is an environment that expects a person to be constantly positive, leaving no room for difficult or difficult feelings.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to regularly compare the scale of ideas with real forces and resources, so that growth does not turn into exhaustion. It is also important to allow yourself the pauses and unusual emotions of this period, without trying all the time to maintain the image of a person who always has it easy.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to accept success and recognition as a natural result of sincerity, and not as something that must be constantly proven or maintained at the cost of one's own strength.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Where am I trying to maintain the image of constant success and joy, ignoring the real fatigue of this period?
- Do I share the growth of this period with others, or do I keep the recognition to myself?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one large-scale plan for this period and compare it with the real resource of time and energy.
2. Consciously share the success or good news of this period with someone who supports you.
3. Once every few weeks, allow yourself a break without the requirement to stay energetic and productive.
