# 16. The Tower — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the sixteenth arcana is a time of sudden upheavals that destroy what has long been unstable: illusions, outdated agreements, situations that were held only by habit or fear of changing something. This is a difficult, but purifying time, after which life will most likely become more honest and stronger.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ability not to break under a blow, but to find in it an impetus for real growth. This is a good, if stressful, time to finally acknowledge the truth of a situation that has been trying to be ignored for a long time and start building something on a more real foundation.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this period brings liberation from what has long exhausted itself: an unexpected change opens the way to better circumstances than a person himself would have dared to choose. There is an inner strength, hardened by the experienced shock, and clarity about what is really important and what was just an illusion.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the period can bring a series of crises one after the other, sharp conflicts or outbursts of aggression directed at oneself or others. A repetition of a traumatic scenario from the past is also possible, if its lesson was not learned after the previous shock.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is exacerbated if a person has long ignored clear signs that some structure - relationship, work, lifestyle - can no longer withstand, and resistance to change only increases the force of the coming blow. The second trigger is an unlived trauma from a past concussion that manifests as a painful reaction to even minor changes now.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to honestly admit as early as possible that it is no longer possible in life, and to start restructuring consciously, rather than waiting for it to happen by force. It is also important to give yourself time and support to live with the shock if the shock has already occurred, instead of immediately rushing to restore everything as it was.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to allow the obsolete to fall without trying to hold on to it at any cost and to use the released energy to build something stronger and more honest.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What structure in my life now rests only on habit or fear, and not on real strength?
- What did the last major upheaval teach me, and did I really learn this lesson?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one situation that clearly requires restructuring and take the first step to change without waiting for a crisis.
2. If the shock has already occurred, give yourself at least a few days to recover before making important decisions.
3. Once a month, check that there is no tension building up that could explode into a sudden conflict or breakdown.
