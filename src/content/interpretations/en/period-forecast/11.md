# 11. Strength — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

This period requires endurance. Something difficult or “unruly”—a situation, a person, or an emotion of your own—needs patient, consistent work rather than a quick solution. This is a time to remain calm and persistent without forcing events or losing composure.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ability to endure without panic. What is being resolved now often depends not on how sharply you can press, but on how long and calmly you can remain steady in the face of difficulty.

## Positive Expression

In the positive, the period is experienced as conscious taming. A person neither runs from a difficult task nor tries to force it into submission, but patiently brings it to completion step by step. This period often produces a tangible strengthening of trust, reputation, or inner confidence.

## Negative Expression

In the negative, the same demand for endurance becomes pressure and exhaustion. The person either tries too aggressively to force the situation, provoking resistance, or becomes so depleted by prolonged tension that they lash out at loved ones or themselves.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

Attempts to make a situation change faster than it can, together with a prolonged lack of recognition or support precisely when effort is needed most, can shift the energy into the negative.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

Remind yourself that taming is a gradual process and forcing it usually only increases resistance. Consciously schedule restorative pauses during this demanding period instead of waiting for complete exhaustion.

## Main Task

Move through this period with endurance and gentleness rather than pressure or exhaustion, emerging with stronger inner power and greater trust from those around you.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Where am I trying to force something that needs more time and patience?
- Do I allow myself restorative pauses during this demanding period?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one situation in this period that requires patience rather than pressure, and consciously slow down within it.
2. Schedule one restorative pause this week without waiting for complete exhaustion.
3. Notice the moment you are tempted to press harder, and choose a calm, consistent action instead.
