# 10. Wheel of Fortune — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

This is a turning-point period. Circumstances that seemed unchangeable begin to move, and what has remained at a standstill gains a chance to change for the better. This is a time to watch closely for opportunities that appear almost by chance and to be ready to act faster than usual.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is openness to an unexpected turn. What begins or ends now is often determined less by careful planning than by the readiness to recognize the right moment and use it while it lasts.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, the period is experienced as conscious movement: a person trusts that circumstances are aligning in their favor while acting decisively wherever an opportunity opens. This period often brings a tangible improvement—in affairs, relationships, or inner well-being—that comes more easily than expected.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same changeability becomes anxiety or chaos: a person either clings to the old out of fear of change, obstructing the natural movement, or scatters their energy across too many new directions at once without bringing any of them to fruition.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

Attempts by others to keep the situation unchanged precisely when the period's natural movement calls for flexibility can shift the energy into the negative. So can a lack of clear focus—when too many opportunities appear at once and the person cannot tell which one to pursue.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

Decide in advance on one or two areas where you most want to use the momentum of this period, and focus your attention there instead of scattering it across everything at once. It is also helpful to release consciously what has clearly run its course rather than holding on to it out of fear of emptiness.

## Main Task

Move through this period as a conscious turning point rather than as chaotic rushing or frozen resistance to change—and emerge with a concrete result and stronger trust in your own ability to seize the moment.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What opportunity in this period have I already noticed but not yet used?
- What am I holding on to out of fear of change even though it has clearly run its course?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one or two areas to focus on during this period instead of scattering your attention across everything at once.
2. Take a concrete step toward an opportunity you have already noticed but postponed.
3. Consciously release one outdated commitment or habit that is obstructing the natural movement of this period.
