# 8. Justice — Period Forecast

## In Brief

A period under the energy of the eighth arcana is a time when the causes and consequences of past decisions become visible. It is a favorable moment for honest reckoning, settling old accounts—financial or emotional—and addressing situations that require an objective rather than emotional assessment. Anything that has long remained a “half-truth” tends to become fully clear during this period.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is a heightened sense of justice and the ability to see situations soberly. It becomes easier to notice where agreements have been broken and where they have been honored, and to make decisions based on facts rather than personal sympathies.

## Positive Expression

In its positive expression, this period brings fair resolutions to long-standing matters: repayment of a debt, an honest agreement, acknowledged responsibility, or, conversely, well-deserved recognition. Decisions made now reflect the real balance of interests and have a good chance of enduring because they are not built on the concealed resentment of either party.

This is also a favorable time to restore trust where it has been undermined—through an open conversation and honest acknowledgment of one's part.

## Negative Expression

In its negative expression, this period may aggravate old grievances: past injustices resurface, along with the temptation to “finally prove” who was right. The risk is turning a period of reckoning into a period of revenge, wasting energy on a battle over principle instead of genuinely restoring balance.

## What Can Pull the Energy into the Negative

The period becomes more difficult when a person encounters someone else's impunity—a situation where someone “gets away with it” and justice is not immediate. A second trigger is having to acknowledge one's own fault at the very moment when blaming circumstances would be easier.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It helps to distinguish consciously between what can be corrected now and what must be released because consequences will unfold without personal intervention. It is also important to approach financial or relational reckonings as a means of closure, not as a way to “punish” the other party.

## Main Task

The main task of this period is to bring honest closure to what has long required it, remaining a fair witness to the situation rather than its judge.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What old account—financial or emotional—is long overdue for closure?
- Where do I want victory over a particular person rather than justice?
- What from the past can I release now, trusting that consequences will unfold on their own?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one unresolved financial or relational situation and take a step toward closing it.
2. Have a conversation in which you honestly acknowledge your share of responsibility.
3. Consciously release one grievance whose consequences no longer depend on your personal intervention.
