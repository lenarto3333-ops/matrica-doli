# 17. The Star — Period Forecast

## Brief Essence

The period under the energy of the seventeenth arcana is a time of renewed hope, creative discovery and a growing sense of perspective after a difficult stage. This is an auspicious, albeit delicate time, when a person's talent and dreams have a chance to become visible to others, if given at least a little concrete form.

## Natural Strength

The strength of this period is the ability to see light even where darkness reigned until recently, and to share this vision with others so that they too begin to believe in the best. This is a good time for creative endeavors, professional recognition and healing after the fatigue or disappointments of the previous stage.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the period brings real inspiration, an influx of new ideas, and perhaps notable recognition of talent — publicity, praise, new proposals or projects. There is a feeling that a difficult time has passed and a real, not an illusory, perspective is opening up ahead.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the period can bring disappointment due to inflated expectations, when dreams do not withstand a collision with reality, or excessive enthusiasm for one's own exclusivity, which repels the environment. The opposite extreme is also possible - deep despair, when a person loses touch with his own dreams and talent after one sharp disappointment.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The period is exacerbated if a person lives in dreams for a long time without any specific step towards their realization, and reality sooner or later reminds of itself with a sharp contrast. The second trigger is an excessive dependence of self-esteem on external recognition, due to which any criticism or indifference of others is perceived as a personal disaster.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It's helpful to give dreams and inspiration concrete form as early as possible—a plan, a first step, a rough draft—so that hope is grounded in reality, not just imagination. It is also important to learn to support your own belief in yourself from within, without waiting for constant confirmation from the outside.

## Main Task

The main task of the period is to keep alive the hope for the best, while at the same time grounding it in concrete actions, so that talent and inspiration bring a real, and not just an imaginary result.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What dream or idea of mine currently lives only in the imagination and needs at least one concrete step towards realization?
- How strongly does my mood during this period depend on someone else's recognition or praise?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one dream or idea of this period and determine the first concrete step towards its realization.
2. Notice the moments when expectations from a situation or people become unrealistically high, and consciously adjust them.
3. Once every few weeks, check if your hope is based on real actions or if it remains only a beautiful picture in your head.
