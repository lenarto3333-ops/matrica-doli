# 9. The Hermit — soul task: your path of development

## Brief Essence

This energy is not a gift but a task: it keeps the soul from fulfilling its main purpose until it is consciously worked through. A family habit, formed over years, of staying silent about pain and carrying it alone becomes a challenge worth recognizing — the sooner you begin, the gentler the outcome.

## Natural Strength

A person carrying the Hermit comes into life with the mission of stopping a family habit of suffering in silence. Across several generations, the family may have grown used to not asking for help, hiding problems behind closed doors, and treating solitude as the only safe place. This person has a rare ability to look deep — into their own history and the family's — and that is exactly why they can be the first to name what the family has stayed silent about for decades, returning that wisdom to loved ones instead of keeping it only for themselves.

## In the Positive

+ **The first to speak about pain.** Dares to name what the family has kept silent about for decades.
+ **Shares the family's wisdom.** Passes deep insights on to loved ones instead of keeping them only to herself.
+ **Accepts requests for help.** Allows herself to receive support, not only give it.
+ **Support without judgment.** Helps loved ones without lecturing them.
+ **Honest conversations instead of silence.** Chooses to talk where the family is used to silence.
+ **Sees the root of the problem.** Understands the deeper cause, not just the symptom of family struggles.
+ **The right to be weak.** Lets loved ones off the hook from always having to be strong.
+ **Calm in a crisis.** Remains a steady support when the family is under strain.
+ **Passes the experience on.** Shares hard-won wisdom with the younger family members.

## In the Negative

- **Hides problems even from loved ones.** Conceals difficulties even from those she trusts.
- **Avoids support she needs.** Refuses help even when it's genuinely necessary.
- **Withdraws from family matters.** Stays on the sidelines of family affairs when involvement is needed.
- **Carries others' pain alone.** Takes on the family's burden without sharing it.
- **Won't ask for help.** Prefers to manage alone even when it's exhausting.
- **Disappears for long stretches.** Drops out of family life for extended periods.
- **Silence instead of conversation.** Chooses quiet where an honest conversation is needed.
- **Withdraws during a crisis.** Turns inward in difficult moments instead of being present.

## Recommendations

- Tell one person close to you about something you've kept hidden until now.
- Once a month, consciously ask loved ones for support, even in small things.
- Share your own experience and lessons with the younger generation of the family.
- When you notice the urge to disappear during a crisis, stay and make your presence known.
- Start a tradition of honest family conversations, free of judgment and advice "from above."

## What You Will Gain

A family where silence is no longer the only way to get through pain; a deep, trusting bond with loved ones; and wisdom that finally works not just for you, but for the whole family.
