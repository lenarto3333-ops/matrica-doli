# 14. Temperance — soul task: your path of development

## Brief Essence

This energy is not a gift but a task: it keeps the soul from fulfilling its main purpose until it is consciously worked through. Family conflicts kept quiet for years for the sake of apparent peace call for conscious reconciliation — the sooner you begin, the gentler the outcome.

## Natural Strength

A person carrying Temperance comes into life with the mission of reconciling a divided family. Where a family has split into "camps" for years — over grievances, inheritance, or old misunderstandings — this person has a natural gift for finding common ground between sides that seem irreconcilable. Her strength lies in diplomacy and patience, in the ability to gradually, without sudden moves, bring back together what once seemed permanently split apart.

## In the Positive

+ **Reconciles those who've fallen out.** Brings together relatives who haven't spoken for years.
+ **Finds compromise.** Looks for a solution that works for both sides of a dispute.
+ **Heals old rifts.** Gradually mends long-standing cracks in the family.
+ **Broaches painful topics diplomatically.** Raises difficult subjects gently, without escalating.
+ **Keeps the balance among relatives.** Doesn't take sides, staying neutral and steady.
+ **Brings calm.** Eases tension at difficult family gatherings.
+ **Builds trust between generations.** Bridges older and younger family members step by step.
+ **Harmonizes different views.** Finds common ground between dissimilar positions in the family.
+ **Moves gently toward reconciliation.** Doesn't force it, but never gives up on bringing the family back together.

## In the Negative

- **Avoids conflict.** Sidesteps a clash instead of resolving it.
- **Puts off an important conversation.** Delays a conversation that's long overdue.
- **False calm at the cost of truth.** Chooses the appearance of peace over honesty.
- **Afraid to take a side.** Avoids taking a position even when one is needed.
- **Searches for the perfect compromise.** Drags out a decision looking for an option that pleases everyone without exception.
- **Stays silent for the sake of the relationship.** Hides her opinion to avoid risking the relationship.
- **Lets a rift continue.** Doesn't step in, and the family split drags on for years.
- **Dissolves between sides.** Tries so hard to please everyone that she loses her own position.

## Recommendations

- Initiate a conversation between relatives who've fallen out, instead of waiting for them to make peace on their own.
- Set a specific deadline for a family matter that's being postponed "for the sake of peace."
- Honestly state your position in a family conflict instead of staying neutral to keep the peace.
- Organize a family gathering aimed specifically at reconciliation, not at avoiding the topic.
- Acknowledge even small steps the family takes toward each other.

## What You Will Gain

A family that gradually heals old rifts; a reputation as someone who reconciles rather than avoids; and a deep sense of harmony earned through real reconciliation, not silence.
