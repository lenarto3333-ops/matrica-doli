# 16. The Tower — soul task: your path of development

## Brief Essence

This energy is not a gift but a task: it keeps the soul from fulfilling its main purpose until it is consciously worked through. Here the issue is family upheavals repeating from generation to generation — and it falls to this person to stop the cycle rather than carry it forward.

## Natural Strength

A person carrying the Tower comes into this life with a heavy but important mission: to withstand and stop the family catastrophes that hit again and again — sudden losses, breakups, hidden crises exploding at the worst possible moment. From birth, this person senses cracks long before it becomes obvious that something is collapsing, and that's exactly why the role falls to her of being the first to say the truth out loud, instead of silently waiting for the collapse. This is an energy capable of turning the family's experience of collapse into a foundation stronger than anything that came before it.

## In the Positive

+ **Stops cycles of catastrophe.** Breaks a family pattern of repeating crises.
+ **Speaks uncomfortable truths.** Dares to say what others would rather keep quiet.
+ **A pillar in moments of crisis.** Remains a steady point when everything around is shaking.
+ **Acknowledges secrets and falseness.** Doesn't turn a blind eye to family lies or hidden things.
+ **Rebuilds after a collapse.** Able to restore the family after a serious shock.
+ **Resilience to shocks.** Withstands personal crises without losing her footing.
+ **Courage for radical change.** Dares to take decisive steps when necessary.
+ **Composure in a panic.** Stays calm and capable when everyone around is losing their head.
+ **Lets go of what doesn't work.** Parts easily with what has already lost its meaning.

## In the Negative

- **Repeats the pattern of collapse.** Reproduces the family's experience of catastrophe instead of stopping it.
- **Outbursts of anger.** Tension breaks out in sharp flare-ups at loved ones.
- **Destroys ties without need.** Severs relationships that could have been preserved.
- **Silently accumulates grievances.** Stores up hurts quietly, without voicing them.
- **Expects disaster even in calm times.** Doesn't trust the quiet, always waiting for the next blow.
- **Sudden breaks without warning.** Cuts off relationships abruptly, without a chance to talk.
- **Resists change until the end.** Clings to the old until everything collapses on its own.
- **Passes the trauma on to the children.** Her own pain shows up in how she reacts to her children.

## Recommendations

- Name out loud the family "crack" everyone senses but no one talks about, and start an honest conversation.
- Before abruptly cutting ties with loved ones, pause and check whether it's really necessary.
- Find a safe way to "let off steam" so tension doesn't explode onto loved ones.
- Work through one past family shock that still affects your reactions in the present.
- Consciously build a new, honest structure of relationships to replace the one that collapsed.

## What You Will Gain

The chance to stop the family's cycle of repeating crises, preserve and restore the relationships worth keeping, and an inner resilience that grows not from avoiding hardship but from honestly going through it together with the family.
