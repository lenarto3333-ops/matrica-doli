# 6. The Lovers — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to bring people together around shared values: to choose sincerely who to build alliances, partnerships, and communities with, and for what purpose.

## Positive

+ **Sincere alliances.** Builds partnerships and teams on real shared values, not momentary gain.
+ **A diplomatic bridge.** Finds common ground between people or groups with differing views.
+ **Freedom to choose direction.** Doesn't cling to an outdated partnership out of inertia once the paths have diverged.
+ **Honest communication.** States her own values and expectations from a collaboration directly.
+ **Uniting around an idea.** Naturally gathers people who share a common goal or vision.
+ **Sensitivity to insincerity.** Quickly senses falseness in business or community relationships.
+ **Willingness to make a hard choice.** Able to walk away from a profitable but unethical partnership.
+ **Warm charisma in negotiations.** Earns trust through sincerity rather than manipulation.
+ **Balancing interests within a team.** Can account for several parties' needs at once, without fully sacrificing any of them.
+ **Loyalty to a chosen path.** Once she commits to a cause or team, stays devoted to it through hard times.
+ **Inspiring by example.** Shows others what a healthy, equal working partnership looks like.

## Negative

- **Indecision on key choices.** Struggles for a long time to choose between career directions or projects.
- **Dependence on others' approval.** Makes important decisions while looking over her shoulder at colleagues' or partners' opinions.
- **Disillusionment with an idealized partnership.** Overly high expectations of colleagues or the team lead to painful disappointments.
- **Avoiding confrontation for the sake of harmony.** Stays quiet about disagreement so as not to spoil a pleasant working atmosphere.
- **Wavering between directions.** Frequently switches projects or teams in search of the "perfect" fit.
- **Losing her own voice in an alliance.** Dissolves into the values of a stronger partner or group.
- **Holding on to a failing partnership too long.** Stays in an ineffective collaboration for fear of seeming disloyal.
- **Comparing herself to others.** Measures her own success through the lens of teammates' achievements.
- **Idealizing new acquaintances.** Trusts new partners or projects too quickly, without testing them in practice.
- **Emotional swings in collaboration.** Enthusiasm alternates with disappointment depending on colleagues' moods.
- **Struggles to say "no" to a valuable contact.** Agrees to unfavorable terms just to keep the relationship.

## Recommendations

- Before making an important professional decision, define your own selection criteria separately from others' opinions.
- Agree on the terms of a collaboration clearly at the start, rather than hoping things will sort themselves out.
- Allow yourself to end a partnership that no longer matches your values, without guilt.
