# 12. The Hanged Man — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to offer a community or team a different view where everyone is stuck: to be a catalyst for rethinking when familiar solutions no longer work.

## Positive

+ **A fresh look at long-standing problems.** Sees a solution where others have already given up, by looking from an unusual angle.
+ **Willingness to question everything.** Isn't afraid to challenge an established approach that no longer works.
+ **The value of a pause.** Suggests stopping to rethink the situation instead of rushing into action.
+ **Independence from popular opinion.** Can hold a position even when it contradicts the majority.
+ **Tolerance for uncertainty.** Remains calm in a state of "not yet knowing" until real understanding emerges.
+ **A catalyst for new ideas.** Her unconventional questions open team discussions in a new direction.
+ **Self-sacrificing dedication to the cause.** Willing to set aside personal gain temporarily for the community's long-term result.
+ **Observant outside the usual flow.** Notices patterns that get lost in others' daily rush.
+ **Accepting unfavorable decisions on principle.** Chooses what's right, even when it isn't the fastest or most profitable path.
+ **Steadiness while waiting.** Doesn't panic when a result or answer isn't ready yet.
+ **Provoking useful discomfort.** Her questions push the team out of the zone of automatic decisions.

## Negative

- **Getting stuck in waiting.** Waits so long for the "right moment" that the opportunity is lost.
- **Passivity instead of action.** Analyzes a situation from every angle but never moves to concrete steps.
- **Confusing to the team.** Colleagues don't understand why a seemingly obvious decision is being held up.
- **Excusing inaction as "a different perspective."** Hides a fear of acting behind supposedly deeper reflection.
- **Losing project momentum.** Her need for a pause slows down a team that needs a fast result.
- **Isolation through unconventionality.** The team sees this stance as odd and keeps its distance.
- **Martyrdom for recognition.** Unconsciously sacrifices herself to earn the team's sympathy or gratitude.
- **Struggles to convey the value of a pause.** Can't explain to leadership why "doing nothing" right now is actually a strategy.
- **Disappointment when others don't see the same thing.** Feels hurt that the team doesn't share her unconventional vision.
- **Stuck between two decisions.** Can't choose a direction for years, holding on to a state of limbo.
- **Missed career opportunities.** Lets the right moment for advancement pass by while waiting too long for "ideal conditions."

## Recommendations

- Set yourself a specific deadline for pausing and reflecting, after which you move to action.
- Explain the value of a delayed decision to the team in plain words, not through silent stalling.
- Choose one option, even an imperfect one, instead of lingering between alternatives.
