# 20. Judgement — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to honestly sum up what's been done and pass the lesson on to the next generation: to assess results without embellishment and to be the keeper of the community's or organization's collective memory.

## Positive

+ **Honest assessment of the path traveled.** Able to objectively sum up a project or stage without embellishing the result.
+ **Responsibility for collective memory.** Preserves and passes on important lessons to the next generations within the team.
+ **Willingness to admit mistakes publicly.** Isn't afraid to say a particular decision or approach didn't work.
+ **Ethical consistency.** Lives and acts in line with her stated values, even when it's difficult.
+ **The ability to honestly sum up the team's results.** Conducts a solid analysis of outcomes without hunting for someone to blame.
+ **A voice of accountability in the organization.** Reminds the team of commitments and values when they're forgotten.
+ **Transparency in reporting.** Provides honest, accurate information about the state of things, even when the news isn't good.
+ **The ability to learn from the past.** Uses past experience as a foundation for better decisions in the future.
+ **A fair assessment of everyone's contribution.** Acknowledges each team member's real role in the shared outcome.
+ **The courage to speak uncomfortable truths about the past.** Doesn't hush up failed decisions to protect a reputation.
+ **A catalyst for honest reflection.** Helps the community see the real state of things and draw conclusions.

## Negative

- **Excessive severity toward past decisions.** Judges her own or others' mistakes too critically, instead of simply moving through them and on.
- **Getting stuck analyzing the past.** Gets so absorbed in summing things up that it hinders moving forward.
- **Public condemnation without tact.** Points out others' mistakes sharply, without considering context or people's feelings.
- **Perfectionism in evaluating results.** Devalues real achievements by fixating on what didn't work.
- **Difficulty forgiving.** Holds on to others' past missteps for a long time, even after the person has changed.
- **Exhaustion from being the team's judge.** The constant responsibility for honest assessment is tiring and isolating.
- **Rigidity in moral judgments.** Ignores the nuances of a situation, applying a universal standard to everything.
- **Fear of her own insufficient contribution.** Constantly checks whether her own life lives up to her stated values, which is exhausting.
- **Struggles to let go of an outdated judgment.** Keeps judging a colleague or situation by past behavior, ignoring changes since.
- **Public repentance instead of action.** Spends too much energy admitting fault instead of taking concrete corrective steps.
- **Loss of trust through excessive criticism.** Colleagues avoid sharing honest information, fearing harsh judgment.

## Recommendations

- When summing up, name not only the failures but also the team's or project's real achievements.
- Before publicly pointing out someone's mistake, think through a framing that helps rather than humiliates.
- Set a point after which a past mistake is considered closed and no longer brought up.
