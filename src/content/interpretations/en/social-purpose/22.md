# 22. The Fool — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to bring a fresh outlook and lightness into rigid social structures: to be an unprejudiced innovator who dares to start with a clean slate where everyone else is afraid to take the risk.

## Positive

+ **A fresh, unbiased outlook.** Offers solutions unburdened by "that's how it's always been done" or fear of error.
+ **Lightness in complex processes.** Eases unnecessary tension on the team with her ease and humor.
+ **Courage to start from zero.** Isn't afraid of a radically new direction once the old one has run its course.
+ **Openness to unconventional ideas.** Offers solutions that more experienced colleagues never even considered.
+ **Natural adaptability.** Easily picks up a new role, field, or environment without fear of looking like a beginner.
+ **A catalyst for renewing rigid rules.** Asks simple questions that dismantle outdated, unjustified constraints.
+ **Contagious spontaneity.** Inspires the team to try new things without excessive planning or fear.
+ **Ease meeting new people.** Quickly connects with an unfamiliar audience or partners.
+ **Freedom from status games.** Doesn't fight for hierarchical recognition, which lets her act sincerely and openly.
+ **Willingness to learn on the fly.** Takes on something new without waiting for full readiness or ideal conditions.
+ **Simplicity in explaining complexity.** Breaks tangled topics down into simple, accessible steps for others.

## Negative

- **Rash decisions.** Takes on something new without assessing the real consequences for herself or the team.
- **Avoiding responsibility.** Easily moves on to the next idea, leaving the previous one unfinished.
- **A lack of seriousness about important matters.** Humor and lightness are sometimes out of place in situations that call for gravity.
- **An unstable reputation.** Frequently changing direction keeps colleagues from seeing her as a reliable partner.
- **Loss of trust through inconsistency.** Promises enthusiastically but doesn't always follow through.
- **Ignoring others' experience.** Sets out to reinvent the wheel without weighing what predecessors already built.
- **Fleeing routine duties.** Avoids the boring but necessary parts of the work in favor of what's new and interesting.
- **Struggles to build a long-term strategy.** Lives in the moment, which gets in the way of planning years ahead.
- **Naivety about risk.** Underestimates the real dangers of a new venture out of excessive optimism.
- **Being seen as not serious.** Leadership or colleagues don't entrust her with important, responsible roles.
- **Scattered without depth.** Tries lots of new things but rarely achieves true mastery in any one of them.

## Recommendations

- Before a new start, ask yourself at least one practical question about consequences and resources.
- Deliberately carry at least one thing you've started through to a concrete, finished result every year.
- Learn from more experienced colleagues, rather than relying solely on your own spontaneity and intuition.
