# 1. The Magician — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to open new directions for a community or organization: to launch projects and initiatives no one else has dared to start, and to lead those willing to follow.

## Positive

+ **Initiator of change.** Offers new solutions and directions when a team or community has stalled.
+ **Mentoring by example.** Teaches younger colleagues to act, not just plan.
+ **Public confidence.** Speaks, presents ideas, and represents the team with assurance.
+ **Fast mobilization.** Able to rally resources and people behind a new cause in a short time.
+ **Contagious energy.** Her enthusiasm sets things moving where others were only discussing possibilities.
+ **Courage to take on the new.** Doesn't wait for perfect conditions to launch a public initiative.
+ **Personal example of action.** Shows the team that it's possible to act instead of waiting endlessly.
+ **Confident negotiation.** Negotiates on behalf of the group or organization without hesitation.
+ **Building projects from scratch.** Easily launches new institutions, directions, and initiatives where nothing existed before.
+ **Willingness to take responsibility.** Takes on a difficult decision when others step back.
+ **Charismatic representation.** Naturally becomes the voice of the community or team to the outside world.

## Negative

- **Solo decisions.** Makes important decisions for the team or group without consulting anyone.
- **Exhausting pace.** Sets a pace so fast that colleagues or the community can't keep up and burn out.
- **Eroded trust.** Frequent changes of direction undermine trust in new initiatives.
- **Reluctance to share credit.** Finds it hard to acknowledge others' contribution to shared success.
- **Intolerance of slower colleagues.** Reacts sharply to those who can't keep the set pace.
- **Excessive control over processes.** Keeps holding on to what should long since have been delegated.
- **Competition instead of cooperation.** Competes publicly where teamwork is what's actually needed.
- **Blocking successors.** Becomes the one irreplaceable link in the process, without grooming a replacement or successor.
- **Frustration at a slow response.** Takes offense when an initiative isn't picked up instantly.
- **Chronic leader's fatigue.** Never hands over the wheel until completely burned out.
- **Public conflicts over primacy.** Competes for recognition where the result matters more.

## Recommendations

- Before launching a new initiative, ask the team or community for input instead of just announcing the decision.
- Deliberately prepare and hand off part of your projects to successors, staying a mentor rather than a sole leader.
- Once a quarter, track how much of what you've started has been finished, and hold off on new launches until earlier ones are complete.
