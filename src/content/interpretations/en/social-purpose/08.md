# 8. Justice — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to be a voice of fairness wherever decisions are made: an arbiter who weighs the interests of different parties and sets rules everyone considers just.

## Positive

+ **Objective assessment of situations.** Looks at a conflict or decision from several angles before drawing a conclusion.
+ **Trust as an arbiter.** People turn to her when an unbiased decision on a disputed matter is needed.
+ **Setting fair rules.** Formulates criteria and procedures the team perceives as just.
+ **Transparency in allocating resources.** Makes sure reward and recognition match real contribution.
+ **Willingness to stand up for the truth.** Doesn't stay silent when she sees injustice, even when it's uncomfortable.
+ **A balanced leadership style.** Balances demandingness and support in managing people or projects.
+ **An ethical compass for the team.** Becomes the moral compass colleagues look to in difficult situations.
+ **Consistency in decisions.** Applies the same criteria to everyone, making no exceptions for "her own people."
+ **The ability to admit when someone else is right.** Changes position when the other side's arguments are more convincing.
+ **Auditing and checking systems.** Naturally spots where processes or agreements operate unfairly, and fixes it.
+ **Calm confidence in a dispute.** Backs her position with facts, not emotional pressure.

## Negative

- **Excessive criticism.** Judges colleagues and decisions too harshly whenever something seems "unfair."
- **Paralyzing deliberation.** Searches so long for a perfectly balanced decision that the moment to act passes.
- **Struggles to forgive rule-breaking.** Holds a grudge against those who once behaved dishonestly.
- **Rigid application of rules.** Insists on the letter of procedure even when context calls for an exception.
- **Fatigue from the judge's role.** Wears herself out constantly settling others' conflicts and disputes.
- **Public exposure without tact.** Points out injustice directly, without considering who it will blindside.
- **Excessive self-demand.** Judges her own decisions as harshly as others', which is exhausting.
- **Distrust of subjective arguments.** Dismisses others' emotional or intuitive reasoning, demanding facts only.
- **Isolation through principle.** Loses allies by insisting on the truth where compromise would serve better.
- **Delays from checking details.** Verifies everything so meticulously that processes slow down.
- **Resentment over unfair treatment of herself.** Takes dishonest treatment of her personally very hard.

## Recommendations

- Set yourself a deadline for decisions so that deliberation doesn't turn into stagnation.
- Before publicly exposing an injustice, think about how you frame it — so the fact is heard, not the offense.
- Allow yourself exceptions to your own rules when the situation genuinely calls for it.
