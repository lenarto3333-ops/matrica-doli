# 15. The Devil — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is to see the hidden mechanisms of power and dependency within systems: to expose unhealthy structures or free others from them, depending on whether this force expresses in the positive or the negative.

## Positive

+ **A subtle sense of power and gain.** Understands the real motives of people and systems behind the facade of official statements.
+ **Exposing hidden dependencies.** Spots toxic structures in an organization before others do.
+ **Freeing others from constraints.** Helps colleagues or the community see which rules are worth abandoning.
+ **A pragmatic sense of the limits of the possible.** Knows exactly what's realistically achievable in negotiations or a deal.
+ **Resistance to manipulation.** Recognizes others' attempts at pressure or control and doesn't give in to them.
+ **Honesty about material interests.** Names financial or power motives directly where others conceal them.
+ **The strength to walk away from something profitable but harmful.** Able to leave a system that takes more than it gives.
+ **Understanding an organization's shadow processes.** Sees informal mechanisms of influence that the official structure doesn't reveal.
+ **Helping others break a dependency.** Becomes a support for colleagues who want to leave toxic working relationships.
+ **Directness on difficult topics.** Isn't afraid to call things by their name where others tactfully stay silent.
+ **Strategic thinking under constraints.** Finds workable solutions even within rigid, unfavorable systems.

## Negative

- **Involvement in unhealthy power systems.** Gets drawn into toxic hierarchies for the sake of gain or status.
- **Exploiting others' weaknesses.** Uses others' dependency or insecurity for her own advantage.
- **Dependence on status or control.** Her self-worth rests on having power over the situation or people.
- **Cynicism about others' motives.** Sees only self-interest where there's actually sincerity.
- **Getting drawn into manipulative deals.** Agrees to unfavorable or unethical terms for short-term gain.
- **Struggles to leave a toxic partnership.** Stays in harmful business relationships longer than she should.
- **Using fear to gain influence.** Maintains control over the team or situation through others' anxiety.
- **Disregarding ethics for the sake of results.** Justifies questionable methods of reaching a goal.
- **Isolation through distrust.** Constant suspicion of colleagues' motives makes genuine collaboration difficult.
- **Dependence on having power recognized.** Chases a position of status even when it does her harm.
- **Hidden arrangements.** Plays a double game in negotiations, which ultimately undermines trust in her.

## Recommendations

- Before closing a profitable deal, check whether it unfairly harms someone.
- Deliberately leave toxic working relationships or structures, even ones that bring status or gain.
- Use your ability to see hidden power mechanisms to protect the team, not to manipulate it.
