# 14. Temperance — Social Purpose

The social mission of this energy is revealed in her role within the wider world: among colleagues, community, society. Her main calling is to reconcile opposites and bring harmony to tense situations rather than taking sides.

## Positive

+ **A natural mediator.** Sees value in both sides of a conflict and finds a solution that works for everyone, not just one party.
+ **Trusted by different camps.** People turn to her for advice in difficult, tense situations within a team or community.
+ **Lowering the temperature of a conflict.** Speaks in a way that eases tension instead of inflaming it further.
+ **Movement instead of a freeze.** Finds a compromise that moves things forward instead of preserving the standoff.
+ **Diplomatic language.** Frames both sides' positions so each one feels heard.
+ **A stabilizing presence.** Her mere presence at a meeting lowers the risk of escalation.
+ **Patient balancing.** Doesn't rush a decision until all the important interests have been accounted for.
+ **An example of harmony.** Shows the team through her own behavior that balance is possible even on a difficult issue.
+ **A subtle sense of timing.** Knows when to step in and when to let the parties reach understanding on their own.
+ **Creative compromises.** Offers unconventional options no one had considered before.
+ **Uniting scattered groups.** Brings together people with different interests around a common goal.

## Negative

- **Chronic indecisiveness.** So afraid of taking sides that she avoids any clear public position at all.
- **Invisibility instead of influence.** The harmonizing role turns into having no real influence on decisions.
- **Avoiding responsibility.** Hides behind neutrality when the situation actually calls for taking a clear stand.
- **Overly long deliberation.** Searches so long for the perfect balance that the moment to act is lost.
- **A blurry position.** Speaks so cautiously that no one understands her real stance.
- **Fatigue from constant peacemaking.** Wears herself out being the buffer between sides, without recognition for the role.
- **Compromise for its own sake.** Sometimes settles for a decision that satisfies no one, just to release the tension.
- **Avoiding confrontation even when necessary.** Misses the moment when a clear "no" is needed.
- **Dissolving her own opinion.** Accounts for others' positions so much that she loses her own voice on an important issue.
- **Others exploiting her neutrality.** The parties in a conflict pressure her, trying to win her to their side by leaning on her peacemaker reputation.

## Recommendations

- Before an important meeting, formulate your own position in advance, rather than improvising it under pressure from the parties.
- Practice saying a clear "I believe" instead of constantly hedging with "on one hand... on the other hand."
- Notice the moments that call not for harmonizing but for a decisive step — and allow yourself to take it.
