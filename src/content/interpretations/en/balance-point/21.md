# 21. The World — the balance point

The point where money and relationships connect in The World is fed by a drive toward wholeness and scale — a wish to carry a project through to a complete, polished form. But that very drive easily becomes a trap that keeps postponing life for later.

## Positive

+ **A large-scale, completed project.** Carries an idea through to genuine completion — a valuable asset for steady income.
+ **Openness right now.** Stays available in the relationship without waiting for the "perfect moment" of financial success.
+ **Wholeness in the present.** Experiences completeness in small, ongoing moments, not only in the grand result.
+ **Seeing the big picture.** Keeps the long-term plan in view without losing touch with today.
+ **Valuing what's already done.** Recognizes what's already been achieved instead of focusing only on what's missing.
+ **Vulnerability for the sake of connection.** Opens up to people and opportunities even while the work is still unfinished.
+ **Sharing what's unfinished.** Shows the partner the imperfect, current version of a project or plan instead of waiting for the ideal result.
+ **Closeness without conditions.** Doesn't demand financial completeness of herself before allowing the relationship in.
+ **Realistic expectations of the partnership.** Doesn't wait for a perfect partner, but builds a real, living connection.
+ **Balance between scale and the present moment.** Combines a big goal with the ability to appreciate what already exists now.

## Negative

- **Postponed closeness.** The relationship waits for a finished version of herself that never fully arrives.
- **Financial success as a condition for closeness.** Only allows herself openness "after" reaching stability or success.
- **Perfectionism in the relationship.** Expecting an ideal partnership gets in the way of building a real, living connection.
- **Closed off from opportunity.** The drive toward perfection shuts out contacts that require vulnerability.
- **A sense of "not yet."** Unfinished business — financial or personal — signals that it's not yet time to show herself to the world.
- **Focus only on what's lacking.** Sees only what's missing, ignoring the value of what's already been done.
- **Waiting for the perfect moment.** Puts off important steps until reaching an imagined bar of perfection.
- **A hidden version of herself.** Shows the partner only finished results, hiding the process and the unfinished parts.
- **Losing the present for the sake of the future.** Wholeness keeps getting pushed to "later," once everything is ready.
- **Isolation from perfectionism about her work.** The imperfection of her own project keeps her from openness and contact with people.

## Recommendations

- Name one closeness or conversation you're putting off "until better times," and take a step toward it this week.
- Choose one unfinished project and consciously acknowledge the value of what's already been done, instead of focusing only on what's missing.
- Share with your partner the unfinished, imperfect version of a current project or plan, instead of waiting for the ideal result.
