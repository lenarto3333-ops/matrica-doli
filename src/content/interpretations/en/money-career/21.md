# 21. The World — Career and Professional Fulfillment

## Brief Essence

The career history of the twenty-first energy is the story of a person who naturally gravitates to large-scale, completed affairs and knows how to bring the disparate parts of a project or team into a single whole. Professions related to global projects, coordination of large teams, peacekeeping, travel or any activity where it is important to bring things to a real result are close to such a person.

## Natural Strength

Natural strength is manifested in the ability to see a project or business as a whole, to unite people and resources around a common goal and to bring the work to a real completion, and not to the eternal "almost done". The talent here is in the broad perspective that allows you to see possibilities where others see only scattered fragments.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, a person regularly brings projects to completion, unites people and ideas around him and confidently goes to a wider, even international level of activity. She derives deep satisfaction from a recognized, real result, not from the process for the sake of the process.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, a person either gets stuck in endless refinement of a project that has long been ready to be completed, fearing that it is "not yet perfect enough", or narrows his own professional field, being suspicious of new colleagues, markets or work formats. It is also possible to feel an internal hostility to changes in the team or industry, which prevents you from moving on.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is an environment where the result is never considered good enough, so one learns to grind endlessly instead of finishing. The second trigger is an experience when openness to new things has turned into disappointment, due to which the habit of protecting oneself by narrowing the circle of trust appears.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The first step is to consciously determine the moment when the project is truly ready, and allow yourself to call it finished, even if you are tempted to rework it again. The second step is to regularly go beyond the boundaries of the usual professional circle: new contacts, markets or formats of cooperation expand, not threaten. This text is not financial advice.

## Main Task

To learn to bring career matters to a true conclusion without endless grinding and to keep a professional view wide and open to new things, not defensively narrowed.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is there a project or matter that I keep in the "not quite ready" status longer than it really needs to be?
- How open am I to new people, markets or formats in my professional field?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one project that has long been "almost finished" and determine a specific date for its actual completion.
2. Make a list of professional contacts and areas in which you have not appeared for a long time, and choose one step to go beyond the usual boundaries.
3. Check once a quarter whether you see your career as a complete picture or only as a set of separate tasks.
