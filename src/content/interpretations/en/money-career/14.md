# 14. Temperance — Career and Professional Fulfillment

## Brief Essence

The career history of the fourteenth energy is a history of gradual, harmonious growth: a person is realized not through sudden jumps, but through patient, step-by-step development in areas where balance, diplomacy and the ability to combine different approaches are valued. Such a person is close to professions related to mediation, a creative combination of styles and concern for internal or external harmony.

## Natural Strength

A natural force is manifested in the work of a mediator, consultant, designer, therapist or specialist in the field of wellness - where you need to be able to bring together opposing interests, styles or needs and find an elegant solution. A person is able to bring peace and order to chaotic work processes without losing flexibility.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, a person builds a career around the ability to bring harmony: successfully conducts negotiations, combines different directions into a coherent project, gradually and persistently moves towards the goal, without rushing and without stopping. The career develops evenly, due to the trust of colleagues and the reputation of a person with whom it is easy to negotiate.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same ability to balance turns into an inability to determine the direction of development: a person tries different things a little at a time for years, afraid to choose one thing, and because of this does not reach depth in any matter. Another manifestation is excessive compliance at work, when a person constantly agrees with everyone to avoid conflict, and loses his own professional position.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is the fear of making a mistake with the choice of a career direction, due to which a person avoids a final decision and scatters efforts between several options at the same time. The second trigger is a work environment that encourages constant compromises, due to which a person gradually loses his own voice and professional confidence.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

The first step is to choose one direction of development, at least for a certain period, and give yourself time to move in it gradually, without changing course every time a doubt arises. The second step is to learn to defend one's professional position even when it disrupts the usual harmony in the team.

## Main Task

To learn to combine the natural gift for balance with the willingness to make a clear choice and go further in it, and not dissolve in endless compromises and options.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What career direction do I choose "little by little", being afraid to finally venture on it?
- Where at work do I give up my own position to avoid conflict?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one career direction that you will focus on for the next few months.
2. Identify one work situation where you should stand up for your position more clearly instead of automatically compromising.
3. Plan development in this direction in small, consistent steps, not in one big leap.
