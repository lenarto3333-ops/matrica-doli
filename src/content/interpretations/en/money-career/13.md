# 13. Death — Career and Professional Fulfillment

## Short summary

The career history of the thirteenth energy is a story of bold endings: a person is realized not by keeping one path forever, but by being ready to close the stages that have exhausted themselves in time and resolutely move on to a new one. Such a person is close to professions and roles where the ability to transform outdated systems is valued, rather than preserving them.

## Natural force

The natural force manifests itself in work related to reformation, crisis management, restructuring, repurposing — where you need to finish the old in order to build a better one. A person is able to see that a model, process or structure has exhausted itself and find the determination to change it.

## Manifestation in the plus

On the plus side, a person builds a career around the ability to timely and decisively complete ineffective: closes outdated projects, exits from roles that no longer develop, initiates reforms where others are afraid to change something. A career develops through a series of conscious transformations, each of which opens a new, stronger stage.

## Manifestation in the minus

In the negative, the same ability to change turns into either career inertia - a person clings to a position or industry that has long exhausted himself for years, out of fear of starting anew, or into abrupt, thoughtless dismissals and breaks that leave a person without a plan and support.

## What can be negative

The strongest trigger is the fear that nothing equivalent will appear after the end of the usual career stage, which causes a person to hold on to the outdated for longer than it is worth. The second trigger is accumulated frustration from long-suffering, which erupts into a sharp, emotional breakup without a prepared plan.

## How to return energy to the plus side

The first step is to regularly and honestly assess whether the current role or direction is still being developed or is already being held as a matter of habit. The second step is to plan a career change, prepare the transition in advance: new skills, contacts, financial cushion, instead of waiting for the moment of emotional breakdown.

## The main task

Learn to timely and thoughtfully complete self-exhausting career stages, turning the natural ability to transform into a controlled, not chaotic process.

## Questions for self-reflection

- What career role or direction do I keep only out of habit, although they have not developed me for a long time?
- Do I prepare the transition to a new stage in advance, or do I wait for the moment when I have to act suddenly?

## Practical plan

1. Honestly evaluate your current role or direction: is it still developing you, or has it already exhausted its potential.
2. If a change is imminent, make a concrete plan for a gradual transition — new skills, contacts, resources.
3. Take one decisive step towards ending the stale stage this month by preparing the transition in advance.
