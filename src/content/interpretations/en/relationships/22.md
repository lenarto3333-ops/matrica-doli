# 22. The Fool — Relationships and Partnership

## Brief Essence

In a partnership, this energy brings a rare lightness and freshness - next to such a person, the partner often feels that the relationship does not freeze in a difficult, predictable routine, but remains alive, open to surprises and new joint experiences. This energy knows how to deeply trust the relationship process itself - not to try to control every step of the couple forward, but to believe that sincerity and spontaneity will lead two people to where they will really be good together. The main challenge of the partnership of this arcana is twofold: on the one hand, there is a risk that lightness of character turns into irresponsibility in a relationship - a person avoids serious obligations, promises and does not fulfill, runs away from the couple's difficulties as soon as they require constant efforts, and not just a pleasant beginning; on the other hand, there is the risk of the opposite breakdown of the same energy, when instead of natural freedom and trust in the partner, there is fear, defensiveness and tight control over the relationship in an attempt to protect oneself from any risk of being abandoned or disappointed. The true maturity of the partnership of this arcana is to learn to trust fate and the partner, maintaining sincerity and lightness, while being responsible for the consequences of one's own decisions in the relationship, combining freedom with reliability, not with flight or fear.

## Natural Strength

The natural resource of this energy in a relationship is the ability to bring freshness and movement to a partnership: a person with this energy rarely allows the relationship to freeze in monotony, he instinctively looks for new shared experiences, new topics for conversation, new ways to spend time together, and it is thanks to this that the couple does not lose lively interest in each other for a long time.

The second strength is sincere openness without hidden games: this person tells the partner the truth about his own feelings, even when it is uncomfortable, he does not hide disappointment or joy for tactical gain, and it is this directness that often gives the partner a feeling of real, unfeigned intimacy.

The third power is trust in the natural development of the relationship: when a couple is going through a difficult period or uncertainty, this person naturally believes that the right path will be found by itself, and does not try to forcefully accelerate or control every step of life together.

The fourth force is a deep respect for personal freedom, one's own and that of a partner: a person naturally gravitates to relationships in which there is space for everyone's individuality, and not just merging into one whole, and it is this respect for individuality, despite all its complexity, that ultimately makes the connection stronger and more alive.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, a partnership is built on sincere trust: a person knows how to let go of excessive control over exactly how the relationship should develop and allows it to grow naturally, believing that the sincerity and care of two people will lead the couple to a place where it will really be good for them.

Positive communication is filled with lightness and warmth: a person knows how to bring play, humor and spontaneity even to difficult conversations, and this helps the couple to overcome difficulties without losing the feeling that they are still on the same side.

On the plus side, this energy brings a rare quality to the relationship — the ability to start anew after a conflict or crisis: instead of holding grudges for a long time, a couple with this energy knows how to let go of the past and sincerely start a new, fresh phase of the relationship.

The desire for freedom becomes a tool for healthy closeness: a person is not afraid to give the partner space for their own interests and independence, understanding that trust and respect for each other's individuality make the bond stronger, not weaker.

Another sign of a positive partnership is a healthy attitude to responsibility: a person enjoys the lightness and spontaneity of the relationship, but does not run away from difficult conversations or serious decisions of the couple, but follows this path together with the partner to the end, while maintaining sincerity, and not turning obligations into a heavy burden.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

For a woman, a positive partnership manifests itself as a sincere, direct intimacy, free from manipulation or hidden expectations: she tells her partner what she feels, trusting that honesty strengthens the bond, not destroys it.

She knows how to maintain a lively interest in her partner even in a long, stable relationship: instead of perceiving him as something already completely known and predictable, she curiously opens new facets of life together, bringing lightness where others see only routine.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, a positive partnership manifests itself as the ability to build a relationship with a partner in which there is both freedom and reliability at the same time: he knows how to enjoy the spontaneity of life together, while not losing honesty regarding his own obligations to her.

He is naturally able to sense the moment when the relationship does not need another serious conversation, but just lightness, a game or a joint adventure, and this ability to relieve tension in time makes the relationship calmer and happier for both.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the partnership can develop in one of two opposite scenarios. The first is irresponsibility in a relationship: a person avoids serious commitments, promises closeness and constancy, and then runs away as soon as the relationship requires constant, and not just pleasant, efforts, leaving the partner with a feeling of betrayal of trust and open questions.

With this manifestation of the minus, a person can easily enter into new relationships or flirtations, looking for the thrill of a new beginning, and just as easily disappear when the first difficulties appear, confusing freedom with the absence of any responsibility to another person.

The second, opposite manifestation of the negative is defensive closedness and control in the relationship: instead of natural trust in the partner, a person begins to fear any unpredictability in the couple, clings to strict rules and expectations, and perceives the partner's spontaneity as a threat to the stability of the relationship.

The third negative manifestation, closely related to the second, is hidden anxiety under the mask of control: instead of trusting the natural development of the relationship, the person tries to calculate each step of the couple in advance, fearing that any uncertainty will lead to loss or disappointment.

Another manifestation of the minus is superficiality at important moments: a person is so used to keeping relationships light that he avoids deep, serious conversations exactly when the couple most needs honest, vulnerable intimacy, not jokes and evasions.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, irresponsibility often looks like a series of bright, but short-lived infatuations - new relationships or flirtations captivate her completely at the beginning, and then lose their appeal as soon as the partner expects constancy and depth. The opposite side is sudden closedness and control: a woman can become overly anxious about the partner's unpredictability, trying to know his every move in advance to avoid possible disappointment.

The tendency to run away in the negative manifests itself especially acutely at moments of important decisions of the couple: it can easily promise a serious step in the relationship in a rush of feelings, and then suddenly retreat as soon as the reality of the commitment becomes palpable.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, the negative manifestation is often disguised as "I'm just a free person" or "I don't like frameworks" - in fact, this is a way to avoid the vulnerability of real, long-term intimacy, which requires constant presence. Instead of honestly admitting his fear of commitment, he keeps the relationship deliberately light, avoiding talking about the future or deeper feelings.

The opposite manifestation is categorical control over the relationship: a man with this minus can become suspicious or harsh about any spontaneity of his partner, fearing that unpredictability in a couple will inevitably lead to loss or betrayal.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is the moment when the relationship passes from an easy, pleasant beginning to a phase that requires constant, sometimes difficult efforts: the first serious conflict, household routine, the need to agree on long-term plans. It is then that natural lightness most easily breaks down either into an escape from responsibility or into anxious control.

The second trigger is when the partner offers something unexpected or spontaneous in the relationship, and the natural trust suddenly shrinks into a defensive position of "it's too risky, I have to control everything."

The third trigger is the long-term lack of honest conversation about the expectations of the couple: when neither partner dares to say out loud what they really want from the relationship, the accumulated uncertainty sooner or later breaks through either a sharp flight or an anxious, excessive control.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The first step is to consciously practice honesty about your own fears about commitment: it's helpful to tell your partner directly when you feel like running away or controlling instead of acting on that impulse silently.

The second step is to learn to distinguish true trust in a partner from control disguised as concern: when there is a desire to know in advance every move of the other half, it is worth trying to approach the uncertainty with curiosity, not anxiety.

The third step is to practice bringing important conversations or decisions to the end of the couple, even when it would be easier to turn everything into a joke or put it off "for later".

The fourth step is to agree with your partner on regular moments of honest, warm debriefing of the relationship - a space in which you can talk about both the freedom that everyone needs and the responsibility that both are ready to bear.

The fifth step is to consciously choose a balance between spontaneity and stability according to the real needs of the couple: better one honestly agreed way to combine ease and reliability than constant extremes of escape or rigid control.

## Main Task

The main task of this energy in a partnership is to learn to trust fate and a partner, maintaining sincerity and lightness, without slipping into irresponsible escape from the couple's obligations, nor into anxious control due to fear of unpredictability. When this task is fulfilled, the partnership becomes a space of true freedom: it knows how to appreciate the spontaneity and live movement of the relationship, to remain honest and reliable in what is important, and to consciously build a common future together, trusting what is already really built between two people.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I see through important conversations and decisions in our relationship, or do I tend to run away or turn everything into a joke when the going gets tough?
- Do I trust my partner enough to give him room for spontaneity, or do I try to control every unpredictable step out of fear?
- Am I honest with my partner about my own fears before commitment, do I hide them with ease or, on the contrary, with excessive strictness?

## Practical Plan

1. Once a week, honestly tell your partner about one fear or desire to escape from a certain topic, instead of silently avoiding the conversation.
2. Having noticed the desire to control an unpredictable step of the partner, stop and ask yourself the question: "What is interesting in this uncertainty, and not only what is disturbing in it?"
3. Choose one moment this week to start something new and spontaneous with your partner — a little joint adventure without a predetermined plan.
4. Once this week, complete a conversation or a couple's decision that you previously wanted to postpone or turn into a joke.
5. Discuss with a partner one joint plan for the future and honestly determine what responsibility each is ready to take on.
6. Once a month, honestly evaluate together with your partner whether the relationship is built on trust and freedom, or on hidden escape from each other or excessive control that should be let go.
