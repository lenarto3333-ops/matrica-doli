# 19. The Sun — Relationships and Partnership

## Brief Essence

In a partnership, this energy brings special warmth and joy - next to such a person, the partner often feels that the relationship is filled with light, lightness and sincere interest in each other. This energy knows how to generously give attention, support and inspiration, making everyday moments brighter just by its presence. The main challenge of the partnership of this arcana is twofold: on the one hand, there is a risk that this same fiery energy turns against the person himself — self-eating, when he exhausts himself, trying to always be the one who maintains a positive atmosphere in the couple, hiding his own fatigue or pain; on the other hand, there is the risk of pride and burnout of the partner, when a person unconsciously expects that a loved one always matches his mood and energy, or avoids difficult conversations so as not to destroy the easy atmosphere of the relationship. The real maturity of the partnership of this arcana is to learn to give warmth without exhausting yourself, and to have the courage to talk with your partner about the difficult as freely as to share the joy.

## Natural Strength

The natural resource of this energy in a relationship is the ability to make a partnership warm and alive: a person with this energy is sincerely interested in the life of a partner, celebrates his successes and supports him in moments of doubt so that it is difficult to feel unnoticed next to him.

The second power is the ability to create an atmosphere of generous acceptance in a relationship: where others skimp on compliments or gratitude, this energy naturally and often speaks of what they value in a partner, making the relationship a space of recognition rather than silent obviousness.

The third strength is the ability to think about the partnership on a large scale: this person is not afraid of big joint plans, travel or ambitious plans for the couple, and it is this willingness to scale that gives the relationship a sense of shared adventure and inspiration.

The fourth power is the sincere, contagious joy that a person shares with his partner: he knows how to find a reason to celebrate even in the mundane, and this ability, if he has learned to serve two, and not one, makes the everyday life of the couple brighter.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, the partnership is built on sincere, warm mutual understanding: a person generously shares attention and joy without demanding an immediate return of the same intensity, and at the same time knows how to sense when the partner needs a different pace or mood.

Communication in the plus is filled with sincere openness: a person knows how to talk about his own feelings directly, without games and decorations, and is just as sincerely interested in the internal state of the partner, not being satisfied with only a superficial "everything is fine".

On the plus side, this energy brings a rare quality to the relationship — the ability to accept difficult periods without running away in a positive way: instead of immediately turning a difficult conversation into a joke, partners honestly experience difficulties together, trusting that sincerity strengthens the bond stronger than hidden lightness.

The ability to think big becomes a source of common inspiration for the couple: big plans, trips or joint dreams keep the relationship alive, while remaining realistic and supported by real actions of both.

Another sign of a positive partnership is healthy emotional boundaries: a person generously gives warmth without forgetting his own resource and does not expect his partner to constantly match his brightness or energy, allowing him to have his own pace and mood.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, a positive partnership manifests as a warm, sincere presence that does not need constant confirmation: she generously gives her partner attention and recognition, while allowing herself to show fatigue or doubt when they really are, instead of playing the role of an eternally positive companion.

She knows how to trust her own joy, without requiring her partner to constantly match her energy: instead of expecting the same enthusiasm every moment, she appreciates his different pace and leaves room for him to manifest in his own way.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, a positive partnership is manifested as the ability to sincerely inspire his partner, without demanding that she always support his ideas or pace: he knows how to share enthusiasm and at the same time respect when she needs a different rhythm or calmness.

He naturally knows how to share his own vulnerability, without fear of seeming "not strong enough": openness about his own doubts or fatigue in a relationship does not become for him a weakness, but a way to build a deeper, more honest closeness.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the partnership can develop in one of two opposite scenarios. The first is narcissism in a relationship: a person tries so hard to be a source of positivity and lightness for a partner that he hides his own fatigue, sadness or dissatisfaction, until the accumulated internal tension breaks through with an unexpected breakdown or silent withdrawal.

With this manifestation of the negative, the partner often does not even guess about the true state of a loved one, because he is used to seeing only his bright, supportive side, and it is this distance between the external image and the internal state that gradually undermines the sincerity of the relationship.

The second, opposite manifestation of the negative is the pride and burnout of the partner: a person unconsciously expects that the partner always corresponds to his mood, energy or pace, and reacts painfully when he needs silence, a different rhythm or simply does not share enthusiasm at a specific moment.

The third manifestation of the negative, closely related to the second, is the avoidance of difficult topics in order to maintain a light atmosphere in the couple: a person turns a difficult conversation into a joke or optimism, leaving the real problem unresolved, because an honest discussion threatens the usual image of a carefree, easy relationship.

Another manifestation of the negative is an excessive need for constant admiration from the partner: without realizing one's own value beyond recognition, a person feels anxious when a partner is less enthusiastic or attentive than usual, and may unconsciously compete for the role of the main source of warmth even in one's own family.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, narcissism in a relationship often looks like an exhausting internal demand to always maintain a warm atmosphere in a couple — she can hide her own fatigue or disappointment for years, fearing to spoil the image of a light, cheerful partner, until it breaks through with sudden exhaustion or distance. The opposite side is pride, when a woman unconsciously expects her partner to always adjust to her mood and feels devalued if he doesn't.

The tendency to burn out the partner in the negative manifests itself especially acutely in moments when she needs support: instead of a direct request, she expects that the partner will guess to match her energy, and feels disappointed or offended when this does not happen.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, the negative manifestation is often disguised as tireless optimism in the relationship - in fact, it is a way to hide his own fatigue or doubt in the relationship, which he considers inappropriate for the image of a strong, always positive partner. Instead of acknowledging the difficulties, he continues to maintain an easy atmosphere until the internal distance with his partner becomes tangible and difficult to overcome.

The opposite manifestation is pride, when a man gets used to being the main source of energy and ideas in a couple and begins to devalue the initiative or other pace of his partner, expecting that she will adapt to his vision of the relationship.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is the experience of a past partnership where generosity or sincerity was not appreciated or, on the contrary, was used against the person himself, which leaves a deep fear that any vulnerability in the relationship is deceptive and will sooner or later turn into disappointment.

The second trigger is the long-term need to "keep face" in front of the partner, hiding true feelings in order to maintain the image of a light, carefree person. This depletes this energy faster than any open conflict, because it breaks the very thing for which it builds intimacy - sincere, warm contact.

The third trigger is a partner who doesn't notice or appreciate the generosity, energy, and attention that a person sincerely puts into the relationship. The lack of recognition is perceived by this energy particularly acutely, and it is precisely where the warmth goes unnoticed that the risk of either exhaustion or a haughty need to prove one's worth increases.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The first step is to learn to admit your own tiredness or sadness to your partner openly, instead of hiding them behind the usual cheerfulness: sincerity strengthens intimacy more than an impeccable positive image.

The second step is to practice letting go of the expectation that your partner will always match your mood and energy: it's worth letting them have their own pace without taking it as personal rejection or indifference.

The third step is to consciously create a regular space for honest conversations as a couple, where you can talk about difficult things, without immediately turning everything into a joke or positive.

The fourth step is to learn to directly ask for support when you need it, instead of waiting for your partner to guess to respond to your energy with the same enthusiasm.

The fifth step is to practice generosity that takes into account one's own resource: before giving warmth or taking on the role of the main source of joy in a couple, it is worth honestly checking whether you have the inner strength for this right now.

## Main Task

The main task of this energy in a partnership is to learn to give warmth without exhausting yourself, and to have the courage to talk with your partner about difficult things as freely as sharing joy. When this task is accomplished, the partnership becomes a space of sincere, living warmth, where both feel that they are truly valued: it knows how to live through difficult periods without escaping into the recorded positivity, share vulnerability sincerely and leave each other space for different pace and mood, instead of staying in the cycle of self-absorption of one or haughty expectations of the other.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I allow myself to show my partner real tiredness or sadness, or do I continue to play the role of a person with whom it is always easy and fun?
- Do I expect my partner to constantly adapt to my mood and energy, or do I leave room for his different pace?
- Do I avoid difficult conversations with my partner in order to maintain a light atmosphere, even when honesty is more important than comfort?

## Practical Plan

1. Once a week, consciously share with your partner a real feeling or fatigue that you would have hidden behind cheerfulness.
2. Choose one moment this week to have an honest conversation about a difficult topic in a relationship that you would have liked to avoid with a joke or something positive.
3. Having noticed the expectation that the partner should match your mood, consciously let it go and let the partner be at his own pace.
4. Once a week, directly ask your partner for support or attention, instead of waiting for him to guess on his own.
5. After a moment of exhaustion from the role of "always a positive person", consciously stop and allow yourself an honest rest, informing your partner about it.
6. Once a month, honestly evaluate together with your partner whether the relationship is built on sincere, mutual warmth, or on the hidden exhaustion of one and the silent expectations of the other.
