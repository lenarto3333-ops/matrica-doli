# 13. Death — Natural Talents

## Short summary

The talent of the thirteenth arcana is the ability to bring about change where others are stuck in the old through fear or habit. This is not destruction for the sake of destruction, but a real gift to sense when a structure, process, or way of doing things has exhausted itself, and to decisively make room for a new one. This talent rarely looks soft or comfortable, but it is he who often saves where the old is already inhibiting development. In the Matrix of Destiny, this point marks a person's core talent — the most positive expression of energy in the matrix, a genuine gift from above, and it is through this very energy that a person feels a living connection with higher powers and receives their support.

## Natural force

The basis of this talent is the ability to distinguish what still has value from what continues to exist only by inertia. This rare combination of honesty of view and inner courage makes such people valuable precisely in moments of crisis and reform, when it is necessary to remove the superfluous to make room for the viable.

The second aspect of talent is a natural resistance to the pain of transition. Where others fear even the thought of ending familiarity, this energy knows how to push through the discomfort of change, keeping in mind an image of an outcome that is worth the transformation.

The third facet of this talent is the ability to inspire others to make necessary changes, showing that the end of the old is not a loss, but a release of a resource for a new stage.

## Manifestation in the plus

On the plus side, this talent manifests itself as the ability to initiate and carry out real transformations - in a project, a team, one's own life - where the outdated and ineffective have accumulated. A person naturally becomes the one who takes the unpopular but necessary decision to end.

Talent also manifests itself as the ability to make changes with respect for the people and processes they touch—resolutely but not violently, with explanation and care for the transition period, rather than a sudden cliffhanger without preparation.

Another sign of positive manifestation is the ability to quickly recover from losses or failures, perceiving them as part of the natural cycle of renewal, rather than as a personal catastrophe.

### Manifestation in women is positive

In a woman, this talent is often manifested as the ability to delicately but decisively complete what has passed away - in relationships, work, everyday life - creating a smooth transition to the new instead of a sharp upheaval for herself and loved ones.

### Manifestation in the husband is positive

In a man, this talent often manifests itself as a determination to take the unpopular but necessary step of reform - to abandon an outdated approach, structure or agreement - even if it causes resistance from those around him.

## Manifestation in the minus

In the negative, this talent can turn into destructive sharpness - a person breaks ties, gets rid of people or projects so sharply that it causes unnecessary pain where it was possible to act more gently. Transformation does not turn into renewal, but into chaos.

Another manifestation of negativity is the opposite extreme: paralysis before the need for change, when a person clings to an outdated job, relationship, or habit for years, fearing the unknown more than the harm of inaction.

Another negative feature is a cynical attitude to losses, when a person is so used to endings that he stops appreciating what is still alive and destroys too soon, out of inner anxiety, and not out of real need.

## What can be negative

More often than not, this talent is blocked in an environment that punishes any change and demands constancy at any cost - then the energy of transformation is either suppressed or bursts through with a sharp disruption. The second trigger is a personal experience of a painful, unprepared loss in the past, which has taught us to either avoid endings or cut things off in advance so as not to experience the pain again.

## How to return energy to the plus side

It is useful to consciously train the skill of "honest diagnosis" — to regularly ask yourself what is no longer working in current affairs and needs to be completed, without waiting for a crisis. It's also important to learn to make changes in stages, with explanation and support for those affected, rather than abrupt changes. Another step is to practice letting go of small things: things, habits, agreements, in order to remain flexible in the face of larger transformations.

## The main task

The main task is to use the ability to see the old and let it go for real renewal, not for destruction for destruction's sake or, conversely, to justify inaction.

## Questions for self-reflection

- What in my life or work has clearly exhausted itself, but I still hold on to it because of fear or habit?
- When was the last time I ended something abruptly, although I could have done it more gently and with more respect for others?
- What do I lack more — the courage to complete or the patience to go through the transition gradually?

## Practical plan

1. Make a list of three things in your work or personal life that are clearly out of date, and define a specific completion step for each.
2. Make one planned, prepared change — a conversation, a decision, a refusal — instead of waiting for the situation to force you to act abruptly.
3. Notice one situation this week where you should be more gentle with people during the inevitable change, and act accordingly.
