# 15. The Devil — Natural Talents

## Brief Essence

The talent of the fifteenth arcana is natural magnetism and the ability to see the essence of things and people through any facade. It is a gift that attracts attention, trust and resources without apparent effort, as well as a keen sense of what a person really wants - even if he himself is not yet aware of it. This talent makes such a person a natural master of influence, persuasion and creation of material wealth, and that is why the greatest skill here is to use this power honestly.

## Natural Strength

The basis of this talent is penetrating psychological vision: the ability to understand the true motives, fears and desires of a person in a few minutes of communication, which he carefully hides from others and even from himself. This is a rare skill, invaluable in negotiations, sales, psychology or any work with people.

The second aspect of talent is a powerful personal charisma that attracts the attention of the audience, clients or partners naturally, without excessive efforts of persuasion. People trust this energy almost instinctively, feeling confidence and strength in it.

The third facet of talent is a practical sense of money, resources, and opportunities: the ability to see where value is hidden and turn a desire or need into concrete material benefit for oneself and others.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this talent is clearly revealed in sales, negotiations, marketing, psychological counseling or any role that requires an understanding of human nature and the ability to influence honestly - a person achieves results not by pressure, but by accurately hitting the real need of the interlocutor.

Talent is also manifested in entrepreneurship and financial management: a natural sense of profitable opportunities and the ability to attract resources turn into stable wealth, which a person knows how to multiply and use wisely, and not just accumulate for the sake of accumulation.

Another sign of a positive manifestation is the ability to be a mentor or a guide for others in matters of confidence, attractiveness and personal strength: a person sincerely learns not to be afraid of his own magnetism, but to manage it consciously.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, this talent is often manifested as a bright, attractive presence, combined with a subtle understanding of people - she knows how to be persuasive, while remaining sincere and not turning into a game or manipulation.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, this talent often manifests itself as a sure, practical grasp in matters of profit or influence—the ability to see a profitable opportunity before others and to pursue it decisively, while being fair to those with whom he deals.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, this talent turns into manipulativeness - a person uses knowledge of other people's weaknesses and his own attractiveness not for honest influence, but in order to get what he wants by bypassing the will of another, playing on his fears or desires.

Another manifestation of the negative is dependence on the same influence and benefits that talent can bring: excitement, the pursuit of money or power gradually conquers the person himself, turning the gift of freedom into a new form of slavery.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

More often than not, this talent is distorted in an environment where the end justifies any means and honesty is considered a weakness - then natural insight turns into a tool of manipulation. The second trigger is a persistent lack of self-worth, where a person tries to compensate for the inner emptiness with external success, status, or control over others.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to regularly check one's own motives in influencing people: does this influence lead to an honest, mutually beneficial agreement, or to the hidden subjugation of someone else's will to one's own. It is also important to consciously monitor whether the desire for profit or influence does not turn into a new addiction that is difficult to give up even when it is harmful.

## Main Task

The main task is to use insight, magnetism and a sense of wealth as tools of honest influence and real wealth, not as means of covert control over others or over yourself.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I use people's understanding to help them or to gain an advantage over them?
- What controls me now more strongly than my own will - money, recognition, habit or a person?
- Where in my work or business can honesty and profit go hand in hand, and not one at the expense of the other?

## Practical Plan

1. To analyze one recent situation of influencing someone and honestly determine whether it was an honest deal or a covert manipulation.
2. Apply the talent of seeing the essence of things to really help someone this week, without hidden benefit for yourself.
3. To notice one habit or need that drives my decisions more than I would like, and take a step toward greater freedom from it.
