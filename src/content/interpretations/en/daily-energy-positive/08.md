# 8. Justice — Daily Energy in the Positive

## In Brief

The day supports honest conversations, fair agreements, and decisions that call for objectivity rather than emotion. Today it is easier to see a situation in balance, acknowledge your share of responsibility, and restore equilibrium where it has been disrupted.

## Natural Strength

The day's strength is a clear inner compass for truth and balance. It is easy to distinguish genuine injustice from your own exaggeration. Negotiating terms and debts, correcting an old mistake, and having conversations that require honesty without blame are all favored.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, calm objectivity emerges: you see both sides of the situation without rushing to judgment. Honesty sounds like fact rather than a weapon, making it easier for people to hear you even when the subject is uncomfortable. Agreements work well when every party clearly understands their part.

## What to Support Today

Settle an old debt—financial or emotional—honor a promise that has long awaited fulfillment, or apologize where you were genuinely wrong. This is also a good day for decisions that require careful consideration: reviewing a contract, dividing responsibilities, or offering honest feedback.

## What to Avoid Today

Do not turn a fair intention into moralizing or a public trial of someone else's behavior. Categorical judgments about who is “right” and who is “wrong,” made without trying to hear the other side, can quickly undo the day's advantages.

## The Day's Main Message

The truest justice today is the kind that begins with honesty toward yourself. Once you see your own part in a situation, everything else becomes easier to see clearly as well.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What debt—financial, verbal, or emotional—is worth settling today?
- Where should I acknowledge my share of responsibility before speaking about someone else's?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one unfinished agreement and take a step toward honoring it.
2. Have one conversation in which honesty matters more than comfortable silence.
3. In the evening, weigh the day in your mind without judging yourself or others.
