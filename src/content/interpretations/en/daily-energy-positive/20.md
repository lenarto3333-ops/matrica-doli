# 20. Judgement — Daily Energy in the Positive

## Brief Essence

Today is a favorable day for an honest look at one's own path without being overly harsh — right now it's easier to see what conclusions from the past are already ready and take a step into something new. This is a day of clarity, inner truth and a feeling that you can finally let go of what has long been outlived.

## Natural Strength

Today, the gift of honestly evaluating one's own actions and decisions, hearing an inner call for renewal, and feeling the support of one's family and past experiences is especially tangible. This power gives clarity where there were doubts before and allows you to trust your own intuition.

## Expression in the Positive

It is a good moment for honest summaries, forgiving oneself for past mistakes and decisive steps towards a new stage that has long been ripe. It is easier to have conversations with the family, in which you can put an end to the old misunderstanding, as well as decisions based on inner values, and not on fear.

## What to Support Today

It is worth supporting any desire to honestly sum up - in relationships, affairs or one's own attitude towards oneself - and draw one calm conclusion from it. Trusting intuition also works well: if a clear feeling "it's time to move on" appears inside, you should trust it today.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid being too harsh on yourself for past decisions, as if one honest conclusion is not enough and you have to scold yourself again. It is also necessary to avoid trying to repeat or prove something from the ancient past - today a calm movement forward is more valuable than repeated proof of one's own rightness.

## Main Message of the Day

Today, your strength lies in the ability to honestly look at the path you have traveled and allow yourself to renew without guilt. Consciously used, this energy opens a true rebirth after everything that has already been experienced.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What honest conclusion from the past am I already ready to draw and let go of?
- Do I trust the inner sense that it's "time to move on" today, even if it's frightening?

## Practical Plan

1. Draw an honest conclusion about one situation or relationship and allow yourself one calm takeaway.
2. If the opportunity arises, have a conversation with family that puts a close on an old misunderstanding.
3. Take one step toward a new stage that rests on inner values rather than fear.
