# 13. Death — Daily Energy in the Positive

## Short summary

Today is a favorable day to consciously finish what has already exhausted itself and make room for something new. This is a day of bold decisions to let go - not destructive, but cleansing, after which it becomes easier to breathe.

## Natural force

Today it is easier to see clearly what is no longer serving you and find the determination to let it go without too much drama. It's a good time to finish projects, conversations, habits or relationships that have long needed a sharp end.

## Manifestation in the plus

It's a good moment to take a decisive step where you haven't had enough courage for a long time - to leave an old role, close a topic that has exhausted itself, start something new in the vacated place. Changes today are felt not as a loss, but as a relief.

## What to support today

It is worth supporting any honest decision about completion, rituals of saying goodbye to the old, clearing space - physical or internal. Anything that helps symbolically close one stage before starting another works well.

## What to avoid today

It is better to avoid excessive abruptness at the moment of completion - even the right decision should be made with respect for what is being completed. It is also worth avoiding postponing the obvious end just because of the fear of the unknown that comes after it.

## The main message of the day

Today, letting go is not loss, but making room for something new. Allow yourself to complete what is asking for completion with dignity, not resistance.

## Questions for self-reflection

- What exactly is ready to be completed today, and do I have the courage to admit it?
- How can I let go of the old with respect and not with harshness?

## Practical plan

1. Identify one thing, relationship, or habit that has been draining you for a long time, and take a concrete step toward ending it today.
2. Conduct a small ritual of farewell to the old stage - clean up, throw away the excess, symbolically close the topic.
3. Write down what new sense of freedom appears after something old has been let go.
