# 16. The Tower — Daily Energy in the Positive

## Brief Essence

Today is an auspicious day to resolutely destroy what has long since exhausted itself and take a step towards something new, more honest. This is the day of a breakthrough — a sudden clarification or a bold decision that opens the way, instead of continuing to hold on to the usual.

## Natural Strength

Today, inner strength is especially acutely manifested - the ability to withstand a shock and not break, but even become stronger after it. This strength gives courage to look the truth in the eye where before you wanted to close your eyes and endure.

## Expression in the Positive

A good moment to finally finish something that has long exhausted itself - a project, a habit, a situation - and feel the relief of this step. It's easier to find the courage to have an honest, even harsh, conversation that clears the way instead of continuing to keep the tension under the surface.

## What to Support Today

It is worth supporting any decision that requires determination and willingness to let go of the old form for the sake of a new one. Physical release of accumulated tension also works well - movement, activity, anything that helps the energy of the shock to find a healthy outlet.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid the temptation to direct today's intensity into conflict or harshness where a firm but calm word can be dispensed with. It is also worth avoiding excessive dramatization of small difficulties - not every change today has to turn into a crisis.

## Main Message of the Day

Today, your strength lies in the ability to withstand an earthquake and build something stronger in its place. Consciously used, this breakthrough energy releases the stale faster and more honestly than any gradual procrastination.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What have I finally found the courage to finish or tear down today, because it had long outlived its purpose?
- Where did I choose an honest, even blunt conversation instead of continuing to hold tension beneath the surface?

## Practical Plan

1. Finish one thing today — a task, a habit, a situation — that has long outlived its purpose, and notice the sense of relief.
2. Give accumulated tension a healthy physical outlet — movement, activity, anything that lets the energy of the shock discharge.
3. If an honest conversation has been building, have it today, calmly but firmly, instead of putting it off.
