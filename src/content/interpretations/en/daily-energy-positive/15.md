# 15. The Devil — Daily Energy in the Positive

## Brief Essence

Today is a favorable day for using your own attractiveness and ability to see the essence of things — to convince, negotiate, attract attention and resources with natural magnetism, not force. This is the day when charisma works for you if you act honestly and openly.

## Natural Strength

Today, the ability to feel what people around you really want and to see hidden motives behind words and actions is especially acute. This insight gives an advantage in negotiations, sales, any business where it is important to understand the true desires of another person.

## Expression in the Positive

A good time to attract attention to a project, an idea or your own person in a natural way - through genuine interest and presence, not pressure. It is easier to agree on favorable terms, feel the mood of the audience or partner and say exactly what really resonates.

## What to Support Today

It is worth supporting any activity related to persuasion, presentation of oneself or one's work, financial arrangements. A genuine interest in people and a willingness to openly admit one's desires instead of hiding them works well.

## What to Avoid Today

It is best to avoid the temptation to use today's attractiveness to manipulate or pressure someone for a quick result. It is also important to avoid focusing too much on material gain alone—it narrows the very depth of vision that is today's greatest strength.

## Main Message of the Day

Today, your strength lies in genuine magnetism and the ability to see through people and situations. Used honestly, this power attracts what is desired by itself, without the need to control or deceive anyone.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Where today can I use my attractiveness honestly, and not to put pressure on someone?
- What do I really see in a person or a situation, looking beyond the superficial words?

## Practical Plan

1. Have one important conversation or negotiation, relying on sincere interest in the interlocutor, and not on pressure.
2. Notice one situation where you can see a hidden desire of a person, and take this into account in communication with him.
3. Allow yourself to openly admit one of your own desires instead of masking it.
