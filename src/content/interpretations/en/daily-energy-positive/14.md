# 14. Temperance — Daily Energy in the Positive

## Brief Essence

Today is a favorable day for finding harmony where there has been tension for a long time - to reconcile the conflict, to combine the incompatible, to do step by step what previously seemed impossible at once. This is a day of natural diplomacy, soft power and a creative combination of opposites.

## Natural Strength

Today it is easier to see that two sides of a conflict or two desires are not really mutually exclusive, but can complement each other if approached calmly and gradually. It's a good time for reconciliation, creative solutions, and bringing some beauty and order to a chaotic situation.

## Expression in the Positive

It is a good time to act as a mediator in someone else's dispute or to resolve your own internal conflict by finding a solution that suits all parties. Everything that is done today gradually, step by step, with attention to detail, has a chance to turn out to be particularly harmonious and beautiful.

## What to Support Today

It is worth supporting any attempt at reconciliation, creative work that combines different styles or ideas, as well as a slow, step-by-step approach to things instead of rushing. Everything that brings a sense of elegance and order works well - from organizing the space to a delicate conversation.

## What to Avoid Today

It is better to avoid sharp, categorical statements and decisions made "all or nothing" - today the strength lies precisely in gradualness and softness. It is also worth avoiding excessive compromise where a clear position is actually needed - harmony does not mean a complete rejection of one's own opinion.

## Main Message of the Day

Today, your strength lies in the ability to combine opposites and bring calmness to stressful situations. Trust in gradualness: what is done step by step is better today than what is done suddenly.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Where today can I build a bridge between two seemingly opposite sides?
- What of the plans should be done gradually, instead of trying to complete everything at once?

## Practical Plan

1. Find one stressful situation—yours or someone else's—and propose a solution that takes both sides into account.
2. Spend time on a task that requires patience and gradualness, instead of trying to complete it in one fell swoop.
3. Add a little beauty or order to the space or relationships around you today - even a small gesture makes a difference.
