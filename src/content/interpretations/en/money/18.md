# 18. The Moon — Money and Financial Fulfillment

## Brief Essence

The financial history of this energy is connected with a subtle intuition and a rich imagination: money willingly comes to the place where a person knows how to feel the non-obvious - the mood of the market even before it manifests itself in numbers, the hidden need of the client, the subtext of negotiations that no one spoke aloud. The source of well-being here is not a cold calculation, but the ability to consciously direct the imagination to the desired result, clearly imagine a specific financial goal and then consistently, step by step, translate this image into reality.

The weakness manifests itself in two opposite directions. The first is that financial anxiety turns into gloomy, catastrophic scenarios: a person imagines the worst possible development of events so vividly that he is afraid of any financial step, hides money "just in case" or avoids looking at the real state of his accounts altogether. The second is an escape into an illusion: a person dreams of sudden financial success, imagines wealth in detail, but replaces this idea with real actions, and the dream remains just a pleasant picture in the head.

## Natural Strength

The main resource of this energy is intuition, capable of catching financially important information even before it becomes obvious to others: a change in the mood of the client, the riskiness of the deal, the moment when it is worth waiting and when to act without hesitation. This sensitivity to the unmanifest often saves from loss where rational analysis has not yet seen the danger.

The second power is a developed imagination as a creative tool: a person is able to imagine the desired financial result in detail and vividly, and it is this internal picture that becomes a reference point that directs daily decisions and actions in the right direction, gradually turning the imaginary into reality.

The third power is sensitivity to the atmosphere and subtext in business communication: a person reads the mood of negotiations, the hidden doubts of a partner or the true motives of a proposal, which gives him an advantage where others focus only on words and formal numbers.

## Expression in the Positive

In addition, imagination works as a conscious tool for materialization: a person does not just dream about money, but regularly and concretely visualizes the desired result, after which he reinforces this image with real, consistent actions - and it is the combination of vivid imagination with discipline that turns the idea into a stable income.

The second plus sign is work in which sensitivity to the non-obvious is valued: consulting, psychology, coaching, creating an atmosphere (space design, hospitality, branding), analyzing market sentiments, creative professions that work with symbols and subconscious images - a person earns precisely because he knows how to feel what others cannot see.

The third sign is accurate financial intuition: a person feels in time when a deal is not fair, when a partner should not be trusted, when it is worth leaving a risky project even before the appearance of clear alarm signals, and this ability saves his capital where a purely rational approach is too late.

The fourth plus sign is emotional stability in financial matters: a person accepts his own anxieties and doubts about money, not suppressing them and not denying them, but living calmly, thanks to which he makes financial decisions soberly, and not under the influence of momentary fear or euphoria.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

A woman in a plus knows how to trust her own financial sense even when it contradicts the "logical" advice of those around her: she feels which offer is really safe and which only seems profitable, and is not afraid to act according to this inner knowledge, supporting it with real fact-checking.

She consciously works with imagination as a resource: she regularly and in detail imagines the desired level of income or a specific financial goal, and this practice, combined with concrete steps, becomes a natural way for her to gradually change financial reality.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

A man in the plus knows how to combine business intuition with sober analysis: he trusts the feeling when something is "not right" in a deal or partnership, but always checks this feeling with facts before making a final decision, and it is this combination that saves him from expensive mistakes.

He is able to keep a long-term financial goal in mind even when the current situation is difficult, and uses this internal image as a compass that helps him stay on course amid daily mood swings or circumstances.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, financial anxiety develops into obsessive, catastrophic scenarios: a person imagines bankruptcy, job loss, or financial catastrophe so vividly and often that these images begin to be perceived almost as a fact, and not as one of the possible, often unlikely options for the development of events - and this fear paralyzes any financial decisions.

This same extreme often manifests itself as an avoidance of reality: a person is afraid to open an account statement, count debts or make a real budget, because the anxiety of facing the numbers seems more unbearable than the uncertainty itself, and because of this, the financial situation imperceptibly worsens.

The opposite extreme is flight into an illusion: a person vividly imagines wealth, "says" the desired income or lifestyle, but replaces these ideas with real actions, believing that the desire or the right mood will sooner or later bring money without specific, sometimes boring and consistent steps.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The strongest trigger is financial uncertainty or instability, which activates the tendency to think catastrophically and causes the imagination to paint worst-case scenarios instead of realistic ones. The second trigger is the experience of financial deception or betrayal of trust in the past, which creates a chronic suspicion of partners and clients even where there is no real basis for distrust. The third trigger is a prolonged period when the actual financial steps seem too difficult or exhausting, prompting an escape into a pleasant but idle dream of easy and quick success.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The first step is to learn to distinguish between real financial intuition and anxious fantasy: intuition is usually calm and concrete, while an anxious scenario is emotionally charged, repetitive, and usually exaggerated. It's helpful to write down your financial fears and back each one up with real facts before making a decision influenced by it.

The second step, if you recognize a paralyzing fear in yourself, is to gradually, in small steps, regain contact with real numbers: regularly, even weekly, calmly review the state of accounts and debts, not letting anxiety dictate the complete avoidance of this topic. The third step, if you recognize yourself as escaping into an illusion, is to write down at least one specific step that will bring you closer to it this week after each practice of visualizing the desired financial result.

It is also helpful to regularly practice accepting your own financial emotions—anxiety, shame, envy—without trying to suppress them or rationally “undo” them: it is the acceptance, not the denial, of emotions that gradually reduces their power over financial decisions. ## Main Task

The main financial task of this energy is to learn to use imagination and intuition as a tool of conscious creation, and not as a source of disturbing illusions: to trust the inner sense where it is based on real experience, and at the same time to reinforce each bright financial image with concrete, consistent actions. The real financial realization of this arcana comes when the imaginary and the real cease to be opposites and begin to work together.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I imagine catastrophic financial scenarios more often than is justified by real facts?
- Am I not replacing the dream of money with real actions, believing that the right attitude will bring results by itself?
- How much do I trust my own financial intuition and do I test this intuition with facts before acting?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down the three financial fears that pop up in your mind most often, and back each one up with real-world facts and figures.
2. Once a week, calmly review the state of your accounts and debts, even if it causes anxiety.
3. Choose one financial goal and imagine it in detail for a few minutes every day for a week, and then immediately take one concrete step towards it.
4. If you notice suspicion towards a partner or client, check whether it is based on real facts or on past experience transferred to the current situation.
5. Practice accepting financial anxiety without suppression: name the emotion out loud or on paper, let it be, without trying to immediately "fix" it.
6. Once a month, compare your financial dreams with concrete actions taken during this time, adjusting the balance between imagination and real steps.
