# 22. The Fool — Birthday, Visible Character, and First Talent

## Brief Essence

The twenty-second arcana at the birthday point describes a person who from birth carries a rare lightness of being in himself - he does not cling convulsively to the old, knows how to start with a clean slate where others are stuck in doubt, and goes towards the unknown with curiosity rather than fear. Since childhood, this person is often felt by the environment as a breath of fresh air: he is sincere without calculation, spontaneous without malicious intent, and it is this open, unprotected naturalness that often gives permission to others to relax a little and stop playing a role. The main talent of this arcana is a deep, almost instinctive trust in the flow of life: a person does not try to control every step forward, he knows how to let go of excessive caution and move where experience leads, believing that the road will turn out right even when there is no turning point ahead. The challenge of this energy is twofold: on the one hand, lightness, taken to the extreme, turns into irresponsibility and adventurism - a person takes on new things and immediately quits, promises and does not fulfill, runs away from the consequences of his own decisions, leaving behind a chaos that others have to clean up; on the other hand, the same energy of freedom can flow in the opposite direction, turning into restrictions: a person, out of fear of the unknown, locks himself in rigid frameworks, is afraid of the slightest risk, and himself gives up the most valuable thing he had from birth - his own freedom. The true maturity of the twenty-second arcana is to learn to trust fate and sincerely accept new things, while being responsible for the consequences of one's decisions, combining freedom with inner support, not with flight or fear.

## Natural Strength

The main feature of this energy is natural ease of ascent: a person with the twenty-second arcana on their birthday rarely thinks long before taking a step into a new business, a new city or a new acquaintance, they almost physically feel relief from moving forward, and that is why the environment often turns to them when fresh energy is needed to move a stagnant matter from its place.

From this lightness grows the rare gift of starting with a clean slate: such a person does not carry with him the burden of past failures or resentment, he knows how to leave behind what no longer works and move on without the internal burden that often prevents others from moving.

Another natural strength is sincere, unguarded openness: a person with this arcana says what he thinks and feels as he feels without ulterior motives or playing to the public, and it is this raw sincerity that often wins people over even when the words seem too direct.

The basic motivation of this energy is the desire for true freedom: this person feels a deep, almost physical satisfaction from the absence of rigid frameworks, schedules and obligations that constrain movement, and it is the feeling of free space that gives him the feeling that life has meaning.

The natural talent of this point is trust in fate: a person with this energy knows how to let go of control where others try to calculate everything in advance, and it is this willingness to trust the unknown that often opens doors for them that would be closed to those who are afraid to take a step into the void.

## Expression in the Positive

When this energy is revealed, the ease of ascent works as a real gift to quickly adapt to new circumstances: a person does not waste energy resisting change, he knows how to master in a short time where others are stuck for months, and it is thanks to this flexibility that his life rarely seems stuck or monotonous.

In everyday life, a positive manifestation makes a person a natural catalyst of freshness: it brings to any environment the feeling that you can start anew, try something different, let go of an old habit, and it inspires others to allow themselves a little more spontaneity.

In a relationship with the new and unfamiliar, a positive manifestation is a sincere curiosity without fear: a person easily tries new things, new directions, new acquaintances, and it is this openness that allows him to accumulate an extremely diverse, rich experience.

The natural trust in the flow of life, which is a feature of this arcana, becomes a real support in difficult times: a person does not panic when the plan collapses, he believes that a way will be found, and it is this faith that often really leads him to an unexpectedly successful decision where a logical calculation would lead to a dead end.

Another sign of a positive state is a healthy attitude to responsibility: a person knows how to combine freedom of action with an honest willingness to be responsible for the consequences of his own choices, he does not run away from what he himself created, but follows this path to the end, while maintaining lightness and not turning responsibility into a heavy burden.

Finally, the positive manifestation of this energy is noticeable in the sincere openness of character: a person does not play roles, does not hide feelings for the sake of profit, and it is thanks to this directness that others feel that it is possible to be real with them, and not play by the rules of the social game.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, this positive energy often manifests itself as a direct, warm sincerity that attracts people: she says what she feels without mannerism or hidden calculations, and that is why those around her trust her even in delicate matters.

Such women usually have a rare ability to easily start new stages of life: they perceive moving, a new job, a new relationship not as a threat to stability, but as a natural part of a lively, mobile life, and it is this flexibility that protects them from stagnation.

Another trait is a healthy trust in fate: such a woman knows how to let go of excessive control and believe that she does not have to keep everything in her hands, and it is this trust that often brings people and opportunities into her life that she would never have found if she had tried to plan everything in advance.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, a positive manifestation of this energy often looks like the ability to easily take risks for the sake of new experiences: he is not afraid to start a business, the outcome of which is unpredictable, and it is this willingness to act without guarantees that often leads him to discoveries that are inaccessible to those who wait for complete certainty.

Such men usually have a talent for charging their surroundings with fresh energy of movement: they offer new ideas, new directions, new ways of doing familiar things, and it is thanks to this ease that the team or family around them rarely gets stuck in a routine.

In his attitude to responsibility, a man with this positive energy shows a mature balance: he enjoys freedom of action, but does not run away from the consequences of his own decisions, but calmly and honestly takes responsibility for them, combining lightness of character with reliability of actions.

## Expression in the Negative

When this energy doesn't find a healthy outlet, it manifests in one of two opposite but equally exhausting ways. The first is irresponsibility and adventurism: the very lightness that should give freedom turns into a habit of taking on new things and immediately abandoning them, making promises and not fulfilling them, running away from the consequences of one's own decisions, leaving behind chaos, disappointed people and unfinished business.

With this manifestation, a person confuses freedom with the absence of any obligations: he easily enters relationships, projects or agreements and just as easily disappears from them, as soon as the first difficulties appear or serious responsibility is needed, and not just a pleasant beginning.

The second, opposite manifestation of the negative is limitation: instead of natural lightness and trust in fate, a person begins to fear any risk, clings to a familiar, safe routine and perceives the slightest change as a threat, although in fact freedom is his natural state of balance.

With this manifestation, a person builds a cage for himself from rules, schedules and "this is not possible", refusing new experiences, travels or acquaintances that could revive him, and gradually loses the same light, sparkling character that was once his greatest gift.

The third manifestation of the negative, closely related to the first, is the avoidance of responsibility for the consequences: the unmanifested energy of freedom sometimes turns into a habit of blaming circumstances or other people for one's own rash decisions, instead of honestly admitting one's role in what happened.

Another typical feature of the minus is superficiality where depth is needed: a person is so used to easily jump from one thing to another that he avoids everything that requires long-term, consistent attention, and precisely because of this, many valuable things in his life remain underdeveloped.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, irresponsibility often looks like a series of bright, but unfinished beginnings - a new business, a new hobby, a new relationship captures her completely, and then just as suddenly loses its appeal, as soon as she has to make constant efforts. The opposite side is a sudden closure to the new: a woman can become overly cautious, afraid of any risk, and gradually narrow her own life to a narrow, safe circle of familiar affairs.

The tendency to adventurism in the negative manifests itself especially acutely in matters of promises and agreements: a woman can easily promise something in a fit of inspiration, and then just as easily forget or abandon her word when the initial enthusiasm passes.

In a relationship with her own freedom, the minus can manifest as a chronic fear of being "bound": she avoids serious obligations not because she really does not want it, but because of a deep fear of losing the lightness that she considers the only guarantee of her own integrity.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, the negative manifestation is often disguised as "just such a character" or "I am a free person" - in fact, it is a way to avoid serious obligations that require constancy. Instead of accepting responsibility for the consequences of his own decision, he finds an explanation why the situation was not his fault and easily moves on, leaving behind unanswered questions.

The opposite manifestation is a sudden, sometimes categorical caution: a man with this minus can become too prudent where he would have acted boldly before, fearing any risk and clinging to a proven, safe path even when intuition tells him to try something new.

In relation to a new experience, a man with this minus is inclined to grab at many directions at once, dispersing his forces and not bringing any of them to a real result, or, on the contrary, to completely close himself off from any changes, fearing that he will lose control over his own life.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The most common trigger is a situation that requires long-term, consistent responsibility just when a natural impulse pulls to something new: a routine duty, a long-term project, an obligation that has already lost its original appeal. It is at the moment when you have to stay and see things through that it is easiest to jump either into a sudden flight from responsibility or into a paralyzing fear of any next step.

The second trigger is rigid frameworks, schedules or rules imposed from the outside: when natural ease is suddenly squeezed by external control, this energy feels an almost physical resistance, which can result either in open rebellion and irresponsible flight, or, conversely, in submissive but exhausting self-denial.

The third trigger is the experience when a spontaneous decision led to real, painful consequences: it is then easy to make the false conclusion that any risk is dangerous and start building walls of limitations around yourself instead of simply learning to be more careful in your choices.

The fourth trigger is prolonged monotony, in which there is no room for new experiences: when a person lives in the same rhythm for years without any freshness, the accumulated thirst for freedom sooner or later breaks through a sharp, chaotic breakdown into adventurism.

Finally, the negative is the need to take on serious, long-term responsibility, when the inner fear of losing lightness pushes in the direction of running away or, on the contrary, limiting oneself unnecessarily - precisely where a mature balance between freedom and duty is needed, this energy experiences the greatest internal resistance.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The first step is to learn to distinguish true freedom from escape: you should honestly ask yourself whether the desire to quit or change something comes from a real feeling of exhaustion, or from the habit of running away as soon as the need for constant effort appears.

The second step is to consciously train the skill of completing even small tasks: small, regular practices of completing what has already been started help to strengthen internal resistance without losing natural ease.

The third step is to learn to take responsibility for the consequences of one's decisions without self-flagellation: it is useful to regularly honestly admit one's role in what happened, and at the same time not turn this recognition into an excuse for harsh self-condemnation.

The fourth step is to practice informed risk, not panic: when there is a desire to try something new, you should ask yourself a simple question - what exactly is attractive about this step, and am I ready to take responsibility for its consequences.

The fifth step is to regularly check whether caution has turned into a hidden cage: if you notice that fear drives decisions more often than curiosity, you should consciously allow yourself a small, safe step in the direction of the new.

The sixth step is to consciously choose activities and relationships in which you can combine freedom with real stability: a path that has both ease and depth is better than constantly jumping from place to place or, conversely, stuck in a safe but suffocating routine.

## Main Task

The main task of this energy at the point of birth is to learn to trust the flow of life and wholeheartedly accept new experiences, without falling either into irresponsible escape from the consequences of one's own decisions, or into fear that locks in a narrow, safe framework. This involves the development of inner maturity: the ability to keep one's word and bring important things to the end, and to keep one's natural lightness alive, and not turn it into a constant escape from everything that requires stability.

When this maturity is formed, the need to either chaotically rush from one new task to another, leaving behind a mess, or to close in a narrow, safe circle out of fear of life, disappears. The desire for freedom ceases to be an excuse for irresponsibility and becomes a real gift - the ability to live easily, trust fate and meet each new day with an open heart, while being responsible for the path that a person chooses himself.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I bring important matters to the end, or am I used to abandoning them as soon as the initial enthusiasm disappears?
- Do I remain open to new experiences, or do I increasingly hide behind familiar, safe frameworks out of fear of the unknown?
- Am I honestly responsible for the consequences of my own decisions, or do I tend to look for the cause of failure in circumstances or other people?

## Practical Plan

1. During the week, for one started but unfinished matter, determine one specific step to its completion and do it, despite the desire to switch to something new.
2. Once this week, honestly admit to yourself or another person your own role in a situation that turned out to be unsuccessful, without shifting responsibility to the circumstances.
3. Having noticed the desire to avoid risk due to fear, consciously take one small, safe step towards a new experience.
4. At the end of the week, set aside twenty minutes to honestly evaluate where freedom worked for the benefit of life, and where it turned into an escape from what is important.
5. To try something new and unfamiliar once - a new business, place or acquaintance - and record what exactly you felt: ease or hidden fear.
6. Once a week, review the list of your own promises and agreements, consciously choosing those that should be completed before starting new exciting ideas.
