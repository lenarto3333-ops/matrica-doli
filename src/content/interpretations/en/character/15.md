# 15. The Devil — Birthday, Visible Character, and First Talent

## Brief Essence

The fifteenth arcana at the birthday point describes a person with a rare personal magnetism who, from childhood, attracts attention without making any visible effort. She knows how to see through people and situations—she reads true desires, fears, and motives beneath polite words and official masks—and it is this deep understanding of the essence of things that makes her a natural player in the world of influence, temptation, and material success. The first talent of this energy is the ability to attract resources, attention and people, understanding what they really want. The main challenge is not to allow this power to turn into a dependence on one's own desires or a tool of manipulation: the real maturity of this arcana is when magnetism serves honest influence and real wealth, and does not become a chain that controls the person himself.

## Natural Strength

The main feature of this energy is personal magnetism, which is felt physically, even before the first word. A person with a fifteenth arcana on birthdays attracts attention in the room, arouses curiosity and a desire to get closer, even when he is not trying to make an impression. This charisma is not a learned skill, but a natural quality that manifests itself in childhood.

From this magnetism grows a rare insight: the ability to see what is hidden under the surface - the real motives of a person according to his words, the real price of a thing according to its price tag, the true state of affairs according to the official version. This energy is rarely deceived by the external facade, because it instinctively looks for what is hidden under it.

Another natural force is a keen sense of human desires and weaknesses. A person with this arcana intuitively understands what drives other people - ambitions, fears, temptations - and this understanding, if not used against others, becomes the basis of deep empathy and the ability to negotiate.

The basic motivation of this energy is possession: the desire to have influence, resources, confidence in one's own power to attract the desired. It's not always about money literally - often it's about power over the circumstances of your own life, about the feeling that you can get what you want, and not depend on someone else's will.

The natural talent of this point is the ability to see value where others do not notice it, and the ability to materialize what is conceived. This energy is rarely poor by choice: it knows how to turn an idea, attraction or insight into a real, tangible result.

## Expression in the Positive

When this energy is revealed, magnetism works as a sincere, attractive influence: people are drawn to this person not because he controls them, but because there is a sense of strength, confidence and living energy around him. She inspires by example, not by pressure.

In work and material affairs, positive manifestation gives a rare talent to see the real value - of a project, a person, an opportunity - where others see only the surface. This makes such a person a natural entrepreneur, investor or strategist, able to build real wealth through understanding, not through delusion.

In relationships with people, a positive manifestation is a deep insight without manipulation: a person understands what the interlocutor wants, and uses this understanding to find a solution that is beneficial for both, and not to subjugate someone else's will to his own.

The power of desire, which is a natural feature of this arcana, turns into a powerful personal discipline: a person knows how to want something really badly and go for it, without losing control over himself in the process.

Another sign of a positive state is freedom from addictions in the broadest sense: a person enjoys the pleasures of life - success, attention, comfort - without becoming a slave to them, maintaining the ability to say "enough" at any moment and remain himself.

A positive expression is also the ability to distinguish true influence from control through fear or guilt. A person understands: power that rests only on the dependence of others on it is fragile and dishonest, while real authority is built on the value that it actually provides.

Finally, the positive manifestation of this energy is often visible in the ability to transform temptation into creativity: passion, excitement, ambition are directed not at destruction, but at creation - business, art, influential project - that brings real benefit to the environment.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, this positive energy often manifests itself as an attractive, confident femininity that does not need to prove its worth through constant approval from others. She feels her own power to attract what she wants—attention, opportunities, resources—and uses this gift openly, without shame or need to belittle herself.

Such women usually have a keen instinct about people: they quickly feel who is sincere and who is playing a part, and they know how to use this knowledge for their own protection and development, and not for manipulation. Decisions are made from a position of strength, not fear of abandonment or neglect.

Another trait is the ability to combine sensuality with self-control: she knows how to enjoy life to the fullest - relationships, beauty, pleasures - without losing her own boundaries and without allowing any passion or person to control her.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, the positive manifestation of this energy often looks like the natural, unforced charisma of a leader to whom people gravitate without coercion. He does not need to push or threaten to gain influence - people follow him because they feel the power and confidence he radiates.

Such men usually have a sharp business instinct: they see the real value of a deal, project or person much faster than their surroundings and are able to act decisively where others hesitate, recognizing the true essence of the situation.

In a relationship with his own desires, a man with this energy in the positive knows how to combine passion with discipline: he allows himself to want strongly and pursue the desired resolutely, but always remains the master of his own urges, and not their hostage.

## Expression in the Negative

When this energy does not find a healthy outlet, magnetism turns into a tool of manipulation: a person uses charisma and insight not for honest influence, but in order to get what he wants, playing on other people's weaknesses and fears.

The natural power of desire in the negative turns into addiction in the broadest sense: it can be a literal addiction to substances, but more often - addiction to a certain person, to recognition, to the adrenaline of success, to control over others, when a person feels alive only when he gets another dose of what he wants.

Insight, which in the positive is the gift of empathy, in the negative turns into the ability to accurately hit the weak points of others - consciously or semi-consciously. A person knows what the interlocutor is afraid of or wants, and uses this knowledge to get what he wants, not to help.

In relation to material things, the negative manifestation often looks like an insatiable thirst for accumulation or control: no matter how much money, power or attention there is, it is always not enough, and self-worth is measured only by external attributes of success.

Jealousy and possessiveness are another typical negative trait. A person is so afraid of losing what he considers his own - a partner, status, influence - that he goes into control mode, jealously watching every step of others and perceiving any independence they have as a threat.

The relationship with one's own freedom in the negative also weakens: a person gets so used to the power of a certain habit, person or pattern of behavior over him that he loses the ability to imagine life without it - even when he realizes how much this dependence exhausts him.

Another sign is cynicism masquerading as insight: a person is so used to seeing hidden motives behind every action that he stops believing in sincerity at all, suspecting manipulation even where there is none.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, the negative state of this energy often looks like using attractiveness and influence as the only tool to get what she wants — she may unconsciously get used to getting her way through the manipulation of other people's feelings, instead of a direct and honest request or agreement.

The negative tendency to addiction manifests itself especially acutely in relationships: a woman can remain attached to a person or pattern that harms her for years, convincing herself that she cannot do without it, even when she understands the destructiveness of the relationship.

In relation to a partner, the negative can manifest itself as chronic jealousy and the need to control: they monitor, suspect, and check, turning the relationship into a battleground for possession instead of a space of trust.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, the negative manifestation of this energy is often disguised as business acumen - in fact, it is a willingness to achieve a goal at any cost, including manipulation and pressure on people whom he considers only a means to get what he wants.

The tendency to addiction in the negative manifests itself especially clearly through obsession with work, success, status things or the adrenaline of risk: he can live for years in pursuit of the next dose of achievement, not noticing how this pursuit devastates the rest of his life.

In a relationship, a man with this energy in the negative is prone either to strict control over his partner out of jealousy and fear of loss, or, conversely, to a series of superficial relationships in which he uses his own magnetism without investing in true intimacy.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The most common trigger is a lingering sense of lack: lack of money, attention, recognition, or love in the past, which leaves a deep fear that what you want will never be enough. This fear leads to hoarding and control instead of trusting in one's own ability to attract the desired again.

The second trigger is an experience of betrayal or deception that has taught this person not to trust the sincerity of others. Instead of open influence, the habit of manipulating the first "until I was manipulated" appears, which over time becomes a toxic style of relationships with everyone around.

The third trigger is an environment full of temptations and easy access to pleasures without internal boundaries. Without conscious discipline, this energy can easily slip into addiction—to substances, relationships, excitement, or any other form of instant gratification that promises quick relief.

Another typical trigger is the feeling of losing control over an important area of life. When this energy feels out of control, it reacts by increasing control over what can still be controlled, often the people around it, which manifests as jealousy and pressure.

Finally, the chronic lack of real closeness, replaced by a series of superficial victories — in business, relationships, and status — leads to the negative. When external success does not bring internal satisfaction, there is a temptation to drown this emptiness with an even larger dose of the same thing that is no longer working.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

The first step is to honestly admit if there is a habit, person, or pattern in life that is driving the behavior more than the person is driving it. Acknowledging addiction—in any form, not necessarily literal—is the first and most difficult step to freedom.

The second step is to train yourself to use insight for the benefit of others, not just for your own benefit: to consciously use the ability to see through people to help, not to find leverage.

The third step is learning to distinguish true desire from compulsive craving: Before acting on a strong urge, it's worth pausing to ask whether it's a choice or an automatic response driven by habit or fear of scarcity.

The fourth step is to consciously build value and influence through real contribution, not through control over people: the more one creates real wealth and trust, the less need to maintain power through fear or manipulation.

The fifth step is to work with jealousy and the fear of loss of direction, not through increased control over the partner: often behind jealousy hides the old fear of one's own inadequacy, which is worth exploring separately.

The sixth step is to regularly remind yourself that the real power of this energy is not in possessing other people or things, but in freedom: the ability to want strongly, get what you want honestly, and be yourself in the process.

## Main Task

The main task of this energy at the point of birth is to learn to consciously own one's own magnetism and insight, and not to allow them - or the own desires to which they lead - to own one's self. This involves the development of inner honesty: the ability to distinguish real, living influence from manipulation, and true desire from compulsive addiction, with which one tries to fill an ancient void.

When this honesty is formed, the need to either control others for fear of loss or to chase the next dose of pleasure, status, or attention to quell inner anxiety disappears. Magnetism and insight cease to be a tool of power over others and become a real gift - the ability to see the essence of things and people and attract into your life a sincere, honest wealth based on real value, not on fear or dependence.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is there a habit, person or pattern in my life that controls me more than I control them?
- Do I use my own insight and attractiveness to help others, or to get what I want at any cost?
- What is behind my jealousy or need to control - a sincere feeling or an ancient fear of lack?

## Practical Plan

1. List any habits, relationships, or patterns that feel like addictions, and honestly assess how much they really drive the behavior.
2. During the week, before each strong desire or temptation, pause and ask: "is this my conscious choice or an automatic urge?".
3. One week to consciously apply the ability to understand people in order to help someone, and not to gain personal benefit.
4. Find a specific way to build value or income through real contribution, not through control over people or situations.
5. Once a month, honestly examine the root of your own jealousy or need to control — write down the fear behind it.
6. Make a list of what gives real, long-term satisfaction, and consciously choose it instead of a quick, compulsive dose.
