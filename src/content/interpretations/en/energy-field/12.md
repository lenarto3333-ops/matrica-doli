# 12. The Hanged Man — how to strengthen your energy field

The Hanged Man's energy field is fed by a pause, a change of perspective, and the willingness to temporarily "do nothing" — and drained by endlessly waiting for the "right moment," which turns into simply getting stuck.

## Positive

+ **A deliberate pause.** Allows themselves to stop without guilt, knowing it's needed for new understanding.
+ **A change of perspective.** Looks at a situation from an unusual angle before drawing conclusions.
+ **Accepting uncertainty.** Sits calmly with "I don't know yet" instead of forcing a premature decision.
+ **Letting go of control.** Lets a process unfold naturally instead of trying to speed up the outcome.
+ **Creative contemplation.** Uses idle time for observation and new ideas rather than anxiety.
+ **Flexibility of view.** Changes their perspective easily when new information comes in.
+ **Trust in their own timing.** Gives themselves however much time a decision genuinely needs to mature.
+ **Accepting sacrifice for growth.** Deliberately gives up something smaller for the sake of deeper understanding.
+ **Meditative practices.** Regular quiet and attention to breath support clarity.
+ **A sense of humor about their own indecision.** Takes periods of not-yet-clarity lightly.

## Negative

- **Stuck in waiting.** Waits for the "perfect moment" so long that the opportunity passes.
- **Passive self-sacrifice.** Gives up their own needs without gaining any new understanding in return.
- **Paralyzing uncertainty.** Stays inactive for so long that it becomes more draining than a mistake would be.
- **Avoiding responsibility.** Hides behind "it's not time yet" to dodge a hard decision.
- **Inner confusion.** Changes their point of view so often that they lose their own footing.
- **Emotional numbness.** Freezes in inaction even when a situation calls for a response.
- **Isolation in their own thoughts.** Gets so absorbed in their own perspective that they lose touch with reality around them.
- **A postponed life.** Constantly waits for "later" instead of living in the present moment.
- **Loss of others' trust.** People stop relying on them because of chronic indecision.
- **Exhaustion from inaction.** Passivity that leads to no real insight drains energy just as much as overactivity does.

## Recommendations

- Set yourself a clear time limit for the pause — it should end in a concrete step, not stretch on indefinitely.
- Regularly ask yourself whether this is a genuine pause for understanding or an escape from deciding.
- Write down the new perspective you gain, so the pause produces a real result instead of just delaying action.
