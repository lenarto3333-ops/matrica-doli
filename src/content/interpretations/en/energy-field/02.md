# 2. The High Priestess — how to strengthen your energy field

The High Priestess's energy field is fed by silence and contact with their own intuition — and drained by constant noise, over-openness to other people's energy, and staying silent about their own needs.

## Positive

+ **Time alone.** A daily pause free of other voices makes it possible to hear their own intuition.
+ **Trust in gut feeling.** Makes decisions based on an inner signal, even when logic hasn't caught up to explain it.
+ **Quiet over noise.** Limits the flow of unnecessary information to keep perception clear.
+ **Keeping records.** Writes down dreams and hunches, training the connection to the subconscious.
+ **Natural rhythms.** Syncs the day's pace to their own energy rather than someone else's schedule.
+ **Symbolic practices.** Meditation or quiet rituals bring a real surge of clarity.
+ **Selective openness.** Shares their inner world only with those who genuinely deserve the trust.
+ **A protected space.** Keeps a physical place no one enters uninvited.
+ **Listening to the body.** Notices when intuition shows up as a physical sensation — and trusts that signal.
+ **A pause before answering.** Takes time to "check in" internally before deciding, instead of rushing to react.

## Negative

- **Silent buildup.** Hides real needs behind a veil of mystery until the inner tension becomes unbearable.
- **Information overload.** Constant consumption of news and other people's opinions drowns out their own inner voice.
- **Over-openness.** Soaks up other people's emotions like a sponge, unable to tell their own feelings from someone else's.
- **Ignoring intuition.** Acts against an inner sense because of pressure from others — and regrets it later.
- **Isolation instead of solitude.** Withdraws so completely that they lose support they actually need.
- **Constant availability.** Answers every request immediately, leaving no time to recover.
- **Hidden resentment.** Never voices disagreement, then quietly drifts away without explanation.
- **An overstimulating environment.** Noise, bright light, and crowds wear down sensitive perception faster than expected.
- **Self-doubt.** Keeps looking for confirmation of their own feelings in other people's words instead of trusting them outright.
- **A blurred boundary.** Absorbs the emotional state of whoever's around and forgets to return to their own.

## Recommendations

- Set aside at least twenty minutes of complete silence every day, screens and conversation off.
- After time with draining people, consciously check in with your own state — what's mine, what's someone else's.
- Keep brief notes on dreams or hunches to build trust in your inner voice.
