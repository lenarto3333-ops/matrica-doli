# 19. The Sun — how to strengthen your energy field

The Sun's energy field is fed by sincere joy and open communication — and drained when lightness becomes a mask hiding unresolved, difficult issues.

## Positive

+ **Sincere joy.** Lets themselves openly enjoy small things without holding back.
+ **Warm connection.** Regular contact with people they can easily be themselves around.
+ **Simplicity in expression.** States their own needs plainly, without complications or hints.
+ **Play and lightness.** Sets aside time for things that bring pure enjoyment, with no practical purpose.
+ **Owning their achievements.** Shares their successes openly, letting themselves accept others' admiration.
+ **A sunny environment.** Time outdoors and in the light visibly keeps their energy up.
+ **Sincerity in hard moments.** Isn't afraid to show vulnerability even in a state they'd usually cover with a smile.
+ **Infectious energy.** Shares warmth with people around them without draining their own resources.
+ **Accepting attention.** Lets themselves be the center of attention without awkwardness or self-deprecation.
+ **Childlike curiosity.** Approaches new experiences with openness and excitement rather than wariness.

## Negative

- **Fleeing into lightness.** Avoids hard topics to keep the mood positive, even when it's worth stopping to face them.
- **The mask of constant cheer.** Hides real feelings behind a smile, afraid to "spoil" the mood.
- **Shallow connections.** Keeps many light relationships while avoiding deeper, more vulnerable closeness.
- **Dependence on attention.** Loses their inner resources when they don't get enough recognition from others.
- **Ignoring fatigue.** Keeps "shining" for others even when they need rest themselves.
- **Fear of disappointing.** Avoids talking about hardships so as not to upset others with their own state.
- **Oversharing.** Shares too much personal detail with people who haven't earned that trust.
- **Exhaustion from the "sunshine" role.** Constantly props up the group's mood, forgetting their own needs.
- **Avoiding deep analysis.** Skips hard decisions by choosing "not to think about it right now."
- **Disappointment from the contrast.** Their mood crashes hard when reality doesn't match the expected lightness.

## Recommendations

- Let yourself talk openly about difficulties instead of hiding them behind a constant smile.
- Regularly check whether you're propping up someone else's mood at the cost of your own resources.
- Set aside time for deeper, more vulnerable conversations with a few close people, not just light chat.
