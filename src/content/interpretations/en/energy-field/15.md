# 15. The Devil — how to strengthen your energy field

The Devil's energy field grows stronger through a conscious freedom from imposed rules and dependencies — and drains when comfort or a familiar attachment starts taking more than it gives.

## Positive

+ **A conscious choice.** Chooses habits and relationships deliberately, not out of inertia or fear of being alone.
+ **Freedom from what's imposed.** Drops rules and expectations that don't match their own values.
+ **Healthy limits on pleasure.** Enjoys the material and sensory world without sliding into dependency.
+ **Honesty with themselves.** Admits when a habit or relationship has started causing harm, instead of denying it.
+ **A regular audit of habits.** Periodically checks which patterns still serve them and which no longer do.
+ **Physical freedom.** Allows themselves pleasure without shame and without excess.
+ **Breaking harmful ties.** Finds the courage to leave relationships or situations that are draining them.
+ **Spotting manipulation.** Notices when someone is playing on their fears or desires, and doesn't fall for it.
+ **Material grounding without dependency.** Values stability and resources without making them the whole point of life.
+ **Accepting their shadow.** Acknowledges their own weaknesses and temptations without self-judgment, which gives them the strength to work with them.

## Negative

- **Dependent patterns.** Returns to harmful habits or relationships even after recognizing their cost.
- **Fear of losing comfort.** Clings to a convenient but draining situation purely out of fear of change.
- **Denying the problem.** Refuses to admit that a habit or relationship is already causing harm.
- **Material attachment.** Measures their own worth by possessions, status, or money.
- **Falling for manipulation.** Lets others control them through guilt or fear.
- **Excessive indulgence.** Loses control over pleasures that start running their life.
- **Hidden shame.** Hides their own desires or habits out of fear of judgment, building up inner conflict.
- **The "relapse and guilt" cycle.** Breaks their own limits, then punishes themselves instead of calmly returning to balance.
- **Toxic connections.** Stays in a draining relationship out of habit or fear of being alone.
- **Loss of will.** Gets so used to their limitations that they stop even trying to break free.

## Recommendations

- Once a month, honestly review your habits and relationships: what gives you energy, and what only drains it.
- Practice small steps out of a harmful pattern instead of waiting for "final" resolve to arrive.
- Allow yourself pleasure consciously and in moderation, without punishing yourself for it with guilt.
