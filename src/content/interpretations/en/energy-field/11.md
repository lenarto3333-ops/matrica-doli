# 11. Strength — how to strengthen your energy field

Strength's energy field grows through patience, self-acceptance, and a gentle but steady influence — and weakens when emotions are held back too long, or when endurance turns into self-suppression.

## Positive

+ **Patient endurance.** Rides out hard periods without the urge to force an immediate fix.
+ **Self-acceptance.** Doesn't fight their own weaknesses, but gradually integrates them.
+ **Gentle influence.** Persuades through calm consistency rather than pressure or raised voices.
+ **Timely emotional release.** Lets feelings out in small doses instead of waiting for them to build up.
+ **Physical stamina.** Regular moderate exercise strengthens both the body and inner resilience.
+ **Calm in hard conversations.** Stays composed where others lose control.
+ **Support without pressure.** Helps loved ones gently, leaving them room to reach their own decision.
+ **Trust in their own strength.** Knows they'll cope, even when a situation looks threatening.
+ **Grounding practices.** Breathing exercises and bodily awareness help maintain balance.
+ **Accepting vulnerability.** Lets themselves show weakness in front of loved ones without shame.

## Negative

- **Suppressed emotions.** Holds feelings in so long that they break out in a sudden burst.
- **Silent overexertion.** Carries too much without showing how hard it actually is.
- **Losing the gentleness.** Shifts from patience to harsh pressure once their resources run out.
- **Ignoring their own limits.** Keeps "holding on" even after the body has already signaled exhaustion.
- **The role of eternal support.** Constantly props others up, forgetting they need support too.
- **Inner struggle.** Spends energy on a hidden fight against their own emotions instead of accepting them.
- **Freezing up.** In a hard situation, goes still instead of acting, afraid to show vulnerability.
- **Bottled-up resentment.** Silently endures things that should have been said out loud right away.
- **Exhaustion from restraint.** Constant self-control with no release gradually cuts off access to their own feelings.
- **Loss of sensitivity.** Gets so used to enduring that they stop noticing their own fatigue or pain.

## Recommendations

- Find a regular, safe outlet for emotions — movement, conversation, creativity — before tension has a chance to build.
- Let yourself ask for support instead of only being the one who provides it.
- Practice the pause "what am I actually feeling right now" several times a day.
