# 14. Temperance — how to strengthen your energy field

Temperance's energy field is fed by gradual steps, diplomacy, and a fine sense of balance — and drained when avoiding extremes turns into constantly putting off important decisions.

## Positive

+ **Gradual steps.** Moves toward a goal in small, steady steps rather than sudden bursts.
+ **Natural diplomacy.** Finds a compromise where others see only conflict.
+ **Inner harmony.** Regularly checks the balance between work, rest, and relationships.
+ **Creatively combining opposites.** Finds a solution that accounts for several seemingly incompatible needs at once.
+ **A calm pace.** Avoids sudden overload by spreading energy evenly through the day.
+ **A fine sense of measure.** Knows when to stop — in work, conversation, effort — before exhaustion sets in.
+ **Gentle mediation.** Helps others find common ground while staying neutral.
+ **An aesthetic of moderation.** Values simple, harmonious solutions over excess.
+ **Patience with the process.** Gives themselves and the situation time to mature instead of forcing a result.
+ **Balance of give and take.** Watches that giving and receiving in a relationship stay even.

## Negative

- **Chronic indecision.** Avoids extremes so consistently that important decisions get postponed indefinitely.
- **Blurred boundaries.** Wants so badly to avoid conflict that they never stand up for their own position.
- **Losing their own voice.** Keeps searching for a compromise even where a clear stance is what's needed.
- **Stuck in the "golden middle."** Avoids risky but necessary steps just to keep the peace.
- **Emotional restraint.** Smooths over their own reactions so much that others never grasp their real feelings.
- **Exhaustion from balancing.** Spends a lot of energy maintaining equilibrium in situations that just call for action.
- **Avoiding hard conversations.** Puts off a direct talk, hoping the tension will dissolve on its own.
- **Loss of individuality.** Adapts to everyone around them so much that they lose their own definition.
- **Accumulated unfulfillment.** Constantly doing "a bit of this, a bit of that" keeps anything from reaching real depth.
- **Fatigue from mediating.** Wears themselves out regularly smoothing over other people's conflicts instead of living their own life.

## Recommendations

- Once a month, deliberately make one decision without compromise, where it's genuinely called for.
- Practice voicing disagreement directly instead of only looking for a way to make everyone happy.
- Pick one area of life where you allow yourself to move faster and more decisively than usual.
