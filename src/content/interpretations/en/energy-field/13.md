# 13. Death — how to strengthen your energy field

Death's energy field is fed by the ability to let go of what's already run its course and open up to a new stage — and drained when change is held back by force, or when an ending drags on without a clean close.

## Positive

+ **A conscious ending.** Puts a clear full stop on what's already spent, instead of keeping it around "just in case."
+ **Accepting cycles.** Treats the end of one stage as the natural start of another.
+ **Rituals of parting.** Deliberately marks an ending with a conversation, an action, or a symbolic gesture, rather than leaving it hanging.
+ **Ease in letting go of things.** Regularly clears out what no longer serves a purpose.
+ **Openness to the new.** Welcomes the unfamiliar without fear once the old has truly left.
+ **Honesty about the ending.** Calls something finished when it's finished, even when that's painful to admit.
+ **Trust in renewal.** Knows that fresh resources always follow an ending.
+ **Supporting others through change.** Helps loved ones through loss without minimizing their pain.
+ **A flexible identity.** Lets themselves change instead of clinging to an old version of who they were.
+ **Accepting natural loss.** Lets grief run its full course instead of rushing to "forget and move on."

## Negative

- **Fear of endings.** Holds on to what no longer works purely out of fear of the unknown ahead.
- **A drawn-out goodbye.** Never puts a clear full stop, leaving a situation "half-finished" for years.
- **Sharp, destructive breaks.** Forces an abrupt rupture where a gradual transition would have worked fine.
- **Attachment to an old identity.** Clings to a role or self-image that lost its meaning long ago.
- **Avoiding grief.** Skips the stage of feeling the loss, jumping straight to a "new life" — and it catches up with them later.
- **Hoarding what's no longer needed.** Keeps people, things, and habits around long after they've served their purpose.
- **Fear of irreversibility.** Avoids final decisions to keep a "way back" open.
- **Freezing up before change.** Meets inevitable transformation with anxiety instead of acceptance.
- **Comparing to the past.** Keeps measuring a new stage against the old one, never letting it show itself on its own terms.
- **Exhaustion from resistance.** Spends more energy holding on to what's leaving than the goodbye itself would take.

## Recommendations

- Once a season, deliberately review what's already ended in your life, and mark it with a clear gesture or decision.
- Let yourself fully feel the grief before moving on to a "new beginning."
- Clear physical space of things that no longer match who you're becoming.
