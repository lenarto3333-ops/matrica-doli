# 18. The Moon — how to strengthen your energy field

The Moon's energy field is fed by accepting their own emotions without trying to rationalize them — and drained by anxiety and illusion, when reality gets replaced by fears.

## Positive

+ **Accepting emotions.** Lets feelings simply be, without rushing to explain or fix them.
+ **Trust in the subconscious.** Pays attention to dreams, images, and hunches as a source of real information.
+ **Gentle grounding.** Regular contact with the body and nature stabilizes sensitive perception.
+ **Honesty about their own fears.** Names anxiety in concrete words instead of letting it grow in the shadows.
+ **A natural rhythm.** Syncs with the cycles of sleep, the moon, and the season to keep their mood stable.
+ **Creative expression of emotion.** Uses art, writing, or movement to give feelings an outlet.
+ **Caution with information noise.** Limits alarming news and content that fuels their fears.
+ **Telling fear from reality.** Checks an anxious thought against the facts before fully believing it.
+ **Support in sensitive periods.** Reaches out for help when their emotional state is especially unstable.
+ **Tenderness toward themselves.** Treats their own sensitivity as a strength rather than a weakness to hide.

## Negative

- **Chronic anxiety.** Lets fears grow without ever checking them against reality.
- **Illusory thinking.** Replaces facts with their own worries or fantasies.
- **Emotional swings.** Mood shifts sharply at the smallest outside trigger.
- **Avoiding clear answers.** Talks about their own needs vaguely, leaving room for guesswork.
- **Absorbing others' emotions.** Can't tell their own state from the mood of those around them, picking up other people's anxiety.
- **Escaping into fantasy.** Avoids a difficult reality by sinking into daydreams or illusions instead of acting.
- **Nighttime worry.** Anxious thoughts intensify in the evening and interfere with proper rest.
- **Hidden resentment.** Never voices disagreement directly, drifting away silently instead.
- **Loss of self-trust.** Keeps doubting their own perception, looking for confirmation from outside.
- **Overloaded sensitivity.** Spends too long in an emotionally draining environment with no pause to recover.

## Recommendations

- Keep a brief emotion journal to tell real signals apart from anxious fantasies.
- Limit your exposure to alarming content, especially in the evening.
- After time in an emotionally draining environment, deliberately "return" to your own state through breath or movement.
