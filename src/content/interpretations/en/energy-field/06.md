# 6. The Lovers — how to strengthen your energy field

The Lovers' energy field is fed by sincerity and a free choice made from the heart — and drained by indecision, anxiety over making the "right" call, and sacrificing their own wants for the sake of harmony.

## Positive

+ **A sincere choice.** Makes decisions that resonate with an inner feeling rather than what "looks right."
+ **Open dialogue.** Talks about their feelings directly instead of hinting or expecting others to guess.
+ **Harmony in close relationships.** Regular warm contact with important people keeps their emotional tone up.
+ **Accepting duality.** Allows themselves to hold contradictory feelings without needing to resolve them right away.
+ **Trust in the heart.** Chooses partners, pursuits, and company by resonance rather than calculation.
+ **Room to think things through.** Gives themselves time for an important decision instead of caving to pressure to rush.
+ **Mutual sincerity.** Surrounds themselves with people they can be genuine with, no mask required.
+ **Clarity of values.** Knows clearly what matters to them and checks every choice against it.
+ **Ease in relationships.** Doesn't dramatize small misunderstandings, but talks them through right away.
+ **Physical closeness.** Hugs, touch, and intimacy with a loved one visibly recharge their energy.

## Negative

- **Chronic indecision.** Gets stuck between options so long that both time and energy for the actual decision run out.
- **Fear of the "wrong" choice.** Avoids final decisions to dodge mistakes, staying stuck in limbo instead.
- **Self-sacrifice for the sake of harmony.** Gives up their own wants so as not to upset someone else.
- **Dependence on others' opinions.** Decides based on what others will approve of rather than their own feeling.
- **Emotional swings.** Sharp mood shifts from uncertainty in a relationship drain more than an actual conflict would.
- **Avoiding hard conversations.** Stays quiet about disagreement to keep up an appearance of peace, and lets distance build.
- **Idealizing the choice.** Waits for the "perfect" option instead of acting on what's actually available.
- **Dissolving into the relationship.** Loses their own identity by constantly adapting to a partner or the people around them.
- **Endless comparing of options.** Analyzes every possibility for so long that the moment to act passes.
- **Guilt over their own choice.** Keeps second-guessing a decision already made, robbing themselves of peace.

## Recommendations

- Before deciding, ask yourself what you actually want — not which option looks the safest.
- Set yourself a deadline for deliberation and act even without a hundred percent certainty.
- Once a week, say out loud to someone close one feeling you'd normally keep to yourself.
