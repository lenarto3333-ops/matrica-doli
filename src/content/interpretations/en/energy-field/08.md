# 8. Justice — how to strengthen your energy field

This energy field grows stronger through logic, honesty, and care for the body — a healthy lifestyle, nutrition, movement — as well as through emotional and mental balance: relaxation, meditation, harmony with themselves and the world.

## Positive

+ **Bodily discipline.** Regular movement and balanced nutrition directly strengthen the energy body.
+ **Clear logic.** Weighs decisions carefully, looking at a situation from several angles, which calms the nervous system.
+ **Kept promises.** Follows through on commitments — which keeps their energy running along a stable, honest track.
+ **Emotional balance.** Regularly checks in on their own state and doesn't let tension build unnoticed.
+ **Relaxation practice.** Meditation or mindful breathing supports inner harmony every day.
+ **Fairness in action.** Holds themselves and others to the same standard, no double standards.
+ **Responsibility without overload.** Takes on exactly as much as can honestly be delivered.
+ **Harmony with themselves.** Sets aside time to sit with their own thoughts instead of putting it off "someday."
+ **Balance of give and take.** Watches that relationships and work don't tip too far in one direction.
+ **Honest dialogue.** Voices disagreement right away instead of silently keeping score of unfairness.

## Negative

- **A neglected body.** Ignores physical activity and nutrition until the body starts to malfunction.
- **Inner perfectionism.** Judges themselves for the smallest "unfairness" in their own actions harsher than they'd judge anyone else.
- **Emotional buildup.** Never makes time to relax, and tension gradually turns into chronic fatigue.
- **Broken promises.** Takes on too many commitments and can't deliver any of them well.
- **An unbalanced rhythm.** Lives without a clear sleep and rest schedule, which drains the energy body.
- **Excessive judgment.** Constantly evaluates themselves and others through the lens of "fair or not," wearing down both sides.
- **A neglected meditation practice.** Puts off quiet and harmony for a "later" that never comes.
- **Inner imbalance.** Gives too much and receives too little — or the reverse — without noticing in time.
- **Silent score-keeping.** Accumulates a sense of unfairness without ever voicing it right away.
- **Chronic bodily tension.** Mental imbalance shows up physically — headaches, tense muscles, disrupted sleep.

## Recommendations

- Build at least fifteen minutes of relaxation or meditation into your daily routine.
- Watch the balance of physical activity and nutrition as closely as you watch honesty in your dealings.
- Before taking on a new commitment, honestly assess whether you have the resources to fully deliver on it.
