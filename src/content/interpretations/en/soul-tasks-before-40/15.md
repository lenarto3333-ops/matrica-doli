# 15. The Devil — Soul Tasks Before Age 40

## Brief Essence

A soul with the energy of the Devil comes into this life with a powerful gift of attraction and a deep sense of human desires - and with the task of learning to master this gift, not to be enslaved by it. By the age of forty, the main task is to recognize what or who really controls the choice, and to free yourself from any addiction that has imperceptibly replaced free will.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this soul is innate magnetism, the ability to see through people and situations, to sense hidden desires and motives where others see only the surface. This is a gift of great influence and material success, which becomes a real power only when combined with honesty about one's own desires and respect for other people's freedom.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, one uses this gift to build genuine influence, relationships, and wealth based on deep understanding of self and others, not manipulation. She knows how to enjoy the material world and passion, while remaining free - desires serve her, not the other way around.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same power turns into addiction: a person can be captive to a habit, substance, relationship or own need to control others for years, not noticing that he has long since lost his freedom of choice. Manipulative relationships are also possible — both in the role of one who subjugates and one who submits to someone else's authority.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, an early environment where love or security was associated with submission to someone else's will or, on the contrary, with the ability to control and manipulate for the sake of survival, leads to a disadvantage. It is also facilitated by the experience when the natural passion or desire was condemned, because of which the person learned to either hide it or lose control over it.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

A key step toward forty is to honestly identify which habits, relationships, or patterns of behavior have long ruled life instead of the person themselves, and gradually regain freedom of choice in each of them. The second is to learn to use personal attraction openly, naming your own desires, instead of acting through covert pressure or manipulation.

It is also useful to develop a healthy relationship with the material world and pleasure - not denying them ascetically, but learning to enjoy without excessive dependence.

## Main Task

By the age of forty, learn to consciously own your own magnetism and a deep vision of human desires, freeing yourself from any addiction that replaces true freedom of choice.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What habit, attitude or pattern of behavior has been driving my decisions lately instead of myself?
- Where do I use my attractiveness honestly, and where do I use it to gain control over someone?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one addiction or pattern of behavior that limits your freedom of choice, and take the first conscious step toward breaking free from it.
2. During the week, track the times when you use influence or charisma and honestly evaluate the motive each time.
3. Practice openly stating your desires to loved ones instead of acting on hints or pressure.
