# 16. The Tower — Soul Tasks Before Age 40

## Brief Essence

A soul with the energy of the Tower comes into this life with a powerful potential for inner strength, hardened through trials - and with the task of learning to move through crises without destroying themselves or others. By the age of forty, the main task is to turn the experienced upheavals into a source of strength, and not a source of chronic anxiety or aggression.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this soul is the ability to endure what would break others and build on the ruins something more real than what was before. This is the gift of real resilience, which becomes a strength only when a person learns to consciously live the shock, and not to avoid it or freeze in it.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, a person uses this gift to resolutely let go of the old and build a life on honest, not illusory foundations, even when it requires a painful farewell to the usual. She knows how to turn a crisis into a springboard and help others get through their own upheavals.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same power turns destructive: a person can live for years in a cycle of crises, which he himself provokes, or carry unspoken aggression and anxiety in his body, which erupts in sharp outbursts. Chronic avoidance of any change due to fear of experiencing another shock is also possible.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, a sudden trauma experienced in childhood or youth - loss, betrayal, a sudden change in circumstances - after which the world began to seem like an unreliable place, puts one in the negative. This is also facilitated by an environment where emotions, especially anger, were either tightly suppressed or, on the contrary, exploded uncontrollably.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

The key step to turning forty is to realize which crises in life repeat themselves according to a similar scenario, and to honestly understand what lesson has not yet been learned. The second is learning to experience strong emotions, particularly anger, in a safe way, instead of either keeping them inside or taking them out on others.

It's also helpful to gradually learn to trust new structures in life—relationships, plans, projects—without constantly expecting another crash just because one actually happened.

## Main Task

By the age of forty, learn to consciously move through the inevitable upheavals, turning them into a source of strength and clarity, rather than a cycle of repeated destruction for yourself or others.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What crisis in my life repeats itself again and again according to a similar scenario?
- Where do I direct the accumulated tension or anger — to a constructive change, or to the destruction of myself or others?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one crisis from the past and honestly name the lesson it should have taught you.
2. Find a healthy, regular way to experience strong emotions—moving, talking, creating—before they build up to an explosion.
3. Check every few months whether you are clinging to an outdated structure in life just out of fear of a new upheaval.
