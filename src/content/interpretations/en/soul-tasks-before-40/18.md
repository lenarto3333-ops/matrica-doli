# 18. The Moon — Soul Tasks Before Age 40

## Brief Essence

A soul with the energy of the Moon comes into this life with deep intuition, a rich imagination and a close connection with the subconscious - and with the task of learning to trust this inner world without losing touch with reality. By the age of forty, the main task is to learn to distinguish true inner knowledge from a disturbing illusion and to accept one's own emotions, rather than run away from them.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this soul is a subtle sensitivity to the atmosphere, moods and what is hidden behind the words and actions of other people. This is a gift that becomes a real power only when a person learns to consciously direct his imagination to the creation of what is desired, and not to the drawing of fears.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, a person uses this gift to deeply understand himself and others, create what he wants with his imagination, and trust his inner voice in important decisions. She becomes for others an example of how you can be sensitive and at the same time stable, without losing your footing in reality.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same power turns to two extremes: either constant anxiety and suspicion, when imaginary scenarios overshadow real facts, or deep suppression of one's own emotions, when a person rationalizes everything in order not to feel real pain. Possible long-term immersion in fantasy and isolation instead of real actions and relationships.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, childhood or youth experience, when the child's emotions were devalued or ridiculed, and she learned to hide or explain them instead of experiencing them, leads to a negative. This is also facilitated by an environment where a chaotic or anxious atmosphere at home has taught us to constantly expect the worst.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

A key step into your forties is to learn how to balance gut feelings with real facts so that intuition remains a reliable tool and not a source of constant anxiety. The second is to gradually allow yourself to experience emotions as they arise, instead of suppressing them or hiding behind rational explanations.

It's also helpful to regularly practice mindful imagination—consciously creating an image of your desired future, instead of letting your imagination run wild, where it easily slips into anxious scenarios.

## Main Task

By the age of forty, learn to trust your own intuition and accept your emotions without suppression or over-rationalization, turning your rich imagination into a creative tool rather than a source of fear.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- How often do I take disturbing thoughts for real facts without checking them?
- Do I allow myself to feel emotions as they are, or do I immediately try to explain or suppress them?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one anxiety that has been bothering you for a long time and check which parts of it are supported by facts and which parts are just assumptions.
2. During the week, practice conscious visualization of the desired result instead of scrolling through disturbing scenarios.
3. Once every few months, check whether you have learned to accept your own emotions, and not just explain or suppress them.
