# 22. The Fool — Soul Tasks Before Age 40

## Brief Essence

A soul with the energy of the Jester comes into this life with a natural gift of openness to new experiences and a sincere trust in the flow of life - and with the task of learning to carry this freedom along with responsibility for one's own decisions. Until the age of forty, the main task is not to let this openness turn into either reckless adventurism or fear of any change.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this soul is the ability to easily start with a clean slate, without dragging behind the burden of past failures or expectations, and sincerely trust that life will lead you where you need to go. It is a gift that becomes a real power only when a person learns to be responsible for the consequences of his freedom, and not just to enjoy it.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, a person uses this gift to boldly try new things, easily adapt to changes, and inspire others with his sincerity and spontaneity. She trusts fate, but does not lose a sober look at the consequences of her own steps and brings important matters to a conclusion.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the same capacity for freedom turns to two extremes: either constant adventurousness, when new passions replace each other, and serious obligations and consequences of decisions are ignored, or the opposite extreme - a deep fear of any change, due to which a person clings to a limited, safe, but unsatisfactory life for years. Exhaustion from constant rash jumps or, on the contrary, from a suffocating routine is also possible.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, an environment where adult responsibility was demanded too early, leaving no room for play and spontaneity, or, on the contrary, where freedom was never accompanied by consequences, due to which responsibility was never formed, often leads to a disadvantage. This is also facilitated by a painful experience in which an attempt to change something ended in failure, after which the fear of any new step appears.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

The key step to forty is to learn to take responsibility for the consequences of one's freedom, without losing lightness and trust in life. The second is to consciously regain the right to new experiences where fear has narrowed life to a familiar and safe circle.

It is also useful to regularly check whether a person lives with trust in the flow of life, or runs away from it - either into reckless adventure or into protective immobility.

## Main Task

By the age of forty, learn to combine freedom and new experiences with responsibility for one's own decisions, without slipping into either irresponsibility or self-limitation out of fear.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- How many decisions in my life, for the consequences of which I was not really responsible?
- What important new experience am I giving up just out of fear of losing my usual security?

## Practical Plan

1. Write down one decision, the consequences of which you still avoid recognizing, and take a concrete step to take responsibility for it.
2. Choose one area of ​​life where you cling to the usual out of fear, and try one small new step in it.
3. Once every few months, check whether your life is closer to trusting freedom or to one of the extremes - adventure or stagnation.
