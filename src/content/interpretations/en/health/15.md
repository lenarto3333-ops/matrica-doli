# 15. The Devil — Chakra Health Map

> This section describes only the traditional esoteric associations and symbolic counterparts accepted in the popular chakra tradition. It is not a medical diagnosis, does not establish the causes of any diseases and does not replace a doctor's consultation. If there are real symptoms or health concerns, this is a reason to consult a specialist, and not to rely on an esoteric interpretation.

## Brief Essence

In traditional chakra symbolism, the fifteenth arcana is associated with the sacral chakra, or Swadhisthana, a center associated in popular esotericism with sensual pleasure, attraction, magnetism, and the ability to attract the desired. This is not a medical statement about the causes of diseases, but a traditional symbolic view: if a person suppresses his own desires for a long time or, on the contrary, allows them to control every decision, in this tradition it is customary to pay attention to the state and well-being associated with this symbolic zone, and to pay more attention to a conscious, rather than an automatic, relationship with one's own urges.

## Natural Strength

In traditional symbolism, this energy is associated with Swadhisthana's natural power to attract what is desired—attention, resources, people, opportunities—by the very quality of one's presence, without the need to demand or prove it. Symbolically, it is associated with a general feeling of inner magnetism and a warm, inviting life force that is felt by others even without words.

The second traditional association is natural insight: the ability to feel what other people really want, to see hidden motives and unspoken needs behind outward behavior. In this symbolic system, this sensitivity is considered to support the harmonious state of the sacred center when used for genuine contact rather than covert manipulation of others.

## Expression in the Positive

In the traditional symbolic understanding, the positive expression of this energy is associated with a general feeling of warm, attractive vitality, which does not need external confirmation: a person is symbolically attributed with expressive charisma, a lively look, free, confident movements, a general feeling of bodily pleasure from simple things - taste, touch, movement. They are also associated with a healthy desire for pleasure, which does not turn into excess, and a natural ease in dealing with her own sensuality.

In this tradition, it is considered beneficial to regularly allow yourself conscious pleasure—without shame or excess—and to be in honest touch with your own desires, naming them rather than hiding them. This is seen as a symbolic support for the harmonious state of the sacred center.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In traditional symbolism for a woman, the favorable state of this energy is associated with the ability to openly feel and show one's own attractiveness, without diminishing it or using it as a tool of manipulation. It is recommended to symbolically maintain regular, warm contact with the body and sensuality - movement, touch, pleasure without haste.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

For a man in this tradition, a favorable state is associated with the ability to recognize one's own power of attraction and the desire to prove it without the need for aggression or control. Symbolically, it is considered beneficial to indulge in sensual pleasure without feeling that it diminishes power or status.

## Expression in the Negative

In traditional symbolism, either the chronic suppression of one's desires or the obsessive, insatiable pursuit of them is associated with a state of sacral chakra imbalance. This is not a medical diagnosis, but rather a symbolic description of an experience that is traditionally associated with a general feeling of tension or heaviness in the lower abdomen, an uneven appetite—for food, pleasure, stimulation—and a feeling that one's own urges rule the person, rather than the other way around.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, this can symbolically manifest as an obsessive need to control a partner or a situation due to jealousy and fear of loss, which is traditionally associated with a feeling of tightness in the abdomen and inner restlessness that does not disappear even after getting what is desired.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, this can manifest symbolically as an addiction to a certain habit, substance, or pattern of behavior that is difficult to let go even when aware of the damage, which in this tradition is associated with heaviness in the lower abdomen and uneven, "ragged" bodily energy—surges of desire that alternate with exhaustion.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

In traditional symbolism, triggers include prolonged concealment or condemnation of one's desires, an environment where open sensuality or ambition is seen as shameful, and constant access to easy, quick sources of stimulation—food, shopping, attention—that displace deeper, true pleasure. The continued need to use charm or influence to survive or control circumstances, instead of genuine contact, is considered in this tradition to be one of the strongest debilitating factors for this particular symbolic center.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

General recommendations that are safe for anyone accepted in this symbolic tradition - and at the same time useful regardless of any esoteric views:

1. Perform smooth movements of the hips and lower abdomen — dancing, stretching, walking — to symbolically support the sacral center.
2. Allow yourself conscious, moderate pleasure during the day instead of suppressing desires or pursuing them uncontrollably.
3. Practice pausing briefly before an automatic response to an urge—for food, shopping, texting—to distinguish a real urge from a habit.
4. Pay attention to warm water procedures, which symbolically support the feeling of bodily fluidity and pleasure.
5. Calling one's own desires honestly - to oneself or to a loved one - instead of hiding them or acting as deceivers.
6. Limit access to sources of easy, quick stimulation that replace deeper pleasure, preferring slower, meaningful joys.
7. Notice when jealousy or the need to control another person replaces trust in one's own attractiveness, and consciously return attention to oneself.
8. If necessary, seek support from a doctor or psychotherapist — this complements and does not replace any spiritual practice.

## Main Task

In traditional symbolism, the main "task" of this energy is the ability to consciously own one's own desires and attractions - without suppressing them for the sake of external "correctness" and not allowing them to control every decision. This is a general recommendation to maintain a balance between pleasure and self-control, not a medical prescription.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I allow myself conscious pleasure, or do I suppress desires or pursue them without measure?
- Do I feel like I'm driving my own trains, or are they driving me more often?
- How often does jealousy or the need to control replace my trust in my own attractiveness?
- Do I use charm and influence for sincere contact, or as a way to get something hidden?
- Do I turn to people or professionals for support when I feel that some habit or urge is controlling me more than I would like?

## Practical Plan

1. Perform several smooth movements of the hips and lower abdomen, paying attention to the feeling of bodily freedom.
2. Identify one desire or habit that has been driving you lately and name it honestly, without self-judgment.
3. Carry out a warm water procedure, focusing on the feeling of pleasure without rushing.
4. If there are signs of addiction, compulsive cravings or health concerns, consult a doctor or psychotherapist.
5. Allow yourself one conscious, moderate pleasure today, naming it to yourself in advance.
