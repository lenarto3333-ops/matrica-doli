# 13. Death — Chakra Health Map

> This section describes only the traditional esoteric associations and symbolic counterparts accepted in the popular chakra tradition. It is not a medical diagnosis, does not establish the causes of any diseases and does not replace a doctor's consultation. If there are real symptoms or health concerns, this is a reason to consult a specialist, and not to rely on an esoteric interpretation.

## Short summary

In traditional chakra symbolism, the thirteenth arcana is associated with the root chakra, or Muladhara, a center associated in popular esotericism with a sense of security, stability, and the ability to let go of what no longer supports your foundation. This is not a medical statement about the causes of diseases, but a traditional symbolic view: if a person is afraid to complete what has already exhausted himself, or, on the contrary, destroys the foundations of his life too abruptly and recklessly, in this tradition it is customary to pay attention to the state and well-being associated with this symbolic zone, and to pay more attention to the feeling of support and conscious renewal.

## Natural force

In traditional symbolism, this energy is associated with Muladhara's natural ability to renew the foundation without destroying the sense of support: the ability to let go of the old while maintaining basic stability. Symbolically, it is associated with a general feeling of solid, calm confidence that even after the completion of a certain stage, there is still ground under one's feet to lean on.

A second traditional association is the natural urge to renew the foundations of life: in this symbolic system, the ability to timely and consciously complete what has passed away is considered to be what maintains the harmonious state of the root chakra, as long as this renewal does not turn into a chaotic destruction of everything at once.

## Manifestation in the plus

In the traditional symbolic sense, the "plus" of this energy is associated with a general feeling of stability, which is not afraid of change: a person symbolically feels solid support under his feet even when he completes an important stage of life. She is credited with a tendency towards an even posture, a calm, confident step, a sense of general physical stability and endurance, which does not depend on how much everything around changes.

In this tradition, it is considered auspicious to regularly maintain a sense of connection with the body and the earth, to allow oneself to consciously complete old stages, and to trust that when completed, the foundation is renewed, not lost. This is seen as symbolic support for the harmonious state of the root chakra.

### Manifestation in women is positive

In traditional symbolism for a woman, the favorable state of this energy is associated with the ability to feel self-reliance even during significant changes - moving, ending a relationship or a stage of life. It is recommended to symbolically support practices that strengthen the feeling of connection with the body and the earth: calm walking, contact with the feet, feeling the weight of one's own body.

### Manifestation in the husband is positive

For a man in this tradition, a favorable condition is associated with the ability to recognize that the old support has exhausted itself, and consciously build a new one, not holding on to the form for the sake of stability itself. Symbolically, it is considered useful to regularly check whether his confidence rests on a living foundation, and not on a habit that has already lost its meaning.

## Manifestation in the minus

In traditional symbolism, either excessive clinging to the old foundations of life for fear of losing support, or, on the contrary, a sudden and thoughtless destruction of everything is immediately associated with a state of root chakra imbalance. This is not a medical diagnosis, but a symbolic description of an experience that in this tradition is usually associated with a general feeling of heaviness or tension in the lower body and legs, a feeling of "the ground slipping away from under the feet", inner anxiety or loss of a sense of stability.

### Manifestation in a woman is negative

In a woman, it can symbolically manifest itself as a panicked retention of the usual way of life even when it has long been exhausting, which is traditionally associated with tension in the lower back and legs and a general feeling of internal fatigue from the constant fear of change.

### Manifestation in the husband is negative

In a man, this can be symbolically manifested as sudden, rash decisions to tear everything down and start over, without respect for what has already been built, which in this tradition is associated with general tension in the hips and lower back, which arises from an internal conflict between the fear of getting stuck and the desire to let go too abruptly.

## What can be negative

In traditional symbolism, triggers include the sudden loss of a familiar support, the forced need to complete something important without the right to prepare, as well as an environment where any change is perceived as a threat or betrayal. The prolonged prohibition of the natural renewal of life's foundations, when a person is constantly kept in an outdated form against his inner need to move on, is considered in this tradition to be one of the strongest debilitating factors for this very symbolic center.

## How to return energy to the plus side

General recommendations that are safe for anyone accepted in this symbolic tradition - and at the same time useful regardless of any esoteric views:

1. Regularly devote time to grounding practices — calm walking barefoot, conscious contact of feet with the surface, feeling the weight of one's own body.
2. Consciously determine which sphere of life has already exhausted itself, and allow yourself to gradually, and not abruptly, complete this stage.
3. Perform simple exercises for the legs, lower back and lower body that relieve accumulated tension.
4. Create small rituals of completion and beginning - cleaning the space, symbolic gestures that mark the transition to a new stage.
5. Allow yourself to feel temporary instability during the change, without trying to immediately find a new "solid ground" at any cost.
6. Practice slow, deep breathing with a focus on the feeling of support in the legs and pelvic area.
7. Avoid sharp, impulsive decisions to destroy everything at once — give preference to gradual, conscious renewal.
8. If necessary, seek support from a doctor or psychotherapist — this complements and does not replace any spiritual practice.

## The main task

In traditional symbolism, the main "task" of this energy is considered to be the ability to renew the foundation of life without panic and without excessive destruction - to allow oneself to complete what has exhausted itself, while maintaining a basic sense of support. This is a general recommendation to maintain a balance between stability and renewal, not a medical prescription.

## Questions for self-reflection

- Do I allow myself to complete what has already exhausted me, or do I hold on to the old for fear of losing support?
- Do I feel a connection with my own body and the earth when there are significant changes in my life?
- How often are my decisions about change abrupt and impulsive, rather than gradual and deliberate?
- Do I have a feeling that a new support will appear after completing one stage?
- Do I turn to people or professionals for support when the sense of stability is shaken?

## Practical plan

1. Identify one area of ​​life that needs a conscious completion, and outline gradual steps towards it.
2. Allocate time this week to practice grounding — a walk, contact with the feet, calm breathing.
3. Perform several exercises for the legs and lower back, if physical tension is felt in this area.
4. If there is long-term anxiety, a feeling of loss of support or concern about the state of health, consult a doctor or psychotherapist.
5. Create a small completion ritual for the phase that is coming to an end and mark the beginning of a new one.
