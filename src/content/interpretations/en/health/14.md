# 14. Temperance — Chakra Health Map

> This section describes only the traditional esoteric associations and symbolic counterparts accepted in the popular chakra tradition. It is not a medical diagnosis, does not establish the causes of any diseases and does not replace a doctor's consultation. If there are real symptoms or health concerns, this is a reason to consult a specialist, and not to rely on an esoteric interpretation.

## Brief Essence

In traditional chakra symbolism, the fourteenth arcana is associated with the heart chakra, or Anahata, a center associated in popular esotericism with the ability to combine opposites, maintain inner balance, and maintain a harmonious exchange between the body and the senses. This is not a medical statement about the causes of diseases, but a traditional symbolic view: if a person holds back feelings for too long or, on the contrary, rushes between extremes, in this tradition it is customary to pay attention to the state and well-being associated with this symbolic zone, and to pay more attention to an even, calm rhythm of life.

## Natural Strength

In traditional symbolism, this energy is associated with Anahata's natural ability to maintain a balance between opposites without sacrificing any of them: the ability to combine reason and feelings, activity and rest, own needs and the needs of others. Symbolically, it is associated with a general feeling of an even, calm internal rhythm, in which there are no sudden changes.

The second traditional association is the natural urge to harmonize everything that comes into conflict: in this symbolic system, the ability to find a middle ground, to blend opposites into something whole, is considered to be what maintains a harmonious state of the heart chakra, if this middle ground does not turn into a constant suppression of one's feelings for the sake of external peace.

## Expression in the Positive

In the traditional symbolic understanding, the positive expression of this energy is associated with a general feeling of uniform vitality, which does not depend on sudden surges or declines: a person is symbolically attributed to a calm, stable rhythm of breathing and heartbeat, ease of movement, a feeling of internal composure without tension. They are also associated with a tendency to a soft posture, even, relaxed breathing, a general feeling of physical harmony between different parts of the body.

In this tradition, it is considered auspicious to regularly maintain moderation in everything—exertion, rest, nutrition, emotional expression—avoiding both exhausting extremes and the stagnation of overcaution. This is seen as symbolic support for the harmonious state of the heart chakra.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In traditional symbolism for a woman, the favorable state of this energy is associated with the ability to smoothly combine different roles and feelings, without being torn between them and without suppressing any part of herself. It is recommended to symbolically support practices that maintain an even rhythm: calm breathing, moderate physical exertion, alternation of activity and rest without sudden changes.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

For a man in this tradition, a favorable state is associated with the ability to recognize and experience feelings gradually, without driving them inside for the sake of an image of imperturbability. Symbolically, it is considered useful to regularly check whether restrained tension does not accumulate under external calm, and to give it an outlet in a soft, moderate form.

## Expression in the Negative

In traditional symbolism, either long-term suppression of feelings for the sake of external harmony, or, on the contrary, sharp emotional swings between extremes is associated with a state of imbalance of the heart chakra. This is not a medical diagnosis, but a symbolic description of an experience, which in this tradition is usually associated with a general feeling of unevenness in the chest area, unstable breathing, a feeling of an inner gap between what a person shows and what he actually lives.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, it can symbolically manifest itself as a chronic restraint of one's own needs for the sake of preserving peace around, which is traditionally associated with a feeling of tightness in the chest area and general fatigue from constantly balancing on the edge of one's own strength.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, this can be symbolically manifested as sharp, unexpected outbursts of feelings after a long period of restraint, which in this tradition is associated with uneven breathing and tension in the chest area, which appears from an internal conflict between the desire to remain calm and the accumulated need to express feelings.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

In traditional symbolism, the triggers include the chronic pressure to constantly be "the one who reconciles", the need to repeatedly restrain one's own feelings for the sake of mutual agreement, as well as an environment where any deviation from balance is perceived as weakness or a betrayal of peace. The prolonged prohibition of the natural, gradual expression of emotions, when one is constantly forced either to decide instantly or to postpone the decision endlessly, is considered in this tradition to be one of the strongest debilitating factors for this particular symbolic center.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

General recommendations that are safe for anyone accepted in this symbolic tradition - and at the same time useful regardless of any esoteric views:

1. Practice even, calm breathing with an emphasis on smooth exhalation to maintain a sense of balance in the chest area.
2. Avoid extremes in exercise and rest, preferring a moderate, regular rhythm of the day.
3. Perform simple exercises to open the chest and shoulders, which relieve the accumulated tension in this area.
4. To allow oneself to experience feelings gradually instead of restraining them to the extreme point or pouring them out immediately and sharply.
5. Create small moments of creativity or aesthetic harmony during the day - they symbolically support the state of the heart center.
6. Practice moderation in diet and regimen, avoiding both strict restrictions and excesses.
7. Consciously determine one decision instead of endless procrastination — this reduces internal stress from constant weighing.
8. If necessary, seek support from a doctor or psychotherapist — this complements and does not replace any spiritual practice.

## Main Task

In traditional symbolism, the main "task" of this energy is the ability to maintain an even, lively rhythm between opposites - without suppressing feelings for the sake of external peace and without rushing to extremes from accumulated tension. This is a general recommendation to maintain a balance between restraint and sincerity, not a medical prescription.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I allow myself to experience feelings gradually, or do I hold them back until the moment when they come out suddenly?
- Do I feel an even, calm rhythm in my body throughout the day, or does it constantly go astray?
- How often do I sacrifice my own needs for the sake of maintaining external harmony?
- Am I able to make one decision in time, or am I stuck in an endless search for the perfect balance?
- Do I turn to people or professionals for support when I feel an inner gap between peace and accumulated feelings?

## Practical Plan

1. Take a few minutes to practice steady breathing, paying attention to the sensations in the chest area.
2. Identify one area of life where restrained tension has accumulated, and allow yourself a gradual, rather than abrupt, release from it.
3. Perform several exercises for the chest and shoulders, if physical stiffness is felt in this area.
4. If there is long-term anxiety, uneven well-being or health concerns, consult a doctor or psychotherapist.
5. Create a small moment of creativity or aesthetic harmony today, even the simplest.
