# 19. The Sun — Readiness for a Relationship

## Brief Essence

Readiness for a relationship in this energy is tested by whether a person is able to sincerely share warmth and joy with a partner, without turning the relationship into a stage where you must always shine, and without burning yourself or your partner out with the constant need to be a source of light for two. When natural generosity does not demand confirmation in return and leaves room for difficult, difficult conversations, the connection becomes a space of living warmth, and not an exhausting spectacle of an eternal celebration.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this energy in readiness for a relationship is genuine warmth: a person does not play love or care, but truly feels the joy of sharing it with a partner, and it is felt immediately.

The second strength is the scale of feelings: when this person loves, he loves generously and openly, is able to inspire his partner to joint big plans and believe in a joint future without small fears and limitations.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, readiness manifests itself as the ability to build relationships on sincere, generous warmth: partners feel accepted and supported, and a person shares joy without expecting that the partner must constantly confirm her value in return.

Readiness in a plus also means the ability to stay close in difficult moments of a relationship, and not only in light and pleasant ones, not to run away from difficult conversations for the sake of maintaining a light, cloudless atmosphere.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, readiness in the plus is manifested as a warm, sincere presence next to her partner, inspiring and supporting him without the need to constantly be the center of attention or to receive recognition for every show of care.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, readiness in the plus is manifested as the ability to lead a relationship with a large-scale, generous vision of a joint future, inspiring his partner with his confidence, and at the same time remaining honest about his own doubts or fatigue, and not just playing the role of strong and cloudless.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, unpreparedness manifests itself as narcissism within a relationship: a person assumes the role of an eternal source of joy and support for a partner, hides his own fatigue or dissatisfaction behind a smile, until internal exhaustion begins to eat away at the very closeness that he tried to preserve.

Another manifestation of unpreparedness is the pride and burnout of the partner: a person is so used to being the main source of energy and initiative in a couple that he devalues ​​the partner's contribution, puts pressure on the scale of his own expectations and avoids difficult topics in order not to spoil the image of an ideal, always happy relationship.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, unpreparedness in the minus can manifest as a constant role of "the one who keeps the mood" in a couple: she hides her own fatigue or disappointment behind the concern for the atmosphere in the relationship, until the internal exhaustion breaks through with a sharp emotional breakdown or loss of interest in the partner.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, unpreparedness in the minus can manifest as a pressure of the scale of his own ambitions and plans on his partner, when her pace or doubts are perceived as a brake, and difficult or vulnerable conversations are deliberately avoided in order to preserve the image of a confident, indomitable man.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The trigger is often the role of the "sun of the family", which a person learned in childhood - when her sadness or weakness were invisible, and she received love and attention only for cheering everyone up. The second trigger is a relationship in which the generosity has not been returned for a long time: the person has given warmth and support for a long time without receiving enough in return, and this exhaustion gradually turns into either hidden self-loathing or irritated superiority towards the partner.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to admit to your partner your own fatigue, doubts or need for support, instead of playing the role of an eternally sunny person. The second step is to consciously stay in the difficult conversations with the relationship, rather than bypassing them for the sake of ease, and notice when generosity turns into pressure or exhaustion for one of the two.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to share warmth and joy in a relationship, without demanding it in return and without burning yourself from the inside, and to have the courage to live difficult topics with your partner as openly as bright and joyful ones.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I allow myself to show my partner fatigue or vulnerability, or do I always try to look strong and calm?
- Do we avoid difficult conversations with our partner just to keep the atmosphere light?
- Do I feel that my generosity in the relationship is coming back to me, or have I been living in exhaustion from constant giving back for a long time?

## Practical Plan

1. This week, tell your partner honestly about your own fatigue or doubt once, instead of the usual role of the cheerleader.
2. To notice a topic that the couple usually avoids for the sake of lightness, and deliberately bring it up in a calm conversation.
3. Discuss with your partner how balanced giving and support have been in the relationship recently, and rebalance if necessary.
