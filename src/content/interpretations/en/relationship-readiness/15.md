# 15. The Devil — Readiness for a Relationship

## Brief Essence

Relationship readiness in this energy is associated with the ability to create a deep, magnetic connection with a partner, based on honest passion, not on the power of one over the other: when inner freedom is stable, attraction becomes a space of true intimacy. When a person himself depends on the external - substances, habits, other people's approval - the relationship easily turns into a field of ownership, jealousy and hidden control instead of a sincere union of two free people.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this energy in readiness for a relationship is the ability to see a partner truly, through any masks, to understand his real needs and desires more deeply than he himself is aware of them. This creates an opportunity for a rare, almost intuitive intimacy in which the partner feels truly known.

The second power is a powerful personal magnetism that attracts and holds the attention of a partner naturally, without games or calculations. Relationships with such a person are rarely fresh - they always have passion, depth and a sense of living attraction.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, readiness is manifested as the ability to build relationships based on sincere passion and deep mutual knowledge, in which magnetism does not conquer, but combines: partners are attracted to each other freely, without fear of losing themselves or controlling the other.

Readiness in the positive also means the ability to keep a balance between the depth of intimacy and respect for the partner's autonomy - a person does not try to possess another or dissolve him in himself, but builds a relationship where the power of attraction strengthens trust, and does not create dependence.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In a woman, readiness in the positive is manifested as a bright, attractive femininity, combined with a deep understanding of a partner - she knows how to be desirable and at the same time free, without turning the relationship into a struggle for control.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In a man, readiness in the positive is manifested as a confident, stable force of attraction - the ability to be a reliable, passionate partner who is aware of his own influence on the other and does not deliberately abuse it for power in the relationship.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, unpreparedness manifests itself as possessive jealousy and the need to control a partner - the fear of losing him turns into surveillance, restrictions or emotional pressure, when love is replaced by trying to keep the other by force, not by trust.

Another manifestation of unreadiness is dependence on the relationship itself or on a specific person: the partner becomes something without which the person feels as if he cannot function, and this dependence makes the relationship suffocating and exhausting for both.

### Expression in Women (Negative)

In a woman, unpreparedness in the negative can manifest as intense jealousy and attempts to emotionally bind a partner to herself - through drama, guilt or excessive control over his time and environment, instead of trusting him.

### Expression in Men (Negative)

In a man, unpreparedness in the negative can manifest as a hidden desire to possess a partner - to control her choices, environment or independence - masquerading as concern, when in reality it is a fear of losing power over the relationship.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The trigger is often one's own insecurity or inner emptiness that a person tries to fill with a partner, substance or habit instead of turning to their own source of strength. The second trigger is the experience of betrayal or loss in the past, after which control over the partner seems the only way to protect oneself from repeated pain.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to honestly recognize the moment when jealousy or the need to control a partner appears, and to look for its real source - one's own insecurities, not the behavior of the other. The second step is to consciously build trust instead of control: talk about fears directly, instead of acting through pressure, surveillance, or silent limitations.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to attract and hold intimacy through the power of honest passion and trust, not through control, dependence or fear of loss, and to be a free partner for a free person around.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Have I tried to keep a partner with control or pressure instead of trust?
- How much does my well-being in a relationship depend on my partner, and how much does it depend on me?
- Where does my jealousy really come from - from a real threat or from my inner insecurity?

## Practical Plan

1. Notice this week one moment of jealousy or the desire to control a partner and name its real reason.
2. Replace one habitual act of control or surveillance with a direct, honest conversation about your fear or need.
3. Take one step toward greater personal independence from your partner — a class, meeting, or decision based solely on your own strength.
