# 13. Death — Readiness for a Relationship

## Short summary

Being ready for a relationship in this energy is about being able to let go of old patterns, images of former partners, or outdated scripts to make room for a real, new connection: when there is courage to complete what is no longer alive, relationships are built on pure, present choice. When a person clings to the past or, on the contrary, abruptly breaks off everything at the first hint of difficulties, the partnership either cannot begin or is destroyed prematurely.

## Natural force

The natural strength of this energy in relationship readiness is the ability to honestly recognize when a connection or pattern of relationship behavior has exhausted itself, and not keep it artificially alive out of pity or fear of loneliness. A person with this energy rarely remains in a dead relationship out of inertia.

The second strength is the ability to enter a new relationship, completely letting go of the previous experience, without transferring resentment, fears or expectations from the past to the new partner. This gives a rare freshness of view to each new connection.

## Manifestation in the plus

On the plus side, readiness manifests itself as the ability to consciously complete what is no longer working in a relationship—certain patterns, roles, and, if necessary, the relationship itself—with dignity, not despair or blame. A person does not hold on to the form if there is no content left in it.

Readiness in the plus also means the ability to completely turn the page of the past experience before building a new one, and the ability to support a living, evolving relationship, allowing it to transform with both partners, rather than being frozen in one form forever.

### Manifestation in women is positive

In a woman, readiness in the plus is manifested as the ability to decisively end a relationship or a pattern of behavior that is already causing damage, while maintaining dignity and respect for herself and her partner, and not descending into drama or protracted conflict.

### Manifestation in the husband is positive

In a man, willingness in a plus is manifested as the courage to admit when a relationship or the usual way has exhausted itself and take the necessary step to change, instead of maintaining the appearance for years, behind which there is nothing.

## Manifestation in the minus

In the negative, unpreparedness manifests itself as an inability to let go of past relationships even after a formal breakup — a person continues to live with memories, comparisons, or the hope of returning what is no longer there, and this prevents being present in a new relationship.

Another manifestation of unpreparedness is the opposite extreme: the habit of abruptly and harshly ending a relationship at the first serious difficulty, instead of going through the crisis and giving the relationship a chance to transform.

### Manifestation in a woman is negative

In a woman, unpreparedness in the minus can manifest as a long-term emotional stuck in the past relationship - comparing the new partner with the previous one, fear of repeating the old pain - which does not allow the new relationship to develop on its own terms.

### Manifestation in the husband is negative

In a man, unpreparedness in the minus can manifest itself as a sharp, cold break in the relationship at the first signs of difficulties, when it is easier to break things off than to go through an uncomfortable, but necessary process of change.

## What can be negative

The trigger is often a painful experience of past loss or betrayal, which has taught us to hold on too tightly to the familiar repetition of pain, or, on the contrary, to cut ties in advance so as not to feel the loss again. The second trigger is a relationship that has clearly exhausted itself, but the fear of loneliness or the unknown forces them to maintain it artificially.

## How to return energy to the plus side

It is useful to consciously carry out the psychological closure of the previous experience in the relationship - to admit what happened, to let go of resentments and expectations, before fully opening up to a new relationship. The second step is to learn to see difficulties in the current relationship as an opportunity for transformation, and not immediately as a signal to break up, paying attention to whether the relationship has truly exhausted itself or is simply going through a difficult phase.

## The main task

The main task is to find the honesty and courage to complete what is truly exhausting in the relationship, and at the same time trust the connections that can transform and grow with you.

## Questions for self-reflection

- Do I have a past relationship experience that I still haven't fully let go of?
- Do I tend to hold on to old relationships or, on the contrary, break off everything at the first difficulty?
- What will help me to distinguish a relationship that is worth ending, from those that are just going through a difficult stage of transformation?

## Practical plan

1. Write down what it is from the past experience of the relationship that still needs a conscious completion, and take one step towards it - symbolic or real.
2. In your current relationship, honestly evaluate one behavior pattern or habit that is no longer working and discuss it with your partner instead of silently building up tension.
3. To notice this week the moment when there is a desire to abruptly end a difficult conversation or situation in a relationship, and instead consciously stay in it one step longer.
