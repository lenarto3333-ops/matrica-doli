On a karmic level, this arcana is tied to a fear of making the wrong decision at a critical moment, which switches on prolonged indecision in matters concerning children or parents — things that require action stay suspended, waiting for the "right moment."

**Main mistakes:** postponing important family conversations and decisions indefinitely; a passive "I'll wait for it to resolve itself" stance in situations where loved ones need your support now; a hidden resentment toward loved ones for not understanding your inner pause.

**How to turn it positive:** use your ability to see situations from another angle to make thoughtful yet timely decisions — don't let the pause turn into endless waiting.
