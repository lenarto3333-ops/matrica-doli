On a karmic level, this arcana carries a fear of uncontrollable turns of fate — a memory of a time when chance once changed everything for the worse. In its shadow expression, this surfaces as anxiety about the future of children or parents, and attempts to control what naturally unfolds on its own.

**Main mistakes:** resisting the inevitable changes in family life — children growing up, parents aging, circumstances shifting; trying to "prepare for every possibility," which turns into overprotectiveness; anxiously expecting the worst instead of trusting life's flow.

**How to turn it positive:** practice trusting that change is a natural part of life, not a threat; focus on what you can give your loved ones now, rather than trying to control the future.
