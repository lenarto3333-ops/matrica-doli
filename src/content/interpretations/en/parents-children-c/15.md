On a karmic level, this arcana carries a fear of repeating an ancestral pattern of dependency or a lack of freedom — perhaps a memory of ancestors whose lives were constrained by circumstance or someone else's will. In its shadow expression, this fear shows up as worry that children or parents will fall into a dependency they can't escape.

**Main mistakes:** imposing strict bans and total control "for their own safety," which breeds secrecy in return; unconsciously reproducing codependent patterns within your own family; turning a blind eye to real dependency patterns in loved ones out of fear of acknowledging the problem.

**How to turn it positive:** name dependent patterns — your own and the family's — directly and without judgment, and search together for a path to real freedom, not just its appearance.
