This arcana carries a deep-seated fear of not being a "good enough" pillar for the family — sometimes rooted in your own childhood, where care was lacking. In its shadow expression, this spills into anxious overprotectiveness toward children or parents, as if disaster would strike without your constant involvement.

**Main mistakes:** smothering care that leaves loved ones no room for independence; sacrificing yourself for the family to the point of complete burnout; jealousy and resentment when your care feels "insufficiently appreciated."

**How to turn it positive:** learn to care for yourself as generously as you care for others — only a full reserve lets you give care without exhausting yourself or secretly expecting gratitude in return.
