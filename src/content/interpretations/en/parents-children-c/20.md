On a karmic level, this arcana carries a fear of judgment for past decisions — perhaps a memory of ancestral guilt or an unjust verdict. In its shadow expression, this shows up as constant inner judgment: you evaluate your own parenting, or your relationship with your parents, with excessive harshness, afraid of repeating others' mistakes.

**Main mistakes:** turning that harsh self-judgment onto children or parents in the form of criticism and nitpicking; being unable to forgive yourself or your loved ones for past missteps in the relationship; anxiously "replaying" the past instead of building the present.

**How to turn it positive:** learn to draw lessons from the past without self-flagellation — honestly acknowledge mistakes, forgive yourself and your loved ones, and use that experience to rebuild the relationship.
