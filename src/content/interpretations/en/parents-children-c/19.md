This arcana carries a karmic fear of losing warmth and openness within the family line — perhaps a memory of a time when sincerity was unsafe. In its shadow expression, this shows up as worry about the emotional state of children or parents, which you try to drown out with forced lightness and cheerfulness.

**Main mistakes:** avoiding serious and painful topics in the family under the excuse of "let's not talk about sad things"; dismissing loved ones' worries and struggles by rushing to cheer them up; superficial communication instead of genuine emotional closeness.

**How to turn it positive:** allow yourself and your loved ones to experience not only joy but difficult feelings too — real warmth in a family includes the right to be heard in pain, not only in laughter.
