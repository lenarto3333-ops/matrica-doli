This arcana suggests that in past lives, your soul and your loved ones' souls may have lost each other or been in conflict. Because of this, in your current life a strong subconscious fear for the life, health, and fate of your children or parents can flare up in the shadow expression. This fear often disguises itself as rigid control and an unwillingness to let loved ones go.

**Main mistakes:** clinging to the past, to old grievances long outlived, unwillingness to release past negative experiences; outbursts of harshness, aggression, uncontrolled emotion, or attempts to force change on loved ones; resisting natural transitions (such as children growing up and becoming independent).

**How to turn it positive:** learn to see life and relationships as a continuous cycle of renewal. Release grievances toward your parents in good time, and give your children the freedom to walk their own life path.
