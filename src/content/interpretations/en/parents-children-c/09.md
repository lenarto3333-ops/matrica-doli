This arcana carries a karmic fear of being misunderstood or abandoned if you reveal your true depth to loved ones — perhaps an echo of past rejection. In its shadow expression, worry for children or parents pulls you into even deeper withdrawal exactly when they need your support most.

**Main mistakes:** retreating into yourself during moments of family tension instead of talking; refusing to share your worry, which leaves loved ones feeling distanced and rejected; expecting loved ones to "figure it out themselves" without direct explanation.

**How to turn it positive:** use your gift for deep reflection to share your insights with the family instead of hiding them — time alone should restore your connection, not replace it.
