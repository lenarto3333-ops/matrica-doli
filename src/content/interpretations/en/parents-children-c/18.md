This arcana gives you a profoundly deep, delicate inner world and an intuitive sense of your loved ones. At its best, an invisible, almost telepathic connection exists between you and your children or parents.

**Main mistakes:** withdrawing into closedness, emotional detachment, and reluctance to discuss problems; escaping family troubles into an imaginary world, illusions, or dependencies (games, TV shows); an overwhelming fear for your children's future, health, and life that smothers them with your anxiety.

**How to turn it positive:** learn to put your intuitive worry into words instead of hiding within yourself — an open conversation about your feelings turns your fine sensitivity into a pillar for the family instead of a source of alienation.
