This arcana carries a karmic fear of "apostasy" — breaking with family traditions and beliefs, which in the past could have meant being cast out of the family. In its shadow expression, this becomes anxiety whenever children or parents choose a path different from the one accepted in the family.

**Main mistakes:** imposing your own beliefs and life script as the only correct ones; dismissing a child's or parent's differing opinion; using guilt and obligation ("we invested so much in you") as tools of pressure.

**How to turn it positive:** share your experience as an invitation, not an order — let your loved ones take from your knowledge whatever resonates with them and find their own path.
