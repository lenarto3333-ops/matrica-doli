This arcana carries a karmic fear of extremes and conflict — perhaps a memory of a devastating quarrel or rupture within the family line. In its shadow expression, worry about peace in the family makes you avoid any sensitive topics involving children or parents, just to preserve the appearance of harmony.

**Main mistakes:** staying silent about real problems to "keep the peace"; constant compromises at the expense of your own needs and boundaries; postponing important decisions out of fear of disturbing the family's fragile balance.

**How to turn it positive:** remember that real harmony isn't built by avoidance but by honestly discussing what's difficult — gradually raise uncomfortable topics instead of waiting for tension to build up.
