This arcana carries a karmic fear of your own rage or helplessness — perhaps a memory of a situation where emotions spiraled out of control and hurt loved ones. In its shadow expression, you spend years suppressing worry and irritation over your children or parents until it erupts in a sudden outburst.

**Main mistakes:** long, silent patience instead of talking about your feelings in time; sudden flares of anger, disproportionate to the trigger, after long suppression; guilt after an outburst that pushes you right back into suppressing your emotions.

**How to turn it positive:** learn to express your strength gently and in good time — name irritation or worry in words at an early stage, instead of waiting for the boiling point.
