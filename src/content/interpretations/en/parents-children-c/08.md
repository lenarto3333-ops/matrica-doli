On a karmic level, this arcana is tied to a fear of injustice or betrayal within the family line, which switches on a sharp, anxious vigilance: any imbalance of attention or care in the family feels like a threat.

**Main mistakes:** constantly weighing "who owes whom more" among children, parents, and yourself; excessive criticism of loved ones and yourself over the smallest unfairness; letting unspoken grievances pile up instead of talking openly about balance.

**How to turn it positive:** replace the scorekeeping with an honest conversation about everyone's actual needs — in family relationships, honesty matters more than mathematical fairness.
