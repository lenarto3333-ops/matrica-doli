This arcana carries a karmic memory of moments when losing control brought catastrophe to the family line — the loss of home, support, or the safety of loved ones. In its shadow expression, this surfaces as a subconscious fear: if you don't keep everything under personal control, something bad will happen to your children or parents.

**Main mistakes:** total control over your loved ones' decisions and actions disguised as care; an inability to delegate even small everyday matters; irritation and dismissiveness when a child or parent shows their own initiative, which you perceive as a threat to your authority in the family.

**How to turn it positive:** channel your will to control into a will to support — trust your loved ones to handle part of the tasks themselves, even if the result isn't perfect.
