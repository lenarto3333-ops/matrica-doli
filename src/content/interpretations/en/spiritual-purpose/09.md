# 9. The Hermit — Spiritual Purpose

## In Brief

The spiritual purpose of the ninth arcana is to journey inward before guiding anyone else. This is the energy of a seeker who deliberately steps away from the noise to find the truth beneath the surface of things. Only after genuinely completing this journey do they gain the right and the ability to guide others. The purpose here is not to leave people forever, but to return to them with wisdom gained in silence.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this energy is the ability to endure the solitude of the search without fleeing difficult questions about meaning, the self, and the world by escaping into external commotion. Where others drown anxiety in noise, this soul enters the silence to hear its own answer.

A second strength is rare honesty with oneself: a person with this energy is not satisfied with convenient, ready-made answers from others and continues searching until they feel an inner resonance with the truth.

## Positive Expression

In its positive expression, this spiritual purpose unfolds as the path of an inner seeker who matures into a teacher, mentor, or spiritual guide—not by title, but because their words truly help others find their own way, having been tested through personal experience.

Positive fulfillment of this purpose also means knowing how to hold space for another person’s search without imposing ready-made answers. The person shares the wisdom they have gained in a way that allows the other to find their own truth rather than borrow someone else’s.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

For women, the positive expression of this spiritual purpose often appears as the quiet inner strength of a trusted adviser whom others seek out in life’s hardest moments. She does not try to rescue them or decide on their behalf, but helps them see what they already know deep down.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

For men, the positive expression of this spiritual purpose often unfolds as an independent search for truth through practice, study, or long periods of solitary reflection, after which his words begin to carry natural weight for those who follow him.

## Negative Expression

In its negative expression, this spiritual purpose becomes stuck at the stage of withdrawal and never matures into a return. A person enters solitude not as a space for searching, but as a refuge from the world, and remains there, replacing a spiritual path with justified isolation.

Another negative sign is spiritual pride: the depth one has gained begins to foster a quiet sense of superiority over those who “are not ready yet,” instead of becoming a bridge to them.

## What Can Push the Energy into the Negative

Most often, the energy turns negative after an experience in which a person’s sincere search or seriousness was mocked or dismissed. This closes off the desire to share what has been discovered and reinforces the habit of keeping the truth to oneself. A second trigger is pressure to give immediate answers or share wisdom before the inner process of understanding has truly reached completion.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to consciously define periods of solitude as temporary, with the intention of returning and sharing what you have found rather than treating withdrawal as a permanent state. It is also important to find one person or a small group you can trust with the results of your search before addressing a wider audience. Another step is to check regularly whether your depth is motivated by a desire to help or by a desire to feel superior to others.

## Main Task

The main task is to follow the solitary path to truth, then consciously return to others and become a bridge instead of remaining on the far side of the divide.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I use solitude to search for an answer, or as a way to avoid people altogether?
- With whom could I share the wisdom I have gained but still keep to myself?
- Is my depth turning into quiet condescension toward those who think more simply?

## Practical Plan

1. Set a specific end point for your current period of solitary searching and choose a date when you will share the result with someone.
2. Choose one person you can trust with your conclusions or lived experience, and speak with them this week.
3. Notice one moment of inner superiority toward someone else’s “superficiality” and consciously turn it into a desire to explain more simply.
