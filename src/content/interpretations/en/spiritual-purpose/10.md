# 10. Wheel of Fortune — Spiritual Purpose

## Brief Essence

The spiritual purpose of the tenth arcana is to become a living example that trust in life and personal responsibility do not contradict one another. This is the energy of the wheel itself: it turns regardless of human will, but each person decides whether to seize the right moment when it comes. The purpose here is not to wait passively for luck but to move consciously and actively with the flow of life.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength of this energy is the ability to preserve hope and calm even when circumstances seem chaotic or unfavorable. Where others become trapped in despair, this soul intuitively knows that every difficult stretch will pass.

A second strength is a rare flexibility of spirit: a person with this energy does not cling desperately to one form or one path of spiritual inquiry, but allows themselves to move and change along with their understanding of life, trusting that the process itself will lead to the necessary answers.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, spiritual purpose appears as the path of a conscious co-creator of their own destiny who matures into a guide for others—a person whose presence reminds people that there is a way out of every difficult situation, and whose example inspires action rather than merely waiting for a miracle.

Purpose in the positive also means knowing how to share hope in a way that is supported by real, concrete steps rather than remaining empty reassurance.

### Expression in Women (Positive)

In women, spiritual purpose in the positive often appears as warm, practical support for those going through a difficult life transition—she does not merely say that everything will be all right, but helps them find a concrete next step.

### Expression in Men (Positive)

In men, spiritual purpose in the positive often appears as the quiet courage to be the first to take a risk where others hesitate, demonstrating through his own example how trust in the process can be combined with decisive action.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, spiritual purpose becomes stuck at the stage of empty optimism: the person tells others that “everything will be all right” without offering any real support or action, and over time this superficial reassurance ceases to inspire trust.

Another sign of the negative is spiritual fatalism: the belief that everything is predetermined, used as a convenient excuse to make no effort.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

The most common trigger is a prolonged series of setbacks that shakes trust in life and leads either to despair or to hiding behind careless optimism instead of taking real action. A second trigger is the demand to give immediate, confident answers where time is actually needed to accept uncertainty.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is helpful to check consciously whether your trust in life is supported by real action or remains only words. It is also important to acknowledge your own role in what happens instead of placing all responsibility on “fate” or “luck.” Another step is to share not only hope but also concrete support regularly with those passing through a difficult period.

## Main Task

The main task is to become a living bridge between trust in the process and personal responsibility for your actions, rather than remaining on either opposite shore—anxious control or passive waiting for a miracle.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I support my trust in life with real action, or do I leave it only in words?
- Whom could I help with a concrete step instead of empty reassurance?
- Am I hiding behind fatalism to avoid responsibility?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one situation in which you rely on hope alone, and add a concrete plan of action.
2. Support someone going through a difficult period with practical help, not only words of encouragement.
3. Once this week, consciously take responsibility for an outcome instead of explaining it as “fate” or “luck.”
