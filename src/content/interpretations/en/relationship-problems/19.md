# 19. The Sun — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in a relationship is a partner who is so used to being a "light" for others that he hides real fatigue, doubts or pain behind constant cheerfulness, leaving the other without access to real feelings. The second typical knot is pride or an excessive need for recognition, when one of the partners unwittingly "burns out" the other by demanding to constantly maintain his mood and pace.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is genuine warmth, optimism and the ability to fill relationships with joy and lightness, which make the couple attractive to those around them. The problem arises not because of the generosity of feelings itself, but because it turns into an obligation to stay bright all the time, even when it is really difficult.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, this energy makes the relationship a space of sincere joy and support: partners openly share successes and warmth, at the same time giving each other the right to difficult days without the requirement to "hold the mark". Difficult periods pass more easily, because there is trust in the couple, and not just a joint holiday.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties are exacerbated: one of the partners can hide his true feelings behind a constant smile, while exhaustion builds up inside, or, on the contrary, demand from the other constant support of his own good mood, devaluing his right to another emotion. Pride is also possible, when attention and admiration are taken for granted, and the partner gradually feels like only a spectator of someone else's brilliance.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, an aggravation occurs when one of the partners from childhood used to "deserve" love with a good mood and success, and now is afraid to show the other true fatigue or weakness. The second trigger is a relationship where the admiration and recognition of one partner has become the main currency, due to which the other gradually loses his own voice.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to openly share not only joy, but also fatigue or doubt, allowing the partner to see the whole picture, and not just the bright part of it. The second step is to consciously notice your partner's contribution and feelings, and not just expect him to support your own mood and pace.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to be a sincere source of warmth in the relationship, without turning it into a performance or a demand, and to create a space where both partners can show not only joy, but also vulnerability.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Do I allow my partner to see the real me when it's hard, not just cheerful and positive?
- How often do I expect my partner to support my mood and pace without leaving room for his own state?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one moment this week to honestly tell your partner about fatigue or doubt that you usually hide behind cheerfulness.
2. Consciously ask your partner about his condition, without waiting for him to adapt to your mood.
3. Once a month, discuss together whether there is enough room in your relationship for unhappy, real feelings.
