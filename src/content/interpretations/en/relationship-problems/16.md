# 16. The Tower — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in relationships is explosive conflicts that arise suddenly and sharply, as if the tension that has been building up for a long time imperceptibly suddenly bursts forth. The second typical node is a relationship built on an unstable foundation, which from time to time experiences serious crises, because it never had an honest, solid foundation.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is the ability not to be afraid of conflict and to honestly, even harshly, name what has not been working in the relationship for a long time. The problem arises not because of this directness itself, but because of the fact that unlived tension accumulates silently until it erupts in a sharp, almost destructive form.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this energy creates relationships that can withstand serious trials and become stronger after them: partners are not afraid to honestly clarify hot topics while the tension is still small, and together they rebuild what is outdated. A crisis, when it does occur, becomes a point of real convergence, not of rupture.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, difficulties intensify: there are sharp, explosive quarrels, outbursts of anger, words or actions that hurt deeper than intended, and the desire to burn bridges instead of looking for a way out. A recurring crisis is also possible - relationships repeatedly reach the point of breaking up in a similar scenario, because the root cause has never been worked out.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Aggravation most often occurs when one of the partners holds back irritation or dissatisfaction for a long time, without naming it directly, until it explodes with a force disproportionate to the specific occasion. The second trigger is an unlived trauma from a past relationship or family, due to which even a minor conflict today is perceived by the body as a threat of disaster.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to name irritation and dissatisfaction at once, in small, honest portions, instead of remaining silent until the explosion builds up. The second step is to consciously figure out after a crisis which outdated part of the relationship actually needed to be rebuilt, and not just go back to the usual, as if nothing had happened.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to live the inevitable tension in the relationship consciously and in time, turning potential explosions into honest conversations that strengthen, not destroy intimacy.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What irritation or dissatisfaction have I been silently accumulating lately instead of naming it directly?
- Is the crisis repeating in my relationship according to a similar scenario, and what lesson have I still not heard from it?

## Practical Plan

1. The next time you feel irritation, tell your partner right away, in small words, without waiting for it to build up.
2. If there's been a major fight or crisis recently, discuss together which outdated part of the relationship was actually behind it.
3. Once a month, check together whether there is a silent tension in the relationship that can explode suddenly.
