# 6. The Lovers — Common Relationship Difficulties

## In Brief

The typical relationship difficulty of this energy is chronic indecision about the relationship itself: a partner may spend a long time wondering whether this is truly the right choice or whether to keep searching for a “better” option, even while in a perfectly good partnership. Another common knot is avoiding a direct conversation about genuine feelings for fear of causing hurt or disrupting harmony, which eventually leads to accumulated misunderstanding.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength behind these difficulties is a deep sensitivity to the quality of emotional connection: the person genuinely wants the relationship to be sincere rather than formal or forced. The problem arises not from indifference, but from an excessive fear of making the “wrong” choice, which paralyzes the ability to trust a decision once it has been made.

Another underlying strength is the capacity to feel a partner deeply. This makes the person a sensitive and attentive conversational partner, even when their current doubts feel acute.

## Positive Expression

In the positive, this energy resolves its typical difficulties through honest conversation about doubt: partners openly discuss uncertainty instead of concealing it, and that very sincerity strengthens trust rather than undermining it. Conflict becomes an opportunity to understand each other better, not an occasion for blame.

The positive expression also brings the ability to trust the choice already made instead of constantly looking back at imagined alternatives, while accepting that no relationship is perfect by definition.

## Negative Expression

In the negative, the difficulties intensify: one partner senses the other's constant emotional uncertainty, which undermines the feeling of security in the relationship. Arguments arise over how seriously each partner regards their shared future, with both sides feeling that their emotions are being undervalued.

Another typical negative expression is the accumulation of unspoken feelings, when one partner stays silent about dissatisfaction or doubt to preserve harmony until the tension reaches a critical point.

## What Can Shift the Energy into the Negative

Problems most often intensify when the relationship demands a concrete decision or commitment, activating a deep fear of making the wrong choice. Another trigger is a past experience in which an earlier relationship choice led to disappointment.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It helps to name moments of doubt openly instead of hiding them, and to work with your partner to understand the source of the uncertainty. The second step is to check in regularly about whether both partners' feelings remain sincere and alive, rather than holding on to the relationship solely from habit or fear of change.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to trust the choice you have made and speak about doubts directly instead of silently allowing uncertainty to accumulate.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- In what situations do I hide my true feelings to avoid disturbing the harmony?
- Do I trust the choice I have made in this relationship, or do I constantly compare it with imagined alternatives?
- What am I truly afraid of losing when I avoid making a clear decision?

## Practical Plan

1. Have a conversation with your partner about a doubt or feeling you have been hiding.
2. Once this week, consciously say aloud what you truly feel instead of maintaining silent harmony.
3. Write down three reasons why the choice you have made in this relationship deserves your trust.
