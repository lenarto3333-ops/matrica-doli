# 22. The Fool — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in relationships is the avoidance of serious commitments, when closeness is desirable, and responsibility for a shared future is frightening and postponed. The second typical knot is the opposite: the fear of any change in the relationship, due to which a person clings to a partnership that no longer brings joy, simply because it is familiar.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is the sincerity and ease with which a person is able to start a new relationship without the burden of past resentments, and true trust in a partner. The problem arises not because of openness itself, but because it either loses connection with responsibility, or, on the contrary, is suppressed by such fear that any change in the couple seems a threat.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, this energy makes the relationship a space of real freedom: partners are sincere with each other, easily try new joint experiences and at the same time are responsible for the promises made to each other. Trust in a couple does not interfere with honesty, and spontaneity does not cancel out concern for consequences.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties intensify: one of the partners may avoid serious conversations about the future, make promises and not fulfill them, or it is easy to pay attention to new hobbies outside the relationship, running away from the depth. Or, on the contrary, the couple gets stuck in a suffocating routine, afraid of any change or new joint experience that could revitalize the relationship.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, aggravation occurs when closeness begins to be felt as a limitation of freedom, and a person unconsciously looks for ways to distance himself from it. The second trigger is the experience when trying to change something in the relationship ended in pain, after which there is a fear of any new step together.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to call a spade a spade - to speak directly about the fear of commitment or change, instead of escaping into a new flirtation or into a silent routine. The second step is to consciously introduce small doses of new shared experiences into the relationship, which do not threaten the couple, but refresh it.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to combine freedom and sincerity in relationships with responsibility for promises made, without falling into either avoiding obligations or into a frozen, lifeless routine.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is there a promise or topic in my relationship that I avoid because of fear of responsibility?
- Am I clinging to my current relationship only out of fear of change, and not because it truly fulfills me?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one topic that you avoid discussing with your partner for fear of commitment, and start that conversation.
2. Bring one new shared experience into the relationship this month — a small trip, activity, or acquaintance.
3. Once every few weeks, check together whether the relationship provides space for freedom and at the same time for trust and responsibility.
