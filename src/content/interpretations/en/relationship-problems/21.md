# 21. The World — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in relationships is the expectation of the perfect, "perfect" couple or relationship, which makes real, live relationships constantly seem not ready enough to relax into. The second typical node is defensive closure, when the partner or his environment is perceived as something foreign, and instead of union, internal hostility appears.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is the ability to see relationships as a whole picture, to combine different aspects of two people's lives into one common whole, and to sincerely appreciate completed, final agreements. The problem arises not because of the desire for integrity itself, but because it turns into an unattainable ideal or a protective narrowing of the circle of trust.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, this energy makes the relationship a space of true unity: partners know how to bring important conversations and agreements to a real conclusion, and not leave them hanging in the air. A couple easily opens up to new shared experiences — travel, new people, joint big plans — which strengthens the bond, not threatens it.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties are exacerbated: one of the partners can endlessly search for the "ideal" relationship, devaluing the real one through constant comparison with an imaginary model, or, on the contrary, narrow the shared world of the couple, being suspicious of friends, family or new experiences of the partner. It is also possible to have hidden hostility towards everything that goes beyond the usual way of relationships.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, aggravation occurs when a couple is not in the habit of honestly ending important conversations, and every topic remains "a little unfinished". The second trigger is an experience where openness in a relationship has turned into disappointment, after which the habit of protecting oneself with narrowing of trust and control appears.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to bring important conversations and agreements as a couple to a clear, mutually recognized conclusion, rather than leaving them open. The second step is to consciously maintain the couple's openness to the wider world: shared new acquaintances, plans or experiences, instead of being confined to just the two of them.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to appreciate a real, living relationship as a complete value, not an unattainable ideal, and to keep the couple open to the world instead of protective closedness.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Is there an important conversation or agreement in our relationship that we have not brought to a clear conclusion?
- How open are we with our partner to new joint experiences, and not just stick to the usual circle?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one unfinished topic in the relationship and agree with your partner to bring it to a clear, joint solution.
2. During the month, try one new joint experience — a trip, meeting, or activity — that goes beyond the usual boundaries of a couple.
3. Once every few weeks, check together whether there is room for reality in the relationship, and not just for the ideal image.
