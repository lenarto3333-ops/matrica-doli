# 18. The Moon — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty with this energy in a relationship is an unsettling suspicion, when a partner is ascribed feelings, intentions, or thoughts that are not actually there, and an imaginary scenario is taken as reality. The second typical node is the partner who hides true emotions behind silence or rational explanations, leaving the other alone with uncertainty about what is really going on.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is a deep sensitivity to the mood and state of the partner, the ability to catch what has not yet been said out loud. The problem is not the sensitivity itself, but the fact that it is replaced by a disturbing fantasy instead of a real check of what the partner feels.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, this energy makes the relationship a space of deep emotional contact: partners feel each other without words and at the same time openly check assumptions, rather than silently relying on them. Difficult periods pass more easily, because in a couple there is space to admit and experience emotions, and not to hide them.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties are exacerbated: one of the partners can constantly suspect the other without real grounds, imagining betrayal, indifference or disappointment where they do not exist. Or the partner closes down emotionally, suppressing his own feelings, and the other is left alone with a feeling of anxious uncertainty.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

Most often, aggravation occurs when one of the partners has experienced betrayal or deception in the past, and now any uncertainty is automatically perceived as a threat. The second trigger is a long-term habit of hiding true feelings in a relationship, due to which the partner gradually ceases to understand what is really happening.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is helpful to learn to openly test anxious assumptions with words, instead of silently believing an imagined scenario as fact. The second step is to consciously share real emotions with your partner, even uncomfortable ones, instead of hiding them behind silence or rational explanations.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to trust the intuitive feeling about the partner, while checking it with reality, and to create a space in the relationship where emotions can be lived openly, and not hidden or limited.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Have I recently believed a disturbing assumption about a partner without verifying it with words?
- How openly do I share real emotions with my partner, and not hide them behind silence or explanations?

## Practical Plan

1. The next time you have a nagging suspicion about your partner, say it out loud and test it with a direct question instead of guessing.
2. Choose one moment this week to honestly tell your partner about an emotion you usually hide.
3. Once a month, discuss together whether there is enough space in your relationship for the open manifestation of true feelings.
