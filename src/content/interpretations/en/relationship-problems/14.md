# 14. Temperance — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in relationships is the excessive smoothing of conflicts: a person for years gives in to his own needs in order to maintain external peace, until the accumulated dissatisfaction breaks out in a sharp, disproportionate explosion. The second typical node is chronic indecision in a relationship: a person cannot decide for years whether to stay or leave, and lives in a constant state of limbo.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is an innate gift of diplomacy and the ability to bring peace to strained relationships, to see the position of both sides and find a solution that suits everyone. The problem does not arise from this ability itself, but from the fact that the fear of conflict forces it to be used to avoid honesty, rather than for true reconciliation.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this energy solves typical difficulties through honest, gradual dialogue: the partner openly talks about his own needs and boundaries, while maintaining respect for the other party, and conflicts pass more smoothly, because they do not accumulate for months. Relationships develop evenly, through trust, not through silent concessions.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties are exacerbated: the partner silently tolerates what does not suit him, so as not to "spoil" the relationship, until the accumulated resentment erupts in a sharp quarrel or a sudden emotional break. There are also cycles of indecision — sometimes relationships on the verge of breaking up, then reconciling again without real changes, because no decision is reached.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

More often than not, aggravation occurs when the partner holds back the dissatisfaction for a long time, fearing to spoil the harmony, until the tension reaches the limit and erupts out of control. The second trigger is the fear of making a final choice about the future of the relationship, because of which a person remains in uncertainty for years, instead of honestly deciding what he wants.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to voice dissatisfaction in small, regular portions, without waiting for it to accumulate to a critical point. The second step is to consciously set deadlines for important decisions in the relationship, so that a measured approach does not turn into endless procrastination.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to build harmony in relationships through honesty and timely conversations, not through silent patience or endless procrastination.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What do I silently tolerate in a relationship for the sake of keeping peace, although it has not satisfied me for a long time?
- What decision about the future of the relationship have I been putting off for too long?
- Do I warn my partner about my dissatisfaction in time, or do I wait until it breaks out by itself?

## Practical Plan

1. Identify one topic that you have been silent about for a long time in the relationship, and schedule a calm conversation about it in the near future.
2. If there is indecision about the future in the relationship, set an honest deadline for a final decision.
3. Practice expressing small displeasures right away, rather than putting them off for later.
