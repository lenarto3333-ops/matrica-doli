# 12. The Hanged Man — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical relationship difficulty for this energy is becoming stuck over important decisions. The person postpones discussing the future, necessary changes, or personal needs while waiting for a “right moment” that never comes. Another common knot is a partner’s sense of emotional distance when the person withdraws too deeply, leaving the relationship seemingly on pause.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength behind these difficulties is the ability to see the relationship from an unusual angle and notice what a partner misses through habit or haste. The problem arises not from indifference, but because this contemplative ability sometimes grows into excessive detachment.

Another underlying strength is the ability not to act impulsively during conflict, allowing time to become calm before speaking or deciding.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this energy resolves common difficulties by using the pause consciously. A partner takes time to consider a difficult issue, then returns with an honest, thoughtful answer instead of escaping the conversation forever. Seeing the situation differently helps reveal unconventional ways out of conflicts that seemed deadlocked.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties intensify. Important conversations are delayed for months, and the partner feels abandoned in uncertainty, unable to tell whether the relationship is moving forward at all. Arguments arise around emotional unavailability: one partner seeks clarity and a decision, but receives silence and a request for “a little more time.”

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

The situation most often deteriorates when a partner asks for a concrete answer or decision before inner clarity has arrived. Instead of honestly saying, “I need time,” the person simply disappears from contact. Another trigger is fear that every possible decision might be wrong, which paralyzes the attempt to choose at all.

## How to Return Energy to the Positive

Tell your partner honestly when you need time to think instead of disappearing in silence; this is the difference between a considerate pause and abandonment. The second step is setting realistic deadlines for important relationship decisions so the pause does not become endless waiting.

## Main Task

The main task is to combine the natural capacity for contemplation with a willingness to return to your partner with an honest answer, allowing the pause to strengthen the relationship rather than create distance.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- Which important conversation with my partner am I postponing while waiting for the right moment?
- Do I tell my partner honestly when I need time to think, or simply disappear from contact?
- What exactly prevents me from making a decision about our relationship?

## Practical Plan

1. Choose one postponed conversation with your partner and schedule a specific time for it this week.
2. The next time you need a pause during conflict, tell your partner honestly instead of falling silent without explanation.
3. Review one relationship decision being delayed without a clear reason and set a deadline for making it.
