# 2. The High Priestess — Common Relationship Difficulties

## In Brief

The typical difficulty of this energy in relationships is chronic lack of communication: a partner may sense that important feelings or needs are being hidden, even when everything appears calm on the surface. A second common knot is testing a partner through silence instead of direct conversation, with one person expecting the other to guess their dissatisfaction or desires without a word.

## Natural Strength

The natural strength behind these difficulties is a deep sensitivity to a partner's emotional state: the person truly can sense the finest nuances of the other person's moods and needs. The problem arises not from indifference, but from a habitual tendency to hide their own feelings instead of expressing them with the same subtlety with which they perceive the feelings of others.

The second strength behind this is an ability to protect the relationship's intimacy and keep private matters from public view, making this person a reliable partner in the most vulnerable areas.

## Expression in the Positive

In the positive, this energy resolves its typical difficulties through gradual but conscious openness. Partners agree to voice important feelings directly, even when uncomfortable, rather than leaving each other to guess. Conflict becomes an opportunity to better understand what has gone unspoken, not an accusation that one person has failed to understand the other.

The positive also brings an ability to recognize when silence is harming the relationship more than an uncomfortable truth could, and to choose to speak.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, the difficulties intensify: a partner feels emotional distance without understanding its cause because the other person does not explain what is happening. Arguments arise over why important subjects are never discussed directly, with each person feeling held at arm's length.

Another typical negative expression is the accumulation of unspoken dissatisfaction, which eventually erupts in a sharp emotional outburst. To the partner it seems sudden and disproportionate because they had no idea that tension had been building gradually.

## What Can Pull This Energy into the Negative

Escalation occurs most often when a partner insists on immediate, complete openness, activating a deep fear of becoming too vulnerable. A second trigger is past experience in which openness in a relationship led to pain or betrayal of trust.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It helps to openly name the moment when silence begins to feel like distance and work with the partner to find a way of sharing important things at a safe pace. A second step is to check in regularly about whether anything remains unspoken, rather than assuming that silence means there are no problems.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to voice important feelings in time instead of accumulating silent distance that ultimately harms the relationship more than an honest conversation ever could.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- What am I hiding from my partner while hoping they will guess it themselves?
- Do I test my partner's feelings through silence instead of direct conversation?
- What am I truly afraid of when I avoid complete openness in a relationship?

## Practical Plan

1. Have a conversation with your partner about one unspoken feeling that has been building for a long time.
2. Once this week, consciously voice an important need directly instead of expecting your partner to guess it.
3. Ask your partner directly whether they feel emotional distance, and listen to the answer without becoming defensive.
