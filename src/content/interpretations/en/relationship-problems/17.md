# 17. The Star — Common Relationship Difficulties

## Brief Essence

A typical difficulty of this energy in a relationship is the need for constant admiration and recognition from a partner, due to which any reaction that is not bright enough is perceived as not enough love. The second typical node is a partner who never seems to be truly present, because he lives in his own dreams and fantasies, leaving the other face to face with the everyday reality of the relationship.

## Natural Strength

The natural force behind these difficulties is the ability to inspire a partner, to see the best in him and to maintain hope even in difficult periods for the relationship. The problem is not because of the light itself, but because it either begins to demand constant validation in return, or is detached from the real needs of the relationship.

## Expression in the Positive

On the plus side, this energy makes the relationship a space of mutual inspiration: partners sincerely support each other's dreams and talents, and each feels like a slightly better version of himself next to the other. Difficult periods pass more easily, because there is always someone in the couple who is able to remind of perspective and meaning even in dark times.

## Expression in the Negative

In the negative, difficulties intensify: one of the partners may constantly need admiration and confirmation of his own uniqueness, taking offense at any criticism or mundanity of the relationship. Or the partner is emotionally "absent" — immersed in his own dreams, projects or fantasies so much that the other has to bear the weight of the couple's real worries on his own.

## What Can Push You Into the Negative

More often than not, aggravation occurs when one of the partners does not receive recognition or support for his talents and dreams outside the relationship for a long time, and begins to seek overcompensation for this within the couple. The second trigger is disillusionment with the reality of the relationship, when the partner escapes into the fantasy of the "perfect" instead of working on what is actually there.

## How to Return the Energy to the Positive

It is useful to learn to openly name the need for recognition and support, instead of silently expecting admiration from a partner and being offended when it does not come. The second step is to consciously bring dreams and inspiration back into the reality of the relationship through concrete actions together, rather than leaving them only as beautiful conversations or fantasies.

## Main Task

The main task is to learn to be a source of sincere, stable inspiration for the partner, while remaining really present in the everyday life of the couple, and not only in joint dreams of the future.

## Questions for Self-Reflection

- How much do I need admiration or recognition from my partner, and how do I feel when I don't get it?
- Am I (or my partner) running away in dreams and fantasy instead of being present in the real, sometimes mundane moments of the relationship?

## Practical Plan

1. The next time you feel the need for recognition from your partner, say it directly instead of waiting silently or resenting it.
2. Choose one common dream or idea and take the first concrete step together towards its realization this week.
3. Once a month, check together whether you devote enough time and attention to the real, everyday life of the couple, and not just to joint fantasies about the future.
