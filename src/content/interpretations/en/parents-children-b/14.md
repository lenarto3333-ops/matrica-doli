The 14th arcana gives a natural sense of balance and harmony, but in the negative it turns into endless trying to please everyone at once: you avoid hard choices and open conflict, and put off important family decisions until the tension builds up on its own.

**What to avoid in communication:** glossing over disagreements to keep up an appearance of calm; putting off important family decisions "for later"; trying to please everyone at once, which leaves no one with a straight answer.

**How to turn it positive:** use your gift for balance to find compromise through open conversation, not by avoiding the topic. Harmony, in the positive, doesn't come from staying silent about a problem—it comes from resolving it gradually and honestly.
