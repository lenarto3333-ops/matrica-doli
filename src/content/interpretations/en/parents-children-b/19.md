The 19th arcana carries colossal, powerful energy. The most common pitfall here in the negative is "scorching" loved ones with your love and overprotectiveness. If you fixate on just one person (a parent or a child) and pour all the force of your control and worry onto them, the effect is destructive—like a ray of sunlight focused through a magnifying glass onto a single point.

**What to avoid in communication:** trying to force your worldview onto loved ones or tightly control their every move; excessive demands for order, discipline, and obedience; instilling guilt in children or fostering emotional dependence on a parent's judgment.

**How to turn it positive:** the Sun should shine on many, not just one person. To bring your relationships into harmony, it's vital that you channel your intense energy outward into the wider world: express yourself through creativity, take part in community activities, get involved in various projects. Then a warm, serene, and joyful "sun" will settle over your family.
