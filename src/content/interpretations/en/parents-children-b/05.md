The 5th arcana gives a talent for explaining complex things and passing on experience, but in the negative it turns every conversation into an endless lecture: you instruct your loved ones, tell them "the right way" to do things, and don't notice that dialogue has become a monologue from the podium.

**What to avoid in communication:** a lecturing tone and constant moralizing instead of empathy; dismissing your children's or parents' experience as "wrong" simply because it differs from yours; insistently pushing your own beliefs and traditions onto others.

**How to turn it positive:** share your knowledge not as a teacher but as someone close who is genuinely curious to hear another point of view. Ask more than you preach—then your wisdom will feel like support instead of pressure.
