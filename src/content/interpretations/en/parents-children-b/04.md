The 4th arcana gives a gift for building order and taking responsibility, but in the negative it turns the home into a barracks: rules and discipline become more important than feelings, and any deviation from the established order is treated as a threat that must be corrected firmly.

**What to avoid in communication:** an authoritarian tone and demands for unquestioning obedience; punishing the smallest rule-breaking without explaining why; emotional coldness disguised as "it's for their own good."

**How to turn it positive:** keep the structure, but add warmth to it—explain the rules instead of simply demanding compliance, and leave room for flexibility. Order in the family should rest on trust, not discipline alone.
