The 8th arcana gives a sharp sense of fairness and the ability to weigh a situation carefully, but in the negative it turns into a judge's bench: you hand down verdicts to your loved ones, rigidly tally who's right and who's wrong, and hold on to every "injustice" for a long time.

**What to avoid in communication:** setting up rigid rules and constantly monitoring your loved ones' behavior; holding impossibly high standards of fairness and condemning the smallest slip-ups; comparing your children or relatives against each other—"who got more."

**How to turn it positive:** turn harsh judgment into honest dialogue—explain your expectations and admit your own mistakes as openly as you point out others'. Fairness, in the positive, is built on mutual trust, not verdicts.
