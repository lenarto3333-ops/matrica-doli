The 18th arcana gives a delicate sensitivity to atmosphere and hidden moods, but in the negative it turns into a fog of things left unsaid: you grow anxious, imagine what others must be thinking and feeling, and instead of a direct conversation, you fill the family with an emotional rollercoaster of suspicion and hints.

**What to avoid in communication:** projecting your own fears onto a child or parent without checking them in words; leaving things unsaid and dropping hints instead of asking directly; anxious control born from assumptions rather than facts.

**How to turn it positive:** check your feelings with words—ask directly instead of guessing. Your sensitivity to the unspoken becomes a gift to your family when you name your anxiety out loud instead of silently passing it on to your loved ones.
