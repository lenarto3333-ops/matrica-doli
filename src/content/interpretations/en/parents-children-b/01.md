The 1st arcana grants powerful willpower and a gift for taking the initiative. The main pitfall in the negative is turning into a family dictator: you make decisions for everyone without asking what your loved ones think, sincerely convinced you're doing it for their own good.

**What to avoid in communication:** the habit of deciding everything for your child or partner, leaving them no room to choose for themselves; sharp orders instead of suggestions; irritation when loved ones act more slowly, or differently, than you would yourself.

**How to turn it positive:** direct your will toward supporting your loved ones' initiative instead of replacing it with your own. Ask before you decide, and let your children and parents try their own path—even if it doesn't look like the most efficient one to you.
