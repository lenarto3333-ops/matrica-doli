The 10th arcana gives a knack for sensing the right moment and adapting easily to change, but in the negative this turns into unpredictable attention within the family: today you're engaged and generous, tomorrow you're distant and absorbed in something new, and your loved ones never know what to expect from you.

**What to avoid in communication:** sharp swings in involvement—from hyper-engagement to suddenly vanishing from your child's or parent's life; trying to forcibly hold on to a relationship or a person who is naturally changing; making promises that depend on your mood in the moment.

**How to turn it positive:** build stable anchors into your relationships—regular rituals of attention that don't depend on shifting circumstances. Then this energy's changeability becomes flexibility, not a source of anxiety for your loved ones.
