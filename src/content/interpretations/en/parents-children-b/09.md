The 9th arcana gives depth of self-knowledge and the ability to find answers in silence, but in the negative it turns into a locked door: instead of talking through a difficulty with your loved ones, you withdraw into yourself, grow distant, and your family gradually learns not to bother you with "unnecessary" conversations.

**What to avoid in communication:** pointedly withdrawing and avoiding conflict instead of resolving it; long silences that your loved ones experience as rejection; refusing to share even important feelings under the excuse "I'll work it out myself."

**How to turn it positive:** give yourself time alone, but come back to your loved ones ready to talk about what was going on inside you. Your depth becomes a real support for the family when you share your insights, instead of just disappearing into silence with them.
