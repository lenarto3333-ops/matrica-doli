The 16th arcana gives resilience in a crisis and the ability to adapt quickly, but in the negative it fills the family with a subconscious expectation of disaster: you unconsciously brace for everything to "fall apart any moment," and sometimes even provoke conflict yourself just to release the tension.

**What to avoid in communication:** stirring up anxiety and catastrophizing minor problems; picking fights during calm periods because the calm itself feels suspicious; harsh, destructive words in an argument that are hard to take back afterward.

**How to turn it positive:** learn to trust the stable, calm periods in your family instead of hunting for a hidden threat in them. Your resilience in a crisis becomes a real support for your loved ones when you draw on it after genuine difficulties arise, rather than manufacturing them yourself.
