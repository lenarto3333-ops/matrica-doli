The 6th arcana gives sincerity and a heightened sense of choice, but in the negative this energy turns into an endless back-and-forth: you waver between decisions, unconsciously compare your children or partner to some "alternative," and fill the family with an atmosphere of unpredictability and unspoken jealousy.

**What to avoid in communication:** comparing your loved ones to each other or to other families; inconsistent decisions, with one rule today and another tomorrow; unconsciously singling out a "favorite" through small signs of attention.

**How to turn it positive:** learn to make a conscious choice and stick with it, explaining your reasoning to your loved ones instead of leaving them to guess. This energy's sincerity shows its positive side when everyone in the family feels chosen exactly as they are, without comparisons.
