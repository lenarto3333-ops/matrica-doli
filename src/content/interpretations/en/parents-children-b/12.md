The 12th arcana gives the ability to see a situation from an unusual angle, but in the negative it turns into the posture of a martyr: you get stuck waiting, passively endure an uncomfortable position within the family, and silently reproach your loved ones for not noticing your sacrifice.

**What to avoid in communication:** performative patience and phrases like "I'll endure anything for the family"; passivity in situations that call for direct action or conversation; silent reproach instead of an honest conversation about your own needs.

**How to turn it positive:** use your gift for pausing to make sense of a situation, then act, rather than staying stuck in inaction. Seeing things from another angle, in the positive, helps you find unexpected solutions—it isn't an excuse to stay stuck.
