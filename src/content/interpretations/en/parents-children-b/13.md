The 13th arcana gives a powerful gift for letting go of what has run its course and starting new chapters, but in the negative this energy shows up as abruptly severing ties: instead of a difficult conversation, you'd rather slam the door—emotionally or literally—leaving your loved ones without explanation.

**What to avoid in communication:** sudden, irreversible decisions made in a moment of hurt ("we're done talking"); threatening to cut off the relationship as a way to pressure a child or parent; avoiding difficult conversations by cutting off contact altogether.

**How to turn it positive:** use your ability to let go not to end relationships, but to end outdated patterns of communication within them—forgive old grievances and start a new chapter together, instead of writing the person off.
