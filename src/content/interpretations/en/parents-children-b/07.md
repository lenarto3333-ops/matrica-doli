The 7th arcana gives powerful drive and the ability to move toward a goal, but in the negative this energy becomes a nonstop race: you drag your loved ones along at your own pace, demand the same results and speed from them, and get irritated when someone "slows down" the shared momentum.

**What to avoid in communication:** pressuring others to "be first" or "keep up with me"; competing with your child or parent for leadership in the family; impatience with other people's slower pace or mistakes along the way.

**How to turn it positive:** direct your drive toward shared family goals instead of racing your loved ones, and let everyone move at their own pace. In the positive, winning means the whole family arrives together—not that you cross the finish line first, alone.
