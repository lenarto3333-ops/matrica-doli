The 20th arcana gives an honest view of the path you've walked and the ability to draw conclusions, but in the negative this gift turns into an inner tribunal: you pass harsh judgment not only on yourself but on your loved ones—constantly bringing up your parents' or children's past mistakes instead of moving forward.

**What to avoid in communication:** dragging up your loved ones' old mistakes in every new argument; passing strict moral judgment on decisions that can no longer be changed; demanding repentance or endless explanations for the past.

**How to turn it positive:** use your capacity for reflection to draw conclusions and move forward, instead of holding your loved ones in the past. Real healing comes through forgiveness—of yourself and of your family—not through handing down a verdict.
